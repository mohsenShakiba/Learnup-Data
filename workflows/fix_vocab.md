# Fix vocab
Extract the vocabs from the conversation and put them in the vocab list.

## Rules
[] IF the vocab is new and it has never been used in the previous conversations put them on the first line, after the title
[] If the vocab has been used in previous conversations put them in the second line.

Example:

Conversation 1:
New vocabs: Hi, Nice, Meet, You
Used vocabs: 
Hi, Nice to meet you.

Conversation 2:
New vocabs: Hello, Day
Used vocabs: Nice
Hello, Nice day.
