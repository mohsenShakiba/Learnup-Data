Meeting My Parents' Flight
A: Aren't you leaving for the airport soon? I thought you wanted to set off at half past four.
B: I have a bit more time now. I've just checked the live flight info, and my parents aren't due in until six.
A: Oh, what happened? Did they miss their connection in London?
B: Yes, their first plane had an unexpected wait on the ground before it took off. Flights up from London are quite frequent, though, and Mum texted to say they're on the next one now.
A: That's lucky. And since they've already been through passport control in London, they won't have to queue again when they get here.
B: Exactly. Once they collect their suitcases, they can walk straight out.
A: When will you head out, then? Most people will be driving home from work after five, and it's just started raining heavily.
B: True. Maybe I'll leave at quarter to five after all and take the river road.
A: Take care round that sharp curve before the bridge, because water always collects there in heavy rain. Will you be waiting outside the main entrance, or using the car park?
B: The short-stay car park. The road outside the entrance is strictly for buses and taxis now, so I booked a space online and got a good reduction on the price.
A: Oh, nice. Is that the new car park behind the airport hotel?
B: Yes, and the email came with a detailed map of the way in, so I won't get lost.
A: By the time you bring them back here, it'll be past seven, so I'll have put Leo to bed.
B: Thanks. They won't have eaten a proper meal since lunch, so your vegetable soup will be very welcome. Once Dad's had a hot bowl of soup, he'll be back to his usual self.
A: I'll get the sofa bed ready in the study now so they can settle in straight after dinner. Do they know where you're meeting them?
B: Not yet, because Mum's phone is off for the flight. I'll send her a text now to say I'll be inside, and it'll come through almost instantly when they land.
A: Good idea. Message me before you leave the car park so I can heat the soup.
B: I will. Thanks!

Vocabulary: curve, detailed, frequent, info, instantly, reduction, self, settle, strictly, unexpected
