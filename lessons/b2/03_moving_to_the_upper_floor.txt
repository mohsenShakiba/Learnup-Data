Moving Offices Tomorrow
A: Are we really moving upstairs tomorrow? That's awful timing, with our report deadline on Friday.
B: Yes. IT will be installing the new monitors upstairs tomorrow, and our internal network will be down for the day.
A: Wait, I still need last month's sales figures, and I couldn't open the database just now.
B: They're moving the database this afternoon. They'll have finished the transfer by six, so I can send you the figures before I leave.
A: That's a relief. I can work on the report from home tomorrow, then.
B: Do you need anything else before we pack up our desks?
A: No, just those figures. There are only a few minor changes to make after that. When can we use the new room?
B: The schedule says they'll have connected all the computers by nine on Friday morning.
A: That doesn't leave much time if anything goes wrong.
B: True. Let's plan to send the report from home on Friday morning too, and we can settle in after the deadline.
A: Good idea. Who's letting the moving team in tomorrow?
B: I am. I'll be in the meeting room downstairs from eight in case they need anything.

Vocabulary: database, deadline, install, internal, minor, monitor, relief, settle, timing, transfer
