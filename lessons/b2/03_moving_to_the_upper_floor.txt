Moving to the Upper Floor
A: Are we really moving to the upper floor tomorrow? The deadline for our report is Friday.
B: Yes. The technical crew will be installing the new monitors all day tomorrow. Our internal network will be off until the evening.
A: But I need the customer records to finish the report.
B: I'll have finished the database transfer by six tonight. Then I'll send you a copy of the figures.
A: That's a relief. I can work on the report at home while the crew is here.
B: Do you need anything else before we pack up?
A: No, those figures are all I need. When can we use the new desks?
B: The schedule says they'll have connected all the computers by nine on Friday.
A: That doesn't leave much time if something goes wrong.
B: Then let's plan to send the report from home on Friday too. We can settle in after the deadline.
A: Good idea. Will you be here to let the crew in tomorrow?
B: Yes, at eight. I'll deal with any minor problems while you finish the report.

Vocabulary: crew, database, deadline, install, internal, minor, monitor, relief, settle, transfer, upper
