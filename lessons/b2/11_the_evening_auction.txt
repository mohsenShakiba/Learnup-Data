The Evening Auction
A: Where's the clock for tonight's auction? Did Mr. Harris bring it in?
B: Yes, this afternoon. It's in the glass case by the door.
A: Is it genuine? I thought it was a modern copy when I saw the photo.
B: Mrs. Green, the local art collector, checked the mark on the back. She says it's two hundred years old.
A: Have we set a minimum price?
B: The committee agreed to start the bidding at two hundred pounds.
A: What about the ring? Do we give it back if nobody offers enough?
B: Yes. There's a reserve price of a hundred pounds on that one.
A: Good. Any idea how many people are coming?
B: Fifty have booked since the newspaper article. The publicity seems to have helped.
A: My neighbour can't come, but she wants to contribute. Can she send us some money?
B: She can make a donation directly to the shelter. I have their details here.
A: I'll send them to her. Then let's put the numbered cards on the chairs before everyone arrives.

Vocabulary: auction, bid, collector, committee, contribute, donation, genuine, minimum, publicity, reserve, shelter
