The Evening Auction
A: Have all the items for tonight's evening auction been placed on the front tables?
B: Almost. I'm still waiting for the tall clock, which was promised by Mr. Harris last week.
A: Actually, he brought it in an hour ago. Before I arrived, the committee had already locked it in the glass case because it's so precious.
B: Is it a genuine old clock, or a modern copy?
A: Mrs. Green, who is a local art collector, checked the mark on the back and said it was made two hundred years ago.
B: That's wonderful! Did the committee set a minimum price for it?
A: Yes, the initial bid will start at two hundred pounds, and we've put a reserve price on the small ring too.
B: Do you think enough wealthy guests will come tonight to buy the expensive pieces?
A: I hope so. Thanks to the newspaper publicity this week, fifty people had booked seats before the office closed yesterday.
B: Even if someone can't afford the big items, they can still contribute a small donation at the door.
A: Exactly. Every pound we raise tonight will be given directly to the winter shelter for homeless families.
B: Let's put the numbered cards on the chairs now so we're ready when the doors open.

Vocabulary: auction, bid, collector, committee, contribute, donation, genuine, homeless, initial, minimum, precious, publicity, reserve, shelter, wealthy
