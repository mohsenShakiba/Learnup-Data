Preparing for a Supplier Call
A: Are you ready for our three o'clock call with the box company? This negotiation could affect our delivery costs for the whole year.
B: Yes, I've checked the numbers. We had a disagreement last month over their late deliveries, so we need a clear stance today.
A: They want to increase their prices by ten pounds a box; nevertheless, their delivery times have actually got worse since January.
B: Exactly. Furthermore, two of our biggest customers have asked us to switch to sustainable paper packaging by the autumn.
A: Can this company undertake to supply recycled boxes in the sizes we need?
B: They say they can, but we must insist on a written promise about delivery dates before we sign a new contract.
A: What if a delay does arise during our busy winter season?
B: Then the contract should state that they reduce the bill for any week when they miss our Friday deadline.
A: Do you think we can reach a fair resolution on the price this afternoon?
B: If we agree to a two-year deal, I think we can get a good outcome for both sides.
A: All right, let's call them now and start with the recycled box samples.

Vocabulary: arise, disagreement, furthermore, insist, negotiation, nevertheless, outcome, resolution, stance, sustainable, undertake
