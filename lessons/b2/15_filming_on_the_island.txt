Filming on the Island
A: Can we watch that recording you made near the shore on our island trip?
B: Yes, here it is. The water was shallow enough to see the sand clearly.
A: Look at that little one! It swims right beside the camera.
B: It seemed curious about the lens. I just held the camera still.
A: Were you in the water?
B: No. I lay on a flat rock and held the camera just under the water. That's why the angle is so low.
A: Look, they're emerging from under that rock. You captured the whole group!
B: I waited quite a while for that. I didn't want to disturb them.
A: What's that moving across the sand? Is it a bigger fish?
B: Just the shadow of our boat. It was floating nearby.
A: Oh, I see. Can I send this to my dad? He loves wildlife.
B: Of course. I'll send you the file. That little fish is my favourite part of the whole trip.

Vocabulary: angle, beside, capture, curious, disturb, emerge, float, lens, nearby, shadow, shallow, shore, wildlife
