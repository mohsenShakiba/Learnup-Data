Filming on the Island
A: Can we watch what you filmed near the shore on your island trip?
B: Sure, here it is. See that little blue fish right at the edge of the water?
A: Oh, there it is! It's swimming right beside your camera.
B: Yeah, it seemed really curious about the lens. It kept coming back for another look.
A: I can't tell from this angle. Were you in the water with it?
B: No, I was lying on a flat rock at the edge with the camera just under the water.
A: Look, loads more are coming out from under those stones now! How long were you lying there?
B: About twenty minutes. I had to keep really still so I wouldn't disturb them.
A: Wait, what's that dark shape moving across the sand? A bigger fish?
B: No, just the shadow of a little fishing boat that was floating nearby.
A: Ah, I see it now. Could you send this to me? My dad loves wildlife, and he'd really enjoy it.
B: Of course, I'll send you the file now. That curious little fish was my favourite bit of the whole trip.

Vocabulary: angle, beside, curious, disturb, float, lens, nearby, shadow, shore, wildlife
