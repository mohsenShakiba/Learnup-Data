Painting the Guest Room
A: Look at the time. It's already six, and we've barely painted half the guest room.
B: This bit above the window is so awkward to reach. I wish we had a taller ladder.
A: Careful, don't stretch that far. That small ladder doesn't look very steady anyway.
B: You're right, I'm coming down. I won't attempt that top part until we can borrow a bigger one from next door.
A: Good idea. Anyway, if we don't stop now, we'll be late for our anniversary dinner.
B: Oh, of course. The table's booked for eight, isn't it? If only we hadn't started this today.
A: Tell me about it. Once we'd had that big crack in the wall filled, I thought the painting would only take an afternoon.
B: Do you think we should ask a decorating firm to finish the rest?
A: Let's see how we feel tomorrow. We've bought all the paint now, anyway.
B: Fair point. Can you help me carry the ladder out into the hall? I don't want to drag it and scratch the floor.
A: Sure, I'll take this end. Then I'll wash the brushes while you put the paint away.
B: Good plan. That still gives us an hour to get ready.

Vocabulary: anniversary, attempt, awkward, barely, crack, drag, firm, ladder, scratch, stretch
