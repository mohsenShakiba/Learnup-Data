Painting the Guest Room
A: Look at the time. It's already six, and we've barely painted half the guest room.
B: This bit above the window is so awkward to reach. I wish we'd borrowed that tall folding ladder from next door.
A: Careful, don't stretch that far. That small ladder doesn't look very steady anyway.
B: You're right. I'm coming down. I'll ask next door if we can borrow theirs tomorrow.
A: Good idea. Anyway, if we don't stop now, we'll be late for our anniversary dinner.
B: The table's booked for eight, isn't it? If only we hadn't started this today.
A: I know. Once we had that big crack in the wall filled, I thought we'd get the painting done in an afternoon.
B: Do you think we should ask a decorating firm to finish the rest?
A: Maybe. Let's see how we get on with the taller ladder first.
B: All right. Can you help me carry this one into the hall? I don't want to drag it and scratch the floor.
A: Sure, I'll take this end. Then I'll wash the brushes while you put the paint away.
B: Good plan. That still gives us an hour to get ready.

Vocabulary: anniversary, awkward, barely, crack, drag, firm, folding, ladder, scratch, stretch
