Painting the Guest Room
A: Look at this wall. We've been painting for four hours, and we've barely covered half the guest room. I wish we had a taller ladder for these high sides.
B: Me too. My back hurts, and this awkward curve above the window is impossible to reach properly. If only the ceiling weren't so high!
A: Honestly, I wish we hadn't attempted to do such extensive work by ourselves before our anniversary dinner.
B: You're right. If only we'd hired that local decorating firm last month! We could have had the whole room painted in two days.
A: At least we got the deep crack in the wall filled on Tuesday, and we had those loose power wires made safe by the worker.
B: True, that was our biggest obstacle. Listen—can you hear that water tap running in the bathroom again?
A: Yes! I wish it wouldn't make that noise every five minutes. We really need to get the bathroom pipe repaired next week.
B: And I wish your brother wouldn't call every hour to ask if the house is ready for Saturday's party.
A: He's just excited. Have we had the wooden chairs covered with the new green fabric yet?
B: Yes, the shop modified the seats and delivered them this morning. They look very neat, so be careful not to scratch the wood when we move the table back.
A: Good. Once this wall is dry, we can put up the table decorations and get the curtains washed tomorrow afternoon.
B: Deal. Let's finish this last wall so we can finally sit down and rest.

Vocabulary: anniversary, attempt, awkward, barely, crack, curve, decoration, extensive, fabric, firm, ladder, loose, modify, neat, obstacle, scratch, tap, wire
