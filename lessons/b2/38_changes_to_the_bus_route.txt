Changes to the Bus Route
A: Have you seen the notice at the bus stop about our morning route to the station?
B: No, I've just got here. Are they changing the times again?
A: They want to extend the route to the new homes west of town, just past the aircraft factory.
B: Does it say how much longer our journey will take?
A: Yes. It says the journey will take forty minutes instead of twenty-five.
B: Fifteen minutes longer? We'll miss the eight o'clock train if we leave at the same time.
A: That's what worries me. But the people working at the factory have no public transportation yet.
B: Fair point. Does it say whether the buses will run more often?
A: Yes, every ten minutes instead of every twenty. The factory's started making parts for a new jet, so there are more people travelling there now.
B: Having buses every ten minutes could help, but we already lose time in the traffic near the bridge.
A: Didn't the mayor promise a bus lane there last year?
B: Yes. That could reduce the delay. Are passengers getting a say before this is final?
A: Yes. They want input from people who already use the bus. There's a link to a form here. It says to keep our comments brief.
B: I'll mention the bridge. Last week a mass of people got off the five o'clock bus and filled the whole path. It was hard just to get across the road.
A: Yes. We'll have to check the new times. Oh, here comes our bus now.

Vocabulary: aircraft, brief, delay, extend, input, jet, lane, mass, mayor, transportation
