Choosing Next Summer's Holiday
A: The cruise offer I showed you ends on Friday. Seven nights, five ports, and all the meals are included. Should we book it?
B: I can see the appeal, but I have mixed feelings about a week on a ship. We'd only get a few hours in each port.
A: True, but we'd see five places instead of one. And the ship looks magnificent, with three pools and a theatre.
B: It also has three thousand other passengers. I'd rather stay in one place and actually get to know it.
A: Like the resort your sister went to? It's on a tiny tropical island. It looks gorgeous, but there's nothing else there.
B: She loved it. She said the beach was the best she'd ever seen, and she learned to dive while she was there.
A: I've always wanted to learn to dive. Could we do that from the ship as well?
B: I bet it costs extra. My colleague went on a cruise last year, and everything apart from the meals cost extra, the trips on shore, for instance.
A: Then it's potentially not as cheap as it looks. Let me check what's actually included.
B: And the resort? Does the price include flights?
A: No. The flights are separate, and it's a twelve-hour journey. We'd lose a day at each end.
B: So one is crowded and rushed, and the other is isolated and a long way away. Neither is perfect.
A: What would you be satisfied with? A quiet beach, somewhere to swim, and decent food?
B: Yes, if we're not limited to one restaurant every evening.
A: The website says the resort has four restaurants, and a boat goes to the main island twice a day.
B: Then I'd choose the resort. It's our first overseas holiday together, and I want it to be memorable, not just cheap.
A: Agreed. I'll message them tonight about a room for our dates and ask about the diving course.
B: And I'll look at flights. We should book both before the prices go up.

Vocabulary: appeal, bet, cruise, dive, gorgeous, instance, isolated, limited, magnificent, memorable, mixed, overseas, potentially, resort, satisfied, tropical
