Choosing Next Summer's Holiday
A: The cruise offer I showed you ends on Friday. Seven nights, five ports, and all the meals are included. Should we book it?
B: I can see why you like it, but I have mixed feelings about a week on a ship. We'd only get a few hours in each port.
A: True, but we'd see five places instead of one. And look at this cabin! You can sit outside and watch the sea from your own room.
B: It also has three thousand other passengers. I'd rather stay in one place and actually get to know it.
A: Like the hotel your sister went to? It's on a tiny tropical island. It looks gorgeous, but wouldn't we get bored with nothing else around?
B: She didn't. She said the beach was the best she'd ever seen, and she learned to dive while she was there.
A: I've always wanted to learn to dive. Maybe we could do that from the ship too.
B: I bet it costs extra. My colleague went on a cruise last year. Almost everything apart from the meals was extra, even the short trips on shore.
A: Let me check the offer page. You're right, shore trips aren't included, so the cruise is potentially much more expensive than it looks.
B: What puts you off the island, then?
A: I'm worried I'd feel cut off, especially if we're limited to one hotel restaurant every evening.
B: We wouldn't be. My sister said the hotel has four restaurants, and a boat goes to the main island twice a day.
A: Oh, I didn't realize that. I'd be happy staying there if there's an easy way to visit the main island.
B: The only problem is the flights. They aren't included in the hotel price, and it's a twelve-hour journey.
A: Still, it's our first overseas holiday together. I want it to be memorable. Let's go for the island.
B: Agreed. I'll message them tonight about a room for our dates and ask about the diving course.
A: And I'll compare the flight prices so we can book both this week.

Vocabulary: bet, cabin, cruise, dive, gorgeous, limited, memorable, overseas, potentially, tropical
