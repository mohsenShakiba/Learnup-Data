Buying a Second-Hand Tent
A: Thanks for setting the tent up in the garden so I could check it properly.
B: No problem. It's easier to examine the frame and cloth when it's standing up.
A: How does the frame go together? My old tent only had straight sticks.
B: You insert each long stick into this cloth tube, and it forms a gentle curve over the roof.
A: And the side walls stay almost vertical, so there's plenty of room to sit up inside.
B: Yes. Once the frame is in, you pull the sides out and push the pins firmly into the ground.
A: Is there somewhere to hang a small light inside?
B: There's a strong hook in the middle of the roof, right above the door.
A: What about the floor? The grass is wet from last night's rain, so we can test whether water comes through.
B: Step inside and feel it. The base is thick rubber, so your sleeping bag stays dry even on wet ground.
A: You're right, it's literally completely dry in here. Let me test how the front door fastens.
B: Pull it slowly at the bottom; it was a bit sticky after sitting on the garage shelf all winter, so I cleaned it this morning.
A: It slides up and down smoothly now. The outside colour is a little dull from the sun, but the cloth itself looks strong.
B: If you want it for forty pounds, I'll include the spare pins and the repair bag, which will equip you for a week in the hills.
A: That's fair. Let's take it down together, and you can show me how to pack it into the bag.

Vocabulary: curve, dull, equip, firmly, hook, insert, literally, rubber, slide, sticky, vertical
