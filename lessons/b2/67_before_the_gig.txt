Before the Gig
A: Are you still coming to the gig tonight? I have both tickets on my phone.
B: Yes, but I'm a bit worried about the volume. My ears hurt after the last one.
A: Was that at the same place?
B: No, a smaller place. We were right beside the speakers, and it was really intense. Getting through the crowd afterwards was a nightmare too.
A: Let's stay further back tonight. I know you like this punk band, but we don't have to stand at the front.
B: Would we still be able to see the stage from the back?
A: There's a raised area at the back, near the exit. I've stood there before, and the view was reasonably good.
B: That sounds better. I'm more conscious of how loud these places can be now. I don't want to damage my hearing.
A: Have you brought any ear protection?
B: Yes, these. The packet explains how to fit them.
A: I've brought the same ones. Shall we read the instructions before we put them in?
B: Good idea. And if the noise still feels excessive, I'd like to leave, even if they haven't finished.
A: Of course. We can step outside whenever you need to. You don't have to stay just because I want to.
B: Thanks. I was worried you'd be annoyed if we missed your favourite song.
A: I'd rather go home with you. We can hear the song another time.
B: All right. Let's try these now, then get there early enough to find that area at the back.
A: I'll open the tickets while you read the instructions.

Vocabulary: conscious, excessive, gig, hearing, intense, nightmare, protection, punk, reasonably, volume
