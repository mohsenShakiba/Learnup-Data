Before My First Flight
A: Could I speak to someone before we get on the aircraft? It's my first flight, and I'm losing my nerve.
B: Of course. I'm one of the cabin crew. Is there something in particular you're worried about?
A: The landing, mostly. I've been watching the weather forecast all morning.
B: Have you heard something that's worrying you?
A: It mentioned severe storms near the airport we're flying to. I keep imagining us trying to land in one.
B: I'll ask the captain whether those storms are expected to affect our arrival. I can't tell you from that forecast alone.
A: Thank you. I know I won't overcome this anxiety in five minutes. I just don't want to panic once the doors close.
B: Would it help to talk through what happens after you take your seat?
A: Yes, please. Will there be time to settle in before we depart? The board says ten, and it's almost that now.
B: Yes. Once everyone is on, we'll give the safety instructions and check that everyone is ready before we leave.
A: I've been trying to hide how nervous I am. Somehow, saying it out loud makes it a little easier.
B: You don't have to hide it from us. Let a crew member know if you need help during the flight.
A: My sister is coming with me, but our seats are in different rows. Could we sit together?
B: Let me check what's available. Do you have both seat numbers?
A: Yes, they're here on my phone. Sitting with her would help.
B: Thanks. Wait here with her while I check the seats and ask about the weather.

Vocabulary: aircraft, anxiety, depart, forecast, landing, nerve, overcome, panic, severe, somehow
