After Three Missed Classes
A: I've missed three classes, and I'm not sure where to start. Is the written assessment still due on Friday?
B: Yes, but you can have an extension until Monday because you've been ill.
A: Thank you. I've been trying to read the whole textbook, but I'm getting tired very quickly.
B: You don't need all of it for this task. Concentrate on the two core chapters we discussed in class.
A: My copy is an older edition. Are the chapter numbers the same?
B: No. Look for the chapters called "City Life" and "Public Transport". I'll write down the titles for you.
A: That helps. Do I need to include everything from both chapters?
B: Only the points that are relevant to your question. The objective is to compare two ways of travelling to work.
A: So I could compare the bus with driving, using my own journey as an example?
B: Exactly. Give a detailed example, then discuss the advantages and disadvantages of each.
A: Is there a particular format for the report?
B: Follow the plan on this sheet. You'll find an example below it.
A: Great. What about revision for next week's test?
B: Finish the report first. We have a revision class on Tuesday, and you can bring your questions then.
A: All right. Do I submit the report through the course website?
B: Yes. I'll change your deadline there today so it shows Monday.

Vocabulary: assessment, core, detailed, edition, extension, format, objective, relevant, revision, submit, textbook
