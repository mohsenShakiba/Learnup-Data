Recovering from a Throat Infection
A: Come and sit at the kitchen table. I'll heat some soup for your breakfast.
B: Thanks. Just a small bowl, please. I need to leave for work soon.
A: You're going in? You could barely swallow your soup last night.
B: It's easier this morning. My fever's gone down, and I only have a slight headache now.
A: What did the doctor say about going back?
B: She urged me to rest until Friday. I know I need time to recover, but they're finishing the monthly sales report today. My figures are crucial.
A: Do you still need to send them over?
B: No, I sent them yesterday. I just feel bad leaving the others to finish the report.
A: Was your manager sympathetic when you called?
B: Yes. She assured me they could manage. She even offered to finish my section herself.
A: Then let her. You can thank her when you're back.
B: You're right. And I don't want anyone else catching this infection. I'll message her now and say I'm staying home.
A: Do you want me to bring your phone?
B: It's here. I'll turn it off for a while once I've heard back from her.
A: Probably wise. Your soup's ready.
B: Thanks. I'll eat this and go back to bed.

Vocabulary: assure, crucial, fever, infection, recover, slight, swallow, sympathetic, urge, wise
