Recovering from a Throat Infection
A: Are you really planning to go into the office today? You still sound rough when you speak.
B: I feel much better than yesterday. My fever has gone down, and my headache has gone too.
A: What about your throat? Yesterday evening you could barely swallow your soup.
B: It still hurts when I swallow, and I have a slight pain in my chest, but the medicine from the clinic is helping.
A: Even so, the doctor said a throat infection like this takes several days to heal. She urged you to rest until Friday.
B: I know, but we're finishing the monthly sales report this week. My section is crucial, and the others can't complete the report without my figures.
A: Couldn't you send them the figures from home instead of going into the office all day?
B: The files are already on the shared drive, actually. I just thought I should be there in case our manager has questions.
A: If she needs to check anything, she can call or message you. Was she sympathetic when you phoned her on Monday?
B: Very. She assured me that the team could manage and told me not to rush back before I was ready.
A: Then it wouldn't be wise to go in today. If you don't give yourself time to recover properly, you'll be ill all week.
B: Plus, I suppose the others won't thank me if I pass this infection on to the whole office.
A: Exactly. Have you taken your morning tablet yet?
B: Not yet. The box says I have to take it with food, and I haven't had breakfast.
A: Sit down and I'll make you some warm soup and a hot drink. Do you want one of those vitamin drinks as well?
B: Yes, please. My sister says they make you more immune to winter colds, though I know they aren't a quick cure.
A: Well, a hot drink will help your throat anyway. Message your manager while I heat the soup.
B: I will. I'll tell her I'm staying at home today, and that she can call me if she needs anything for the report.

Vocabulary: assure, crucial, cure, fever, heal, immune, infection, recover, slight, swallow, sympathetic, urge, vitamin, wise
