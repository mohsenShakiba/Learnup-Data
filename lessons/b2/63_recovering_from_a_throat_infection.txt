Missing the Cup Final
A: Come and sit at the kitchen table. I'll heat some soup for your breakfast.
B: Thanks. Just a small bowl, please. I need to leave for work soon.
A: You're going in? You could barely swallow your soup last night.
B: The fever's gone down. I can swallow again. It's talking that still hurts.
A: What did the doctor say about going back?
B: She told me to avoid speaking until Friday. It's going to take time to recover. Trouble is, we're doing a live broadcast today.
A: Haven't they got someone who can do your part? Is the cup final today?
B: Yes. I've done the reporting on the team all year. It's a chance to interview the captain, who took a season off with depression. He wants people to know what helped him come back.
A: I know. Did you talk to the chief producer yesterday?
B: Yes. She said she'd already asked Jo to take my place. Jo's worked on sporting events before, so she knows the teams.
A: Then send her your notes. One more day of shouting over a crowd won't help your throat heal.
B: You're right. And I don't want anyone else catching this infection. I'll tell Jo what the player wanted to talk about so she can ask him about it herself.
A: Do you want me to bring your phone?
B: It's here. I'll turn it off for a while once I've heard back from her.
A: Probably wise. Your soup's ready.
B: Thanks. I'll eat this and go back to bed.

Vocabulary: broadcast, chief, depression, fever, infection, recover, reporting, sporting, swallow, wise
