The Greenhouse While We're Away
A: Here's the key to the side gate. Thanks again for looking after the greenhouse while we're away.
B: Happy to help. Is this tap by the gate working again now?
A: It is, but please use the water in the tank first. It's still about half full.
B: Will half a tank last the whole week in this heat?
A: Probably not. Just use the tap once it runs out. We're trying not to waste tap water while the council's warning about a shortage.
B: Makes sense. Do the herbs need as much water as the tomatoes?
A: No, much less. Since they're in the shade against the wall, half a can does all of them, and the tomatoes take two full cans.
B: Right. Roughly how long does it all take?
A: Ten or fifteen minutes. When the soil's dry, it takes a moment to absorb the water, so pour it in slowly.
B: No problem. Some of these tomatoes look almost ready to eat. Should I leave them on the plants?
A: Please pick any red ones and take them home! We've had a much bigger crop than we expected this summer.
B: Lovely, thank you. Have a great trip, and text me if you remember anything else.
