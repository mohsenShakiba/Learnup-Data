The Greenhouse Crops
A: Have you checked the water level in the main water tank behind the farm building?
B: Yes, it's still almost full. Even if this summer drought lasts another month, we won't face a serious water shortage for our vegetables.
A: That's good news. Most of the farms in this rural area are worried about their wheat fields.
B: Field crops are dry now, but inside our glass greenhouse we can control the heat and limit direct sun exposure.
A: True. By ten o'clock tomorrow morning, the new solar machines will be running, and we'll be pulling cool water up from the deep well.
B: What about the young herb plants on the middle tables? They looked a bit dry yesterday.
A: I've put a dark net above the roof to give them more shade, and I added liquid mineral food to their soil this morning.
B: Will the plants absorb all that water before the afternoon heat arrives?
A: Yes, watering early is the best method in organic agriculture.
B: How much of our summer crop will be ready for the town grocery shops next week?
A: By Friday afternoon, our workers will have picked four hundred boxes, so our total output will be higher than last July.
B: So a large proportion of our summer loan will have been paid off by the end of August.

Vocabulary: absorb, agriculture, crop, drought, exposure, greenhouse, grocery, herb, mineral, organic, output, proportion, rural, shade, shortage, solar, tank, wheat
