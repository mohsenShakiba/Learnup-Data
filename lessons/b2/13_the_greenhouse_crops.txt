The Greenhouse While We're Away
A: Thanks for looking after the greenhouse while we're away. I'll leave the key with you tonight.
B: No problem. Is the tap by the gate working now?
A: Yes, but use the water in the tank first. It's still half full.
B: Will that last the whole week in this drought?
A: I'm not sure. If it runs out, use the tap. The council has asked everyone to use less because of the water shortage.
B: Do the herbs need as much water as the tomatoes?
A: No. I've marked the amounts on these containers. The small one is for the herbs in the shade by the wall.
B: And the large one is for the tomatoes? Roughly how long does it all take?
A: Yes. It takes about fifteen minutes. The soil takes a while to absorb the water, so pour it slowly.
B: Fine. Are any of the tomatoes ready to pick?
A: Yes, we've had a bigger crop than I expected. Please take some home each day.
B: Lovely. I'll come over tonight so you can show me what to do before you leave.

Vocabulary: absorb, council, crop, drought, greenhouse, herb, roughly, shade, shortage, tank
