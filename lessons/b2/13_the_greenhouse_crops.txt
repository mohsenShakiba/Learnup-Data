Watering the Garden While We're Away
A: Here's the key to the side gate. Thanks again for looking after the garden while we're away.
B: Happy to help. Is this tap by the gate working again now?
A: It is, but please use the water in the tank first. It's still about half full.
B: Will half a tank last the whole week in this heat?
A: Probably not. Use the tap once it runs out. The water company asked us to use less this summer.
B: Do the roses need as much water as the tomatoes?
A: No, much less. They're in the shade against the wall. Half a can does them all, and the tomatoes take two full cans.
B: Right. Roughly how long does it all take?
A: Ten or fifteen minutes. Pour slowly and aim for the roots. If the soil's really dry, the water runs straight off the top.
B: Got it. What's this little stick with the pointed end for? Do I dig a hole next to each plant?
A: No, just push it in with your thumb and pull it out. If the soil on it feels wet, that plant doesn't need any water. And pick any red tomatoes you want!
B: Thanks. I'll send you a picture if I'm not sure. Are the tiny holes in some of the tomatoes anything to worry about?
A: Just leave those ones. I found a little green worm in one yesterday.
B: Then I'll check them before I put them in my lunch! Have a great trip.

Vocabulary: dig, pointed, root, rose, roughly, shade, tank, tap, thumb, worm
