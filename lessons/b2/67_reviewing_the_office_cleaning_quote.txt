Reviewing the Office Cleaning Quote
A: Thank you for sending the cleaning proposal. Before we proceed, could we go through the monthly charge?
B: Of course. The quote includes comprehensive cleaning of the floors, desks, kitchens and windows.
A: The total seems excessive for an office of our size. Is there any room for negotiation?
B: Yes. We assumed daily usage. Is the office occupied five days a week?
A: No, usually only on Mondays, Tuesdays and Thursdays. We work from home on Wednesdays and Fridays.
B: Then less frequent visits could mean a considerable saving. Would you like a quote for those three days?
A: Yes, please. Could your team clean the kitchen and bathrooms on every visit and the meeting room once a week?
B: I'll adjust the schedule. Would you still like us to clean the windows each month?
A: No, we already have a window cleaner. Please remove that from the proposal.
B: Of course. I'll list each job separately so you can see how the total is calculated.
A: Thank you. We sometimes hold events at the weekend. Could we book an extra visit afterwards?
B: Yes, but there would be a separate charge.
A: Please include the price for an extra visit in the quote. Any extra work would need our prior approval.
B: Understood. We won't undertake additional work without written permission.
A: That addresses my concerns. When can you send the revised quote?
B: This afternoon. If you accept it, we can arrange a date for the first visit.
A: Please send the new schedule with it. I'll need to check both with our manager before we agree.
B: Certainly. I'll send both together.

Vocabulary: comprehensive, considerable, excessive, frequent, negotiation, occupy, prior, proceed, undertake, usage
