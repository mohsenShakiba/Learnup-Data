Training by the Canal
A: Why are you sitting out here? Are you all right?
B: My left heel hurts. I've been running along the canal, but I had to stop on that steep slope near the bridge.
A: Have you been running further than usual?
B: A little. I've been building up the distance gradually for the annual marathon.
A: What about rest days?
B: I take two a week, and I've been keeping a steady pace. My legs feel stiff today too, though.
A: How long have you been wearing those shoes? They don't look like they're in decent condition.
B: Since last spring. Actually, this one has started to rub against my heel.
A: That sounds uncomfortable. Do you want to come inside and take them off?
B: Yes, please. I won't go running tomorrow.
A: Is the race soon?
B: Not for another two months. There's no need to rush back to training.

Vocabulary: annual, canal, decent, gradually, heel, marathon, pace, rub, rush, slope, steady, steep, stiff
