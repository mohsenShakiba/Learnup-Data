Training by the Canal
A: Why are you sitting on the step? Did you hurt your foot?
B: It's my left heel. I've been running along the canal for the last hour, and I've had to rub it since I came up that steep slope near the bridge.
A: Have you been training for the annual city marathon again?
B: Yes. I've been building up my distance gradually for six weeks, and I'd felt fine until today.
A: You've been going out almost every morning recently. Maybe you haven't been resting enough between runs.
B: Actually, I've been keeping a steady pace and taking two rest days a week, so I don't think that's the problem.
A: Look at the bottom of your running shoes, though. How long have you been wearing that pair?
B: Since last spring, I suppose. I'd planned to buy a new pair last month, but I kept forgetting.
A: No wonder your legs feel stiff! Those old shoes aren't giving your feet decent support any longer.
B: You're right. I'll go inside, stretch carefully, and rest my heel for the evening.
A: Good idea. Once it has recovered, we can go to the sports shop in town and find you a proper pair.
B: Thanks. I'll take a few days off running until the pain goes away.

Vocabulary: annual, canal, decent, gradually, heel, marathon, pace, recover, rub, slope, steady, steep, stiff, stretch
