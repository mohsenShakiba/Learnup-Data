Training by the Canal
A: Why are you sitting out here on the step? Are you okay?
B: It's my left heel. I've been running along the canal, and I had to stop on that steep slope near the bridge.
A: Have you been running further than usual?
B: A bit, yes, but I've been building up the distance gradually for the city marathon.
A: Are you taking enough rest days?
B: Two a week, and I've been keeping a steady pace. My legs feel pretty stiff today too, though.
A: How long have you been wearing those shoes? They look really worn out.
B: Since last spring. Actually, the back of this one has started to rub my heel. I definitely need a decent new pair.
A: No wonder it hurts. Come inside and take them off.
B: Thanks. I'll rest it for a few days and get new shoes at the weekend.
A: Do you still have plenty of time before the race?
B: Yes, two months, so there's no need to rush.

Vocabulary: canal, decent, gradually, heel, marathon, pace, rub, rush, slope, steady, steep, stiff
