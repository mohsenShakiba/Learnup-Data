Sorting the Washing
A: I'm putting a load of washing on. Is everything in this basket ready to go in?
B: Wait, my blue shirt's in there. I accidentally dropped it in with the others.
A: This plain one? What's wrong with putting it in the machine?
B: It's silk. The label says to wash it by hand in cold water.
A: Oh, right, the fabric does feel much lighter than cotton. I'll leave it out, then.
B: Thanks, I'll wash it later. Watch out for my new red top in there too. It hasn't been washed yet.
A: Ah, good point. Better wash that with the dark clothes first. Otherwise, we'll end up with pink shirts.
B: Exactly. Are there enough white things in there for a load on their own?
A: Yes, once I put the dark clothes in a separate pile. Which setting do you use for white shirts?
B: Cotton, and I usually put quiet mode on too.
A: Hang on, it's showing sixty, and these labels say thirty. It won't let me select thirty on the display.
B: Press the little button below the number to change it.
A: There, thirty. I'll start it now.
B: Great, thanks. Give me a shout when it finishes and I'll help hang them up.

Vocabulary: accidentally, basket, fabric, load, mode, otherwise, pile, plain, select, silk
