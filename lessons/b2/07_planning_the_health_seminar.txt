Moving a Talk on Children's Health
A: Can you check the agenda for Saturday's talks on children's health before I send it out?
B: Dr Carter's talk on vitamins is at half past eleven. She told me she needs to leave by eleven for her clinic.
A: Oh, good catch. I'll move her to ten. Is she still bringing those food labels for parents to look at?
B: Yes. Parents asked about protein in school lunches last time. The feedback was that they wanted time to ask her questions about what their kids eat.
A: Let's keep ten until eleven free for her, then. She can do half an hour of questions after the talk. How many people have signed up?
B: Seventy, including some children who have been learning about the organs of the body. They asked if she could bring that big model of a heart again.
A: Seventy? The ground-floor room only seats sixty. Could we use the hall upstairs?
B: The upstairs hall, which seats eighty, is free until twelve. A dad mentioned on the registration form that his hearing is poor. Let's keep him a seat at the front.
A: Good idea. And is there a lift? Last time we had a family turn back at the stairs.
B: Yes, there's one just inside the side door. I'll add that to the form. Could you ask Dr Carter whether she wants us to print anything for her talk? She used a big visual guide to food groups last time.
A: Yes. I'll also let the disability support team know about the room change. They arranged help for two families.
B: Thanks. I'll print copies of the new agenda once she's confirmed.
