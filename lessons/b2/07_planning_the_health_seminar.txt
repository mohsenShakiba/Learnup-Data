Planning the Health Seminar
A: Here's the draft agenda for Saturday's health seminar. Could you check the times before I send it out?
B: Let's see... Ah, Dr. Carter, who runs a nutrition clinic at twelve on Saturdays, has to leave by eleven. You've put her talk at half past eleven.
A: Oh, good catch. I was looking at my old notes. Her talk's half an hour, so I'll move her to ten.
B: She sent me the outline for her talk yesterday, and it looks great. From the feedback after our last event, though, people always want more time for questions.
A: Let's allow half an hour for questions, then. How many people have signed up?
B: Seventy so far. People seem really enthusiastic about it.
A: Hang on, the ground-floor room can only accommodate sixty. We're going to need somewhere bigger.
B: The upstairs hall, which seats eighty, is free until twelve. I checked this morning.
A: Perfect. Let's book that now and close registration as soon as we reach eighty.
B: I'll change the room on the form. Can you send Dr. Carter the updated agenda?
A: Will do, and once she confirms the new time, I'll message the participants about the room change.
B: Great. Then I'll print copies for Saturday morning.

Vocabulary: accommodate, agenda, clinic, draft, enthusiastic, feedback, nutrition, outline, participant, registration, seminar
