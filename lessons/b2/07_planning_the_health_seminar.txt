Planning the Health Seminar
A: Have you finished the draft agenda for next month's health seminar?
B: Almost. Dr. Carter, who runs the city nutrition clinic, has agreed to give the first talk on Saturday morning.
A: That's great news. Her latest article, which was printed in the spring journal, received wonderful feedback from our members.
B: What about the afternoon workshop? Have you heard back from Leo Clark?
A: Yes. Leo, whose research is supported by a local science fund, wants to speak about exercise and sleep.
B: Perfect. Have we chosen a building that can accommodate everyone easily?
A: I called the central medical institute, whose founder used to teach at our college, and their main hall has seats for two hundred people.
B: Will that hall be big enough? Our online registration, which only opened on Tuesday, has already reached a hundred and forty names.
A: If we need extra space, we can also book their library room, which is right across the corridor.
B: Good thinking. Who is leading the final discussion on Sunday?
A: Mina Shah, who works as a senior hospital consultant, is very enthusiastic about leading that session with another specialist.
B: Then we just need to print the programme outline and the evaluation forms for each participant before Friday.

Vocabulary: accommodate, agenda, clinic, consultant, draft, enthusiastic, evaluation, feedback, founder, fund, institute, nutrition, outline, participant, registration, seminar, senior, specialist
