Planning the Health Seminar
A: Here's the draft agenda for Saturday's health seminar. Could you check the times before I send it out?
B: Hang on, Dr. Carter's talk is at half past eleven. She told me she needs to leave by eleven for her nutrition clinic.
A: Oh, good catch. Her outline says she needs half an hour. I'll move her to ten.
B: Can we give her until eleven? The feedback from last time was that people didn't get to ask all their questions.
A: Yes, that gives her half an hour for questions. How many people have signed up?
B: Seventy so far.
A: Hang on, the ground-floor room can only accommodate sixty. We're going to need somewhere bigger.
B: The upstairs hall, which seats eighty, is free until twelve. I checked this morning.
A: Perfect. Let's book that now and close registration as soon as we reach eighty.
B: I'll change the room on the form. Can you send Dr. Carter the updated agenda?
A: Yes. Once she confirms the new time, I'll let the participants know about both changes.
B: Great. Then I'll print copies for Saturday morning.

Vocabulary: accommodate, agenda, clinic, draft, feedback, nutrition, outline, participant, registration, seminar
