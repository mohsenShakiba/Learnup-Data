Planning the Health Seminar
A: Here's the draft agenda for Saturday's health seminar. Can you check the times?
B: Dr. Carter, who runs the nutrition clinic, has to leave at eleven. You've put her talk at half past eleven.
A: Oh, I used the old outline. I'll put her half-hour talk at ten.
B: The feedback from her last talk was good, but people wanted more time for questions.
A: Then let's allow half an hour for those. How many people have signed up?
B: Seventy participants so far. People seem very enthusiastic.
A: Our room can only accommodate sixty. We need more space.
B: The upstairs hall, which seats eighty, is free until twelve. I checked this morning.
A: Let's book it and close registration once we reach eighty.
B: I'll change the room on the form. Can you send Dr. Carter the new agenda?
A: Yes, and I'll check if she's happy with the extra time for questions.
B: Good. I'll print the programme once she confirms.

Vocabulary: accommodate, agenda, clinic, draft, enthusiastic, feedback, nutrition, outline, participant, registration, seminar
