The Hall Smoke Alarm
A: Did you hear the hall smoke alarm again? It's been making that short sound all afternoon.
B: Yeah, with a little flash from the red light each time.
A: Could steam from the shower have triggered it?
B: Nobody's used the shower today. Besides, that usually sets the whole alarm off.
A: Then what does it mean? I'm trying to read and it's really distracting—it's getting on my nerves.
B: Let's check the instructions. First we need to make sure it can still detect smoke properly.
A: Here they are. There's a list of warning sounds on the back.
B: Look—one short sound at one-minute intervals means the battery's running low.
A: Isn't ours connected to the electricity wires in the ceiling?
B: It is, though it has a battery inside too, in case the main power goes off.
A: Can we change that ourselves?
B: Yes, there's a small battery case on the side. Do we have a new battery that size?
A: I'll check the packet in the kitchen, then we can follow the steps here.
B: Great. Afterwards, we just push the case shut until it's secure and press the test button.

Vocabulary: besides, detect, distract, flash, interval, nerve, secure, steam, trigger, wire
