The Hall Smoke Alarm
A: Did you hear the hall smoke alarm again? It's been making that short sound all afternoon.
B: Yeah, with a little flash from the red light each time.
A: Could steam from the shower have set it off?
B: Nobody's used the shower today. Besides, that usually sets the whole alarm off.
A: Then why does it keep doing that? It's getting on my nerves. I can't get through a page of my book.
B: Let's check the instructions. It could be telling us there's a fault.
A: Here they are. There's a list of sounds on the back. It says the frequency of the flashes matters too.
B: One short sound and one flash every minute? That's the warning for a low battery.
A: Isn't ours connected to the electricity wires in the ceiling?
B: It is, though it has a battery inside too, in case the main power goes off.
A: Can we change the battery ourselves? A firefighter came to our building last year and showed us how to check the alarm, but I can't remember how he opened it.
B: Yes. Look under "Commonly asked questions". There's a picture showing where the battery goes.
A: I'll check the packet in the kitchen, then we can follow the steps here.
B: Good. Once the battery's secure, we can push the test button. There's a number to ring here in case of failure.

Vocabulary: besides, commonly, failure, firefighter, flash, frequency, nerve, secure, steam, wire
