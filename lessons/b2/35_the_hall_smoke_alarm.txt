The Hall Smoke Alarm
A: Did you hear that short sound from the hall again? The smoke alarm has been doing it all afternoon.
B: Yes, at regular intervals, with a flash from the red light.
A: Could steam from the shower have triggered it?
B: Nobody has used the shower today. Besides, that would usually activate the full alarm.
A: Then what does this sound mean? It's terribly distracting.
B: Let's check the instructions. We need to know whether it can still detect smoke.
A: Here they are. There's a section about the different warnings.
B: Yes, that's precisely the warning it describes: one short sound with a red light. It says we need to replace the battery.
A: Isn't our alarm connected to the main electricity wires in the ceiling?
B: It is, but it has a battery too, in case the main power goes off.
A: Can we replace that ourselves?
B: The instructions show how to do it. There's a small battery case on the side. Do we have the right battery?
A: I'll check the packet in the kitchen. Then we can follow the steps here.
B: All right. Afterwards, we need to check that the case is secure and test the alarm.
