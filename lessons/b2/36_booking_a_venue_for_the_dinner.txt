Booking a Venue for the Dinner
A: Have many people filled in the questionnaire about our college dinner?
B: Yes, about three dozen so far. There's been a lot of enthusiasm about meeting up after ten years.
A: That's more than I anticipated. We'll need to book a venue soon.
B: I've looked at two places. One is the private hall at the Park Hotel, and the other is the river restaurant.
A: I remember going to a wedding at the Park Hotel. The room was very grand, but I can't recall how many people it holds.
B: It holds sixty, so there's plenty of space. The river restaurant is more lively, though, and the food is cheaper.
A: Does the restaurant have enough space for nearly forty people to sit together?
B: They have an upstairs room that holds forty-five. It would be just for our group, and we can bring our own decorations.
A: That sounds good. What happens if someone cancels after we've confirmed the numbers?
B: They still charge us for that meal. We can change the numbers without a charge until a week before the dinner, though.
A: Then let's ask everyone to pay before that deadline. How should we order the food?
B: The manager asked me to send everyone's meal choices through their website so the kitchen staff can check them.
A: Let's make sure they can provide all the meals we need before we pay a deposit.
B: Agreed. I'll send him the choices we've received so far and ask him to keep the date free while we wait for the rest.

Vocabulary: anticipate, cancel, decoration, dozen, enthusiasm, grand, lively, questionnaire, recall, venue
