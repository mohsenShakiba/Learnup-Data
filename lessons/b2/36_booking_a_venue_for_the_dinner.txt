Booking a Venue for the Dinner
A: Have many people replied to the questionnaire about our college dinner?
B: Yes, about three dozen so far. There's been a lot of enthusiasm about meeting up after ten years.
A: That's more than I anticipated. We'll need to book a venue soon.
B: I've looked at two places. One is the private hall at the Park Hotel, and the other is the river restaurant.
A: I recall going to a wedding at the Park Hotel. That room was very grand, wasn't it?
B: It was, and it's quiet enough for people to talk easily. The river restaurant is much more lively, though, and the food is cheaper.
A: Does the restaurant have enough space for nearly forty people to sit together?
B: They have an upstairs room that holds forty-five. It would be just for our group.
A: That sounds good. What happens if someone cancels after we've confirmed the numbers?
B: They still charge us for that meal. We can change the numbers without a charge until a week before the dinner, though.
A: Then let's ask everyone to pay before that deadline. Have you checked the details people gave us about food they can't eat?
B: I've made a list. The manager asked me to send it via their website so the kitchen can check it.
A: Let's make sure they can provide meals for everyone before we pay a deposit.
B: Agreed. I'll send the list now and ask them to keep the date free while they check.

Vocabulary: anticipate, cancel, dozen, enthusiasm, grand, lively, questionnaire, recall, venue, via
