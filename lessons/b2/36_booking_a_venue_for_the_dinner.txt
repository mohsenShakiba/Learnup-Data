Choosing a Restaurant for the College Dinner
A: How many people have said yes to the college dinner?
B: About three dozen so far. People are really enthusiastic about meeting up after ten years.
A: That's more than I expected. We should book somewhere soon.
B: I've looked at two places. One is the private hall at the Park Hotel, and the other is the river restaurant.
A: I went to a wedding at the Park Hotel once. The room was very grand. There was a ballet class upstairs, though, and we could hear them jumping up and down.
B: The restaurant's more lively. They're holding a punk gig downstairs that night, but it doesn't start until ten.
A: Ten's fine for most of us. Can the restaurant seat nearly forty people together?
B: Yes, we'd be in the upstairs room and we'd finish eating before the band started. It holds forty-five, and we can bring our own decorations.
A: That sounds good. What happens if someone cancels after we've confirmed the numbers?
B: They still charge us for that meal. We can change the numbers without paying extra until a week before the dinner, though.
A: Then let's ask everyone to pay before that deadline. How should we order the food?
B: The manager asked me to send everyone's meal choices through their website so the kitchen staff can check them.
A: Before we book, check the date of the town parade too. Last year the main road closed and my taxi couldn't get near the restaurant.
B: Good point. I'll ask the manager whether we can still get to the front door by car and how late we can stay upstairs after dinner.

Vocabulary: ballet, cancel, decoration, dozen, enthusiastic, gig, grand, lively, parade, punk
