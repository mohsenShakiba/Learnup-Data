Booking a Venue for the Dinner
A: Have many people replied to the questionnaire about our ten-year college dinner?
B: Yes, there's been a lot of enthusiasm. Three dozen people have already said they'd like to come.
A: Thirty-six! That's more than I anticipated. We'll need to book a venue soon.
B: I've looked at two places. One is the private hall at the Park Hotel, and the other is the river restaurant.
A: I recall going to a wedding at the Park Hotel. That room was very grand, wasn't it?
B: It was, and it's quiet enough for people to talk easily. The river restaurant is much more lively, though, and the food is cheaper.
A: Does the restaurant have adequate space for nearly forty people to sit together?
B: They have an upstairs room that holds forty-five. It would be just for our group.
A: That sounds great. What happens if a few people cancel at the last minute?
B: We only pay for the final numbers we confirm a week before the night, so we won't lose money.
A: Then the restaurant is the better choice. Seeing everyone again after ten years will make it a memorable evening.
B: Agreed. By the way, a few people wrote on the form about food they can't eat.
A: Can we send those details to the restaurant via their website once we've booked?
B: Yes, the manager asked for the list two weeks before the dinner. I'll call her now and pay the deposit.

Vocabulary: adequate, anticipate, cancel, dozen, enthusiasm, grand, lively, memorable, questionnaire, recall, venue, via
