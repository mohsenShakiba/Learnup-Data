The Home Insurance Claim
A: Have you spoken to the insurance company about the garden wall?
B: Yes, I've just got off the phone. Because the tree brought it down in that severe storm, we can make a claim.
A: That's a relief. I wasn't sure our coverage included garden walls, or whether they'd say an old wall like that could have collapsed anyway.
B: No, brick walls are covered. Wooden fences are an exception, so we're lucky the tree hit the back wall and not the side fence.
A: Will they pay the full cost of rebuilding it?
B: Almost. We pay the first hundred pounds ourselves, and they'll pay the rest.
A: Great. Will they send an inspector out, or can we get a local building firm in this week?
B: They don't need to visit, as long as we get two written quotes from different firms and send them in with photos of the damage.
A: So we need their approval before any work starts?
B: Exactly. They won't pay out if we start the repairs before they've agreed to the price.
A: How long will it take them to check the quotes?
B: Not long—the woman I spoke to said we should hear back within three days.
A: Good. I'll take photos of the wall and the tree now while it's still light, then sweep the broken bricks off the path.
B: Thanks. I'll call two building firms and see if they can come round tomorrow.

Vocabulary: approval, brick, collapse, coverage, exception, inspector, insurance, rebuild, severe, sweep
