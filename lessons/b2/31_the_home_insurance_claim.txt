The Home Insurance Claim
A: Have you spoken to the home insurance company about the back wall?
B: Yes, I've just finished the call. Since the brick wall collapsed when that tree fell in the storm, we can make a claim.
A: That's a relief. I wasn't sure whether our policy included external walls.
B: It does. Wooden fences are an exception, so we're lucky it was the wall at the back of the garden and not the side fence.
A: Does our coverage include the full cost of rebuilding it?
B: Almost. We have to pay the first hundred pounds ourselves, and they'll pay the rest.
A: Can we ask the local building firm to start this week, then?
B: Not yet. We have to obtain two written quotes from different firms and send them in with photos of the damage.
A: So we need their approval before the work starts?
B: Exactly. They won't pay to rebuild it if we start the repairs before they've agreed to the price.
A: How long will it take them to check the quotes?
B: The woman I spoke to said they're usually quite prompt with claims like this. We should hear back within three days.
A: Good. I'll take photos of the bricks and the tree this afternoon while it's still light.
B: Thanks. I'll call two building firms now and ask if they can come and look at it tomorrow.

Vocabulary: approval, brick, collapse, coverage, exception, external, insurance, obtain, prompt, rebuild
