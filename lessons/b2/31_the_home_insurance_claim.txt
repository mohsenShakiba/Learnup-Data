The Home Insurance Claim
A: Have you spoken to the insurance company about the garden wall?
B: Yes, I've just got off the phone. Since it was the storm that brought the tree down, we can make a claim.
A: We're fortunate, then. I wasn't sure they'd pay for an old garden wall. What did they say about the fence?
B: They'll pay for a brick wall like ours. Wooden fences are an exception, so we're lucky the tree hit the back wall.
A: Will they pay the full cost of rebuilding it?
B: Almost. We pay the first hundred pounds. They'll pay the rest, including repairs to the foundation if the tree damaged that too.
A: Great. Will they send an inspector out, or can we get a local building firm in this week?
B: They don't need to visit, as long as we get two written quotes from different firms and send them in with photos of the damage.
A: So we need their approval before any work starts?
B: Exactly. They won't pay out if we start the repairs before they've agreed to the price.
A: How long will it take them to check the quotes?
B: Not long—the woman I spoke to said we should hear back within three days.
A: Good. I'll take photos now while it's still light, then sweep the broken bricks off the path. Does the price need to include a trip to the dump?
B: Yes. If we want them to pay for it, it has to be on the quote. I'll tell both firms when I call.

Vocabulary: approval, brick, dump, exception, fortunate, foundation, inspector, insurance, rebuild, sweep
