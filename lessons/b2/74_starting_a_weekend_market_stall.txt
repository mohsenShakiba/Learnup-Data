Starting a Weekend Market Stall
A: The market manager has just called. We can have the front stall next Saturday to launch our new cake company.
B: That's wonderful news! Having a place near the main gate should help us gain customers on our first morning.
A: Are you still optimistic that we can bake enough cakes in our home kitchen on Friday night?
B: For the first month, yes. If the demand grows after that, we can rent space in a larger kitchen.
A: How much did it cost to acquire the wooden tables and the cloth roof for the Saturday market?
B: Less than I expected, because I bought them from the founder of the little cake shop on High Street.
A: Oh, did she start out on the market before she opened her shop?
B: Yes, she's a really helpful local entrepreneur, and she told me what sells best before ten o'clock.
A: Did she suggest any ways to get local publicity before our first weekend?
B: She said we should give a few small cakes to the coffee shops nearby and print a simple slogan on our paper bags.
A: Something short about fresh local food would help us establish our name in the town.
B: Exactly. Let's order the paper bags today so they arrive before Thursday.

Vocabulary: acquire, demand, entrepreneur, establish, founder, gain, launch, optimistic, publicity, slogan, stall
