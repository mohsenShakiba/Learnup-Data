Dinner from the Fridge
A: We should use these vegetables tonight. They're going to spoil if we leave them much longer.
B: Soup? We have onions, potatoes and that bunch of carrots. Is there any rice?
A: Half a packet. I was saving it for tomorrow, but we could add a little.
B: Good. I'll chop the onions and put them on. Can you wash the potatoes?
A: Sure. Do the potatoes go straight in raw, or do we boil them first?
B: Straight in raw, once the onions have cooked for a few minutes. Cut them into small pieces so they cook faster.
A: How much water will we need? About a litre?
B: Let's start with half that. We can add more if it's too thick.
A: Do we have any spices we could add for a bit more flavour?
B: There's a hot spice mix in that small tin. Just a little, though. The last soup I made with it was terribly hot.
A: All right. How much soup will this make?
B: Two big bowls. If you want some for lunch too, we can add another potato.
A: Yes, please. I didn't think soup was this quick to make.
B: Yeah, there's hardly any preparation once you've chopped the vegetables. I've been making it most weeks since I started bringing lunch to work.
A: The potatoes are ready. Shall I put them in?
B: Yes, the onions are soft now. Then we'll add the rice and water.

Vocabulary: bunch, chop, flavour, litre, packet, preparation, raw, spice, spoil, terribly
