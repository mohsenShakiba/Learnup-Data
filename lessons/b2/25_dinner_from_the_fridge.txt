Dinner from the Fridge
A: We should use these vegetables tonight. They're going to spoil if we leave them much longer.
B: Soup? We have onions, potatoes, and that bunch of herbs. Is there any rice?
A: Half a packet. I was saving it for tomorrow, but we could add a little.
B: Good. I'll chop the onions. Can you wash the potatoes?
A: Sure. Should the potatoes go in raw?
B: Yes, after we've cooked the onions for a few minutes. Cut them into small pieces so they cook faster.
A: How much water will we need? About a litre?
B: Let's start with half that. We can add more if it's too thick.
A: Do we have any spice to give it more flavour?
B: There's some in the small tin. Just a little, though. It's quite hot.
A: All right. How much soup will this make?
B: Two big portions. If you want some for lunch too, we can add another potato.
A: Yes, please. This needs less preparation than I expected.
B: I've been making it most weeks since I started bringing lunch to work. It's easy to change the vegetables too.
A: The potatoes are ready. Shall I put them in?
B: Yes, the onions are soft now. Then we'll add the rice and water.

Vocabulary: bunch, chop, flavour, litre, packet, portion, preparation, raw, spice, spoil
