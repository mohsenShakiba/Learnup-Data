Choosing a Nursing Course
A: Is this the office for careers advice? I'd like some help choosing a college course.
B: Yes. Come in and take a seat. What would you like to study?
A: Nursing. I've brought the details of two courses, but I'm not sure which one to apply for.
B: Let's have a look. Is there one you prefer so far?
A: The local institute would be easier because I could stay at home. The other course is in another city.
B: Staying at home would help with the cost. Have you looked into funding for either course?
A: The other college offers a scholarship, but only a few students get one. I couldn't rely on that.
B: No, so it's worth comparing the costs without it. What else matters to you about the course?
A: Practical experience. I've been volunteering at the hospital for six months. The nurses there have inspired me to work in healthcare.
B: So you've had a chance to see what the work is like. Is there an area you'd like to specialize in?
A: Working with children. I'm passionate about helping them, but I know I still have a lot to learn.
B: Then this course could be a good fit. It mentions a placement at the local children's hospital.
A: Oh, that sounds ideal. Would I definitely get to go there?
B: It doesn't say whether every student goes there. You'd need to check with the college.
A: I'm going to their open day next Thursday, so I'll ask then. I'd also like to know what support students get. Working with very ill children can be quite emotional.
B: That's worth asking about. Try to talk to some current students too. They can tell you what support they've had during their placements.
A: Good idea. I'll write those questions down and take them with me.

Vocabulary: emotional, funding, healthcare, inspire, institute, nursing, passionate, placement, scholarship, specialize
