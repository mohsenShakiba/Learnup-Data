A Second Look at the Market Story
A: Have you had a chance to read my article on the new street market? It goes to print tomorrow.
B: Yes, I've just finished it. The photos are lovely, but there's a pretty big gap in the story.
A: Really? In what way?
B: You only interviewed people selling things at the market. They're delighted at all the extra customers. Have you spoken to the neighbours?
A: I spoke to a couple of shop managers on my way home. What are you worried I've missed?
B: Both sides need a say if the story's going to be balanced. Did the managers mention why the police were there on Saturday?
A: Yes. One showed me a photo of armed police outside his shop. He'd heard there'd been a shooting, but he didn't see it himself.
B: Have the police confirmed what happened? The photo tells us the police were there, but it doesn't tell us why.
A: I called them. They said someone had found a bullet in the street. Nobody was hurt. Someone's claiming online that a man's already in jail for shooting at a shop window.
B: People will think somebody's sitting in a cell already. Did you ask the police about that post? We can't print a claim like that without checking.
A: They said nobody's been arrested. They're asking for witnesses to call the number in this graphic. Can I put it just under the map?
B: Yes. Let's make the street names bigger too. Can people tell from the article where the bullet was found?
A: I'll check the street name again. I'll change the headline too. It makes it sound like we know there was a shooting.
B: That should fix it. Once this page is done, the whole paper will be ready for publication.
A: Can we check one more thing? There's a quote from a shop manager saying this might threaten the future of the market. Has anyone actually said it could close?
B: I've heard nothing about closing it. The minister who opened it said she'd visit again in the spring. Did the manager give you a reason?
A: No, he didn't. I'll ask the manager why he's worried before we print his comment. If it's just a guess, I can leave it out.
B: Thanks. I'll check it first thing in the morning before I send the file to print.

Vocabulary: armed, balanced, bullet, cell, graphic, jail, minister, publication, shooting, threaten
