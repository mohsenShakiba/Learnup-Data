Meeting a Dog at the Shelter
A: We're interested in adopting this dog. Could we spend a little time with him?
B: Of course. I'll bring him into the garden. He's a bit nervous when people approach him quickly.
A: Is he aggressive, or just afraid?
B: We haven't seen any aggressive behaviour here. He usually moves away from strangers, so let him come to you.
A: Do you know much about his past?
B: He was abandoned outside another shelter. We don't know how long he had lived with his previous family.
A: Poor thing. Would he adapt to a home with children? Ours are twelve and fourteen.
B: Perhaps, but we'd want to see how they interact first. Could you bring them on your next visit?
A: Yes. They're keen, but I've told them it may take patience to build a bond with him.
B: That's sensible. He occasionally takes food from my hand now, but he stayed away from me at first. Here's a little piece you can offer him.
A: He's coming towards us. Should I hold it out?
B: Yes, with your hand low and still. We use food as a reward when he comes over calmly.
A: There, he's taken it. Oh, he's moving away again. Did I do something wrong?
B: No, just give him space. Don't let that discourage you. Several short visits may help him feel more comfortable.
A: Can we come back on Sunday with the children?
B: Yes, at eleven. We can spend some time in this garden again before you decide.

Vocabulary: abandon, adapt, adopt, aggressive, approach, bond, discourage, interact, occasionally, patience, reward
