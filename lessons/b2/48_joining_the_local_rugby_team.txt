Joining the Local Rugby Team
A: Are you still trying to recruit people for your rugby team? I saw the notice at work.
B: Yes, especially in defence. Have you played for a team before?
A: At college, but that was five years ago. How serious is your league?
B: We want to win, but it's still a Sunday activity. Most of us work all week.
A: That's good. In my last team we blamed each other whenever we lost.
B: We have clear rules on conduct. Don't blame each other, shout at people or argue with the referee.
A: Does everyone actually follow them when you're losing?
B: Mostly. After our last defeat, the captain tried to motivate us instead of blaming anyone.
A: That sounds like a good leader. How often do you train?
B: Wednesday evenings. Come this week and see how you get on with us before you join.
A: I'd like that. Do I need to buy a team shirt?
B: No, our sponsor pays for those. You just need boots and something comfortable for training.
A: Who's your next opponent?
B: The hospital team, this Sunday. We also have a local tournament next month, so it's a good time to join.
A: Then I'll start with Wednesday's training. I need to find out whether I can still run for more than ten minutes.
B: Come at six. We'll warm up first, and you can take a break whenever you need one.

Vocabulary: conduct, defeat, defence, league, motivate, opponent, recruit, referee, sponsor, tournament
