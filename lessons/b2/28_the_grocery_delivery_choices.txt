The Grocery Delivery Choices
A: Why is the grocery bill nearly ten pounds higher than the total we saw yesterday?
B: Look at the receipt—they sent organic coffee and organic oranges instead of our usual ones.
A: Did we agree to let them send more expensive items?
B: Look at the settings. Letting them replace missing items is automatic unless you turn it off. I thought the prices would be similar.
A: Apparently the price can differ quite a bit. What's this extra pound on the receipt?
B: That's for the bags. Look at the bottom. "Bags are charged additionally." Here's where we choose what happens when they're out of an item.
A: What are the options?
B: Let them pick another item, choose a second option ourselves, or ask them to leave the item out.
A: Let's do that for the coffee. As for the oranges, I'd be happy with the cheaper, oddly shaped ones. I'm only going to cut them up.
B: Agreed. Half our dairy items were missing too, so they didn't charge us for those.
A: Maybe we should book a morning delivery. There might be a better selection before they start packing all those orders for the afternoon rush.
B: Good idea. I still like the convenience of having the groceries delivered—I just want to know what we'll pay.
A: Me too. Can we return the coffee and oranges they sent this time?
B: Yes. There's a message asking us to call if we don't want them. Have you opened either bag?
A: No, they're both still on the table.
B: Good. I'll ring the shop before we put them away.

Vocabulary: additionally, apparently, automatic, convenience, dairy, differ, grocery, rush, selection, shaped
