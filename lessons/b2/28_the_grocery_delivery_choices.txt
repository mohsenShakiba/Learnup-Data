The Grocery Delivery Choices
A: Why is the grocery bill nearly ten pounds higher than the total we saw yesterday?
B: Look at the receipt—they sent organic coffee and organic oranges instead of our usual ones.
A: Did we agree to let them send more expensive items?
B: Look at the order settings—letting them replace missing items is automatic unless you turn that setting off. I thought they'd pick something equivalent in price.
A: Apparently, that isn't how it works. I didn't expect the final total to vary this much.
B: Under "Preferences", we can choose what happens when an item is out of stock.
A: What are the options?
B: Let them pick another item, choose a second option ourselves, or ask them to leave the item out.
A: Then let's choose a second option for things we really need. We can do without oranges for a few days.
B: Agreed. Half our dairy items were missing too, so they didn't charge us for those.
A: Maybe we should book a morning delivery next time, while they still have a better selection in stock.
B: Good idea. I still like the convenience of having the groceries delivered—I just want to know what we'll pay.
A: Me too. What about today's coffee and oranges? Did they tell us about the changes before the driver arrived?
B: Yes, there's a message from this morning. I hadn't read it. It says to contact the shop if we don't want an item they've chosen.
A: Let's ask what we can do, then. I haven't opened that bag yet.
B: I'll call them. Next time I'll read that message before the driver comes.

Vocabulary: apparently, automatic, convenience, dairy, equivalent, grocery, organic, preference, selection, vary
