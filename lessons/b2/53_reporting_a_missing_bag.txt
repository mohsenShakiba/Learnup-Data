Reporting a Missing Bag
A: Hello. I called the police about a missing bag, and they told me to come here.
B: Yes, this is the right desk. Take a seat and tell me what happened.
A: I think it may have been a theft. My bag disappeared from the cafe opposite the train station this afternoon.
B: I'll take the details. Did anyone witness the theft?
A: Not that I know of. I'd left the bag on the hook under my table while I went up briefly to collect my coffee, and it was gone when I came back.
B: Did you ask the cafe staff whether someone had handed it in?
A: Yes. They checked behind the counter and around the tables. It was such a shock that I kept looking in the same places.
B: Take your time. What was inside?
A: My cards, my driving licence, and a bit of money. My phone was in my pocket.
B: Have you had the cards blocked?
A: Yes, I sorted that out before I came here. I suspect the man at the next table took the bag, though I didn't actually see him touch it.
B: What made you think it was him?
A: He kept staring at my table when I sat down. Then both he and the bag were gone when I returned.
B: I'll make a note of that, though we can't be sure it was him if nobody saw him take it.
A: Of course—I know I don't have any proof. I just wish I'd kept the bag with me.
B: Can you describe what he was wearing?
A: A dark coat—black or dark blue, I couldn't tell in that light. Still, I swear he was watching my table.
B: That's all right. I'll write down "dark coat". Now, what did the bag look like—is there anything on it to distinguish it from other bags?
A: Yes, it's small brown leather with a round button on the front and a scratch on the side.
B: Thank you. I'll add that to the report and take your contact details.

Vocabulary: briefly, distinguish, hook, licence, shock, stare, suspect, swear, theft, witness
