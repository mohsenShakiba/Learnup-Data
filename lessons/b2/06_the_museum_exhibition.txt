The Museum Exhibition
A: I've brought the leaflets for the exhibition opening. Where do you want them?
B: On the front desk, please. Have you seen the temporary barrier across the entrance to the side room?
A: Yes. Is that room still closed? The winter exhibit is in there.
B: One of the wooden panels came loose this morning. It's being repaired now.
A: Have any of the artworks been damaged?
B: No, they're all safe. The paintings have been moved to the main hall while the work is being done.
A: So do I need to change the route for my tour?
B: Yes. The manager has approved a new route through the main hall. Here's the map.
A: Thanks. I've been assigned a group at two. Where should I meet them?
B: At the front desk as usual. I'll explain the change when they collect their tickets.
A: These leaflets still show the old route. Shall I put a copy of the new map inside each one?
B: Please. I'll help you. We still have half an hour before the doors open.

Vocabulary: approve, artwork, assign, barrier, exhibit, leaflet, loose, opening, panel, temporary
