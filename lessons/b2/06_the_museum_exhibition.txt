The Museum Exhibition
A: I've brought the leaflets for today's exhibition opening. Where would you like them?
B: On the front desk, please. Have you seen the barrier across the entrance to the side room?
A: Yes, I just walked past it. Why is that room closed?
B: One of the wooden wall panels came loose early this morning. It's being repaired at the moment.
A: Oh no. Have any of the exhibits been damaged?
B: No, everything's safe. All the artwork from that wall has been moved into the main hall while the work is being done.
A: Does that mean I need to change the route for my afternoon tour?
B: Yes. A temporary route through the main hall has been approved by the manager. Here's the new map.
A: Thanks. I've been assigned the two o'clock group. Since the main hall will be busier than usual, should I still meet them here at the desk?
B: Yes, meet them here as usual. I'll explain the room change when they collect their tickets.
A: These leaflets still show the old route. Shall I put a copy of the new map inside each one?
B: Good idea, thanks. I'll help you. We still have half an hour before the doors open.

Vocabulary: approve, artwork, assign, barrier, exhibit, leaflet, loose, opening, panel, temporary
