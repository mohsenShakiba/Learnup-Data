The Museum Exhibition
A: I've brought the programmes for today's exhibition opening. Where would you like them?
B: On the front desk, please. Did you see the sign across the entrance to the side room?
A: Yes, I just walked past it. Why is that room closed?
B: One of the wooden wall panels came loose early this morning. It's being repaired at the moment.
A: Oh no. Is the big painting with the golden frame all right?
B: Yes, it's fine. We've moved all the artwork from that wall into the main hall.
A: Good. That's the one that gets the most attention. I'm taking a group of teens round at two. Where should I start?
B: The manager's approved a temporary route through the main hall. Here's the new map.
A: Thanks. Their teacher wants them to find out about the origin of one painting for an artistic project. Will the notes about who painted it be in the main hall too?
B: Yes, next to each painting. They can take photos of the notes if they like.
A: These programmes still show the old route. Shall I put a copy of the new map inside each one?
B: Good idea, thanks. I'll help you. We still have half an hour before the doors open.

Vocabulary: approve, artistic, artwork, golden, loose, opening, origin, panel, teens, temporary
