Volunteering at the Nature Park
A: Welcome to the nature park. Before the gates open, I'll show you the path where we do our morning observation of the wildlife.
B: Thanks. What are those wooden boxes high up on the trees near the old cave?
A: Those are for the small brown bats that sleep there during the day and fly out when it gets dark.
B: I had no idea they lived so close to the town. Are they rare in this area?
A: Quite rare now. Protecting their forest territory is essential for their winter survival.
B: What's that blue rope stuck in the bush beside the stream?
A: Rubbish left by visitors, I'm afraid. We always clear it away because old rope is harmful to any small creature that gets caught in it.
B: Have you ever had to rescue an animal that got caught in rubbish here?
A: Yes, a young dog last month. That's why we remind school groups to respect the existence of the animals that live in these woods.
B: Do the children have to walk in complete silence along this path?
A: Not complete silence, but we ask them not to shout or run, because sudden noise will scare the birds away from the trees.
B: That makes sense. I'll put this rope in my rubbish bag now while we walk up to the lake.

Vocabulary: bat, bush, cave, creature, existence, harmful, observation, rescue, scare, silence, survival, territory
