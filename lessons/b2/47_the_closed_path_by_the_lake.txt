The Closed Path by the Lake
A: The gate across our usual path is closed. Are they doing work by the lake?
B: No, look at the notice. Some rare birds are raising their young there, so that section is closed until July.
A: Are those the little birds we saw digging for worms here in April? I didn't know they stayed for the summer.
B: These are different. Look closely at that one by the water. Can you see the white feathers on its head?
A: I can just see one. Is that red spot on its cheek or on the side of its neck?
B: On its cheek, I think. Let's stay here. The notice says our presence near the water can scare the parents away.
A: Right. Does the closed area include the grass beside the path?
B: Yes. The birds lay their eggs in the grass. Even walking through with a dog on a lead can be harmful.
A: Fair enough. I'll try taking a photo from here. Is that a bird just beyond those trees?
B: Yes, by the stream. It's coming this way. Keep your dog close. I know he's loyal, but he likes to run after things.
A: I'll wait, then. Oh, there's a bag caught in the fence inside the closed area.
B: I see it. Those bags can be deadly if a bird gets its head inside one and can't get out.
A: I'll tell the park staff. Can you keep the dog here while I go?
B: Of course. Their office is just by the gate. I'll wait here with him.
A: Hang on, let me get a photo first. That bird's much closer now. What's that flying across the picture?
B: A bat, I think! Let me see the photo. I didn't expect to see one before dark.

Vocabulary: bat, beyond, cheek, closely, deadly, feather, harmful, loyal, presence, stream
