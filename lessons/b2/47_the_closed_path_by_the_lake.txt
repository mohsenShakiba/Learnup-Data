The Closed Path by the Lake
A: There's a barrier across our usual path. Are they doing work by the lake?
B: No, look at the notice. Some rare birds are raising their young there, so that section is closed until July.
A: I didn't know there were rare species here. I thought they were the same birds we see in every park.
B: These are different. Look closely at that one by the water. Can you see the white feathers on its head?
A: Yes. Could we go round the barrier for a better view? We wouldn't make any noise.
B: The notice asks people to stay back. Apparently, our presence can keep the adults away from their young even if we're quiet.
A: Then we should stay here. Does the closed area include the grass beside the path?
B: Yes. They're trying to preserve the birds' habitat, so we shouldn't wander off the main path either.
A: Fair enough. I'll try taking a photo from here. Is that a bird just beyond those trees?
B: Yes, beside the stream. It seems to be moving towards us.
A: I'll wait, then. Oh, there's a bag caught in the fence inside the closed area.
B: Yes, I see it. That sort of litter could blow into the lake.
A: Should we tell the park staff? They could collect it without us going in.
B: Good idea. Their office is by the gate. Let's show them where it is on our way out.
A: All right. Look, the bird's much closer now. I can get a photo from here.
B: Great. Let's get your photo, then go and tell the staff about that bag.

Vocabulary: beyond, closely, feather, habitat, litter, presence, preserve, species, stream, wander
