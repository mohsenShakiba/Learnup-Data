A Reference for a Former Colleague
A: Hi, is this a good time to talk? I'm at home filling in a job application, and I need a reference.
B: Hi! Yes, I have a few minutes. How can I help?
A: Could I put your name down as a referee? Each applicant has to give two names.
B: Of course. What's the job?
A: It's with a small advertising company. They have a reputation for bold, creative ideas.
B: That sounds like a good fit. You're skilled at coming up with ideas that get people's attention.
A: I hope so. The main criterion is experience managing projects from start to finish.
B: Well, you did that here. I always trusted your judgement, and you consistently met your deadlines.
A: Thanks. They might ask about my technical expertise too. I've been using different software in my current job.
B: I'll have to acknowledge that I haven't seen your recent work. Could your current manager talk about that?
A: Yes, she's my other referee. She knows I'm applying.
B: Good. Is there anything you're worried they might ask me about?
A: They might ask about a weakness. I was hoping you wouldn't mention how nervous I used to get during presentations.
B: You were nervous at first, but you improved. I could explain how you practised and then gave the final presentation to that big customer.
A: I'd be happy with that. I don't expect you to pretend everything was perfect.
B: I won't. I genuinely enjoyed working with you. I'd be happy to recommend you.
A: Thanks. I'll send you the job description and the deadline for the reference after this call.
B: Great. Let me know when you've submitted the application.

Vocabulary: acknowledge, applicant, consistently, criterion, expertise, genuinely, judgement, reputation, skilled, weakness
