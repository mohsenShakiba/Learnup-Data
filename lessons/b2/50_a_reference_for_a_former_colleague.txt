Asking a Colleague for a Reference
A: Hi, is this a good time to talk? I'm applying for a new job and need a reference.
B: Hi! Yes, I have a few minutes. How can I help?
A: Could I give the advertising company your number? They want to talk to someone who worked with me at the theatre.
B: Of course. What's the job?
A: They want someone to run a small team doing make-up for TV ads. A lot of the work's with actors, so it's close to what I did with you.
B: That sounds like a good fit. You're skilled at coming up with ideas that get people's attention.
A: They asked about that green creature we made for the children's show. Do you remember whether it was you or me who suggested giving him a single eye? I'd hate to take credit for your idea.
B: The eye was yours. I suggested changing the colour. I had mixed feelings about the blue at first, but the critic from the local paper loved the face.
A: Could you mention the screen test? They asked about the technological side of the job. They want to know if I've seen what my work looks like on camera.
B: Yes. In the theatre the blue looked quite dramatic, and it worked remarkably well in the film too. I'll tell them you tried it under both sets of lights.
A: That's what I remember. Thanks. They've already spoken to the producer, so they know how many people worked on it.
B: Good. Is there anything you're worried they might ask me about?
A: They might ask about a weakness. I was hoping you wouldn't mention how nervous I used to get during presentations.
B: Yes, the first time was hard for you. But by your last presentation you had the confidence to stay and answer every question, even the difficult ones.
A: I'd be happy with that. I don't expect you to pretend everything was perfect.
B: I won't. Watching you overcome that fear was one of my favourite things about working with you.
A: Thanks. I'll send you the job description and the deadline for the reference after this call.
B: Great. Let me know when you've sent the application.

Vocabulary: confidence, critic, dramatic, make-up, mixed, overcome, remarkably, skilled, technological, weakness
