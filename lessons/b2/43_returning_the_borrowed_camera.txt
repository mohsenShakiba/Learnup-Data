Returning the Borrowed Camera
A: Here's your camera. I need to confess something before you put it away.
B: What's happened?
A: It slipped off the table yesterday. There's a nasty mark on the side and a tear in the case.
B: Let me look. Does it still work?
A: Yes, but I haven't checked every setting. I'm ashamed I didn't call you straight away.
B: Why didn't you?
A: I was desperate to get it repaired before you needed it. The shop couldn't look at it until next week.
B: I'd have preferred to hear about it yesterday. I need it for work tomorrow.
A: I know, and I regret waiting. I've felt miserable about it all morning.
B: I appreciate your honesty now. But we need to find out whether I can use it tomorrow.
A: Could we test it here? If there's a problem, I'll pay for you to hire one while yours is being repaired.
B: Yes. It's fortunate that you left the case on. That may have protected the lens.
A: The shop said the same, but they couldn't promise there was no damage inside. Will you forgive me?
B: Of course. It was an accident. Just tell me sooner if anything like this happens again.
A: I will. Shall I clear the table so we can take some test photos?
B: Please. I'll get my other lens so we can see whether the camera works with that one too.

Vocabulary: ashamed, confess, desperate, forgive, fortunate, honesty, miserable, nasty, regret, slip, tear
