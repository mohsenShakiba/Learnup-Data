Returning the Borrowed Camera
A: Here's your camera back. Before you open the bag, I need to tell you what happened to it.
B: What's happened?
A: It slipped off the table yesterday. There's a nasty mark on the side and a tear in the case.
B: Let me look. Does it still work?
A: Yes, though I haven't checked every setting. I'm ashamed to say I didn't call you straight away.
B: Why didn't you?
A: I was desperate to get it repaired before you needed it. The shop had a quick look at the outer case, but they can't open the camera until next week.
B: I'd have preferred to hear about it yesterday. I need it for work tomorrow.
A: I know—I really regret waiting. I've felt miserable about it all morning.
B: Well, I appreciate your honesty, anyway. Let's see whether I can still use it tomorrow.
A: Could we test it here? If there's a problem, I'll pay for you to hire one while yours is being repaired.
B: Yes. Good thing you left the case on—that might have protected the lens.
A: The shop said the same, though they couldn't promise there was no damage inside. I hope you can forgive me.
B: Of course. It was an accident. Just tell me sooner if anything like this happens again.
A: I will. Shall I clear the table so we can take a few test photos?
B: Please. I'll get my other lens so we can see whether the camera works with that one too.

Vocabulary: ashamed, desperate, forgive, honesty, miserable, nasty, outer, regret, slip, tear
