Hiring Short-Term Staff
A: Do you have a minute before you go back to the packing floor? I've just checked next month's orders.
B: Sure. Are they higher than we expected?
A: Much higher. That new space game is ready for launch next month. The shops have ordered twice as many copies as last December.
B: Orders are growing much more rapidly than we planned for, then. Our team won't manage that volume on their own, especially with two people away next month.
A: Yes. Just look at the boxes. The ordinary game fits in one small case. The special one comes with an alien figure and a book about the making of the game.
B: Could we bring in temporary staff on a short-term contract until the end of the busy period?
A: Yes. I called the agency this morning. They have four people free from Monday, all with packing experience.
B: Good. It's important they can tell the boxes apart. Does every special order get a figure?
A: Yes. The book's only for orders before the official launch, though. Orders after that get the figure and a poster of the game's universe instead.
B: Do we have room in the budget to take on all four for six weeks?
A: Yes, if the manager approves it today. What about training? People sometimes confuse the two boxes because there's a picture of the same alien on both.
B: We have two empty tables. I'll set out one complete box of each kind. It's the little extras that hold us up when we have to distribute so many.
A: What about the special golden figures? I heard a customer say there was a lottery for those.
B: There is. One lucky person in every hundred gets one. Those boxes are coming ready to go, so the new people won't have to open them.
A: Good. Can Sam stay with the new team on Monday? He packed the last game. Its popularity took us by surprise and he knows where we lost time.
B: Yes. He'll be at the table beside them. He can also handle any questions from the shops.
A: Great. If our manager agrees today, they can start next Monday. I'll take the figures to her office now.
B: Let me know what she says, and I'll get those spare tables ready.

Vocabulary: alien, distribute, launch, lottery, making, popularity, rapidly, short-term, universe, volume
