Planning the Office Move
A: Have we had the final schedule for taking our team up to the third floor?
B: Yes, the executive team approved it this morning. The change will happen in two stages over the next two weeks.
A: Why aren't we taking everyone up at the same time?
B: Because we have to keep the phone lines open for clients during the transition.
A: Which group goes up in the first phase, then?
B: The administration staff go this Friday afternoon, and they'll occupy the desks near the main lift.
A: Where will the rest of us sit while they're setting up upstairs?
B: We'll stay here and work alongside the technical team until the new network connections are tested.
A: Do we need to pack our own files prior to Friday?
B: Only if you're in the first group. The subsequent stage for our team is the following Friday.
A: How much can we accomplish on those two Fridays if people are packing boxes?
B: Not as much as usual, so we should implement a simpler schedule for answering client messages on both days.
A: Good idea. When do the workers expect the completion of the small meeting rooms upstairs?
B: By Wednesday next week, so they'll be ready before our team goes up.
A: I'll send a message to our team now so nobody starts packing a week too early.

Vocabulary: accomplish, administration, alongside, completion, executive, implement, occupy, phase, prior, subsequent, transition
