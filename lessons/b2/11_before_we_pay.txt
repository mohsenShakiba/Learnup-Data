Before We Pay
A: These shoes are half the price on this website. That looks like a bargain.
B: I've never heard of that shop. Are the shoes genuine?
A: They have the same logo as mine. Look at the photo.
B: A photo doesn't prove much. Have you checked the shop's rating?
A: Five stars, but there are only two reviews. Wait, the comments are identical.
B: Both posted today too. They could be fake.
A: I was about to make the purchase, but there's no option to pay by card. They want me to transfer the money directly.
B: I'd stop there. I'm worried about fraud. Do they give an address or a phone number?
A: Just a contact form. I don't think I trust them with my money now.
B: Have you paid anything yet?
A: No. I wanted to show you the shoes first.
B: Good. Let's look elsewhere. Do you need them this week?
A: No, my old ones are still fine. I'll wait for a sale at the shop in town.

Vocabulary: bargain, elsewhere, fake, fraud, genuine, identical, logo, purchase, rating, trust
