Before We Pay
A: Look at this. The running shoes I wear are half price on this website, with a free pair of socks with every purchase.
B: Hang on, I've never heard of that shop. Are you sure those are the real thing?
A: The photos look pretty convincing. They have the exact same logo on the side as mine.
B: Anyone can copy photos, though. Have you checked the shop's rating?
A: Five stars, but there are only two reviews... Oh wait, the comments are identical.
B: And they were both posted today. Those are almost certainly fake.
A: Let me check the payment page... That's strange, there's no option to pay by card. They want me to transfer the money directly.
B: I'd leave it, then. If someone robbed you that way, it could be hard to get your money back. Is there a phone number on the website?
A: No, just a contact form. Yeah, I definitely don't trust this website now.
B: Have you entered any personal info or sent them any money?
A: No. I haven't filled anything in yet.
B: Good. I'd avoid that shop. Is there any independent information about it on the runners' forum?
A: I'll ask there before I spend anything. My old shoes will last another month or two.

Vocabulary: convincing, fake, forum, identical, logo, purchase, rating, rob, transfer, trust
