Before We Pay
A: Look at this. The running shoes I wear are half price on this website. What a bargain!
B: Hang on, I've never heard of that shop. Are you sure they're genuine?
A: The photos look pretty convincing. They have the exact same logo on the side as mine.
B: Anyone can copy photos, though. Have you checked the shop's rating?
A: Five stars, but there are only two reviews... Oh wait, the comments are identical.
B: And they were both posted today. Those are almost certainly fake.
A: Let me check the payment page... That's strange, there's no option to pay by card. They want me to transfer the money directly.
B: Leave it, then. If it's fraud, it's much harder to get your money back after a transfer. Do they give an address or a phone number?
A: No, just a contact form. Yeah, I definitely don't trust this website now.
B: Have you entered any personal info or sent them any money?
A: No. I haven't filled anything in yet.
B: Good. Let's look elsewhere. Do you need a new pair straight away?
A: Not really, my old ones will last another month or two. I'll wait for a sale in town.

Vocabulary: bargain, convincing, elsewhere, fake, fraud, genuine, identical, info, logo, rating, trust
