Questions for Our Quiz Night
A: Come and sit at the kitchen table. Could you try the questions I've written for Friday's quiz?
B: Sure. Pass them over. Who's coming?
A: About twenty friends from work. I want a friendly contest, but I'm not sure how difficult these questions are.
B: Is everyone playing on their own?
A: In teams. I thought we could choose the teams at random so people get to know someone new.
B: Good idea. Let's see the questions. This first one could cause confusion: "Which country is the largest?"
A: Why? I thought that was an easy opening question.
B: Largest by area or by population? The answers could differ if people understand the question differently.
A: You're right. I'll add "by area". What about the rest?
B: There's a broad range of topics, but all of the music questions are about your favourite bands.
A: I suppose I got carried away. Don't you think people will know those songs?
B: Not everyone. Twenty people from work will probably have quite diverse tastes in music.
A: Fair point. I'll replace most of those. How do the other rounds look?
B: Some of the science questions are too hard. People would need to be a genius to answer them.
A: I'll make those easier too. I wanted the quiz to be interesting, not impossible.
B: You don't need difficult questions to impress people. What about a picture puzzle for the last round?
A: I like that. They could identify familiar places from small parts of photos.
B: That could work. Ultimately, we want people to enjoy themselves. Shall we try one now and see how difficult it is?
A: Yes. I have a photo of a famous building on my phone. I'll show you just the top of it.
B: All right. Don't show me the rest until I've had a guess.

Vocabulary: broad, confusion, contest, differ, diverse, genius, impress, puzzle, random, ultimately
