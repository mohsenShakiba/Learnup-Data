Questions for Our Quiz Night
A: Can you try the questions for Friday's quiz? I want a friendly contest, but I'm not sure how difficult they are.
B: Sure. Is everyone playing on their own?
A: In teams. I thought we could choose the teams at random so people get to know someone new.
B: Good idea. Let's see the questions. This first one could cause confusion: "Which country is the largest?"
A: Why? I thought that was an easy opening question.
B: Largest by area or by population? The answers could differ if people understand the question differently.
A: You're right. I'll add "by area". What about the rest?
B: There's a broad range of topics, but six of the music questions are about the same band.
A: They're my favourite. I suppose I got carried away.
B: Our friends have quite diverse tastes. Some won't know that band at all.
A: I'll replace four of those questions. I want to challenge people, but they shouldn't need to be a genius to enjoy it.
B: Exactly. You're not trying to impress them with how much you know. What about a picture puzzle for the last round?
A: I like that. They could identify familiar places from small parts of photos.
B: Use places everyone has a chance of knowing. Ultimately, we want people to come back next time.
A: I'll use famous buildings and check each answer against the full photo.
B: Send me the new version tomorrow. I'll try it without looking at the answers.

Vocabulary: broad, confusion, contest, differ, diverse, genius, impress, puzzle, random, ultimately
