Questions for Our Quiz Night
A: Come and sit at the kitchen table. Could you try the questions I've written for Friday's quiz?
B: Sure. Pass them over. Who's coming?
A: About twenty friends from work. It's meant to be a fun evening, not a serious contest, so I'm not sure if these are too difficult.
B: Is everyone playing on their own?
A: In teams. I thought we could choose the teams at random so people get to know someone new.
B: Good idea. Let's see the questions. This first one could cause confusion. "Which country is the largest?"
A: Why? I thought that was an easy opening question.
B: Largest by area or by population? Those are two different countries.
A: Oh, you're right. I'll add "by area". What about the rest?
B: There's a broad range of topics, though all of the music questions are about your favourite bands.
A: I suppose I got carried away. Won't people know those songs?
B: They've all got different tastes. Could you play a few seconds of a famous tune and ask them to name it?
A: Oh, that'd be much more entertaining. I'll replace most of mine. How do the other rounds look?
B: The science ones are hard. You'd need to be a genius to say how much oxygen there is on another planet. Who'd know that off the top of their head?
A: You're right. How about a picture of the globe and a question about which ocean we're looking at?
B: Yes, a picture will help. You don't need difficult questions to impress people. How about a picture puzzle for the last round too?
A: I like that. They could identify familiar places from small parts of photos.
B: That could work. Make the first one super easy so they get the idea. Shall we try one now?
A: Yes. I have a photo of a famous building on my phone. I'll show you just the top of it.
B: All right. Don't show me the rest until I've had a guess.

Vocabulary: broad, confusion, contest, entertaining, genius, globe, oxygen, puzzle, random, super
