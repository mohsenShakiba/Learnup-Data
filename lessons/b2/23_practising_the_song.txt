Practising the Song
A: Could we practise that song before the choir meets tomorrow? I keep coming in too early.
B: Sure. Do you know the tune, or do you want to go through that first?
A: I know it now. It's the rhythm after the long pause that confuses me.
B: Let's look at the lyrics. Which line is giving you trouble?
A: This one, just after the keyboard plays on its own.
B: Listen to the last three notes before that line. They're your cue to come in. I'll play them slowly.
A: Oh, I've been starting on the second note. I should have listened to the keyboard more carefully.
B: It's hard to hear it when everyone's singing. Shall I accompany you while you try that part again?
A: Yes, please. Could you count me in the first time?
B: Of course. Listen to those notes. One, two, three...
A: "Follow me home..." Was that right?
B: Yes, that was it! Your timing was right that time.
A: That felt better. I lose confidence when I hear myself starting before everyone else.
B: Would an audio recording of the keyboard part help you practise at home?
A: Yes. Could you include the part just before the pause as well?
B: I'll record that whole section now. Then you can hear how it fits together.
A: Thanks. Let's try it once more before you record it.
B: All right. This time I'll leave the counting to you.

Vocabulary: accompany, audio, choir, confidence, cue, lyric, pause, rhythm, timing, tune
