Lunch with Mum
A: I'm calling about our table for six on Sunday at half past twelve, under Harris. My mum still needs a stick after her operation. Can she get in without using the steps?
B: Yes. There's a side entrance with a level concrete path from the car park.
A: That's good. Steps are the main obstacle at the moment. Can we stop the car close to that entrance?
B: Certainly. You can drop her off there before you park. Will she need assistance getting to the table?
A: My brother will be with her. She's keen to do things herself, though. She doesn't like being dependent on us.
B: Understood. I'll give you a table close to that door, with plenty of room around it.
A: Is that part of the restaurant warm? She feels the cold, so she'd be more comfortable a little further inside.
B: The door closes automatically. Table four is further inside, beside the window. That would be appropriate for your group.
A: Lovely. My elderly uncle is coming too, so comfort matters more than the view. Do those chairs have arms?
B: The chairs there have arms, which should help them get up. Is there anything else we should take into consideration?
A: Where's the bathroom? Can you reach it entirely on the same level?
B: Yes, it's just along the hall. There's plenty of room inside, and the door's light, with a handle that's easy to turn.
A: That sounds fine. Mum was worried she'd have to stay at home again.
B: I'll add a note about the entrance to your booking and reserve table four for you.
A: Thanks. I'll tell my brother to drive to the side door.
B: We'll see you then. I'll have the table ready before you arrive.

Vocabulary: appropriate, assistance, booking, comfort, concrete, consideration, dependent, elderly, entirely, handle, obstacle
