The First Month of Retirement
A: How's retirement treating you? You've been at home for a month now, haven't you?
B: Yes. At first, the freedom was wonderful, but lately the days have started to feel very long.
A: Do you miss the work, or the people?
B: Mostly the people. It's weird to spend the entire day without talking to anyone.
A: You've always been fond of making things. Have you had more time for that?
B: I made a little wooden box last week. Finishing it gave me more satisfaction than anything else I've done this month.
A: There's a craft group at the community hall. They meet on Tuesday mornings.
B: Really? I used to walk past those rooms on my way to work. I never knew who used them.
A: My neighbour goes. They bring their own projects and help each other. Would you like me to introduce you?
B: Yes, thanks. I'd like to pursue it as a hobby, though. People keep telling me to sell the things I make.
A: You don't have to sell anything. You can just make things for the joy of it.
B: That's a relief. I don't want to devote every day to one activity again.
A: One morning a week could be enough. It would give you some company while you work on your next box.
B: And a reason to leave the house. Have you seen the one I finished?
A: No. Is it here?
B: On the shelf. Come and look. I want to try a different shape next time.

Vocabulary: craft, devote, fond, freedom, joy, lately, pursue, retirement, satisfaction, weird
