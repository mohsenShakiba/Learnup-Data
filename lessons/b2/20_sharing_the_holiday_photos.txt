Sharing the Holiday Photos
A: I've put our holiday photos in an online album. Can you check it before I send the link to everyone from the trip?
B: Sure. Oh, you kept that brief clip of me trying to sing!
A: It was hilarious. Do you mind if the others see it?
B: Actually, yes. I'd prefer you to delete it from the album.
A: Of course. There, it's gone. What about the photos from dinner?
B: Those are fine, but my phone number's visible on that piece of paper by my plate.
A: I can edit the photo and cut that part off. Like this?
B: Yes, thanks. Who can see the album? Does the app make everything public automatically?
A: No. I've changed the privacy settings to restrict access to the people I invite.
B: Does that exclude anyone who just gets a copy of the link?
A: Yes, they need an invitation too. But someone with access could still save a photo and share it.
B: That's fine for these. I just didn't want that clip going round.
A: Understood. I'll send you the invitation first so you can check the album on your phone.
B: All right. Then you can send it to the rest of the group.

Vocabulary: automatically, brief, clip, delete, edit, exclude, hilarious, privacy, restrict, visible
