Sharing the Holiday Photos
A: Have a look at our holiday photos. I've put them in an online album to share with everyone from the trip.
B: Hang on, you kept that clip of me singing?
A: Yeah, I thought it was amusing. Can I leave it in?
B: Please don't. Delete that one.
A: Okay, gone. What about this shot from the cafe?
B: That one's cute. Oh wait, you can see my phone number on that hotel form by my cup.
A: Shame. It's a good photo.
B: Can't you just edit that bit out?
A: Good idea. Let me try... There. Is it still visible?
B: No, you can't read it now. Much better. Does this app make everything public automatically?
A: No, I checked the privacy settings. Only people I invite can see it.
B: Even if someone passes the link on?
A: They'd still need an invitation. But anyone who can see the photos could save them and send them on.
B: That's fine. I just didn't want that clip going round.
A: Okay. I'll send you an invite first. Have a look on your phone before I send it to everyone else.
B: Will do. Send it over.

Vocabulary: amusing, automatically, clip, cute, delete, edit, privacy, shame, shot, visible
