Planning Healthier Meals
A: What did the doctor say about your blood test results?
B: Nothing dangerous, fortunately, but she warned me that my weight has gone up and talked about the risks of obesity.
A: Did she suggest a strict diet?
B: No. She said people who starve themselves for two weeks usually give up and go back to their old habits.
A: That's true. What did she recommend instead?
B: A few fundamental changes to what we eat every day, especially avoiding excessive amounts of sweet food and fried meals.
A: We do buy a lot of sweet cakes. What should we eat when we're hungry in the afternoon?
B: Foods with more protein, like eggs or fish, because they satisfy you for longer.
A: That sounds easy enough. Did she say anything about your vitamin levels?
B: Two of them were low, so I wasn't getting everything I needed adequately from our usual meals.
A: If we plan our lunches before we shop, it'll be easier to regulate how much fast food we buy at work.
B: Yes. She said a consistent habit matters more than trying to be perfect for a few days.
A: If we cook extra vegetables and fish in the evening, we can take half for lunch the next day.
B: Let's try that for a month and see whether my numbers decrease by the next check-up.

Vocabulary: adequately, consistent, decrease, excessive, fundamental, obesity, protein, regulate, satisfy, starve, vitamin
