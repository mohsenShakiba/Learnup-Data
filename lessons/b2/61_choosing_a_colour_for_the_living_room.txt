Choosing a Colour for the Living Room
A: Come and look at the living room wall before I put the paint away. What do you think of this green beside the window?
B: That's bold. I thought we'd agreed on something neutral.
A: We had, though the room looks a bit dull with grey furniture, and pure white felt too cold when we tried it in the hall. I wanted to test a small section first.
B: What about the landscape painting above the chairs, though? That's already quite colourful—would green work with it?
A: That's actually where I got the idea. There's a similar green in the trees.
B: Oh, I see. It looks darker on the wall than it did on the card, though.
A: It's still wet, so let's not judge it yet.
B: Fair enough. Let's wait until tonight—we need to see it under artificial lighting anyway, since we're mostly in here in the evenings.
A: All right. I saw a similar colour in a cafe, and it looked stunning behind their wooden chairs.
B: Was the whole room green?
A: No, just one wall. I think that would transform this room too.
B: I'd be happy to try it behind our chairs. I'm not sure about all four walls.
A: Shall I paint a small section there while the brush is still wet?
B: Yes. Then we can compare both sections tonight before we buy a big tin.

Vocabulary: artificial, bold, colourful, dull, landscape, lighting, neutral, pure, stunning, transform
