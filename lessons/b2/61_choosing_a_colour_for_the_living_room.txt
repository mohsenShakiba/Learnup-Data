Choosing a Colour for the Living Room
A: What do you think of the green paint beside the window? I've only done that small section so far.
B: It's a bold choice. I thought we were going to keep the walls neutral.
A: We were, but the room looks a bit dull with grey walls and grey furniture.
B: True. I like that colourful landscape above the chairs, though. Would the green work with it?
A: That's where I got the idea. There's almost the same green in the trees.
B: It looks somewhat darker on the wall than on this card.
A: I noticed that too. I hadn't expected so much variation. Perhaps it needs another coat before we judge it.
B: Have you tried it under artificial light? We mostly use this room in the evenings.
A: Not yet. Shall I close the curtains and put the lamps on?
B: Yes. That should give us a better idea.
A: There. What do you think now?
B: I prefer it like this. It looks warmer with the lamps on.
A: It could really transform the room. I saw a similar colour in a cafe, and it looked stunning.
B: Was the whole room green?
A: No, just the wall behind the seats. The other walls were white.
B: That might work here too. I'd be happy with green behind the chairs, but I'm less sure about all four walls.
A: Let's try a bigger section there and leave it for a few days.
B: Good idea. We can see it in different light before we buy any more paint.

Vocabulary: artificial, bold, colourful, dull, landscape, neutral, somewhat, stunning, transform, variation
