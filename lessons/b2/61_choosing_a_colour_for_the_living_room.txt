Choosing a Colour for the Living Room
A: Come and look at the living room wall before I put the paint away. What do you think of this green beside the window?
B: That's bold. I thought we'd agreed on something neutral.
A: We had, but the room looks a bit dull with grey walls and grey furniture. I wanted to try a small section first.
B: What about that colourful landscape above the chairs? Would the green work with it?
A: That's where I got the idea. There's a similar green in the trees.
B: I see. It's somewhat darker on the wall than on the card, though.
A: Yes, there's more variation than I expected. It's still wet, so we shouldn't judge it yet.
B: Then let's wait until tonight. We need to see it under artificial light anyway, since we're usually in here in the evenings.
A: All right. I saw a similar colour in a cafe. It looked stunning behind their wooden chairs.
B: Was the whole room green?
A: No, just one wall. I think that would transform this room too.
B: I'd be happy to try it behind our chairs. I'm not sure about all four walls.
A: Shall I paint a small section there while the brush is still wet?
B: Yes. Then we can compare both sections tonight before we buy a big tin.

Vocabulary: artificial, bold, colourful, dull, landscape, neutral, somewhat, stunning, transform, variation
