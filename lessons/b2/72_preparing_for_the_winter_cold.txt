Preparing for the Winter Cold
A: Have you seen the weather warning for tonight? The temperature is expected to drop well below freezing.
B: Yes, and the ground is already wet, consequently the garden path could be covered in hard snow by the morning.
A: Do we still have that bag of salt in the garage to help the snow melt near the front steps?
B: I checked earlier, and the bag is nearly empty. We need to buy another one before the shops close.
A: Good idea. We should also check the outside water pipe; it split two years ago because it had no protection from the cold.
B: Is that thin cloth around the outer pipe thick enough for a freezing night?
A: Not on its own. Long exposure to freezing air can still crack the pipe if water is left inside it.
B: Then it's crucial that we turn off the inside tap for the garden and let the last drops run out.
A: I'll do that now. What about Mrs Green next door? We shouldn't let older neighbours feel that bad weather will isolate them from everyone else.
B: I spoke to her at one o'clock. Her downstairs room wasn't sufficiently warm because she was worried about her heating bill.
A: That's potentially dangerous at her age. She shouldn't feel she has to sit in a cold room.
B: I helped her set the heating clock for the coldest hours, and she desperately needed someone to collect her medicine from the shop too.
A: I'm glad we checked on her welfare. Let's pick up her medicine when we go for the salt.

Vocabulary: consequently, crucial, desperately, exposure, isolate, melt, outer, potentially, protection, sufficiently, welfare
