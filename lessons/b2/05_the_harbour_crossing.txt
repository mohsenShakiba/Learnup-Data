The Harbour Crossing
A: Let's sit here on the deck. It's good to be out of that terminal at last.
B: Sorry we spent the whole morning there. If I'd taken the tunnel, we'd have reached the harbour in time for our boat.
A: Don't blame yourself. I agreed to take the coast road too. We didn't know a truck had broken down.
B: I know. I just kept thinking about the extra fee while we were waiting.
A: It was quite reasonable, though. If we hadn't bought flexible tickets, we'd have lost the entire fare.
B: True. I thought I'd ruined the whole holiday.
A: We're only a few hours late. Have you let the hotel know?
B: Yes. They're keeping our room for us.
A: Good. Look at those cliffs!
B: Spectacular, aren't they? Let's stay out here for a bit.
A: All right. Just a few minutes, though—I'm getting cold.
B: Then we'll go and get a coffee in the cabin.

Vocabulary: blame, cabin, cliff, deck, entire, fare, fee, flexible, harbour, reasonable, ruin, spectacular, terminal, tunnel
