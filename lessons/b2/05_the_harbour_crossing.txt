On the Later Boat
A: Let's sit out here on the deck. I've had enough of that crowded waiting room.
B: Sorry we spent the whole morning there. If I'd taken the tunnel, we'd have reached the port in time for the earlier boat.
A: Don't blame yourself. I agreed to take the coast road too. We didn't know a truck had broken down ahead of us.
B: I suppose so. Still, it was annoying having to pay a fee to move to this crossing.
A: Ten pounds each was pretty reasonable, though. If we hadn't bought flexible tickets, we'd have lost the entire price of the trip.
B: True. At one point, I really thought I'd ruined the first day of our holiday.
A: Not at all. We're only a few hours late. Did you let the hotel know?
B: Yes, they're keeping our room for us.
A: Good. Oh, look at those cliffs! I couldn't see them from inside.
B: Spectacular, aren't they? Let's stay out here while we pass them.
A: All right, just for a few minutes, though. It's getting cold in this wind.
B: Okay, let's go inside and get a coffee in a minute.

Vocabulary: blame, cliff, deck, entire, fee, flexible, reasonable, ruin, spectacular, tunnel
