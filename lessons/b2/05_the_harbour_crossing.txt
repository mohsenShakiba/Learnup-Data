The Harbour Crossing
A: At least we're finally sitting on the top deck. Back at the terminal, I thought there wouldn't be any seats left on this afternoon boat.
B: Me too. I still feel terrible that we missed the morning crossing after the boat had departed. If I'd listened to your warning about the accident, we would have taken the tunnel instead.
A: Don't blame yourself. Neither of us knew a truck had broken down in the left lane.
B: Still, if we'd left home half an hour earlier, we would have reached the harbour before the gates closed.
A: Maybe, and then we wouldn't have paid that extra fee to alter our tickets.
B: Actually, the ticket staff were very reasonable. If we hadn't chosen flexible tickets last month, we would have lost the entire fare.
A: That's true—I'd forgotten you picked that option. If you'd bought the cheapest tickets, today's delay would have ruined our holiday plans.
B: And look at the sky now. If we'd crossed this morning in that heavy storm, we wouldn't have seen this spectacular view of the cliffs at all.
A: You're right, the sun makes a big difference. Shall we go inside the cabin and get some hot coffee?
B: Good idea. The wind is getting cold out here.

Vocabulary: alter, blame, cabin, cliff, deck, depart, entire, fare, fee, flexible, harbour, lane, reasonable, ruin, spectacular, terminal, tunnel
