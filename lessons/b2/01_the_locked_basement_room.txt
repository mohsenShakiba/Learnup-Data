The Locked Basement Room
A: You took a while! Did you get the camera kit for our workshop?
B: Yes, it's on the desk. The basement room was locked when I got down there, though.
A: Really? I'd sent a message to the office before I left yesterday so someone could open it for the morning session.
B: Nobody at the front desk had seen a message from you when I asked. Did you send it to their new number?
A: New number? No, I used the old one on my phone.
B: Ah, they changed it last month.
A: Oh, my fault. Sorry you had to sort that out. How did you get in?
B: Fortunately, the manager had left a spare key at the front desk before he went out.
A: Good thing he did. Is everything ready now?
B: Almost. The cable in the bag was too short to reach the wall, so I had to find a longer one. Can you check the camera's display while I adjust the stand?
A: Sure. Up a little bit... That's great, you can see the whole board now.
B: Good, because a few people are already waiting out in the corridor. Let's let them in.

Vocabulary: adjust, basement, cable, corridor, display, fault, kit, session, spare, workshop
