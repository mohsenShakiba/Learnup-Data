The Locked Basement Room
A: You're back! Did you get the camera and display from the equipment facility in the basement?
B: Not from the main room. By the time I walked down the corridor, the clerk had already locked the door and gone to the other building.
A: Why had he left so early? Our training workshop starts at nine.
B: I called him, and he said nobody had informed him about our morning session.
A: That's strange. I'd sent him a message before I left the office yesterday afternoon.
B: Which address did you use? He moved to a different team last month.
A: Oh, no. I've just checked my account—I'd used his old address by mistake. That was completely my fault.
B: Don't worry, we don't need to delay the workshop. Before the clerk went out, he'd left a spare key at the front desk, so I obtained it from the assistant.
A: That was lucky! Did you find the whole equipment kit inside?
B: Yes, and someone had packed an extra cable in the bag too.
A: Great. I'll send the clerk a quick apology now so he knows why we opened the room.
B: Good idea. I've already adjusted the display height, so we can proceed as soon as the first group arrives.

Vocabulary: adjust, apology, basement, cable, clerk, corridor, delay, display, facility, fault, inform, kit, obtain, proceed, session, spare, workshop
