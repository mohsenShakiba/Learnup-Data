The Locked Basement Room
A: You're back! Did you get the equipment for our workshop?
B: Yes, the kit's on that desk. The basement room was locked when I got there, though.
A: Oh. I'd told the clerk we needed it for the morning session.
B: He said nobody had informed him. Had you sent the message to his new address?
A: New address? I used the same one as last time.
B: He changed it last month. There's a note on the door at the end of the corridor.
A: I've just checked my message. I used the old one. My fault. How did you get in?
B: I called him. He'd left a spare key with the assistant at the front desk.
A: I'll send him an apology. Is the camera ready?
B: Almost. The cable in the bag was too short, so I found a longer one. Can you help me adjust the display?
A: Of course. Move it over here so everyone can see it.
B: Thanks. Then we're ready for the first group.

Vocabulary: adjust, apology, basement, cable, clerk, corridor, display, fault, inform, kit, session, spare, workshop
