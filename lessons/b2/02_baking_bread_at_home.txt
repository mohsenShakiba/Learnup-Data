Baking Bread at Home
A: Something smells wonderful in here. Have you been baking all afternoon?
B: Pretty much, since one o'clock. I've been trying to make decent brown bread for weeks, and this is my third attempt.
A: Was your last one that heavy bread we had with soup on Sunday?
B: Don't remind me. It came out completely flat because I'd measured the flour with a coffee cup instead of weighing it accurately.
A: Has weighing the ingredients made a difference today?
B: A big one, and I bought a bag of organic brown flour yesterday. Everything else in the recipe is the same, apart from one minor change.
A: What did you change?
B: Instead of pouring all the warm water in at once, I added it gradually until the mixture felt firm and smooth.
A: Let me look through the oven window. Well, it's risen nicely this time. How much longer does it need?
B: Only a minute, actually. I've been watching the top carefully for the last ten minutes so it doesn't burn. Could you pass me that other oven glove by the board?
A: Here you go. Careful with the hot tin.
B: I will be. The burn on my arm from Sunday has only just healed.
A: Let's hope the bread doesn't stick to the tin.
B: Let me turn it out onto the wooden board. Look, it came straight out. I'm glad I remembered to rub a little oil around the tin.
A: That looks fantastic. If you bake bread like this when my parents visit, you'll really impress my dad. Can we try a slice now?
B: Give it twenty minutes to cool first, or it'll fall apart when I slice it.
A: Fair enough. I'll put the water on for tea while it cools and get the butter out.

Vocabulary: accurately, attempt, decent, firm, gradually, heal, impress, minor, organic, rub
