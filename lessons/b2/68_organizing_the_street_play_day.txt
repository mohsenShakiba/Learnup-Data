Organizing the Street Play Day
A: Have we heard from the council about closing our street to cars on the first Sunday in June?
B: Yes, they've granted us a temporary permit to close the road between two and five o'clock.
A: That's great news. How did you persuade them to approve this initiative so quickly?
B: Over forty inhabitants signed the letter, and the mayor had already spoken in favour of car-free play days.
A: Do we still need to talk to the people who didn't sign?
B: Yes, we should visit every house this week to raise awareness of the times when the road will be closed.
A: What if drivers who live here need to use their cars during the afternoon?
B: They'll still be allowed to drive out slowly if a volunteer walks in front of the car. We want to engage our fellow residents, not annoy them.
A: Good. Are we planning anything special for the children at two o'clock?
B: The local cycling group offered to give a short safety demonstration, and the children want a costume parade along the street.
A: That sounds fun. Older citizens who don't have children could bring chairs out and have coffee together too.
B: Exactly. The whole point is to unite people who usually only wave to each other from their doors.
A: I'll print a simple notice with the times and your phone number so we can deliver it tonight.

Vocabulary: awareness, citizen, demonstration, engage, fellow, inhabitant, initiative, mayor, parade, permit, unite
