Solar Panels for the Roof
A: Did the solar company send their quote after checking our roof yesterday?
B: Yes, it came this morning. They say the back roof faces south, so it's in a great position for solar panels.
A: Would eight panels generate enough electricity for the house?
B: Enough for most of what we use during the day, especially in summer.
A: Did they calculate how much we'd save on our bills each year?
B: Around four hundred pounds a year, based on our usual electricity consumption.
A: Not bad. How much is the whole installation going to cost?
B: Six thousand pounds, including the work on the roof.
A: That's a lot to pay at once. At four hundred a year, it'll take fifteen years to earn that money back.
B: It's definitely a long-term plan. Still, there's a regional scheme to help households cut their carbon emissions.
A: Could we get a grant through that?
B: They think so. If our application's approved, it'll pay for a third of the cost.
A: That makes a big difference. Do we have to apply before we book the work?
B: Yes. They sent the link to the application form, so let's fill it in tonight.

Vocabulary: calculate, carbon, consumption, emission, generate, grant, installation, long-term, regional, solar
