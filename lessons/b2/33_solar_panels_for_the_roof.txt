Solar Panels for the Roof
A: Did the solar company send their quote after checking our roof yesterday?
B: Yes, it came this morning. They say the back roof faces south, so it's in a great position for solar panels.
A: Would eight panels make enough electricity for the house?
B: Enough for most of what we use during the day, especially in summer.
A: Did they calculate how much we'd save on our bills each year?
B: Around four hundred pounds a year. Mind you, they based that on us using the washing machine during the day.
A: Not bad. How much is the whole installation going to cost?
B: Six thousand pounds, including the work on the roof.
A: That's a lot to pay at once. At four hundred a year, it'll take fifteen years to earn that money back.
B: I know. It's a long-term plan. But there's a regional programme to help with the cost. Remember that talk on carbon and global warming? They mentioned it there.
A: Was that the one the electricity corporation runs? I thought it was for businesses.
B: The first part was. This one's intended for homes like ours. If our application goes through, they'll pay a third of the cost.
A: When can we book the work? If the money only comes through after completion, we'd need to borrow the full amount first.
B: They pay the company directly, but we have to apply before work starts. Here's the form.

Vocabulary: calculate, carbon, completion, corporation, installation, intended, long-term, regional, solar, warming
