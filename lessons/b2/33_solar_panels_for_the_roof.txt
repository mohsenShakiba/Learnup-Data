Solar Panels for the Roof
A: Did the power consultant send his report after looking at our roof yesterday?
B: Yes, it arrived this morning. He says the back roof faces south, so it's in a good position for solar panels.
A: Would eight panels generate enough electricity for the house?
B: They'd be sufficient for most of what we use during the day, especially in summer.
A: Did he calculate how much we'd save on our bills each year?
B: Around four hundred pounds a year, if our electricity consumption stays the same.
A: And it would cut our household carbon emissions too. What's the total price for the installation?
B: Six thousand pounds, and that includes the work on the roof.
A: That's a lot to pay at once. It would take fifteen years to get that money back.
B: It's a long-term plan, yes. But there's a regional scheme that helps residents pay for clean electricity.
A: Could we get a grant from them?
B: He thinks so. If they approve our application, it will pay for a third of the cost.
A: That makes a big difference. Do we have to apply before we book the work?
B: Yes. He's sent the link to the application form, so let's fill it in tonight.

Vocabulary: calculate, carbon, consultant, consumption, emission, generate, grant, installation, long-term, regional, solar, sufficient
