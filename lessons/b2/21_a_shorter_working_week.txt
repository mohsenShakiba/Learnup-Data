A Shorter Working Week
A: Could we talk about my hours? I've been struggling to get everything done since Dad came home from hospital.
B: Of course. Do you need some time off?
A: I was hoping to work part-time for a few weeks. My sister can help on Mondays and Tuesdays, but she works on the other days.
B: What sort of hours would be realistic for you?
A: Full days on Monday and Tuesday, and Wednesday until two, if possible. A nurse comes on Wednesday morning, then Dad needs me in the afternoon.
B: We could try that. Which of your tasks would need to go to someone else?
A: Finding someone to take the afternoon calls would be the main priority. I could still finish the weekly reports before I leave on Wednesday.
B: I'll ask Jo whether she's willing to take the calls. I need to check her schedule before we agree to this.
A: I don't want to give her too much additional work.
B: We could share the calls between two people if it's too much for her. How long do you expect to need these hours?
A: About a month. I want to return to full-time work after that, so I'm not asking for a permanent change.
B: Then we can reduce your hours temporarily if we find help for the afternoons. Let's review how it's going after two weeks.
A: Thank you. Shall I send you those hours in writing?
B: Yes, please. I'll speak to the team today and let you know tomorrow.

Vocabulary: additional, full-time, part-time, permanent, priority, realistic, struggle, temporarily, weekly, willing
