A Shorter Working Week
A: Could we talk about my hours? I've been struggling to cope since Dad came home from hospital.
B: Of course. Do you need some time off?
A: I'd prefer to work part-time temporarily. My sister can help on Mondays and Tuesdays, but she has work commitments on the other days.
B: Would three days a week be realistic for you?
A: Yes, if I could finish by two on Wednesday. Dad needs help in the afternoon.
B: We could try that. Which of your tasks would need to go to someone else?
A: The afternoon calls would be the priority. I could still finish the weekly reports during my morning hours.
B: I'll ask Jo whether she's willing to take the calls. I need to check her schedule before we agree to this.
A: I don't want to give her too much additional work.
B: We could share the calls between two people if it's too much for her. How long do you expect to need these hours?
A: About a month. I want to return to full-time work after that, so I'm not asking for a permanent change.
B: Then let's arrange a four-week trial if we can find help for the afternoons. We'll review it after two weeks.
A: Thank you. Shall I send you the hours I can do?
B: Yes, please. I'll speak to the team today and let you know tomorrow.

Vocabulary: additional, commitment, cope, full-time, part-time, permanent, priority, realistic, struggle, temporarily, trial, weekly, willing
