Repairing the Washing Machine
A: Thanks for coming. The washing machine's here in the kitchen. It's been increasingly difficult to start, and now it won't start at all.
B: You mentioned a message on the display when you called. What does it say?
A: It says the door's open, but it seems properly shut. Could something be wrong with the electronics?
B: It could be that, or a mechanical failure in the lock. Let me try the door.
A: So even a small fault in the lock could stop it working?
B: Yes. It won't operate unless it detects that the door is locked. I'll switch the power off now so I can examine the mechanism.
A: Is it worth repairing a machine this old?
B: If it's just the lock, probably. Those are relatively cheap, but I need to check it thoroughly before I give you a price.
A: Can you still get parts for this model?
B: I'll check. Some parts for older models are no longer manufactured.
A: One other thing: the clothes have been coming out wetter than usual.
B: For how long?
A: A few weeks. I meant to mention it when I called.
B: Then I'll also check whether it's removing the water efficiently. The estimate needs to include both problems.
A: Please do. I'd like to know the full cost before we decide on repairs.
B: Of course. I'll explain what I've found before I order any parts.

Vocabulary: efficiently, electronics, failure, increasingly, manufacture, mechanical, mechanism, operate, relatively, thoroughly
