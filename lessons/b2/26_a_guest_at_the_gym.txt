A Guest at the Gym
A: Can I bring my sister to the gym tomorrow? She's visiting for the weekend.
B: Yes. Your membership includes one guest visit a month. Have you used yours yet?
A: No. Can she swim and use the gym on the same visit?
B: The pool and the gym, yes. Classes aren't included in the guest pass.
A: That's fine. Do I need to register her in advance?
B: You can do it now or when you arrive. She'll need to bring photo ID.
A: She has a student card with her photo on it. It shows her date of birth too.
B: That's fine. We ask because the morning swim is for adults only.
A: Great. Is the water deep all the way along? She's still learning.
B: She can stay at the end by the steps. The depth is marked on the wall there. There's a maximum stay of two hours, and you do need to book a time.
A: Could we swim from ten to eleven and use the gym afterwards?
B: Yes, we have spaces at ten. Shall I put you down?
A: Please. Does she need anything apart from a towel?
B: A cap, and something for eye protection if the water bothers her eyes. It's a nice time to go. My sister swims at ten and finds it more enjoyable before the pool gets busy.
A: Great. Can we leave for lunch and come back to the gym?
B: Yes. Keep the guest pass with you. She'll need it to activate the gate again.

Vocabulary: activate, advance, afterwards, depth, enjoyable, ID, maximum, membership, protection, register
