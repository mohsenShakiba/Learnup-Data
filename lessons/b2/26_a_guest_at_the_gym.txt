A Guest at the Gym
A: Can I bring my sister with me tomorrow? She's visiting for the weekend.
B: Yes. Your membership includes one guest visit a month. Have you used yours yet?
A: No. Can she use all the facilities, including the pool?
B: The pool and the gym, yes. Classes aren't included in the guest pass.
A: That's fine. Do I need to register her in advance?
B: You can do it now or when you arrive. She'll need to bring photo ID.
A: Is that compulsory even if she comes in with me?
B: Yes. We check each guest's identity on their first visit.
A: All right. How long is the pass valid?
B: For the day, but there's a maximum of two hours in the pool. You'll need to reserve a time for that.
A: Could we swim from ten to eleven and use the gym afterwards?
B: Yes, there are spaces at ten. Shall I reserve two for you?
A: Please. Is there any other requirement she needs to know about?
B: She'll need a swimming cap. We sell them here if she doesn't have one.
A: I have a spare she can borrow. Can we leave for lunch and come back to the gym?
B: Yes. There isn't a restriction on coming back the same day. Just keep the guest pass with you.

Vocabulary: advance, afterwards, compulsory, facility, ID, maximum, membership, register, requirement, reserve, restriction, valid
