Clearing the Spare Room
A: My sister and her husband are arriving on Friday, and the spare room is still full of old boxes.
B: Let's clear it this afternoon. Most of the things in there were left behind when the room was formerly my brother's study.
A: What's in those bags that are hidden behind the desk?
B: Mainly winter blankets and last year's party decorations. We can put those aside on the top shelf in the hall.
A: What about this old printer by the door? Does it still work?
B: No, a small glass fragment got stuck inside it last year, so it's completely useless now.
A: Then we should get rid of it instead of keeping it in the house.
B: Agreed. We can't just dump old office equipment with the normal rubbish, though; we have to take it to the town recycling place.
A: I'm driving past there tomorrow morning, so I can put it in the back of the car tonight.
B: Great. Once these boxes are gone, we can keep the room permanently clear for guests.
A: If we put the small table under the window and add a lamp, it'll transform the whole space.
B: Yes, it'll look like a proper bedroom again. You carry the printer down, and I'll sweep the floor before we put the clean sheets on the bed.
A: Deal. Hand me that box of cables too so I can take it with the printer.

Vocabulary: aside, blanket, decoration, dump, formerly, fragment, hidden, permanently, rid, sweep, transform, useless
