Agreeing on Game Time
A: Can we talk about your gaming? You've still been online at eleven every night this week.
B: That's when my friends play. If I leave in the middle of a game, they have to finish without me.
A: I understand, but you've been very tired in the mornings. Has it affected your concentration at school?
B: A bit. I nearly fell asleep in class yesterday. Just please don't stop me playing altogether.
A: I'm not trying to stop you spending time with your friends. We just need a finish time that's acceptable on school nights.
B: Ten o'clock? That would give me time to finish a game after dinner.
A: We agreed on ten last month. It's become an ongoing argument because you keep starting a new game just before ten.
B: I know. It's hard to resist when all my mates ask me to stay on for one more.
A: Could you tell them before you start that you're coming off at ten?
B: Yes. If I stop joining new games at nine thirty, I should finish in time.
A: That sounds much more practical. I'm tired of arguing about it every evening too.
B: Would you let me play later on Saturdays, then? Having the same strict rule every night feels unfair.
A: Eleven on Saturdays is fine, once I see you keeping to ten during the week.
B: Fair enough. Can you let me switch off on my own, though, without coming into my room? I want a bit more independence.
A: Yes, as long as you show the discipline to turn it off yourself. Let's try that for a week.
B: Okay. I'll tell everyone tonight before we start playing.

Vocabulary: acceptable, altogether, concentration, discipline, gaming, independence, mate, ongoing, resist, strict
