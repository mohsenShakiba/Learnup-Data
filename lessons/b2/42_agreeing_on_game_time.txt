Agreeing on Game Time
A: Can we talk about your gaming? You've still been online at eleven every night this week.
B: That's when my friends play. If I leave in the middle of a game, they have to finish without me.
A: I understand, but you've been very tired in the mornings. Has it affected your concentration at school?
B: A bit. I nearly fell asleep in class yesterday. But please don't forbid me to play altogether.
A: I'm not trying to stop you spending time with your friends. We need a limit that's acceptable on school nights.
B: Ten o'clock? That would give me time to finish a game after dinner.
A: We agreed on ten last month. It's become an ongoing argument because you keep starting another game just before then.
B: I know. It's hard to resist when everyone asks me to stay. Playing late is the norm in our group.
A: Could you tell them before you start that you have to leave at ten?
B: Yes. If I stop joining new games at nine thirty, I should finish in time.
A: That sounds more practical. I don't want this conflict every evening either.
B: Would you let me play later on Saturdays, then? The same strict rule every day feels unfair.
A: We can agree on eleven for Saturdays. But I need to see that you can keep to the earlier time during the week.
B: Fair enough. Will you let me stop on my own, though? I want a bit more independence.
A: Yes, if you can show some discipline about the time. Let's try it for a week.
B: Okay. I'll tell the group tonight, before we start playing.

Vocabulary: acceptable, altogether, concentration, conflict, discipline, forbid, gaming, independence, norm, ongoing, resist, strict
