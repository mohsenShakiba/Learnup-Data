Before the Choir Concert
A: Can we go over the last page before we start practising again? I keep coming in too early.
B: In the Italian song?
A: Yes. I know the tune, but the rhythm changes after that long pause.
B: Helen said to count four beats before the next line.
A: I lose my concentration when the orchestra starts playing so loudly. I can't hear myself.
B: Look, she's talking to them about the volume now.
A: Then I need to watch her more carefully when we start again.
B: Yes. She has to conduct both the choir and the orchestra, so she wants us to look up during that pause.
A: I need to learn the lyrics properly, then. Could you send me the audio recording from last week?
B: Sure. You can hear the pause clearly on it.
A: Thanks. I'd like to get this right before the local station broadcasts our concert on Friday.
B: Me too. Shall we try it quietly now? I can tap the rhythm on the table.
