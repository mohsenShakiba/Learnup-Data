The Free Coffee Photo
A: Why are people queuing outside already? We don't open for another twenty minutes.
B: They're asking about free coffee. I tried to explain the breakfast offer, but some still assume the coffee's free.
A: Have they seen the photo you posted last night?
B: Yes. Let me check... Oh, no. I've somehow cut off the bottom of the sign.
A: It literally just says "Free coffee". The "with breakfast" bit is missing.
B: I cropped the photo to fit the post. I'm sorry. I didn't notice.
A: Don't panic. Let's make the offer less confusing. Can you replace that photo?
B: Yes. What do we do about the people waiting? There are eight of them.
A: Let's honour the offer they saw. I don't want to disappoint them before they've even come inside.
B: Eight free coffees? We can't do that all morning.
A: Eight won't be a massive loss. I'm more concerned about them thinking we tricked them.
B: Fair enough. What if more people arrive with the old photo?
A: Explain the mistake at the top of the post. If anyone else has the old photo, ask them to speak to me.
B: Okay. Should I change the board as well?
A: Just make "with breakfast" bigger. There’s a marker by the till.
B: I'll do that before I take the new photo. Then I'll start on those coffees.

Vocabulary: assume, concerned, confusing, disappoint, honour, literally, marker, massive, panic, somehow
