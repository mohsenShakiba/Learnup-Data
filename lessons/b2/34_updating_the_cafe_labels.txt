Updating the Cafe Labels
A: We've had another customer enquiry this morning about which of our cakes are made without wheat or dairy.
B: People ask about that almost every day now. We should make the counter signs clearer before the council inspector visits next week.
A: Have the food regulations changed since last year?
B: No. The inspector sent us some guidelines to help us check our signs. Let's compare those with the recipes before we print new tags.
A: What about the lemon cake? Since we modified that recipe to use oil, can we write "no dairy" on its tag?
B: Changing the recipe doesn't ensure there's no dairy in the finished cake. We still use the same bowls and machine for everything.
A: Could traces of dairy get into it from the other cakes?
B: Yes, and even a tiny amount can make someone ill. We shouldn't claim there's no dairy unless we can eliminate that risk.
A: Could we wash the machine and keep two bowls and a knife strictly for the lemon cake?
B: That might help, but we need to check how we prepare it, from start to finish. Let's ask the inspector what else we'd need to change.
A: I'll leave "no dairy" off the tag, then. Can we check the ingredient lists together this afternoon?
B: Yes. We'll also check what the signs need to say about dairy or wheat getting into the cakes from other food.

Vocabulary: dairy, eliminate, enquiry, ensure, guideline, inspector, modify, regulation, strictly, tag, trace, wheat
