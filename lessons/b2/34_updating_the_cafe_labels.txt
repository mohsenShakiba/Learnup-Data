Updating the Cafe Labels
A: We've had another customer enquiry this morning about which of our cakes are made without wheat or dairy.
B: People ask about that almost every day now. We should make the counter signs clearer before the council inspector visits next week.
A: Have the food regulations changed since last year?
B: The rules are the same, but the new council guidelines say we should list every main ingredient on the price tag beside each cake.
A: What about the lemon cake? Since we modified that recipe to use oil, can we write "no dairy" on its tag?
B: There isn't any dairy in the cake itself now, but we can only write that if we ensure it hasn't touched any dairy in the kitchen.
A: That's hard in our small space. We use the same machine for mixing everything.
B: Even a tiny trace of dairy can make some people ill. Unless we can eliminate that risk, the tag needs to say it's made in a kitchen that uses dairy and wheat.
A: Could we wash the machine and keep two bowls and a knife strictly for the lemon cake?
B: Yes, and we can bake it first thing in the morning when the tables are clean.
A: Then we can put "no dairy" on that tag. I'll print the new tags with the full list of ingredients this afternoon.
B: Thanks. Can you show them to me before you put them on the display shelf?

Vocabulary: dairy, eliminate, enquiry, ensure, guideline, inspector, modify, regulation, strictly, tag, trace, wheat
