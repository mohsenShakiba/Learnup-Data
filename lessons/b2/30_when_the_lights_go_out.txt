When the Lights Go Out
A: Did your lights just go out too? I was just starting dinner.
B: Yeah, the street lights are out too. Looks like the power's out on the whole street.
A: Is your phone fully charged? Mine's almost dead. Can you check when it'll be back on?
B: Yeah, hang on... The power company's website is useless. It just says there's a power cut. I'll try calling.
A: Thanks. Where are the candles?
B: On the shelf above the sink.
A: Found them. I'll light one and put it on the table.
B: Good. I'll move these paper bags out of the way.
A: Okay. Have you got through to anyone?
B: I'm still waiting for an operator. The recorded message says it's a widespread problem, but they're hoping to restore power shortly.
A: "Shortly" could mean anything. I'm starving.
B: There are some sandwiches in the fridge. Shall we have those?
A: Yeah. Hopefully it won't be off all night. I only filled the fridge yesterday.
B: It'd be a nightmare to throw all that food away. Let's keep the door closed as much as we can.
A: I'll get the sandwiches out quickly, then. Can you pass me two plates?
B: Here you go. Oh, hang on—someone's answered.

Vocabulary: candle, fully, hopefully, nightmare, operator, restore, shortly, starve, useless, widespread
