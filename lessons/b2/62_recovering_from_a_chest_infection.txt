Recovering from a Chest Infection
A: Your chest sounds much clearer today. Has the fever gone down since you started the medicine?
B: Yes, my temperature has been normal since Wednesday, and it doesn't hurt to swallow now.
A: Good. That shows the tablets are clearing the bacteria that caused the infection.
B: Why do I still feel so tired when I walk upstairs, then?
A: Your lungs are still weak after the illness, so it takes more effort to get enough oxygen when you're active.
B: I was hoping the medicine would cure that tired feeling straight away. I wanted to go back to running this weekend.
A: Give your body time to heal. Going back to hard exercise too soon could do more harm than good.
B: How long does it usually take to recover completely?
A: Another week or two. Your natural immune defence has been working hard, even though the worst part is over.
B: Can I at least walk to the shops if I go slowly?
A: Gentle walks on flat ground are fine, as long as you stop and rest if you feel short of breath.
B: Do I need to finish the rest of the tablets now that my throat feels better?
A: Yes, finish the whole packet so the illness doesn't come back.
B: Okay, I'll take the last two days of tablets and stick to short walks until next week.
