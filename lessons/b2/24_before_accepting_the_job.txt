Before Accepting the Job
A: The hotel has offered me the job, but they want an answer tomorrow.
B: Congratulations! Are you happy with the terms?
A: Mostly. The hourly wage is higher than mine, but the letter doesn't say how many hours I'd get each week.
B: You should clarify that before you accept. Did they mention it at the interview?
A: They said around thirty, but I need a regular income. Do you think I could negotiate a minimum number of hours?
B: It's worth asking. Have they sent you a contract yet?
A: No, just this initial offer. There's information about their pension scheme, though.
B: That's useful for the future, but it doesn't answer your question about next month's pay.
A: Exactly. They also talked about the prospect of promotion. I liked the idea of training for a more senior position.
B: Would you still want this job if that took a few years?
A: Yes, if the hours were reliable. I enjoyed meeting the team, and the work sounds interesting.
B: Then make the hours your main question. Who sent the offer?
A: A representative from their employment agency. She said I could call her with questions.
B: Call her today. She may need to speak to the hotel before she can give you an answer.
A: I'll do that. I can ask for the agreed hours to be included in the contract too.
B: Good idea. Then you'll have something clear to base your decision on.

Vocabulary: clarify, initial, minimum, negotiate, pension, prospect, promotion, representative, scheme, senior, terms, wage
