The Local History Books
A: Hello, I'm trying to find out when the bridge by the square was built. Do you have any local history books?
B: Try this one by a local historian. Let me check the index... Here, there's a photo of the bridge under construction in 1930.
A: Was there a bridge before that one? My grandfather always talked about an older one.
B: He's right. The 1930 bridge replaced a wooden one that came down in a winter storm. They printed part of the old newspaper report here.
A: Can I see? Oh, it says the whole village helped with the rescue. Grandad told me his dad pulled a man out. Are there any pictures?
B: The photos in this book are all from 1930. But see that notebook in the glass case? It's open at a drawing of the old bridge from 1880.
A: Really? Whose notebook was it?
B: It belonged to the founder of the village school. He kept notes on the river. It's in remarkable condition for its age, though the ink has gone quite pale.
A: Is that the old rail line in the drawing? The wooden bridge must have been further north than the one by the square.
B: That's right. See the distant tower in the drawing? It belongs to the old school, just across from where the bridge stood.
A: I walk past there every morning and had no idea. Can I take a photo of the drawing?
B: Yes, photos are fine. You're welcome to borrow the history book, too.
A: Great, I'll take the book to the desk now. My grandfather will be so pleased when I show him that drawing!

Vocabulary: construction, distant, founder, historian, index, ink, notebook, rail, remarkable, rescue
