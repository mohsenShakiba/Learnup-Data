The Local History Books
A: I'm trying to find out when the bridge by the square was built. Do you have any local history books?
B: Yes. This one is by a local historian. There's a chapter about the bridge's origin.
A: It says 1930. But my grandfather said there was a bridge there before that.
B: That's the current bridge. It replaced a wooden one after a storm. Look at this passage.
A: Can I take a photo of it? I'd like to include a short extract in my school project.
B: Yes. What's your project about?
A: How our street has changed. I need a drawing to illustrate what the old bridge looked like.
B: Here's a copy of a notebook from 1880. There's a drawing on page eight.
A: The ink is quite pale, but I can see that the bridge was further north.
B: Yes, beside the former rail station. That's where the supermarket is now.
A: That's remarkable. I walk past it every day and had no idea.
B: The map at the back shows the old road too. You can compare it with a current map.
A: I'll put that drawing next to a photo of today's bridge. Can I borrow the history book?
B: Yes. Bring it to the desk with your library card when you're ready.

Vocabulary: extract, former, historian, illustrate, ink, notebook, origin, passage, rail, remarkable
