Visiting an Uncle in Hospital
A: Thanks for coming straight to the hospital. Have you spoken to the doctor since the ambulance brought Uncle David in?
B: Yes, she came out ten minutes ago. He's had a minor stroke, but he's fully conscious and able to talk to us.
A: What a relief. When Mum called me at work, it sounded like a serious medical crisis.
B: It was frightening at first because he had a sudden, intense pain in his head and couldn't lift his left arm.
A: Has his arm improved at all since they gave him the treatment?
B: Yes, the strength in his hand has improved dramatically over the last two hours, though he still has a slight weakness in his fingers.
A: How is he coping emotionally with staying in the hospital room?
B: He's a bit shaken, naturally, but the nurses here have been very kind and sympathetic.
A: Did the doctor answer your inquiry about how long he'll need to stay in hospital?
B: At least three days for tests. Since he doesn't have any children, you and I are his closest relatives along with Mum, his only sibling.
A: Then let's take turns visiting him so he doesn't feel alone in the evenings.
B: Good idea. You go in and sit with him for a few minutes now while I call Mum with the news.

Vocabulary: ambulance, conscious, crisis, dramatically, emotionally, inquiry, intense, sibling, slight, stroke, sympathetic
