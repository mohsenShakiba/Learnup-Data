The Sleeves on My Jacket
A: Hello. Could you make the sleeves on this jacket a bit shorter? It fits across my shoulders, but you can hardly see my hands.
B: Yes, of course. Let me see it on you. Does it have matching trousers, or is it just the jacket?
A: Just the jacket. It's quite casual, really, though I was hoping to wear it to my sister's wedding.
B: With dark trousers and a white shirt, it would look quite elegant. Let's see how much shorter the sleeves need to be.
A: Good. I don't really want to buy a whole new outfit.
B: Stand the way you normally would. See how low the sleeve hangs over your palm? I'd bring it back to just below your wrist.
A: So about an inch shorter?
B: Yes. Bend your elbow for me before I take the final measurement. Does that feel comfortable?
A: Yes, there's plenty of room. Will you have to move these buttons?
B: I will. Otherwise, they'll be too close to the edge to look neat.
A: Will there be marks where they were?
B: I'll check the fabric under the buttons before I start. If there are marks that I can't hide, I'll call you first.
A: Thanks. How much will it cost?
B: Let me check the inside... Twenty pounds, including moving the buttons.
A: That's fine. Could I collect it on Thursday?
B: Yes. Bring the shirt you'll wear with it if you want to check the length together.

Vocabulary: casual, elbow, elegant, inch, matching, measurement, neat, outfit, palm, wrist
