Having a Jacket Altered
A: Hello. Could you alter this jacket? It fits across my shoulders, but my hands almost disappear inside it.
B: Let me see it on you. Is it for everyday wear?
A: Usually, yes. It's quite casual, but I was hoping to wear it to my sister's wedding.
B: With a white shirt, it would look quite elegant. Let's see where the length needs to change.
A: Good. I don't really want to buy a whole new outfit.
B: That makes sense. Let your arms hang down. I'll mark the fabric just below your wrist, here.
A: So about an inch shorter?
B: Yes. Bend your elbow for me before I take the final measurement. Does that feel comfortable?
A: Yes, there's plenty of room. Will you have to move these buttons?
B: I will. Otherwise, they'll be too close to the edge.
A: Will there be marks where they were?
B: I'll check the fabric under the buttons before I start. If there are marks that I can't hide, I'll call you first.
A: Thanks. How much will it cost?
B: Around twenty pounds. I can give you a precise price once I've checked the inside.
A: That's fine. Could I collect it on Thursday?
B: Yes. Bring the shirt you'll wear with it so we can check that everything looks neat together.

Vocabulary: alter, casual, elbow, elegant, inch, measurement, neat, outfit, precise, wrist
