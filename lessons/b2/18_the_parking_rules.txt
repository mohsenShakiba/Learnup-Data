The Parking Rules
A: Why did the building meeting last so long tonight? I heard loud shouting from the hall.
B: There was a heated debate about the trucks that keep parking in our front yard. Several neighbours were furious after a driver blocked the gate yesterday.
A: I saw that incident from my window. Did anyone speak to the driver?
B: Yes, Paul went outside and accused him of leaving the truck there deliberately, even though the sign says parking is prohibited.
A: How did the driver defend himself?
B: He said he was only dropping off heavy boxes, and he asked why our building didn't have a special zone for shop deliveries.
A: What did the building administration say at the meeting?
B: Their manager agreed that blocking the fire gate was completely unacceptable. However, she dismissed Paul's demand to lock the main gate all day.
A: Why did she oppose that idea?
B: She explained that a locked gate would cause a new conflict whenever visitors or doctors needed to enter quickly.
A: That's a fair concern. Did the committee propose a better way to resolve the problem?
B: Yes, the majority voted to impose a strict new regulation starting next Monday.
A: How will it work?
B: Every car must show a resident card, and the guard has been told to forbid any truck from stopping for more than ten minutes.
A: What if a driver refuses to obey the guard?
B: Repeat offenders will lose their right to enter the yard, and they won't be allowed to appeal the decision.

Vocabulary: accuse, administration, appeal, concern, conflict, debate, defend, deliberately, demand, dismiss, forbid, furious, impose, incident, majority, obey, offender, oppose, prohibit, propose, regulation, resolve, strict, unacceptable, zone
