The Parking Rules
A: Did the meeting sort out the trucks blocking our gate? I heard some shouting.
B: There was a heated debate. Paul was still furious about yesterday's incident.
A: I saw the truck. Did the driver explain why he stopped there?
B: He said he was delivering boxes. Paul accused him of deliberately ignoring the sign.
A: That's hard to prove. But blocking the fire entrance is still unacceptable.
B: Yes. Paul wanted to lock the main gate all day, but that didn't get much support.
A: That would make visits and deliveries harder. What did they propose instead?
B: A delivery zone at the far end of the yard, away from the gate. Drivers can stop there for ten minutes.
A: Do we have enough space for that?
B: The majority agreed to use the two spaces beside the shops. Resident parking will be on the other side.
A: My only concern is what happens when that zone is full.
B: The next driver will have to come back later. The guard will keep the gate clear, and visitors can still come in as usual.
A: That sounds better. When does it start?
B: Monday. I'll send you the notice so you can see which spaces are changing.

Vocabulary: accuse, concern, debate, deliberately, furious, incident, majority, propose, resident, unacceptable, zone
