When the Map Sends Us the Wrong Way
A: We've been driving through this village for ten minutes. Does your phone still say we're close to the hotel?
B: Yes, but the map doesn't look right. Keep going along this road for now.
A: What's the navigation app telling us to do now?
B: Turn through that farm gate. Don't turn there, though—the entrance says private.
A: Can you see somewhere I can stop?
B: Careful round this sharp curve. There's a car park on the left, but the entrance is hidden behind those bushes.
A: Right, we're parked. Let me see your phone. Why is the arrow pointing back towards the gate?
B: It may not have a clear satellite signal. Look at the blue dot—it shows us in the middle of a field.
A: So it's giving us the wrong directions because it doesn't know where we are?
B: Looks like it. Try moving the map back so we can see the whole village.
A: Like this? Oh, there's the highway we left ten minutes ago.
B: Yes. The hotel sent us directions too. Give me the phone and I'll open that message.
A: I'd forgotten about those. Did we take the wrong exit?
B: No, we took the right one. But the message says to stay on the main road until we reach a stone bridge.
A: We haven't passed a stone bridge. The app must have sent us off the main road too soon.
B: It looks that way. These rural roads all look similar to me. Shall I call the hotel and check which way to go?
A: Yes. How far back do you reckon the main road is?
B: About five minutes by car. Let's confirm where to turn before we leave this car park.

Vocabulary: arrow, bush, curve, dot, exit, hidden, highway, navigation, reckon, rural, satellite
