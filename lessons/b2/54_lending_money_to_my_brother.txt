Lending Money to My Brother
A: Sorry I was on the phone for so long. That was my brother Dan. His old car broke down again today, and he's asked if we can lend him two thousand pounds to buy another one.
B: Two thousand? Did the garage say his car isn't worth repairing?
A: Yes, the motor's gone. Without a car, he can't carry his gardening tools or fulfil the jobs he's booked for this month.
B: I have a lot of sympathy for him, and I know he needs a car to earn a living. Do we have that much spare in our savings?
A: We could withdraw two thousand without touching the money for our bills. He has a lot of pride, and he's my only sibling, so I know he wouldn't ask unless he really needed to.
B: I'd like to help him too. Nevertheless, we should think carefully before we lend that much to family.
A: Are you worried he won't pay us back? His gardening work has been much more stable this year.
B: I know he's doing better, and I don't think he's dishonest. But when he borrowed money from your parents, there was a lot of uncertainty about when he'd pay it back.
A: That's true. They never agreed on monthly payments, so it went on for months and caused a bit of anger on both sides.
B: Exactly. I don't want money to come between us and Dan. If we lend it to him, we need a clear plan from the start.
A: What if we ask him to pay back two hundred pounds a month, starting next month?
B: Do you know to what extent his winter income drops? Gardening work is usually quieter after October.
A: Good point. Maybe one hundred a month over the winter would be more realistic, and two hundred in the spring.
B: That sounds fairer. Has he already found another car to buy?
A: Yes, he's going to see a used one on Saturday. He wants to buy it straight away so he can get back to work on Monday.
B: Tell him to have a mechanic check it before he pays anything. Otherwise, he could end up with another bad car.
A: I'll call him back tonight and suggest that. If he agrees to the mechanic and the monthly payments, are you happy for us to lend him the money?
B: Yes. Once we've all agreed, let's put the dates and amounts in a message so everyone's clear.

Vocabulary: anger, dishonest, extent, fulfil, nevertheless, pride, sibling, stable, sympathy, uncertainty, withdraw
