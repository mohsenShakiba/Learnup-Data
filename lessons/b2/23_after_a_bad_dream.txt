After a Bad Dream
A: Sorry, did I wake you? I just had a bad dream.
B: Yes. I heard you scream. Are you all right?
A: I think so. In my dream, I was being chased.
B: Oh, that must have been terrifying.
A: Yes. I felt completely trapped in the dark cave, and I was so relieved when I woke up.
B: Do you want me to turn on the light?
A: Please. I don't dare go back to sleep yet.
B: Come and sit in the kitchen with me for a minute. Would you like some water?
A: Yes, thanks. I feel like such a fool for waking you up over a dream.
B: Don't be silly. It felt real while you were asleep.
A: Could we stay and talk for a bit? Sitting here in silence makes it hard to stop thinking about it.
B: Of course. Let's go and get that water first.

Vocabulary: cave, chase, dare, fool, relieved, scare, scream, silence, terrify, trap
