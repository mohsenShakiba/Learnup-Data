After a Bad Dream
A: Sorry, did I wake you? I just had a bad dream.
B: Yes. I heard you scream. Are you all right?
A: I think so. In my dream, I was being chased through a never-ending cave.
B: Oh, that must have been terrifying.
A: Yes. I was trapped there in the darkness, and there was complete silence.
B: Do you want me to turn on the light?
A: Please. I need a minute to calm my nerves.
B: There. You're back in your room. Would you like some water?
A: Yes, thanks. I don't dare go back to sleep yet.
B: Come and sit in the kitchen with me for a minute.
A: I feel like a fool for getting so upset.
B: Don't. It felt real while you were asleep.
A: Could we talk about something else for a bit?
B: Of course. Let's get that water first.

Vocabulary: cave, chase, dare, darkness, fool, nerve, scream, silence, terrify, trap
