The Missing Visa Forms
A: Are you ready to leave? If we don't go now, we'll be caught in the morning rush, and we can't be late for our visa interview.
B: Wait a second—where is the brown envelope with our forms and income proof? I thought it was on the kitchen counter.
A: It isn't here. Don't panic; it cannot have disappeared by itself.
B: I checked all the pages at ten o'clock last night, after we added your signature to the application form.
A: You were also sorting the household bills at the kitchen table. You must have picked the envelope up with those papers by mistake.
B: Let me look in the study. I might have put it on the desk near the printer, or it may have slipped behind the shelf.
A: Could you have dropped it into the waste paper basket when you cleared the table?
B: No, I can't have thrown it away—the basket is completely empty, and I haven't taken the rubbish out this week.
A: What about your coat pocket? You could have put it there while you were looking for your keys.
B: No, it's too large for my pocket. This is ridiculous! I should have packed everything inside my bag last night instead of leaving it on the table.
A: Calm down. This anxiety won't help us find it. Why did you take the forms into the study anyway?
B: Oh! I needed to copy our marriage certificate and check that both our travel cards were still valid.
A: Then look under that pile of newspapers next to the printer—you must have left the envelope there after you finished copying.
B: You're right, here it is! I assumed I'd brought it back to the kitchen. Let's go before we get any more desperate for time.

Vocabulary: anxiety, assume, basket, certificate, counter, desperate, envelope, household, income, panic, pile, proof, ridiculous, rush, signature, slip, valid, visa
