Checking the Visa Forms
A: Is the envelope on the kitchen counter ready for our visa interview tomorrow?
B: Almost. Can you check this form while I make a copy of our marriage certificate?
A: Wait. This is our old address. Where did you get this form?
B: From an old message. I must have opened the wrong attachment. I assumed the saved form was up to date.
A: Can you change the address on your computer?
B: Yes, I'll print a fresh copy. I should have checked before printing the first one.
A: It's better to find out tonight. Do you have the letter about your income?
B: Yes, it's in the envelope. The list also asks for a household bill as proof of our address.
A: Here's the electricity bill. It has both our names on it.
B: Thanks. The new copy's coming out now. Can you check it against the bill?
A: The address matches now. It just needs your signature at the bottom.
B: Done. I'll put the form and the bill in with the certificate, then the envelope is ready.

Vocabulary: assume, attachment, certificate, counter, envelope, household, income, proof, signature, visa
