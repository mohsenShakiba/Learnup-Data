Checking the Visa Forms
A: Is the envelope on the kitchen counter ready for our visa interview tomorrow?
B: Almost. Could you check this form while I make a copy of our marriage certificate?
A: Hang on, this has our old address on it. Which file did you print?
B: Oh no. I must have opened the attachment from an old message instead of the updated form on my computer. I just assumed it was the right one.
A: Do you still have the updated one on your computer?
B: Yes, here it is. I'm printing it now. I really should have checked it before I printed the whole thing.
A: Better to spot it tonight than tomorrow morning! Did you pack the letter from work about your income?
B: Yes, that's already in the envelope. The list also asks for a recent household bill as proof of address.
A: Here's last month's electricity bill. It has both our names on it.
B: Great, thanks. The new copy's coming out now. Could you check the address against the bill?
A: Yes, the address matches now. Don't forget your signature at the bottom.
B: Done. I'll put the form and the bill in with the certificate, and then the envelope's ready for the morning.

Vocabulary: assume, attachment, certificate, counter, envelope, household, income, proof, signature, visa
