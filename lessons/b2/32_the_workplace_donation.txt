The Workplace Donation
A: Have we decided which local groups are getting this year's staff donation?
B: The committee met this morning to go through everyone's ideas. Two local groups got the most support.
A: Which ones?
B: The winter shelter for homeless people in town and the group that keeps the river clean.
A: Good. I hoped we'd pick the shelter. Can we support both, or do we have to pick one?
B: The managers have agreed to put two thousand pounds into the fund. It'll buy hot meals at the shelter and gloves for people clearing rubbish by the river.
A: That's generous. Both groups will be delighted. What about staff who'd like to help in person?
B: The river group has asked for help planting trees along the path on a Saturday next month.
A: Is that open to families? My son learned about river pollution at school. They had a whole week of activities to raise awareness.
B: Yes, if an adult goes with him. The shelter needs help sorting winter coats and handing out meals on Saturday evenings.
A: For the shelter, is that just kitchen work? I don't have any first aid training.
B: That's fine. It's purely a question of getting the meals out while they're hot. Their winter project is about hunger. They have trained staff there if anyone needs other help.
A: We only gave everyone a week's notice last time. Let's put the dates up today so people can plan ahead.
B: Good idea. I'll write a short message about both groups and ask people to put their names down by Friday.

Vocabulary: aid, awareness, committee, delighted, donation, fund, homeless, hunger, purely, shelter
