The Workplace Donation
A: Have we decided where this year's workplace donation is going?
B: The committee met this morning to discuss the staff's ideas. Two local groups got the most support.
A: Which ones?
B: The winter shelter for homeless people in town, and the river conservation group.
A: Those are both good choices. Can we support both of them, or do we have to choose one?
B: The managers have agreed to put two thousand pounds into the fund, and they're happy for us to divide it between the two.
A: That's generous. What about staff who'd like to help in person?
B: The river group has asked for help planting trees along the path on a Saturday next month.
A: What about the shelter? Do they need practical help too?
B: They need people to sort winter coats and distribute hot meals on Saturday evenings.
A: We should make it clear that taking part is voluntary. Some people can't give up their weekends.
B: Of course. I hope participation will be higher this time, though. We only had six people at the last event.
A: We only gave them a week's notice. Let's put the dates on the board today so people can plan ahead.
B: Good idea. I'll write a short message about both groups and ask staff to sign up by Friday.

Vocabulary: committee, conservation, distribute, donation, fund, homeless, participation, shelter, voluntary, workplace
