The Workplace Donation
A: Have we decided which local groups are getting this year's staff donation?
B: The committee met this morning to go through everyone's ideas. Two local groups got the most support.
A: Which ones?
B: The winter shelter for homeless people in town, and the river conservation group.
A: Those are both great choices—they do vital work locally. Can we support both, or do we have to pick one?
B: The managers have agreed to put two thousand pounds into the fund, and they're happy for us to divide it between the two.
A: That's generous. Both groups will be delighted. What about staff who'd like to help in person?
B: The river group has asked for help planting trees along the path on a Saturday next month.
A: What about the shelter? Do they need practical help too?
B: Yes, they need people to sort winter coats and hand out hot meals on Saturday evenings.
A: We should make it clear that taking part is purely voluntary. Some people can't give up their weekends.
B: Of course. I still hope more people sign up this time, though. Only six of us went to the last event.
A: We only gave everyone a week's notice last time. Let's put the dates up today so people can plan ahead.
B: Good idea. I'll write a short message about both groups and ask people to put their names down by Friday.

Vocabulary: committee, conservation, delighted, donation, fund, homeless, purely, shelter, vital, voluntary
