The Workplace Donation
A: Have the staff sent in their ideas for our workplace donation this year?
B: Yes. The committee met this morning to read them, and two local groups got the most support.
A: Which ones?
B: The winter shelter for homeless people in town, and the river conservation group.
A: Those are both good choices. Can we support both of them, or do we have to choose one?
B: The managers have agreed to put two thousand pounds into the fund, and they're happy for us to divide it between the two.
A: That's generous. Are we still planning a voluntary event for staff who want to help in person?
B: Yes. The river group asked if a team could undertake some planting along the path next month.
A: What about the shelter? Do they need practical help too?
B: They need people to sort winter coats and distribute hot meals on Saturday evenings.
A: How did the last weekend initiative go? Did many people join in?
B: Participation was smaller than we'd hoped because we only gave everyone a week's notice.
A: Then let's put the dates on the board today so people can plan ahead.
B: Good idea. I'll write a short message about both groups and ask staff to sign up by Friday.

Vocabulary: committee, conservation, distribute, donation, fund, homeless, initiative, participation, shelter, undertake, voluntary, workplace
