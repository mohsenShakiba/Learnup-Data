Packing Lunch for the Park
A: Can you help me finish packing lunch for the park? The sandwiches are on the kitchen table.
B: Sure. Is this the bag we're taking? There's something sticky on the bottom.
A: Oh, the drink has spilled. I didn't close the bottle properly.
B: I'll take everything out. Could you grab a cloth?
A: Here. The blanket's wet along one edge too.
B: I'll put it outside to dry. What time are we meeting everyone?
A: One, so we have another hour. I'll wrap the sandwiches while you clean the bag.
B: What about the chocolate on the table? It might melt in this heat.
A: I'll leave it in the fridge until we go. Can you put a packet of tissues with the sandwiches?
B: Sure. Where are we meeting?
A: I suggested the grass alongside the river, near the bridge.
B: There were lots of bugs by the water last time. Can we sit further back?
A: Yes. I'll message everyone and ask them to meet us at the gate instead. Then we can choose a place together.
B: Good idea. The bag's clean now, but the inside's still wet.
A: Let's use the other one. I'll close this bottle firmly and keep it in the side pocket.
B: Thanks. I'll leave the wet bag outside with the blanket.

Vocabulary: alongside, blanket, bug, firmly, grab, melt, spill, sticky, tissue, wrap
