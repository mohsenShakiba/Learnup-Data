Packing Lunch for the Park
A: Is this bag ready for lunch in the park? There's something sticky on the bottom.
B: Oh, the drink has spilled. I didn't close the bottle properly.
A: It hasn't reached the sandwiches, has it?
B: No, they're in a separate box. Could you grab a cloth while I take everything out?
A: Here. The blanket's wet along one edge too.
B: I'll leave it outside to dry. We still have an hour before we need to go.
A: Where's the chocolate? Please tell me it wasn't in that bag.
B: It's on the kitchen table. I was going to wrap it in paper and put it in with the food.
A: It might melt in this heat. Let's keep it in the fridge until we leave.
B: All right. I'll put it beside the cold drinks. Do we need anything else?
A: Some tissues. We can use those after we've eaten.
B: There's a packet on the shelf. Have you chosen a place to meet everyone?
A: I suggested the grass alongside the river. There's a large tree near the bridge.
B: There were lots of bugs by the water last time. I'd rather sit a little further back.
A: We can choose somewhere when we arrive. There's room wherever you go in that part of the park.
B: But how will the others find us if we move away from the bridge?
A: I'll meet them at the gate at one and bring them over. You can stay with the food.
B: That works. I'll finish cleaning this bag and check the bottle again before I pack it.

Vocabulary: alongside, blanket, bug, grab, melt, spill, sticky, tissue, wherever, wrap
