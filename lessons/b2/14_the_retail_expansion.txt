Expanding the Cafe
A: What did our accountant think of the investor's idea of opening a second cafe downtown?
B: She thinks the partnership could work, but she's worried about the rent on that place in the middle of town.
A: The rent's high, yes. But that street's full of people wherever you look. We'd sell a lot more coffee there.
B: We'd sell more, sure. Would that pay the rent? That's what worries me.
A: Would he finance the equipment and the work on the new place?
B: Only about half of it. We'd still have to take out a big loan and be in debt for ten years.
A: Ten years? That sounds risky, especially when butter already costs almost twice what we paid last year.
B: Exactly. Our kitchen's full even now. We couldn't bake enough for both cafes.
A: What if we turned him down and grew this place instead? The woman who founded the shop next door is giving up the building. She asked if we'd like a look inside.
B: That could work. Our savings would pay the extra rent while we set it up. How much bigger is it than this place?
A: I only saw it from the door. The front looked big enough for extra tables, but I didn't see the kitchen.
B: We need to measure it first. Let's arrange to look inside this week, then get some prices for the building work.

Vocabulary: accountant, debt, downtown, finance, found, investor, partnership, risky, saving, wherever
