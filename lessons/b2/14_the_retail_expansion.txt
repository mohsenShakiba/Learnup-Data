Expanding the Cafe
A: Did you get a chance to go through that investor's proposal with our accountant?
B: Yes, we looked over the figures yesterday afternoon. He wants to go into partnership with us and open a second cafe in the middle of town.
A: It's exciting, though, isn't it? A central spot like that could really boost our sales.
B: Our revenue would go up, sure, but that doesn't mean more profit for us. The rent on that street is huge.
A: Wouldn't his money pay for the new equipment and setting the place up, at least?
B: Only about half of it. Even with his money, we'd still have to take out a big loan and be in debt for ten years.
A: Okay, that does sound risky, especially when our profit margin on cakes is already pretty tight.
B: Exactly. Plus, if we opened a second place now, our kitchen here wouldn't have the capacity to bake for both cafes.
A: What if we turned him down and just expanded here instead? Isn't the empty shop next door still available to rent?
B: Funny you say that. I asked the man who owns the building this morning, and it is. Our savings could pay the extra rent while we set it up.
A: Would it give us enough space for a bigger kitchen and extra tables?
B: We need to measure it first. Let's arrange to look inside this week, then get some prices for the building work.

Vocabulary: accountant, boost, capacity, debt, investor, margin, partnership, proposal, revenue, risky, saving
