Preparing the Orchestra Concert
A: Have you seen the town hall since they finished putting up the stage lights for Saturday's concert?
B: Yes, I looked in an hour ago. The hall looks stunning with the new lights above the seats.
A: Good. The local book shop agreed to provide sponsorship for the printed programmes, so those are ready too.
B: That's a relief. Are we still opening the evening with the piece by the young local composer?
A: Yes. She was asked to compose a short piece for the town event, and our orchestra will play it first.
B: The ending of her piece is quite dramatic, especially when the fast drums come in.
A: It is. The middle section is harder for the strings, though; we need to keep the pitch steady when the hall gets warm.
B: Are we still playing the two opera songs after the interval?
A: Yes, and the guest star has a magnificent sound. The audience will love her.
B: She told a very amusing story during yesterday's practice when our music teacher dropped his stick.
A: She made everyone feel calm. If Saturday goes well, it should be a really memorable evening for all of us.
B: My parents are coming, and it'll be a delight to play in a full hall again.
A: Let's get the chairs set out before seven so the string section has time to prepare before the doors open.

Vocabulary: amusing, compose, composer, delight, dramatic, magnificent, memorable, opera, orchestra, pitch, sponsorship, stunning
