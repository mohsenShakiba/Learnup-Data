Talking After a Driving Lesson
A: You handled that busy crossing near the station much better today. How did it feel from the driver's seat?
B: Better than last week, until that white truck pulled out in front of us without a signal.
A: Yes, you'll encounter impatient drivers like that on almost every trip, so it's vital to leave plenty of space ahead.
B: I felt a sudden wave of anger when he cut across our lane, and I nearly shouted at him instead of slowing down.
A: That's a common tendency when someone surprises you, but your instant reaction to slow the car down was the right one.
B: Did I judge the distance accurately when I moved into the left lane afterwards?
A: You did. You checked your mirrors, waited for a safe gap, and didn't let the truck driver rush you.
B: I used to lose my courage whenever the cars behind me got too close.
A: Frequent practice on busy town roads is the only thing that makes that easier.
B: Should we proceed to the faster ring road in next Thursday's lesson?
A: Yes, as long as you obey the speed signs when the limit changes near the bridge.
B: Good. My progress feels quite rapid this month compared with when I started in the spring.

Vocabulary: accurately, anger, courage, encounter, frequent, instant, obey, proceed, rapid, tendency, vital
