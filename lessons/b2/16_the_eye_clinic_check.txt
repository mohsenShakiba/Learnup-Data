At the Eye Clinic
A: Hello. I've come to have my vision checked, but this letter says "Eye Surgery" at the top. Am I having an operation today?
B: No. You're seeing the surgeon so she can assess the problem and discuss possible treatment.
A: That's a relief. I was getting anxious because I hadn't expected an operation.
B: I'm sorry the letter wasn't clear. Have you brought a list of your medication?
A: Yes, but the doctor changed one of my pills last week. I don't remember the new name.
B: Do you have the box with you?
A: No, but my doctor sent me the details in a message. Can I show you that?
B: Yes, that will help us keep your records accurate.
A: If I need surgery, will I have to stay overnight?
B: That depends on which procedure the surgeon recommends. She'll explain how long recovery would take and whether you'd need help at home.
A: Can I ask her about time off work too? My eye has been very sensitive to the lights in the office.
B: Yes. Tell her when that happens and how it affects your work.
A: I'll write that down before I go in. I always forget my questions when I'm nervous.
B: Take your time. She's still with another patient.

Vocabulary: accurate, anxious, assess, medication, overnight, pill, procedure, recovery, sensitive, surgeon, surgery, vision
