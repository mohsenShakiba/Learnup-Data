The Eye Clinic Check
A: Good morning. Have my test results been checked by the eye surgeon yet?
B: Yes, your eyes were assessed twenty minutes ago, and the camera gave us a very accurate picture of the back nerve.
A: Did the doctor find any new problem?
B: No, fortunately the small problem had been detected early enough. This morning's procedure will cure the weak vision in your left eye.
A: Will the doctor operate on it today, or do I need another appointment?
B: The surgery room is being prepared right now, and the whole treatment only takes half an hour.
A: Will I have to stay in the hospital overnight after that?
B: Not at all. You'll rest in the nursing room for two hours, and then your family can drive you home.
A: My brother had a similar treatment last year, and he said his eye felt quite red and tired that evening.
B: A little pain is normal, so a mild pill will be given to you before you leave to relieve any pain.
A: What about washing my face?
B: Keep water away from the eye for three days so you don't get an infection while it begins to heal.
A: Should I wear dark glasses when I go outside?
B: Yes, your eye will be very sensitive to intense light during the first week of recovery, and you'll also use these drops as your daily medication.

Vocabulary: accurate, assess, cure, detect, heal, infection, intense, medication, nerve, nursing, operate, overnight, pill, procedure, recovery, relieve, sensitive, surgeon, surgery, vision
