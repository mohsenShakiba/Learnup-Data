New Rules for Work Computers
A: Have you managed to change your computer password yet? Mine keeps saying my new choice isn't strong enough.
B: Yes. I used the password manager that IT installed. It created a long combination of letters and numbers for me.
A: That sounds complicated. How am I supposed to remember it?
B: The manager stores it for you. I'll show you how to use it. Why did IT impose all these new rules today?
A: Someone at headquarters entered their password on a fake website yesterday. That could have exposed our customers' personal information.
B: No wonder they're worried about security threats. Did you see the second part of the instructions?
A: About using our phones? I haven't read that far yet.
B: Yes. We have to enable an extra security check. It sends us a text with a number to enter when we sign in. Mine arrived almost instantly when I tried it.
A: So I enter that number as well as my password?
B: Right. And they've changed the rules on saving files, too. The new policy prohibits copying company records onto your own external hard drive.
A: That makes sense. Someone could easily leave one of those on a train.
B: Exactly. Every manager has been asked to reinforce that rule at this week's team meetings.
A: Where should I save the files for tomorrow's presentation, then?
B: Put them in the shared space on the internal network. Let's set up your new password first, then I'll show you where it is.

Vocabulary: combination, complicated, enable, expose, headquarters, impose, instantly, password, prohibit, reinforce, threat
