Tools to Share
A: Has the residents' association agreed to the shared tools store room?
B: Yes. They liked the initiative, but we have to explain how we'll pay for it after the first year.
A: I thought the grant would pay for everything we need to acquire.
B: It gives us enough to equip the store room at the start. We still need money for repairs and insurance, though.
A: Is the insurance a monthly payment?
B: No, we pay annually. I've asked for a price, but it hasn't come back yet.
A: Then we shouldn't announce a membership fee until we know that cost.
B: Agreed. We need a sustainable plan. The store room won't be much use if we can't afford to replace broken tools.
A: Could people donate tools they no longer use?
B: Yes, but we'd need someone to check their condition. A strong ladder would be a useful asset; a broken one would give us another bill.
A: I'll consult Jo. She repairs tools for work and might be able to help.
B: Good idea. We should also ask residents what they actually need before we buy anything.
A: Shall I put a short form through everyone's door?
B: Yes. Ask which tools they'd borrow and whether they could help open the store room on Saturday mornings.
A: When do you think we can launch it?
B: Let's wait for the insurance price and Jo's answer before choosing a date.
A: Fair enough. I won't advertise it widely yet. We'll start with the people in these two buildings.
B: That should be practical. We can review the costs after three months before inviting more members.

Vocabulary: acquire, annually, asset, association, consult, equip, initiative, launch, sustainable, widely
