Sharing the Rent
A: Dan's moving out next month. I don't know how I'll pay the rent on my own.
B: If you split the monthly rent with someone else, would it be affordable?
A: Yes. I've worked out a budget, but there's electricity and all the other expenses too.
B: You could agree how much each person will contribute to those before they move in.
A: True. Mum offered me a loan, but that would only pay the rent for a couple of months.
B: Would you feel comfortable sharing with someone you don't know?
A: I'd want to meet them first. I like having someone around, but I need quiet in the evenings.
B: My sister's looking for a room. She works early, so she usually goes to bed before ten.
A: Does she have enough for the deposit? The agency asked Dan for a month's rent when he moved in.
B: I think so. I'll ask her. When does your contract end?
A: In June. I need to check with the agency before anyone else moves in.
B: All right. If they agreed, would you like my sister to come and see the room?
A: Yes. Give me her number, and I'll call her after I've spoken to them.

Vocabulary: affordable, agency, budget, contract, contribute, deposit, expense, loan, monthly, split
