Sharing the Rent
A: Dan's moving out next month, and the agency's putting the rent up too. There's no way I can afford this place on my own.
B: Would it still be affordable if you found someone to split the rent with?
A: Just about. I've worked out a monthly budget. It'd be tight, but I'd need quite a sum of money to get a new place, too.
B: Have you saved anything in case it takes a few weeks to find someone?
A: Not much. Mum offered me a small loan, but that'd only pay the rent for a month or two.
B: Would you feel comfortable sharing with someone you don't know?
A: If I met them properly first, I wouldn't mind. I just need someone quiet in the evenings.
B: Actually, my sister's looking for a room. She starts work at seven, so she's usually in bed before ten.
A: That sounds promising. Could she pay the first month before she moves in? The agency asked Dan to do that.
B: I think so. When does your contract end?
A: In June. I need written permission for anyone new to move in. It's a requirement in my contract.
B: Fair enough. If they say yes, would you like my sister to come round and see the room?
A: Definitely. Send me her number, and I'll message her once I've spoken to them.

Vocabulary: affordable, agency, budget, contract, loan, monthly, promising, requirement, split, sum
