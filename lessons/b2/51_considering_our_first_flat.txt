Considering Our First Flat
A: Shall we sit in this cafe and talk about the flat we've just seen?
B: Yes. I'd like to look at the costs again before we go home.
A: What did you think of it? The estate agent wants to know whether we're interested.
B: I liked it, but we'd be borrowing a large sum. Have you checked how much the mortgage would cost each month?
A: I've asked the mortgage company for an estimate. It would be more than our rent, even with our savings as a deposit.
B: Then we need to include all the costs of ownership. We'd have to pay for repairs ourselves.
A: True. It's a fairly modest flat, though. We wouldn't have a huge place to maintain.
B: The kitchen has potential, but we'd need to replace that old sink before long.
A: I could live with it for a year. I'm interested primarily because of the location. We could both walk to work.
B: I liked that too. I expected an urban area to be noisier, but that street was quiet.
A: And they're building more housing near the station. The agent said this flat would be a good investment.
B: Maybe, but he's trying to sell it. We need to decide whether it would be a comfortable home.
A: Would the second bedroom give you adequate space to work? It looked small to me.
B: I couldn't tell without measuring it. If I worked there every day, I'd need space for my books too.
A: Then let's measure our furniture before we go back. We can check whether it would fit.
B: Yes, and wait for the mortgage figures. I don't want us to agree to a price before we know the monthly cost.
A: I'll tell the agent we're interested but need another visit.
B: Ask for Saturday morning. We can see the flat better in the morning.

Vocabulary: adequate, estate, housing, investment, maintain, modest, mortgage, ownership, potential, primarily, sum, urban
