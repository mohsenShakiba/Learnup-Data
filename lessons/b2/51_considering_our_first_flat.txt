Considering Our First Flat
A: Shall we sit in this cafe and talk about the flat we've just seen?
B: Yes. I'd like to look at the costs again before we go home.
A: What did you think of it? The agent wants to know whether we're interested.
B: It's charming, but home ownership scares me a bit. What would we actually pay each month if we borrowed the money?
A: I've asked for a price on a loan. Even with our savings, we'd pay more than we do in rent.
B: Then we need to think about the extra costs of owning a place, too. We'd have to pay for any repairs ourselves.
A: Yes. Especially in a building of that age. I saw a thin brown line along the living room ceiling. Is the bathroom upstairs right above that?
B: Yes. The agent said water had come through from that bathroom. A fresh coat of paint might hide the mark. I'd want to know if they'd fixed the cause.
A: Me too. It's the location I love. We could both walk to work.
B: And I liked how quiet it was. Who owns that big empty place opposite? Do you know what they're going to do with it?
A: They're turning it into housing. Our agent called that an investment in the area, but he couldn't say how long the building work would take.
B: A year of noise wouldn't worry me. The unknown cost of fixing that ceiling would. Did the agent show you the final page of the report?
A: I saw that last page. Something about the status of the roof repairs. Have they even started?
B: They haven't. There's a note that says the cost will be divided between the six flats. It doesn't give the scale of the work or a price.
A: Let's get a figure for the roof first. If they say a thousand pounds, that doesn't worry me greatly. If they say ten thousand, that's different.
B: Yes, and wait for the figures on the loan. We need to know the full cost.
A: I'll tell the agent we're interested but need another visit. I want to satisfy myself that the ceiling's dry. Can we take someone who does that kind of repair?
B: Ask for Saturday morning. Mum remembers the last time the roof was fixed. She said they were told it would last a lifetime. I'd like to find out when that was.

Vocabulary: charming, greatly, housing, investment, lifetime, ownership, satisfy, scale, status, unknown
