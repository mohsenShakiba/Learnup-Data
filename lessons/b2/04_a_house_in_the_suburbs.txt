A House in the Suburbs
A: Look at this house in the suburbs. It's much more affordable than anything in our district.
B: The garden is lovely, but we'd spend nearly two hours on the train every day.
A: Not if we drove. If we bought a small second-hand car, the trip along the highway would only take thirty minutes.
B: True, but think about the extra expense. With fuel, parking, and car insurance, would we actually save any money?
A: Probably not. We'd need a loan to buy the car too. That doesn't fit our budget.
B: And driving home after a late shift sounds miserable.
A: What if we stayed nearer to town? Look at this two-bedroom flat by the river.
B: That location is much better. If we rented that place, we could walk to the office, and we'd still have a spare room for guests.
A: The agency wants a six-week deposit and a twelve-month contract, though.
B: We have enough for the deposit. Let's see the flat before we agree to anything.
A: All right, I'll call them and ask if we can view it tomorrow after work.

Vocabulary: affordable, agency, budget, contract, deposit, district, expense, highway, insurance, loan, miserable, suburb
