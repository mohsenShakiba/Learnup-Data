A House in the Suburbs
A: Look at this house in the northern suburb. The rent is much more affordable than in our central district.
B: The garden is lovely, but if we lived out there, we'd spend nearly two hours on the train every day.
A: Not if we drove. If we bought a small second-hand car, the trip along the highway would only take thirty minutes.
B: True, but think about the extra expense. If we had to pay for fuel, parking, and car insurance, we wouldn't save anything on our budget.
A: Good point. We'd also need a loan to buy the car, and parking permits from the local council aren't cheap for new residents.
B: Exactly. And if one of us worked late in winter, driving home in the dark would be miserable.
A: What if we stayed nearer to town? Look at this two-bedroom flat by the river.
B: That location is much better. If we rented that place, we could walk to the office, and we'd still have a spare room for guests.
A: The agency wants a six-week deposit and a twelve-month contract, though.
B: We have enough money in our account for the deposit. If I were you, I'd call the agency now before someone else takes it.
A: All right, I'll phone them and ask if we can view it tomorrow after work.

Vocabulary: affordable, agency, budget, contract, council, deposit, district, expense, highway, insurance, loan, miserable, permit, resident, suburb
