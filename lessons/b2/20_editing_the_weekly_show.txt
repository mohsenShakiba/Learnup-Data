Editing the Weekly Show
A: We've been trying to edit this ten-minute film clip for two hours. I wish we had closed the room window before we recorded our conversation!
B: Me too. The constant street noise outside ruins the quiet parts, and it's too complicated to remove by hand.
A: Can't we use that automatic sound tool to eliminate the street noise?
B: I tried, but there's a strange computer bug in the new version, and the programme keeps stopping. If only our computer were faster!
A: Wait—be careful with that key! You almost deleted the whole interview with our guest comic.
B: Don't worry, I saved a copy on the external disk ten minutes ago.
A: Good, because his stories were hilarious. Even my sister, who works as a theatre critic, said his kind of humour was very entertaining.
B: I just wish your phone wouldn't ring every time we sit down to record a casual chat. It interrupted our best story twice!
A: Sorry, I'll turn it off next week. What is the total duration of our weekly show right now?
B: Twenty-four minutes, which is a bit too long for our usual format. We should cut the brief news section at the end.
A: Agreed. Have we had our new channel logo and the short animation finished by the worker yet?
B: Yes, she sent the graphics in a message attachment this afternoon, and they really enhance the look of the picture.
A: Great! Before we launch the next season in May, let's also get this noisy computer part repaired at the shop.
