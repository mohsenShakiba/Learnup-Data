Editing the Weekly Show
A: Listen to the first minute of this interview clip. I wish we'd closed the window before we started recording.
B: That constant street noise is awful. The rest is clear, though. You closed the window just before our guest comic started his first story.
A: Can the automatic sound tool eliminate the noise at the beginning?
B: I tried it, but it cuts out some of his words too.
A: Then let's remove the opening chat and record a brief introduction ourselves.
B: Fine. We can introduce him and start the clip with his first answer.
A: Keep that whole story. It was hilarious.
B: Yes, that stays. I've saved the first version on the external disk, so I'll edit a copy.
A: How long will our weekly show be after the cut?
B: About nineteen minutes, including the news. That fits our usual format.
A: Great. I'll record the new introduction now.
B: Let's close the window and turn our phones off first. I'll check the sound before you do the whole thing.
