The Weekend Cycling Race
A: Have you seen Sam since the race? I went to get some water after you crossed the line, and now I can't find him.
B: He's over by the medical tent. I think he's okay, but he crashed his bike on the last hill.
A: Oh no, what happened?
B: A dog ran out in front of him on the hill. His helmet hit the road pretty hard when he fell, so the race doctor's checking him over.
A: That's tough. Did he have to pull out?
B: No, he finished on foot! His front wheel was bent, but he pushed the bike across the line. You should have heard everyone cheering.
A: Good for him! How's he getting home if he can't ride it?
B: He was relying on a lift from Ben, but Ben's staying for the medals.
A: Wait, did Ben win?
B: Yeah! His first victory over his big rival from the Northside team. Two seconds between them!
A: Brilliant, he really deserved to win after coming second twice this year. Look, there's plenty of room in the back of my car, so I can take Sam and his bike home.
B: Oh, that'd be a huge help. Let's walk over and tell him once the doctor's finished.

Vocabulary: bent, cheer, crash, deserve, helmet, medal, rely, rival, tough, victory
