The Weekend Cycling Race
A: Have you seen Sam anywhere? I went to get some water after you finished, and I still haven't seen him.
B: He's over by the medical tent. Don't worry, he's okay, but he crashed near the end of the circuit.
A: Oh no, what happened?
B: A dog ran out into the road on the back hill, and he fell off when he tried to avoid it. His helmet hit the ground hard, so the race doctor's checking him over, and his front wheel's badly bent.
A: Oh, that's tough luck right near the finish. Did he have to pull out?
B: No, he actually finished on foot! All the spectators near the line started cheering as he pushed his bike across.
A: Good for him! How's he getting home if he can't ride it?
B: Well, he was relying on a lift from Ben, except Ben has to stay behind for the medals now.
A: Wait, did Ben win?
B: Yeah! He finally beat his big rival from the Northside team by two seconds.
A: Brilliant, he really deserved to win after coming second twice this year. Look, there's plenty of room in the back of my car, so I can take Sam and his bike home.
B: Oh, that'd be a huge help. Let's walk over and tell him once the doctor's finished.

Vocabulary: bent, cheer, circuit, crash, deserve, helmet, medal, rely, rival, spectator, tough
