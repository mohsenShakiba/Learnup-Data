The Weekend Cycling Race
A: What a tough race! Did you see what happened to our team mate Sam on the final part of the hill circuit?
B: No, he was right behind me at the bridge, and then he disappeared. He must have had a problem with his back wheel.
A: Look—he's walking towards the tent now, and his front wheel is slightly bent.
B: Thank goodness he's wearing a strong helmet! He must have crashed on that wet turn near the trees.
A: Sam says a dog ran across the road, so he had to stop hard to avoid hitting it.
B: That's terrible luck. If he hadn't stopped there, he could have chased the front group all the way to the finish line.
A: Still, it took real courage and determination to get back on his bike and finish the contest.
B: Definitely. Even the spectators by the fence were cheering for him when he rode in.
A: How did the rest of our group do against our main rivals from North Town?
B: Better than we expected! They usually dominate the summer championship, but today our team managed to overcome their lead on the hills.
A: Did Ben win a medal in the fast group?
B: Yes, he beat his closest opponent by two seconds and took first rank!
A: He really deserved that victory. He should have brought a spare rubber wheel for Sam, though—we relied too much on one repair bag.
B: True, we'll pack more carefully next time. Right now, let's enjoy the satisfaction of a great team outcome.

Vocabulary: bent, championship, chase, cheer, circuit, contest, courage, crash, deserve, determination, dominate, goodness, helmet, mate, medal, opponent, outcome, overcome, rank, rely, rival, rubber, satisfaction, spectator, tough, victory
