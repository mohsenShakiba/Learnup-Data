The Weekend Cycling Race
A: Have you seen Sam? He wasn't behind you at the finish.
B: He's by the tent. I've just spoken to him. He crashed on the hill circuit.
A: Is he hurt?
B: The race doctor is checking him now. His helmet hit the ground, and his front wheel is badly damaged.
A: What happened?
B: A dog ran across the road. He tried to stop and fell off.
A: That's a tough way to end the race. Did he finish?
B: On foot. The spectators were cheering as he pushed his bike over the line.
A: That took determination. How's he getting home?
B: He was relying on a lift from Ben, but Ben's staying for the medals.
A: Did Ben win?
B: Yes. He beat his closest rival by two seconds.
A: He deserved that victory. I can take Sam and his bike home, then.
B: Thanks. I'll tell him when the doctor has finished.

Vocabulary: cheer, circuit, crash, deserve, determination, helmet, medal, rely, rival, spectator, tough, victory
