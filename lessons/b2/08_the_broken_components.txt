The Broken Motor Parts
A: Did the factory agree to replace those broken motor parts?
B: Yes. At first their sales manager denied there was a problem. He didn't believe our mechanic about the damaged steel rings.
A: What changed his mind?
B: The photos I sent him. He rang back to say they'd found a fault on one of their machines and stopped production.
A: How did our boxes get sent out, then?
B: He admitted nobody had checked them properly before they left. I reminded him how urgent our order was.
A: Did he give you a date for the next delivery? Our customer will charge us extra if we finish the job late.
B: Thursday was his estimate. He won't guarantee that until he's checked their stock.
A: Thursday's tight. When will we know for sure?
B: I told him we need an answer by three this afternoon.
A: Let's ask another company for a price just in case. Isn't there an industrial park near Bristol with a place that sells these rings?
B: I've already done that. I sent the details to the place in Bristol an hour ago, and I'll call them if they haven't replied by two.
A: Good thinking. What should we do with the damaged parts?
B: He asked us to keep them in the boxes. Their driver will collect them when the new parts arrive.

Vocabulary: delivery, deny, estimate, guarantee, industrial, mechanic, motor, steel, stock, urgent
