The Broken Components
A: Did the factory agree to replace the broken motor components?
B: Yes, eventually. The sales manager denied that the fault was theirs until the chief mechanic looked at our photos of the damaged steel rings.
A: Did he know what had caused it?
B: He said one of their machines was damaged and they'd suspended production until it was repaired.
A: So why did they send those parts to us?
B: He admitted they hadn't checked them properly. I reminded him that our order was urgent.
A: Did they give you a date for the next delivery? Our customer will charge us a penalty if we're late again.
B: The manager estimated Thursday, but he needs to check their stock first.
A: Thursday is our last possible day. Can they guarantee that?
B: Not yet. I told him we need a clear answer by three this afternoon.
A: Then we need another option. Can you ask the other factory for a price?
B: I've already sent them the details. I'll call if they haven't replied by two.
A: Good. What should we do with the broken parts?
B: The manager asked us to keep them in the boxes. They'll collect them when the new parts arrive.

Vocabulary: chief, component, delivery, deny, estimate, guarantee, mechanic, motor, penalty, steel, stock, suspend, urgent
