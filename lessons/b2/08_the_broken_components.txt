The Broken Components
A: How did your meeting at the factory go? Did they acknowledge that the new motor parts were broken?
B: At first, their sales manager hesitated and denied that the problem had started on their line.
A: Really? Even after we sent them photos of the damaged components?
B: Once their chief mechanic joined us, he admitted that one of the machines they use to manufacture the steel rings had stopped working the previous week.
A: Did he explain why nobody had checked the boxes before the delivery left their store?
B: He said that their staff had been working under urgent deadlines, and he asked whether we had tested every box in the order.
A: What did you tell him?
B: I told him that our team had carried out a full examination that morning, and I warned him not to send any more items from that group.
A: Good. Did they agree to recall the broken items?
B: Yes. They promised to suspend production on that machine immediately, and they assured me that our new order would be their top priority.
A: Did you ask when the new parts would arrive? If we lose another week, we'll have to pay a late penalty to our own customer.
B: Their boss estimated that the next delivery would reach us by Thursday, and he insisted on giving us a two-year guarantee at no extra cost.
A: That sounds fair. Did he sign an updated agreement?
B: Not yet, but he reminded me to send him the revised contract this evening so he can sign it first thing tomorrow.

Vocabulary: acknowledge, assure, chief, component, delivery, deny, estimate, examination, guarantee, hesitate, insist, manufacture, mechanic, motor, penalty, priority, recall, steel, suspend, urgent
