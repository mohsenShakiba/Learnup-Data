The Broken Components
A: Did the factory agree to replace those broken motor components?
B: Eventually, yes. At first, their sales manager denied there was anything wrong with them, even after I told him our mechanic had spotted the damaged steel rings.
A: What changed his mind?
B: I sent him the photos. Ten minutes later, he called back and said they'd found a fault on one of their machines and had suspended production until it was fixed.
A: How did our boxes get sent out, then?
B: He admitted nobody had checked them properly before they left. I reminded him how urgent our order was.
A: Did he give you a date for the next delivery? Our customer will charge us a penalty if we finish the job late.
B: He estimated they could get new parts to us by Thursday, but he needed to check their stock first.
A: Thursday doesn't leave us much time. Can he guarantee they'll be here by then?
B: Not yet. I told him to give us a clear answer by three this afternoon.
A: Let's ask another company for a price just in case.
B: I've already done that. I sent the details to the place in Bristol an hour ago, and I'll call them if they haven't replied by two.
A: Good thinking. What should we do with the damaged parts?
B: He asked us to keep them in the boxes. Their driver will collect them when the new parts arrive.

Vocabulary: component, delivery, deny, estimate, guarantee, mechanic, motor, penalty, steel, stock, suspend, urgent
