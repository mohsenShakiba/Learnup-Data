A Remark at the Team Meeting
A: Can we talk privately about what happened in the meeting? You copied my accent again when I was answering a question.
B: I'm sorry. I meant it as a humorous remark. I didn't mean to offend you.
A: I understand that you meant it to be funny, but I found it offensive. You made my answer sound ridiculous.
B: You're right. People were laughing at the accent instead of listening to your idea.
A: That's what upset me so deeply. I stopped explaining it. I need to be able to speak openly in those meetings.
B: I hadn't thought about it from your perspective.
A: I asked you to stop last week too. You said I was taking a small remark too seriously.
B: I remember. I shouldn't have tried to dismiss your concern.
A: It made it harder to raise the subject again. I don't want to argue every time we have a meeting.
B: You shouldn't have to argue about it. I'll stop copying your accent. I'm sorry I carried on after you asked me to stop.
A: Thank you. I also want to finish explaining the idea that I was talking about.
B: Would you like me to ask the manager to give you time at the next meeting?
A: I'll ask her myself. Please just let me finish when it's my turn.
B: Of course. And if someone else copies your accent, I'll ask them to stop.

Vocabulary: accent, deeply, dismiss, humorous, offend, offensive, openly, perspective, remark, ridiculous
