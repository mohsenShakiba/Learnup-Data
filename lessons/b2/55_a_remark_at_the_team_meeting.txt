A Remark at the Team Meeting
A: Do you have a minute before you leave the office? I'd like to talk about today's meeting.
B: Yes. Let's go into this empty room.
A: Mark copied my accent again when I was answering a question. I asked him to stop doing that last week.
B: I heard him. It was an offensive thing to do.
A: He says he doesn't mean to offend me, but he made my answer sound ridiculous.
B: What did he say when you asked him to stop?
A: That it was just a humorous remark and I was taking it too seriously.
B: That's no reason to dismiss your concern. You've already told him it bothers you.
A: It hurt me deeply today. I stopped explaining my idea because everyone was laughing.
B: I'm sorry I didn't say anything. Would you like me to talk to him?
A: Thanks, but I think I'll speak to our manager. I need to be able to speak openly in meetings without worrying about being laughed at.
B: Would you like me to come with you? I can tell her what I heard.
A: Yes, please. I'm worried she won't take it seriously either.
B: Tell her how it affected you. She needs to understand it from your perspective.
A: I will. I also want to finish explaining my idea at the next meeting.
B: You can ask her for time to do that when we see her.
A: Good idea. I'll ask when she's free to talk to us.
B: Let me know what time she gives you.

Vocabulary: accent, deeply, dismiss, humorous, offend, offensive, openly, perspective, remark, ridiculous
