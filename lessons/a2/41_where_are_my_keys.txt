Where Are My Keys?
A: I can't find my keys anywhere. Did somebody move them?
B: I didn't. Are they on the little table by the door?
A: No, my ring and my watch are there but not the keys.
B: Did you check your pockets?
A: Yes. They're empty. Maybe I left the keys at the cafe yesterday.
B: Are you certain you brought the keys home?
A: Yes. I used them to open the door.
B: Right. What did you do next?
A: I put my shopping on the kitchen table.
B: Did you drop the keys into a shopping bag?
A: I suppose that's possible. Let me look.
B: I'll check under the table.
A: Nothing in the bags. I feel stupid. We're going to be late.
B: Don't worry. I can lock the door with mine. You can look again this evening.
A: But you'll be at work when I get home.
B: Take mine. I'll be home after you.
A: Thanks. I'll just get my coat from yesterday.
B: Good idea. Check those pockets too. Keys don’t just disappear!
A: Oh! They’re in my grey coat!
B: Good. Ready to go?
A: Finally! Yes, let's go.
B: I'll lock the door.

Vocabulary: anywhere, certain, disappear, drop, finally, lock, ring, somebody, stupid, suppose
