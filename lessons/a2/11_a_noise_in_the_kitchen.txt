A Noise in the Kitchen
A: Did you hear that strange noise?
B: Yes. Is someone in the kitchen?
A: I don't know. Is Tom still here?
B: I think he went home.
A: Then who's in there?
B: Don't worry. Perhaps it's the wind.
A: But the window is closed.
B: Let's have a look. 
A: Okay, but be quiet and open the door slowly.
B: I don't see anybody.
A: Wait. There's someone behind the door.
B: It's Tom! What is he doing?
A: Look. He's eating something from the fridge.
B: Is that my cake?
A: Yes, and he’s eating the whole thing!
B: That little thief! Tom, save some for me!

Vocabulary: anybody, closed, fridge, noise, perhaps, save, slowly, someone, strange, thief, whole, wind, worry
