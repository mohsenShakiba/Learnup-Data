The Old Street
A: This street looks very different now.
B: Yes. We both used to live here when we were children.
A: That small house was my home.
B: My home was near the school.
A: There used to be a wide field behind those wooden houses.
B: Yes. We cycled there after school.
A: The traditional market was near the field.
B: Yes, we used to go there every weekend.
A: The community was quiet then and our neighbours were so nice.
B: Yes. Now there are modern apartment buildings everywhere.
A: That's right. I miss the old place.

Vocabulary: building, community, cycle, different, field, modern, neighbour, quiet, traditional, used to, wide, wooden
