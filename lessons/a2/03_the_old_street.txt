The Old Street
A: This street looks very different now.
B: Yes. I used to live here when I was a child.
A: Did you? What was it like?
B: It was quiet. There used to be a large field behind those houses.
A: What did you do after school?
B: My neighbours and I used to cycle around the field.
A: Did all of you know each other?
B: Yes. It was a small community.
A: And now there are modern apartment buildings everywhere.
B: That's right. I miss the old place, but I like the new shops too.

Vocabulary: building, community, cycle, different, field, modern, neighbour, quiet, used to
