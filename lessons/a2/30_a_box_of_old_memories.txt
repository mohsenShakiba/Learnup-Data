A Box of Old Memories
A: What's in that box under your bed?
B: Some things from my old school. This box used to be in my sister's room.
A: Can I have a look?
B: Of course. Be careful with that little toy. It was my favourite.
A: Does this red diary belong to you too?
B: No, it's my twin sister's. Mine is blue.
A: You both had the same teacher, didn't you?
B: Yes, but we were in different classes the next year.
A: Look at this drawing! Is that your school?
B: Yes. I used to draw it every week.
A: You have a good memory. I can't remember my first room at school.
B: I remember the windows. They were enormous!
A: And who's this in the photo?
B: Our teacher. He was always very kind to us.
A: You should send him a copy.
B: Good idea. I have his address in my diary.
A: He'll be surprised, won't he?
B: Yes. And my sister will be happy about her red diary!

Vocabulary: diary, drawing, enormous, memory, surprised, toy, twin
