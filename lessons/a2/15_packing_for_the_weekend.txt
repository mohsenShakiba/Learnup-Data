Packing for the Weekend
A: Is that your bag for the weekend?
B: Yes. I'm nearly ready.
A: Why is it so heavy? I can't lift it.
B: I only packed a coat, a jacket, two towels, and three novels.
A: Why a coat and a jacket?
B: I normally pack both. It's a habit.
A: One is enough. And why two towels?
B: One is for you. You always forget yours!
A: And are three novels really necessary?
B: I want to relax and read on the beach.
A: It's such a short trip. One book is enough.
B: But I can't decide which book to take.
A: Just take the smallest.
B: Fine. I'll pick one.
A: That's better. You have more room now.
B: But I still don't have enough room for my shirts and socks!
A: You always bring too many things.
B: I know, it's a bad habit.

Vocabulary: decide, habit, lift, nearly, necessary, normally, novel, pick, relax, sock, such, towel
