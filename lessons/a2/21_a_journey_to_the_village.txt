A Journey to the Village
A: Hi. We want to visit Green Village today. What's the best way to get there?
B: By train. There's a direct one at ten.
A: How long is the train journey?
B: About half an hour. It's much faster than the bus.
A: And can we get to the station by public transport?
B: Yes. The underground goes straight to the station. It takes ten minutes.
A: It's half past nine now. Will we catch the ten o'clock train?
B: If you leave now, you'll have enough time.
A: Great. Can we buy the tickets here?
B: Of course. Single or return?
A: Two return tickets, please. We're coming back this evening.
B: That's sixteen pounds. Just remember, the last train back leaves at six.
A: Six? That's quite early. What will we do if we miss it?
B: You'll have to take the bus. The last one leaves at seven.
A: Our return tickets are only for the train, aren't they?
B: That's right. You should be at the station by quarter to six.
A: We'll be there early, then. Here's sixteen pounds. Thanks for all the information.
B: You're welcome. You should go now. Enjoy your day!

Vocabulary: catch, direct, information, journey, public, single, straight, transport, underground, village
