A New Chair
A: This furniture store is huge.
B: We only need one chair. What about this blue one?
A: It will match our walls. Is it comfortable?
B: Try it. It's very soft.
A: Yes, but it's more expensive than the red one.
B: The red one is the cheapest chair here.
A: Let me try it. Oh, this is better than our chair at home.
B: Which one do you prefer?
A: The blue one. It's the most comfortable.
B: Do you want to spend an extra fifty dollars?
A: Yes. We'll use it every day.
B: All right. Let's get it.
A: Wait. Will it fit through our front door?
B: Our door is quite narrow.
A: Actually, this chair is wider than the door!
B: Then the red one is our best option. It's smaller.
A: But I really like the blue one.
B: Well, we can buy a bigger house!

Vocabulary: actually, all right, colour, expensive, extra, fit, furniture, huge, match, narrow, option, quite, soft, spend, store
