A Visitor at the Factory
A: Welcome to the factory! Is this your first visit?
B: Yes. I'm excited!
A: Great. First, we have a few rules. Every visitor has to wear one of these yellow jackets.
B: Do I have to wear it in the office too?
A: No, you don't have to wear it here. Just inside the factory.
B: All right. Can I take photos during the visit?
A: You have to ask the manager for permission first.
B: Okay. I understand.
A: You must also follow my instructions, and you mustn't touch any of the equipment.
B: Is it dangerous?
A: It can be. These rules help protect everyone.
B: I understand. Should I stay close to you?
A: Yes, please. It's noisy in there, so you should stay near me.
B: No problem. Do we enter through this door?
A: Yes, but you shouldn't take your bag with you. You can leave it in the office.
B: Oh, right. I'll put it next to my coat.
A: Thanks. Are you ready now?
B: Yes. My jacket is on, and my bag is here. Let's go!

Vocabulary: enter, equipment, factory, follow, instruction, permission, protect, rule, touch, understand, visitor
