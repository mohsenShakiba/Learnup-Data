Bruno Stays with a Friend
A: Can you take care of Bruno on Saturday?
B: Of course. What should I feed him?
A: His usual food is in this bag. You must feed him twice a day.
B: Can I give him anything else?
A: No, he mustn't eat anything else. And he needs fresh water too.
B: Can I take him to the park?
A: Yes. He's very active and needs daily exercise.
B: Is he scared of other animals?
A: No, but he's scared of cars. You should stay inside the park.
B: And does he behave well at home?
A: His behaviour is better after a walk. He usually sleeps quietly.
B: Can I leave him alone for an hour?
A: Yes. A short time alone is fine.
B: Where should he sleep?
A: His bed is in the car. I'll bring it inside.
B: I don't think he needs it.
A: Why not?
B: Look! He's asleep on my bed!
A: I think you should keep your bedroom door closed.
B: Yes, let's bring his bed.

Vocabulary: active, behave, behaviour, care, daily, feed, quietly, scared, usual, usually
