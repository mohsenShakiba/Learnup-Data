A Saturday Job
A: The cafe near school needs a worker on Saturdays. I'm going to apply.
B: Do they employ teenagers?
A: Yes. I spoke to the manager today. He knows I'm sixteen.
B: What do you have to do?
A: Take food to tables and clean them.
B: Do you need any experience?
A: No. The manager will teach me.
B: How much can you earn?
A: Eight pounds an hour. It's five hours every Saturday.
B: And what about your homework?
A: I can do it on Sundays. I want to be more independent and pay for my own things.
B: That’s fair. Do you want a career in this kind of work?
A: My dream is to open a cafe one day. I can learn a lot from this job.
B: That sounds like a good plan for the future.
A: Exactly. I want to try the job first.
B: When can you start?
A: I need an interview first. It's tomorrow.
B: Good luck. Let me know how it goes.

Vocabulary: become, career, dream, earn, employ, future, independent, teach, teenager, worker
