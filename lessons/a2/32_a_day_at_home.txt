A Day at Home
A: Hi, Mia. You weren't in class this morning. Are you all right?
B: Not really. I'm ill. I have a terrible headache, and my stomach hurts.
A: Oh, no. Do you have a temperature too?
B: Yes. I spoke to the doctor this morning. She thinks it's flu.
A: Is anyone at home with you?
B: My dad is here. He's making me something to eat, but I'm not hungry.
A: Did you sleep much last night?
B: No. I feel worse today. I'm too weak to get out of bed.
A: Does your dad know?
B: Yes. He's going to call the doctor again. Did I miss much at school?
A: We did some exercises. I can send you a photo of my notes.
B: Thanks. Was there any homework?
A: Just a few questions on page twenty. You don't have to do them today.
B: But we have an exam on Friday, don't we?
A: Next Friday, Mia. You have another week.
B: Oh! I had the wrong date.
A: No. Get some rest today. We can study together next week.
B: That sounds good. But my book is still at school.
A: I'll get it for you. Can your dad collect it from me after class?
B: Yes, I'll ask him. Thanks. I feel less worried now.
A: Good. I'll send you the notes this afternoon.
B: Thanks, but I'll read them tomorrow. Right now, I just want to sleep.

Vocabulary: exam, flu, headache, homework, ill, note, question, stomach, temperature, terrible, weak, worse
