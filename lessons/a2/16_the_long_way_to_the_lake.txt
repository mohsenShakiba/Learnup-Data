The Long Way to the Lake
A: Is the lake in this park?
B: Yes. Let's go through the gate and look at the map.
A: Do we go left or right?
B: Turn left. Then walk along this road to the bridge.
A: Can we go across the grass? It's shorter.
B: No. Look at the sign. We should avoid the grass.
A: All right. Let's cross the bridge.
B: Wait. There's another gate here. Are we going out of the park?
A: Yes! We're going in the wrong direction.
B: Let me see the map again. Oh, the lake is on the opposite side!
A: So we should go back over the bridge?
B: Yes, sorry. Then down those steps and past the cafe.
A: I see the lake now. It's behind the cafe!
B: Great. We can walk round the lake from there.
A: Another walk? I need a rest!
B: All right. Let's go into the cafe first.
A: Do you know the way to the cafe?
B: Yes. I can see the cakes through the window!

Vocabulary: along, avoid, bridge, cross, direction, gate, grass, lake, opposite, rest, round, side, sign, step
