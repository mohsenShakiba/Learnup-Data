The Long Way to the Lake
A: Where is the lake?
B: It's in this park. Let's go through the gate.
A: Do we go left or right?
B: Turn left. Then walk along this road to the bridge.
A: Can we go across the grass? It's shorter.
B: No. The sign says, "Avoid the grass."
A: All right. Let's cross the bridge.
B: Wait! We're going in the wrong direction.
A: But you said left!
B: Sorry, I meant right.
A: Look at the map. The lake is on the opposite side.
B: Oh, you're right. We should go back over the bridge.
A: Then we go past the cafe and down those steps.
B: Yes, and the road goes round the lake.
A: That's a long walk. I need a rest.
B: Let's get some cake at the cafe first.
A: Now that's a good idea!

Vocabulary: along, avoid, bridge, cross, direction, gate, grass, lake, opposite, rest, round, side, sign, step
