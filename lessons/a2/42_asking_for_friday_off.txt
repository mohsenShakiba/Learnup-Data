Asking for Friday Off
A: Can you check this message before I send it to Dan?
B: Sure. What are you asking for?
A: A day off next Friday. My sister's wedding is on Saturday, and I need to travel.
B: Put the exact date in your request. "Next Friday" isn't always clear.
A: Good point. Do I need to give a reason too?
B: Yes, but a short explanation is enough. Just tell him about the wedding.
A: Is "I want Friday off" OK?
B: That sounds a little strong. Try the phrase "Can I have Friday off, please?"
A: All right. And is "Hi Dan" too informal?
B: No. We all call him Dan at work.
A: Can you check the spelling too?
B: Just one thing: Friday needs a capital F.
A: Thanks. How long do you usually wait for a response?
B: He usually responds the same day.
A: Good. I need to book my train tickets soon.
B: Wait for his answer first. Fridays are busy here.
A: Of course. Do I need to send a message to Lisa too? She organizes our holidays.
B: No. If Dan agrees, he'll send your request to her.
A: Great. I'll send it now.
B: I hope you can get the day off.

Vocabulary: capital, exact, explanation, informal, phrase, reason, request, respond, response, spelling
