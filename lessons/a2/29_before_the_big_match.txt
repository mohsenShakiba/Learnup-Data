Before the Big Match
A: Look at the crowd! Will we find a seat?
B: I hope so. My whole family is here to support my sister.
A: Where is she?
B: Look, she's coming onto the field now.
A: Number nine, near the middle?
B: That's her! Go, Emma!
A: You don't have to shout so loudly. I'm right next to you.
B: Sorry! I get very excited at these games.
A: Do these two schools compete every year?
B: Yes. The other school won last time.
A: Did your sister play in that match too?
B: Yes, she scored two goals. She really wants to win today.
A: Two goals? Maybe I should shout too!
B: Wait, I can't see her now. That man is standing in front of us.
A: There are two empty seats over there.
B: Oh, we're lucky! Let's go before someone takes them.
A: This is better. We can see the whole field from here.
B: Just in time! The match is going to start.

Vocabulary: compete, crowd, goal, loudly, lucky, middle, onto, score, shout, support
