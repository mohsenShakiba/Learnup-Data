Before the Big Match
A: Look at the crowd! There are so many people here.
B: They're here for the school match. My sister is playing today.
A: Which one is she?
B: Number nine, near the middle of the field.
A: Does she compete every week?
B: No, only once a month. She used to be nervous before every game.
A: She looks excited today.
B: Yes. Our whole family is here to support her.
A: Did she score a goal in the last match?
B: Yes, two! But the other school won.
A: Maybe she'll be lucky today.
B: I hope so. If she scores, I'll shout her name!
A: You don't have to shout so loudly. I'm right next to you.
B: Sorry! I get very excited at these games.
A: Someone is walking onto the field with the ball now.
B: Yes. Oh, I can't see. That man is standing in front of me.
A: There are two empty seats over there.
B: Good. Let's move. The match is going to start soon.

Vocabulary: compete, crowd, goal, loudly, lucky, middle, onto, score, shout, support
