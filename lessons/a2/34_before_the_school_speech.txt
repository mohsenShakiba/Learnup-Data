Before the School Speech
A: You look nervous. Is it your speech tomorrow?
B: Yes. Can we practise here? The stage feels so big!
A: Of course. What's your topic?
B: My favourite subject: science. I'll start with a short introduction. Then I'll talk about our plant experiment.
A: The two plants by the window in our room?
B: Yes. I have photos from every week. I want to describe the changes.
A: Good idea. Show me an example.
B: Here's week one. Both plants are small. Now look at week three.
A: Wow. The one on the left is much bigger. What did you do differently?
B: I gave that one more water. The other one only had a little.
A: That's interesting. You should explain that during your speech.
B: I will. Can you check the time for me? I only have five minutes.
A: Sure. I'll sit at the back and check the time. Start from the beginning.
B: "Good morning. Today I'm going to talk about our science project..."
A: Sorry, I can't hear you from here.
B: Really? I'm almost shouting!
A: Try again. Look at me, and take your time.
B: OK. I speak too quickly when I'm nervous.
A: Keep your notes on the table. They'll be useful.
B: And at the end, I'll say, "Thank you for your attention."
A: Yes. Then you can ask for questions.
B: Questions? Now I'm nervous again!
A: Don't worry. You know a lot about those plants. I'll ask you one now.
B: All right. But let me finish my speech first!

Vocabulary: attention, describe, differently, example, experiment, introduction, quickly, science, speech, stage, subject, topic, useful
