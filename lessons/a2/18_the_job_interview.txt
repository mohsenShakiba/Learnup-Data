The Job Interview
A: Why are all your clothes on the bed?
B: I have a job interview tomorrow, and I can't choose a shirt.
A: What's the job?
B: A shop assistant in a clothes shop.
A: You should wear your blue shirt and black trousers.
B: Do I have to wear a tie too?
A: No, you don't have to wear a tie. Just look clean and professional.
B: Okay. I'm a bit nervous. I don't have much experience.
A: That's normal. You should mention your language skills.
B: Good idea. I should discuss the salary.
A: Yes, but try to be polite.
B: I really want this job.
A: I know. What time is the interview?
B: At nine, but I have to be there at eight forty-five.
A: You mustn't be late. How are you going to get there?
B: I'll take the bus at half past eight.
A: That's too late. The bus takes half an hour. You should take an earlier one.
B: You're right. I must get up at seven. I'll take the bus at eight.
A: Good. Now put your clothes on the chair. They're ready for tomorrow.

Vocabulary: assistant, bit, experience, interview, language, mention, normal, polite, professional, salary, skill, tie, discuss
