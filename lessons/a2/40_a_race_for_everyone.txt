A Race for Everyone
A: What are you reading?
B: A poster for a race in the park. Do you want to enter with me?
A: And race against you? You'll easily beat me.
B: It’s just for fun. You can walk if you want.
A: How far is it?
B: Two miles.
A: That sounds a long way. I don't do much running.
B: We can do some training first. It's in six weeks.
A: Are you going to train every day?
B: Twice a week. I'm going to join the group at work.
A: Are they all entering the race too?
B: Some are. They train together after work.
A: My running ability isn’t great. I was always the worst in my class
B: Don't worry. My dad's coming too. He's going to walk the whole race.
A: Oh, so I won't be the only one walking.
B: Exactly. And walking is good for your health.
A: Do you still walk home from work every day?
B: Yes. A walk after work helps with the stress.
A: I usually just go home and watch a film.
B: Come for a walk with me on Tuesday. Is six OK?
A: All right. But let's start with half a mile.
B: Sure. We can try a bit more next time.

Vocabulary: ability, against, beat, easily, health, running, speed, stress, training, worst
