A Busy First Morning
A: You look busy. What are you working on?
B: I'm preparing a report for the boss.
A: Is it for the new project?
B: Yes. The report explains the project tasks.
A: What are your colleagues doing?
B: They're talking to a customer. I'm working alone.
A: Is the report on schedule?
B: No. The manager expects the report soon.
A: Do you need help?
B: Yes, please. Can you check my report?
A: Of course. I'm available now.
B: Excellent.

Vocabulary: available, boss, check, colleague, excellent, expect, explain, manager, prepare, project, report, schedule, task
