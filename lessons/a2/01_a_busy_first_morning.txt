A Busy First Morning
A: You look busy. What are you working on?
B: I'm preparing a report for our manager.
A: Is it for the new project?
B: Yes. My colleagues are talking to a customer, so I have their other tasks too.
A: Is everything on schedule?
B: No. The manager is explaining one more task now.
A: Do you need help?
B: Can you check my report?
A: Of course. I'm available until lunch.
B: Thanks. That will help me finish it.

Vocabulary: available, check, colleague, explain, manager, prepare, project, report, schedule, task
