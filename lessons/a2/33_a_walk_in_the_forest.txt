A Walk in the Forest
A: This is much quieter than the park. How did you find this place?
B: There's a nature group at work. We came here last month.
A: Well, it's lovely. Look at the valley below us!
B: The river is down there. We can follow this track to the bridge.
A: Good. I'm getting warm in this coat.
B: It's cooler by the water.
A: Wait. Is that the bridge? There's water over it!
B: Oh. It rained a lot last night.
A: Can we cross on those stones?
B: They're wet. And it's too far to jump from one stone to the next.
A: You're right. The water looks deep too.
B: Let's go back a little. There's another track through the trees.
A: Does that go to another bridge?
B: No, but it goes along this side of the river. We don't have to cross.
A: Oh, good. I don't want to go home yet.
B: We still have time. Look at those wild flowers!
A: They're beautiful. Let's stop here for a minute. I want a photo.
B: Sure. I'll sit on this rock.
A: Is it dry?
B: Yes. My shoes are dirty enough. I don't want wet trousers too!
A: We should come here again in summer.
B: Definitely. But next time, let's check the weather first.

Vocabulary: deep, dry, jump, nature, rock, stone, track, valley, wet, wild, yet
