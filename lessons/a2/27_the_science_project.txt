The Science Project
A: Are you ready for our science project?
B: Almost. I have two small plants and a sheet of paper.
A: What is our experiment about?
B: Light. This plant will stay near the window. The other one will stay in a dark box.
A: Do we need any special equipment?
B: No. But we must follow the instructions carefully.
A: How much water should we give them?
B: The same amount each day. Here, read this page.
A: All right. What do we have to record?
B: The height of each plant. We should check them every Monday.
A: We can put the numbers in a chart.
B: Yes. Then we can compare the plants after three weeks.
A: Wait. There's an error on our sheet. These two dates are the same.
B: Oh, you're right. The second date should be a week later.
A: I'll change it. Do you have another pen?
B: Here you are. What result do you expect?
A: I'm not sure. That's the interesting part!
B: Well, we should take a photo of both plants today.

Vocabulary: chart, compare, error, experiment, height, record, result, science, sheet
