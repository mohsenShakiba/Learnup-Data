A Present in the Post
A: Hi. I need to send this box to my sister in Spain.
B: Of course. What does it contain?
A: A book of cartoons. It's her birthday present.
B: All right. Put it here, please. I'll check the weight.
A: Is it possible to get it there this week?
B: When exactly does it need to arrive?
A: By Friday. Her birthday's on Saturday.
B: Express post usually takes two or three days, but I can't promise Friday.
A: How much is that?
B: Twelve pounds.
A: Oh. And ordinary post?
B: Six pounds, but that takes about a week.
A: I'll use express, then. I don't want it to be late.
B: All right. Anything else?
A: Yes, two stamps, please. These letters are both going to London.
B: That's another two pounds.
A: So fourteen pounds, including the stamps?
B: That's right.
A: Here you are.
B: Thank you. Have a good day.

Vocabulary: cartoon, contain, exactly, express, including, ordinary, possible, present, stamp, weight
