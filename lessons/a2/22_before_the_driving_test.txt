Before the Driving Test
A: You look worried. What's wrong?
B: My driving test is tomorrow. I don't feel ready.
A: You practise every week. You'll be fine.
B: Yes, but I still make mistakes.
A: What is difficult for you?
B: I get nervous when I want to park.
A: You should ask your teacher for advice.
B: We have one more lesson this afternoon.
A: Good. You can practise with her before the test.
B: I used to feel excited about these lessons. Now I just feel scared.
A: But you're making progress. 
B: That's true. I'm better at driving now.
A: Yes. Your driving is improving.
B: Do you think I'll pass?
A: You have a good chance. You should get some rest tonight.
B: And if I fail, I'll take more lessons and try again.
A: That's right. So don't worry.
B: Thanks. I feel better now.

Vocabulary: advice, driving, fail, improve, mistake, pass, practise, progress, test, worried
