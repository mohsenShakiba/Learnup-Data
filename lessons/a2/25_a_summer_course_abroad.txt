A Summer Course Abroad
A: What are you reading?
B: An article about a summer course in Spain. I want to study abroad.
A: At a university?
B: No, at a small college near the sea.
A: Do you have to attend classes all day?
B: Only in the mornings. The afternoons are free.
A: How much does it cost?
B: Six hundred pounds for three weeks. That includes the lessons and a room.
A: Will you have your own room?
B: No, I'll share with another student. I'm a bit nervous. It's my first visit to a foreign country.
A: You don't have to go alone. Maybe I can join you.
B: Really? Do you want to study Spanish too?
A: Yes, and I'd like to learn about the culture.
B: Then let's apply together. We have to send the forms before Friday.
A: We can complete them tonight.
B: Good. But if we speak English all day, we won't learn much Spanish!
A: True. I'll bring a dictionary. And we can practise every day.
B: All right. This is going to be an exciting summer!

Vocabulary: abroad, apply, article, attend, college, culture, dictionary, foreign, include, join, own, university
