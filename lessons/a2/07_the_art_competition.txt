The Art Competition
A: The teachers are looking at the paintings now. 
B: Are you nervous about the competition?
A: A little. Which painting is yours?
B: The bright one with the moon is mine.
A: It looks beautiful. 
B: Thanks. What about yours?
A: Mine is over there, next to yours.
B: Yours looks good too.
A: Thanks. I added a small detail last night.
B: Which painting is Nina's?
A: The forest painting is hers. It's very creative.
B: And the large city painting?
A: That's Leo's. His is really good too.
B: Ours look very different.
A: I agree, but theirs look good too.
B: Which painting will the teachers choose?
A: I don't know. This is a hard decision.
B: Do you think you have a chance?
A: Maybe. Wait, a teacher is walking towards my painting.
B: She's putting the award next to yours.
A: I won! The award is mine.
B: Great! Let's tell Nina and Leo.

Vocabulary: award, bright, chance, competition, creative, decision, detail, mine, moon, nervous, painting, towards, yours
