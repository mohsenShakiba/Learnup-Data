The Art Competition
A: The teachers are looking at the paintings now. 
B: Are you nervous about the competition?
A: A little. Which painting is yours?
B: The bright one with the moon is mine.
A: It looks beautiful. Mine is near yours.
B: Thanks. I added a small detail last night.
A: Which painting is Nina's?
B: The forest painting is hers. It's very creative.
A: And the large city painting?
B: That's Leo's. His is really good too.
A: Ours look very different.
B: I agree, but theirs look good too.
A: Which painting will the teachers choose?
B: I don't know. This is a hard decision.
A: Do you think you have a chance?
B: Maybe. Wait, a teacher is walking towards my painting.
A: She's putting the award next to yours.
B: I won! The award is mine.
A: Great! Let's tell Nina and Leo.

Vocabulary: award, bright, chance, competition, creative, decision, detail, mine, moon, nervous, painting, towards, yours
