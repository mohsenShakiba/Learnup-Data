A Knock at the Door
A: Hi. I'm from the flat downstairs. Sorry to knock so late.
B: It doesn't matter. Is everything all right?
A: I don't usually complain, but your music is very loud in my bedroom. 
B: Oh, I'm sorry. I'll turn it off immediately.
A: You don't have to turn it off completely. Just a little quieter.
B: It's all right. I need to go to bed anyway.
A: Thanks. I was a bit nervous. My old neighbour was really rude.
B: What happened?
A: We used to argue about the music almost every weekend. I didn't want another argument tonight.
B: That sounds awful. I'll keep the music down from now on, I promise.
A: Thanks. I'm Mia, by the way.
B: Sam. Nice to meet you.
A: You too. Good night.
B: Good night.

Vocabulary: anyway, argue, argument, complain, completely, immediately, knock, matter, promise, rude
