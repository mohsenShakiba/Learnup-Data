Is My Plant Dead?
A: Can you look at my plant? I think it's dead.
B: It looks alive to me. Why are you worried?
A: It used to produce lots of flowers. Now there aren't any.
B: It's winter. This isn't the right season for flowers on this plant.
A: When do they usually appear?
B: In spring. My mum has the same one.
A: Oh. I was worried about it.
B: How often do you water it?
A: I give it water every day.
B: Every day? Feel the earth around the plant.
A: It's very wet.
B: Then it doesn't need so much water.
A: You are an expert on plants.
B: Thanks. I used to kill my plants with too much water. It's a common mistake.
A: That's probably my problem too.
B: Where do you usually keep it?
A: On my desk. It's quite dark there.
B: Try the table near the window. There's more light.
A: Will it grow much bigger?
B: Probably. Mum's is twice this size.
A: I'll move those books and put it there.
B: Good idea. And don't give it water every morning.

Vocabulary: alive, appear, common, dead, earth, expert, grow, kill, produce, season
