The Gas Bill
A: Can you look at this gas bill? It's much higher than usual.
B: How much is it?
A: Sixty-five pounds. We pay about fifty on average.
B: Did they increase the price?
A: No, the price is the same. Look here—we used thirty per cent more gas this month.
B: Really? What's causing that?
A: Is the heating on all day?
B: Let me check the controls. Oh, it is!
A: Why?
B: I was ill a few weeks ago. I changed the times and forgot about it.
A: So we're paying to heat an empty flat?
B: Yes. Sorry. I'll change it now.
A: Good. That will reduce our next bill.
B: What time do you get home?
A: Six. But I'm home at four on Fridays.
B: OK. And mornings?
A: Just an hour before work.
B: Right. It's quite warm in here too. I'll turn it down a little.
A: Not too low! I don't want to wear a coat in here.
B: Don't worry. We'll try it for a week.
A: OK. I'll keep this bill. We can compare it with the next one.
B: Good idea.

Vocabulary: average, cause, compare, control, gas, heat, increase, low, per cent, reduce
