The Broken Bike
A: Why are you pushing your bike?
B: One wheel is broken, and I cannot ride it.
A: Can I look at it?
B: Yes, but I do not know how to fix it.
A: My brother repairs bikes. I'll call him.
B: Can you ask him to help me today?
A: Yes. He can lend us the tools.
B: Great. I can carry them from his house.
A: You can borrow my bike until your bike is ready.
B: That's very helpful. Thank you.
A: No problem. Let's push it to my house.

Vocabulary: borrow, broken, fix, helpful, lend, problem, push, ready, repair, tool, wheel
