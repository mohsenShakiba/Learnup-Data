The Broken Bike
A: Why are you pushing your bike?
B: One wheel is broken, and I cannot ride it.
A: Can I take a look at it?
B: Sure. Do you think you can fix it?
A: No, but my brother repaired my bike once.
B: Great. Can he help me today?
A: Yes. He has the right tools at home.
B: Can I borrow your bike until tomorrow?
A: Of course. I can lend it to you.
B: That's very helpful.
A: No problem. I’ll call him now. 

Vocabulary: borrow, broken, fix, helpful, lend, problem, push, repair, tool, wheel
