The Old Family Photo
A: What's in that large brown box?
B: I don't know. I'm trying to tidy this room.
A: Don't throw it away. It was Grandma's.
B: Let's look inside first.
A: It's full of old photos!
B: Look at this. It’s Grandma’s house.
A: Yes, I remember it now. It was lovely. 
B: We used to visit Grandma there.
A: And these photos at the bottom are from our parents' wedding.
B: Who's that man in the blue suit?
A: Which one?
B: The one with long black hair.
A: That’s Grandpa! he’s smiling.
B: Really? I don't recognize him.
A: Well, he was a lot younger.
B: Let's print a copy of this photo.

Vocabulary: bottom, copy, lovely, print, recognize, remember, smile, suit, throw, tidy, wedding
