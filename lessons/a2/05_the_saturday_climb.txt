The Saturday Climb
A: Are you going to climb Green Hill on Saturday?
B: Yes. I'm going to take the north route.
A: Is that route dangerous?
B: Not really, but it's a long and difficult route.
A: When are you going to start?
B: Early in the morning. We want to reach the top before lunch.
A: Does it have a good view?
B: Yes. You can see the entire forest.
A: What are you going to pack?
B: Water, food, and a warm coat.
A: That sounds heavy.
B: My friend is going to carry the food.
A: Be careful, please. I heard the route near the top is not very safe.
B: Thanks. My friend knows the route well.

Vocabulary: careful, carry, climb, dangerous, difficult, heavy, hill, pack, reach, route, safe, top, view
