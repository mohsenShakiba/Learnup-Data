The Saturday Climb
A: Are you going to climb Green Hill on Saturday?
B: Yes. I'm going to take the north route.
A: Is that route difficult?
B: A little, but it is not dangerous. It has a great view.
A: When are you going to start?
B: Early in the morning. We want to reach the top before lunch.
A: What are you going to pack?
B: Water, food, and a warm coat.
A: That sounds heavy.
B: My friend is going to carry some of the food.
A: Be careful near the top. Some rocks are not safe.
B: It's OK. We're going to take a map.

Vocabulary: careful, carry, climb, dangerous, difficult, heavy, hill, pack, reach, rock, route, safe, top, view
