A Crowded Cafe
A: This cafe is crowded today.
B: Yes. There are a lot of people.
A: There are a few empty tables by the window.
B: Yes, but that part of the cafe is noisy.
A: There are also some tables outside.
B: But the chairs are not comfortable.
A: We only have half an hour.
B: Look, several people are leaving now.
A: Great. Let's take their table.
B: Great, this table is perfect.
A: I agree. Let's order now.
B: Would you like some coffee?
A: Yes, with a little milk.
B: Shall we share a slice of cake?
A: Maybe a slice of chocolate cake.
B: No. It's too sweet for me.
A: What about cheesecake?
B: Great choice. I'll go and order.

Vocabulary: choice, comfortable, crowded, empty, half, noisy, order, part, several, sweet
