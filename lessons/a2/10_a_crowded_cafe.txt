A Crowded Cafe
A: This cafe is very crowded today.
B: Yes, there are a lot of people and not many empty tables.
A: How much time do we have?
B: About half an hour.
A: We have time for coffee.
B: Are there any seats near the window?
A: There are a few over there, but people are using them.
B: Is there much noise in that part of the cafe?
A: Not much. It looks quiet.
B: Look, several people are leaving now.
A: Great. Let's take their table.
B: Good idea. I will get the coffee.

Vocabulary: crowded, empty, half, noise, part, several
