A Film for Tonight
A: Shall we watch a film tonight?
B: Yes. What do you suggest?
A: How about this one? The title is One More Song.
B: I saw that last week. It's about a pop star.
A: Is Leo Gray in it? I don't like his acting.
B: Yes. He's incredible in this film.
A: Really? His last film was terrible.
B: I agree. The dialogue was awful. The characters didn't sound like real people.
A: The story was really bad, and there was too much fighting.
B: This one is different.
A: Is it a sad movie?
B: No, it's actually funny. In my opinion, it's his best film.
A: How long is it?
B: It's rather long. Nearly three hours.
A: Oh. It's already eight, and I need to get up early.
B: Let's save it for Saturday, then.
A: Good idea. We can watch something shorter tonight.
B: How about another episode of The Simpsons? It's only half an hour.
A: Perfect. We can finish it before nine.
B: I'll make some tea.

Vocabulary: act, dialogue, fight, incredible, opinion,  rather, real, suggest, title
