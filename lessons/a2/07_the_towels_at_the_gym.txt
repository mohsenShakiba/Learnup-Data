The Towels at the Gym
A: Is this blue towel yours?
B: No, mine is white.
A: Does it belong to Sam?
B: I do not think so. His is black.
A: There are two more towels on this chair.
B: The red one is Mia's, and the grey one is ours.
A: What about these socks?
B: Maybe they are Leo's.
A: We should put all the lost items on the desk.
B: Good idea. People can find them there.
A: Wait, is this key yours?
B: Yes! Thank you. I need it.

Vocabulary: belong, item, mine, sock, towel, yours
