Lost and Found at the Gym
A: The last class is over. Can you help me check the training room?
B: Sure. There's a jacket on the exercise equipment.
A: I think it belongs to Nina. Hers has a red button on the pocket.
B: You're right. I also found this water bottle next to it.
A: Leo's name is on it, so we can give it back to him.
B: Great. A woman reported a missing black bag with a yellow pocket.
A: Is this it?
B: Yes, that's the right one. I also see a hat and a coat under the chair.
A: We should take all these items to the front desk.
B: Good idea. I'll write down the items, and you can sort the clothing.
A: Okay. Wait, are these keys yours?
B: Yes, they're mine! I looked everywhere for them.
A: Here you are. I'll put the other keys with the lost items.
B: Thanks. Let's take the other items to the front desk.
A: Great. People can ask for them there.

Vocabulary: belong, button, clothing, equipment, item, mine, missing, sort, training, yours
