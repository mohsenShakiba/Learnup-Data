A Surprise for Mum
A: Mum's birthday is on Saturday. How are we going to celebrate?
B: Let's arrange a surprise party at home.
A: Lovely! Should we invite Grandma and Grandad?
B: Yes. I'll send them an invitation today.
A: I'll call them too. They don't always reply to messages.
B: Good. But we must keep it a secret from Mum.
A: Don't worry. I'll ask them to keep it a secret.
B: What gift are you going to buy for her?
A: A book. She talked about it last week.
B: Is it here?
A: No, I'll collect it from the post office tomorrow.
B: What should we cook for the party?
A: I already have some ideas for a special dinner. 
B: Ok, then I'll buy a cake.
A: What time will she get home on Saturday?
B: Usually at seven. But if she finishes work early, she'll be home by six.
A: Then we'll have to be ready by six.
B: Yes. If we start at two, we'll have enough time.
A: Wonderful! She'll be so pleased.
B: Just don't tell Mum!

Vocabulary: already, arrange, celebrate, collect, gift,  invitation, invite, pleased, reply, secret, wonderful
