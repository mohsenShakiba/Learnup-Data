At the Dentist
A: You're very quiet. Are you nervous?
B: Yes. I don't like this waiting room.
A: The dentist is nice. She's my dentist too.
B: I know, but my tooth hurts today.
A: Did the pain start this morning?
B: No, it started last night. I didn't sleep well.
A: There's only one patient before you.
B: Good. What's that loud noise?
A: It's a lorry outside. Look through the window.
B: Oh! I was worried about that noise.
A: Here, there's a cartoon in this newspaper.
B: That's funny. The boy is brushing his teeth with a huge brush.
A: Look! It's bigger than his head!
B: Nobody has a brush like that!
A: You look less worried now.
B: Yes. Thanks. You're a good friend.
A: Of course. Oh, the dentist is at the door.
B: It's my turn, isn't it? All right, I'm ready.

Vocabulary: brush, cartoon, dentist, hurt, lorry, loud, pain, patient
