At the Dentist
A: You're very quiet. Are you nervous?
B: A bit. My last visit to a dentist was two years ago.
A: I used to be nervous too, but regular visits helped.
B: I should come more often. I brush my teeth twice a day, but now this tooth hurts.
A: Is the pain bad?
B: Yes, especially when I drink something cold. I slept badly last night too.
A: Well, you're here now. The dentist is really nice.
B: She's your dentist too, isn't she?
A: Yes. She always explains everything before she starts.
B: That's good. I just hope it isn't anything serious.
A: Let's see what she says.
B: Do you mind waiting here for me?
A: Not at all. I have a book with me.
B: Thanks for coming with me.
A: Of course. There's only one patient before you, so it won't be long.
B: Good. I don't want to wait much longer.
A: Oh, she's calling your name now.
B: It's my turn. All right, I'm ready.

Vocabulary: badly, brush, dentist, especially, hurt, mind, pain, patient, regular, serious
