The Family Recipe
A: This meal smells amazing. Did you cook everything yourself?
B: Almost. Mia made the salad, but I made the main dish myself.
A: What is in it?
B: It's a family recipe, so I cannot tell you everything.
A: It tastes delicious. How do you cut the vegetables so evenly?
B: I use this special knife. It belonged to my grandfather.
A: Really? Can I try it?
B: Sure, but use it carefully. I cut myself with it last week.
A: Maybe you should do the cutting.
B: Then can you please boil some water?
A: Do you need anything else?
B: Can you set the table? Dinner is almost ready.
A: Sure. Where are the plates and forks?
B: They're next to the glasses.

Vocabulary: almost, amazing, boil, carefully, delicious, fork, knife, main, myself, plate, ready, recipe, smell
