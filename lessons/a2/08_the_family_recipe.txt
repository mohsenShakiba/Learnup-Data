The Family Recipe
A: This meal smells amazing. Did you cook everything yourself?
B: Almost. Mia made the salad, but I made the main dish myself.
A: What is in it?
B: It's a family recipe, so I cannot tell you everything.
A: It tastes delicious.
B: Thanks. Be careful with that knife.
A: It's OK. I'm using it carefully.
B: I cut myself with it last week.
A: Then let me help. What can I do?
B: Please boil some water.
A: What can everyone do when the food is ready?
B: They can serve themselves at the table.

Vocabulary: almost, amazing, boil, carefully, delicious, knife, main, myself, recipe, serve, smell, themselves
