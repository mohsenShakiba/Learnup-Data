Rain Before the Concert
A: The sky is getting dark. Look at those clouds.
B: I know. Do you think it will rain during the concert?
A: Probably. A storm will arrive soon.
B: We cannot hold the event outside in this weather.
A: Will the school hall be available?
B: I think so. I'll call and check.
A: Great. I will help you move the chairs inside.
B: Thanks. Then I'll tell the band about the new arrangement.
A: Good. We can keep the audience inside, and the show will continue.
B: Perfect. We have a plan.

Vocabulary: arrangement, audience, cloud, concert, continue, event, hall, hold, inside, perfect, probably, sky, storm
