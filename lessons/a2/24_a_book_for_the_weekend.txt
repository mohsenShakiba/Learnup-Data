A Book for the Weekend
A: Hi. Can I borrow a book? I'm not a member.
B: Of course. You just need to complete this form first.
A: All right. Do I have to pay?
B: No, it's free. What sort of books do you like?
A: Adventure stories. I want something for the weekend, but nothing too long.
B: The fiction section is on your left. Try this book. It's very popular.
A: What's it about?
B: It's about a hero. He discovers an ancient city under the sea.
A: That sounds interesting. Let me see. I know this author.
B: It's her most popular book.
A: It's about a hundred page.
B: You can read a little each day.
A: How long can I keep it?
B: Two weeks. If you need more time, you can call us.
A: Great. Here's my form. I'll take this one.
B: Thank you. Enjoy your book!

Vocabulary: adventure, ancient, author, complete, discover, fiction, form, hero, member, page, popular, section, sort
