After the Film
A: That was a great movie.
B: Really did you enjoy it?
A: Yes. It was really exciting! 
B: I was bored at the beginning.
A: But the race scenes were great!
B: What race?
A: The main character was racing the whole time.
B: Really? I think I missed it.
A: Wait. Were you asleep?
B: Yes, but just a little.
A: Then you missed a big surprise in the last scene!
B: I had a long day at work.
A: Then why did you come?
B: I read a good review. I was interested in the story.
A: You should watch it again. It gets more interesting.
B: Can we watch it at home?
A: Yes, but I recommend some coffee first.
B: Good idea. A large cup for me!

Vocabulary: asleep, beginning, character, exciting, interested, interesting, race, recommend, review, scene, surprise
