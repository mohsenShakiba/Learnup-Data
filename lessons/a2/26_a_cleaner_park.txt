A Cleaner Park
A: Are you free on Saturday morning?
B: Maybe. Why?
A: We're going to clean the park. Do you want to join us?
B: The whole park? That'll take all day!
A: No, just the area near the lake. It looks awful. There's so much paper on the ground.
B: True. And bottles. Who's going to organize it?
A: My neighbour. Look, here's her poster. We're meeting at the gate at ten.
B: Ten? Can I come at eleven?
A: Of course. Even a couple of hours will help. Just wear some old clothes.
B: Do I need to bring any bags?
A: No, my neighbour will provide those.
B: OK. What are we going to do with all the bottles?
A: We'll recycle them. We just need to keep the glass separate from the paper.
B: Right. I'll see you at eleven, then.
A: Great. We're going to the cafe after that.
B: Oh, good. I'll definitely need a coffee!

Vocabulary: area, awful, couple, definitely, ground, organize, poster, provide, recycle, separate
