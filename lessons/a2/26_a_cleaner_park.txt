A Cleaner Park
A: Are you free on Saturday morning?
B: Maybe. Why?
A: We're going to clean the park. Do you want to join us?
B: The whole park? That'll take all day!
A: No, just the area near the lake. It looks awful. There's so much trash on the ground.
B: True. Who's going to organize it?
A: My neighbour. Look, here's the poster. We're meeting at the gate at ten.
B: Ten? Can I come at eleven?
A: Of course. Even a couple of hours will help. 
B: Do I need to bring anything?
A: No, my neighbour will provide bags.
B: OK. What are we going to do with all the trash?
A: We’ll separate the glass and plastic and recycle them.
B: Right. I'll definitely come.

Vocabulary: area, awful, couple, definitely, ground, organize, poster, provide, recycle, separate, trash
