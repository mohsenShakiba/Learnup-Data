After the English Test
A: Did you get your test results today?
B: Yes. My teacher left some comments too.
A: What did your teacher write?
B: There were some positive comments about my writing, but a few negative ones about my listening.
A: What did you find difficult about the listening part?
B: I stopped to think about the meaning of one word, and then I missed the next sentence.
A: I know that feeling. Did your teacher give you any advice?
B: Yes. I'm going to practise with short dialogues. I'll listen once for the main idea and again for the details.
A: And how did the speaking part go?
B: We had a group discussion. I understood the topic, but I was too nervous to say much.
A: We could have a short conversation in English at lunch tomorrow. Just ten minutes.
B: I'd like that. I want to start the next level next term, so I need more practice.
A: And you can help me with my writing. I still make a lot of mistakes.
B: Of course. Bring your homework and we can look at it together.
A: Thanks. Tomorrow at twelve?
B: Yes. Let's meet in the cafe.

Vocabulary: comment, conversation, discussion, feeling, level, meaning, negative, positive, result, term
