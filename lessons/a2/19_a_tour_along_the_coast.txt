A Tour Along the Coast
A: Hi. This is the office for the boat tour, isn't it?
B: Yes. Do you have a ticket?
A: Yes, here it is. The ship leaves at ten, doesn't it?
B: Almost. We meet here at ten and the ship leaves at quarter past ten.
A: Oh, good. I still have time. How long is the tour?
B: About an hour. Our guide knows this region very well.
A: We don't go far out into the ocean, do we?
B: No. We stay a short distance from the coast.
A: Good. It's my first boat trip, so I'm a bit nervous.
B: Don't worry. Our guide will be with you.
A: Can I sit outside?
B: Certainly. But bring a jacket. It can be cold out on the water.
A: I don't have to bring any food, do I?
B: No. We offer food and drinks on the ship.
A: Great. I'll get my jacket. See you at ten!
B: See you then!

Vocabulary: certainly, coast, distance, guest, guide, ocean, offer, quarter, region, ship, tour
