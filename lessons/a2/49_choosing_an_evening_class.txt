Choosing an Evening Class
A: Hello. I'm looking for an English course. Can you help me choose one?
B: Of course. Our adult education courses start next month. What do you want to achieve?
A: I work in a hotel. I want to communicate more easily with our guests.
B: Is there a specific problem at work?
A: I can answer simple questions, but I get nervous on the phone.
B: Then consider our speaking course. You can practise calls in class.
A: Is it all about work, or do you do general English too?
B: Both. We talk about travel, family life and other everyday topics.
A: How many students are there?
B: Eight. You do most activities with a partner.
A: Can I have individual lessons instead?
B: Yes. Those are forty pounds an hour. The group course is twelve pounds a class.
A: That's a big difference. What's the benefit of the individual lessons?
B: You choose the topics, and you can spend the whole lesson on your own questions.
A: The group sounds fine for me. What days are the classes?
B: Tuesdays and Thursdays, from seven to eight.
A: I finish work at six on Tuesdays, but I work late on Thursdays.
B: We have the same course on Mondays and Wednesdays too.
A: That works. Can I join that one?
B: Yes. Let's fill in this form.

Vocabulary: achieve, activity, benefit, communicate, consider, education, everyday, general, individual, specific
