Can You Hear Me?
A: Can you hear me?
B: Not very well. Where are you?
A: In our new office. I'm having trouble with my phone.
B: Try somewhere near a window.
A: Just a moment. How about now?
B: Yes, that's clear now.
A: Good. I have an important call at three.
B: Can you stay there for the call?
A: There's no desk here. I'll try the next room.
B: All right. I'll stay on the phone.
A: Is this better?
B: I can hear you. But what's that loud noise?
A: People in the hall. I'll shut the door.
B: Much better. That room sounds ideal for your call.
A: Yes. But I still need the meeting link.
B: Did you receive my email this morning?
A: No. Can you send it again?
B: Of course. Good luck with the call!

Vocabulary: clear, ideal, link, loud, luck, moment, receive, shut, somewhere, trouble
