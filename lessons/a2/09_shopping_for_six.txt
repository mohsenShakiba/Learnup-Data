Shopping for Six
A: We have the tomatoes, potatoes, and onions.
B: How many lemons do we need?
A: One, maybe two.
B: What's the next item on the shopping list?
A: Let's see. We need some oil.
B: This large bottle of oil is on sale.
A: How much does it cost?
B: Five dollars.
A: We don't need that much oil.
B: But it's cheap, and we can use it later.
A: Okay, let's get it. What else do we need?
B: We also need a bag of fresh vegetables.
A: Is this bag the right size?
B: I think that's the right amount.
A: Okay, I guess we have everything.
B: Great. Do you have enough cash?
A: Yes, I have twenty dollars.

Vocabulary: amount, cash, cheap, cost, enough, fresh, item, lemon, list, oil, onion, potato, sale, shopping, size, vegetable
