Shopping for Six
A: Is the shopping list ready?
B: Almost. How many tomatoes do we need?
A: Ten, and six lemons.
B: How much oil should we buy?
A: One bottle.
B: Do we need any water?
A: No, but we need some fresh vegetables.
B: Fine. How many bowls do we have at home?
A: Eight bowls and ten plates.
B: Good. That's the right amount for six people.
A: Great. Let's go to the supermarket.

Vocabulary: amount, bowl, fresh, lemon, list, oil, plate, shopping, vegetable
