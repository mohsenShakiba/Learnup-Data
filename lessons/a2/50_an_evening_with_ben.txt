An Evening with Ben
A: Thanks for looking after Ben tonight. His grandparents usually help, but they're away.
B: No problem. I like kids. What time will you be home?
A: About nine. The concert finishes at eight thirty.
B: Does he need to go to bed before then?
A: Yes, at eight. He doesn't sleep in the afternoon any more, so he gets tired early.
B: Can he get ready for bed by himself?
A: Yes, but he usually needs help with the buttons.
B: And what about food? Should I cook something for him?
A: No, his dinner is in the fridge. You can give it to him at seven.
B: Is there anything he doesn't like?
A: He sometimes refuses to eat vegetables, but he'll happily eat them with a little cheese.
B: Good to know. Can I give him some chocolate after dinner?
A: Yes, just a little. I hide it at the back of the fridge so he doesn't eat it all.
B: All right. Can we watch a film too?
A: Yes. A short film on Friday is our family tradition.
B: Good. Does he usually cry when you leave?
A: Sometimes, but only for a minute. Read him a story. He likes the book about the little train.
B: All right. Wish me luck!
A: You'll be fine. Call me if you need anything.
B: Enjoy the concert. See you at nine.

Vocabulary: any more, cry, grandparent, happily, hide, himself, kid, refuse, tradition, wish
