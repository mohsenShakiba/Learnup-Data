Moving My Desk
A: I moved everything upstairs except this desk. Can you help me with it?
B: I can try, but my shoulder still hurts.
A: Oh, then let's leave it. I can ask Tom tomorrow.
B: Wait. Can you remove the legs?
A: Yes. They're easy to take off.
B: Then you can carry the top, and I'll bring the legs.
A: Good suggestion. I'll get my tools.
B: Where are you going to put it?
A: In the gap between my bed and the window. 
B: Will it fit under the window?
A: Yes, The height is just right.
B: Good. You'll have more light there.
A: OK, the legs are off. Let's see how heavy the top is.
B: Can you manage it on your own?
A: Yes, it's much lighter now. Can you move those shoes off the stairs?
B: Sure. I'll open the bedroom door too.
A: Thanks. I'll be careful not to hit the wall. I don't want another mark on it.
B: Just take your time
A: OK, let's go slowly.
B: You go first. I'll bring the legs.

Vocabulary: except, gap, height, hit, manage, mark, remove, shoulder, stair, suggestion
