The School Sale
A: Where should I put this lamp?
B: At the front of the table. People can see it there.
A: It has an unusual shape. Is the bottom a piece of wood?
B: Yes. My dad made it. Does it look strange?
A: No, I like it. How much is it?
B: Five pounds. We want to raise a hundred pounds for new library books today.
A: That's a lot. Do enough people know about the sale?
B: I put a poster by the school gate. We can advertise it on the school page too.
A: I'll take a photo of our table. Can you move that square box?
B: Sure. Oh, the bottom is very thin. There are too many books in it.
A: Let's put some in this bag. We don't want them all on the floor!
B: Thanks. That's better. Should we count the books now?
A: Yes. How much are they?
B: A pound each. Can you write that on a sign?
A: Of course. Do we accept cash only?
B: Yes. Mr Green has the money box. He'll be here in a minute.
A: Good. What about that toy boat? I can't see a price.
B: Two pounds. My little brother wants it, but he only has one.
A: He can borrow a pound from me. Is he here today?
B: Yes, he's outside with Dad. I'll tell him.
A: Great. Now, let's take that photo.
B: Wait! Move the price sign. It's in front of the lamp.
A: Oh, right. There. How's that?
B: Fantastic. I hope someone buys it. Dad will be so pleased!

Vocabulary: accept, advertise, count, fantastic, lamp, raise, shape, square, thin, unusual, wood
