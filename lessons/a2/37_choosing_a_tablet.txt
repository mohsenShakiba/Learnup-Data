Choosing a Tablet
A: Hi. I'm looking for a new tablet.
B: Do you have a particular model in mind?
A: No. I mostly read books and watch films on mine.
B: Then try this one. It's small and easy to carry.
A: How much is it?
B: A hundred pounds. It's our best deal at the moment.
A: And the bigger one?
B: Two hundred. It has more features. You can draw on it with a pen.
A: I don't need that. Can I hold it?
B: Of course. Here you are.
A: It seems quite heavy. I read on the bus, so I need something lighter.
B: That's the main advantage of the smaller model. It's much easier to carry.
A: Can I watch a little of a film on that one?
B: Sure. I'll open one for you.
A: The image is clear. I think this one is fine.
B: All right. Do you need help with anything else?
A: Can you help me move the data from my old tablet?
B: Yes. Do you have it with you?
A: It's in my bag. And can I return the new one if I don't like it?
B: Yes. We allow returns for two weeks. Keep the box.
A: Good. I'll take it.
B: Great. I'll get a new one from the back.

Vocabulary: advantage, allow, data, deal, feature, image, model, mostly, particular, seem
