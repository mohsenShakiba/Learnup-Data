The Lost Phone
A: Why did you go back to the station?
B: I realized my phone was not in my pocket.
A: Did you search your bag?
B: Yes, and I searched everywhere at the station.
A: Where did you find it?
B: A passenger noticed it on a seat.
A: How did the passenger return it to you?
B: She called my sister's number from my phone.
A: That was kind of her.
B: Yes. Fortunately, she waited there for me.

Vocabulary: everywhere, fortunately, lost, notice, passenger, pocket, realize, return, search, seat, station
