The Lost Phone
A: Why did you go back to the station?
B: I lost my phone.
A: When did you realize this?
B: At home. My phone was not in my pocket.
A: Did you search your bag?
B: Yes, and I searched everywhere at the station.
A: Where did you find your phone?
B: A passenger noticed the phone on a seat.
A: How did the passenger return your phone?
B: She called my sister from my phone.
A: The passenger was very kind.
B: Yes. Fortunately, she waited at the station.

Vocabulary: everywhere, fortunately, lost, notice, passenger, pocket, realize, return, search, seat, station
