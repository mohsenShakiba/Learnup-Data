Water Under the Sink
A: Why is there water on the kitchen floor?
B: Look inside the cupboard under the sink. It's coming from the bend in that pipe.
A: When did that start?
B: Just now. I was washing a pan when I noticed water by my shoes.
A: I'll put this big plastic container under it. Can you turn the water off under there?
B: No, the main water supply is in the hall cupboard. I'll turn it off now.
A: Okay, the container's under the pipe.
B: Right, the water's off now. Is any still coming from the pipe?
A: Only a few drops, and the container's catching them. The shelf is wet too.
B: I'll move these bottles off it. Can you pass me a cloth?
A: Here. I'll get a towel for the floor too.
B: Thanks. I was cleaning this floor ten minutes ago, and it was dry then.
A: Let's dry the cupboard too, or the water will damage the wood.
B: Good idea. Should I call the building's emergency number?
A: Yes. We can't leave the water off all day.
B: Can you wait here for them this afternoon? I have to leave for work at one.
A: Yes, I'll be home all afternoon.
B: Thanks. I'll call them now.

Vocabulary: bend, cloth, container, damage, emergency, pan, pipe, shelf, sink, supply
