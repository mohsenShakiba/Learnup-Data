A New Use for the Factory
A: Have you seen the plans for the old factory?
B: Yes. A shopping mall. What do you think?
A: It could create more employment. My brother's looking for work.
B: True, but I worry about the environmental impact. That means more cars on our street.
A: The factory had a lot of cars and trucks too.
B: It did. But it's been empty for years. I enjoy having a quiet street now.
A: So you're against the development?
B: Not completely. I like the idea of outdoor tables and space for people to meet.
A: What's the main disadvantage, then? Just the cars?
B: Yes. The new entrance is right opposite our school.
A: Oh, I didn't notice that. Can we ask them to put it somewhere else?
B: There's a meeting on Thursday. A local group has started a campaign for a different entrance.
A: Can anyone go, or is it only for people who own property here?
B: Everyone can go. It's at the library, and we can all share our views.
A: I'll come. Do we vote on the plans there?
B: No, it's a discussion. We can ask questions before we decide what to support.
A: Good. I thought you wanted to stop the whole thing.
B: No. I want the shops. I just don't want a line of cars outside my kitchen window.

Vocabulary: campaign, development, disadvantage, employment, environmental, impact, mall, outdoor, property, vote
