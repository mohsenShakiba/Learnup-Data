A New Use for the Factory
A: Have you seen the plans for the old factory at the end of the road?
B: Yes, they want to turn it into a shopping mall. What do you think about it?
A: Well, it could create more employment here, and my brother's looking for work.
B: That's true, but I worry about the environmental impact. A big mall means a lot more cars on our street.
A: The factory used to have plenty of cars and trucks going past too.
B: It did, but it's been empty for years, and I really enjoy having a quiet street now.
A: So are you against the new development?
B: Not completely. I actually like the idea of outdoor tables and space where people can meet.
A: What's the main disadvantage for you, then? Just the extra cars?
B: Yes, especially because they've put the new entrance on our street, right opposite our school.
A: Oh, I didn't notice that on the map. Can we ask them to put it on the main road instead?
B: A local group has already started a campaign for a different entrance, and there's a meeting about it on Thursday.
A: Can anyone go, or is it only for people who own property here?
B: Everyone who lives here can go. It's at the library, and we can all share our views.
A: I'll come with you. Do we vote on the plans at the meeting?
B: Not yet. This one is just a discussion so we can ask questions before we decide what to support.
A: Good. At first, I thought you wanted to stop the whole thing.
B: Not at all. I want the shops, but I just don't want a line of cars outside my kitchen window.

Vocabulary: campaign, development, disadvantage, employment, environmental, impact, mall, outdoor, property, vote
