Keeping the Flat Clean
A: Can we talk about the kitchen? There's rubbish on the table and dirty plates everywhere.
B: Sorry, most of that is mine. I admit I haven't put much effort into keeping it tidy this week.
A: I know you've been busy at work, although it feels unfair when I end up cleaning the kitchen every night.
B: You're right, it is unfair. Can we divide the jobs more equally?
A: Yes, please. For example, one of us can cook and the other can wash up after dinner.
B: That's a much more practical plan. I generally get home an hour before you, so I can cook during the week.
A: Good. Then I'll wash up and clean the table after we eat.
B: What about the bathroom?
A: We need to clean that regularly too, so let's take turns each weekend.
B: Fair enough. I'll clean it this Saturday, and you can do it next week.
A: Thanks. And could you do me a quick favour before we start dinner?
B: Sure. What is it?
A: Could you take that full bag of rubbish downstairs? There's no space left for any more.
B: Of course. I'll take the rubbish down now, and then I'll wash all these dirty plates before I start cooking.

Vocabulary: admit, divide, effort, equally, favour, generally, practical, regularly, rubbish, unfair
