Keeping the Flat Clean
A: Can we talk about the kitchen? There's rubbish on the table and dirty plates everywhere.
B: I know. We haven't put much effort into cleaning the kitchen.
A: Although we both use it, I'm the one who cleans it every night.
B: That's unfair. I admit I've left too much for you to do.
A: Can we divide the jobs more equally? For example, one of us can cook and the other can wash up.
B: That sounds practical. I generally get home first, so I can cook.
A: Good. I'll wash up after we eat.
B: What about the bathroom?
A: We need to clean that regularly too. We can take turns each week.
B: I'll do it this Saturday. You can do it next week.
A: Fine. And can you do me a favour?
B: What is it?
A: Wash these plates now. There aren't any clean ones left.
B: Yes. I'll clear the table and wash them before I start cooking.

Vocabulary: admit, divide, effort, equally, favour, generally, practical, regularly, rubbish, unfair
