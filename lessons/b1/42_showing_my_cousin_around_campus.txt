Showing My Cousin Around Campus
A: Are you ready for the weekend?
B: Almost! My younger cousin is arriving tonight because she wants to see our university campus tomorrow.
A: Oh, is she thinking about studying here next year?
B: Yes, her ambition is to enter the medical profession.
A: That's exciting. Are you going to show her our building too?
B: Maybe, but first I want to show her the main sights on campus.
A: Don't forget the student union building. Their cafe has the best view over our campus.
B: Good idea, and we can have lunch there till the library opens at two o'clock.
A: Does she already have the school qualifications she needs to apply?
B: She'll get her final results in the summer.
A: Is she staying with you for the whole weekend?
B: Only Saturday night, because she has to be back home on Sunday for school.
A: Well, I'm sure you'll persuade her that this is the best university.
B: I'll try! We've lived apart for years, so having a relative in the same town will be really nice.

Vocabulary: ambition, apart, campus, persuade, profession, qualification, relative, sight, till, union
