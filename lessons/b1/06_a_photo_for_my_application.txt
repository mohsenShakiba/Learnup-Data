A Photo for My Application
A: Can you take a photo of me here by the window? I need one for a new job.
B: Sure. Do you need to send it today?
A: Yes, the application's due at five. I don't have a suitable photo, and the studio near work is closed today.
B: I'm no photographer, but the light's good here.
A: Do I look smart enough in this shirt? I want to make a good impression.
B: You do. Just move slightly to your left.
A: Like this?
B: Yes. That's it. Here, have a look.
A: Oh, I wanted to look cheerful. I just look nervous!
B: Relax your shoulders. You don't have to stand so straight.
A: I never feel very confident in front of a camera.
B: Forget the camera for a second. Tell me about the job.
A: It's at a local school. They need a new art teacher, and their art room is huge!
B: Look, I took another one while you were talking. You're smiling much more naturally in this one.
A: Oh, that's much better. I'll use that one.
B: Great. I'll send it to you now.

Vocabulary: application, cheerful, confident, impression, naturally, photographer, slightly, smart, studio, suitable
