A Photo for My Application
A: Can you take a portrait of me for my job application?
B: Sure. Do you need it today?
A: Yes. The studio near work is closed.
B: I'm not a professional photographer, but we can try. Stand by the window. There's more light.
A: Like this? I want to make a good impression.
B: You look smart, but move slightly to your left.
A: Is this better?
B: Yes. Turn your head a little. That's it. Here's the first photo.
A: Oh, I look nervous.
B: Relax your shoulders. You don't have to stand so straight.
A: I don't feel very confident.
B: That's all right. We have time. Tell me about the job.
A: It's in a school. I can teach art there.
B: That sounds good. There, I've taken another photo. You look more relaxed, and you're smiling more naturally.
A: Much better. I'll use that one.
B: Great. I'll send it to you now.

Vocabulary: application, confident, impression, naturally, photographer, portrait, relaxed, slightly, smart, studio
