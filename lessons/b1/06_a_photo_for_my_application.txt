A Photo for My Application
A: Can you take a photo of me for my job application? I don't have a suitable one on my phone.
B: Sure. Do you need to send it today?
A: Yes, before five, and the photo studio near work is closed today.
B: I'm not a professional photographer, but we can try. Stand by the window. There's more light there.
A: Like this? I want to make a good impression.
B: You look smart, but move slightly to your left.
A: Is this better?
B: Yes. Turn your head a little. That's it. Here's the first photo.
A: Oh, I look really nervous.
B: Relax your shoulders. You don't have to stand so straight.
A: Sorry, I don't feel very confident in front of a camera.
B: That's all right. Forget the camera for a second and tell me about the job.
A: It's at a local school. They need a new art teacher, and their art room is huge!
B: Hold that smile! Look, I took another photo while you were talking. You look much more cheerful, and you're smiling more naturally.
A: Oh, that's much better. I'll use that one.
B: Great. I'll send it to you now.

Vocabulary: application, cheerful, confident, impression, naturally, photographer, slightly, smart, studio, suitable
