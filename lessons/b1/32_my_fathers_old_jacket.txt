My Father's Old Jacket
A: Is that a new jacket? It looks different from your usual clothes.
B: It's my father's. I need a proper jacket for dinner with my new colleagues tonight.
A: I really like it. Those shiny buttons look expensive.
B: Yes, and it's made of wool. He bought it before I was born, though. Is it too old-fashioned?
A: Not at all. Old clothes are fashionable again. Does it fit well?
B: Mostly, though it feels a little tight when I fasten it. Look.
A: Can you sit down in it?
B: Yes, if I'm careful. Oh, wait, one of the buttons is coming off.
A: Do you have a needle? I can put it back on for you before we go.
B: There's one in that little box, I think. Can we just use a pin instead? We're leaving soon.
A: A pin might fall out while we're at dinner. Fixing it will only take a minute.
B: Thanks. Here's the jacket, and I'll get the iron for my shirt while you do that.
A: Good idea. What did your father say when you borrowed it?
B: He laughed and said I finally knew how to dress.
A: That's his way of saying it looks good on you.
B: Maybe, but he also asked me to bring it back tomorrow because he needs it for a party.
A: Then let's make sure that button stays on.
B: Definitely. I don't want him to charge me for repairs!

Vocabulary: fashionable, fasten, iron, needle, old-fashioned, pin, proper, shiny, tight, wool
