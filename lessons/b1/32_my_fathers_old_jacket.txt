My Father's Old Jacket
A: Is that a new jacket? It looks different from your usual clothes.
B: It's my father's. I need a proper jacket for dinner with my new colleagues.
A: I like it. Those shiny buttons look expensive.
B: It's wool. He bought it before I was born. Is it too old-fashioned?
A: Not at all. Old clothes are fashionable again. Does it fit?
B: It's a little tight when I fasten it. Look.
A: Can you sit down in it?
B: Yes, like this. But one button is coming off.
A: Do you have a needle? I can put it back on for you.
B: In that little box, I think. Can you use a pin instead? We're leaving soon.
A: A pin might fall out. This will only take a minute.
B: Thanks. I'll get the iron for my shirt while you do that.
A: Good idea. What does your father think about you wearing his jacket?
B: He laughed and said I finally knew how to dress.
A: That's a nice way of saying he likes it on you.
B: Maybe. He also asked me to bring it back tomorrow. He needs it for a party.
A: Then let's make sure that button stays on.
B: Yes. I don't want him to charge me for repairs!

Vocabulary: fashionable, fasten, iron, needle, old-fashioned, pin, proper, shiny, tight, wool
