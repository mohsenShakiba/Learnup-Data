A Wedding for Two Families
A: Congratulations! How does it feel to be engaged?
B: Lovely, until someone asks about the wedding. Both families have a plan already.
A: And do their plans match yours?
B: Not at all. Ours is a small lunch. Theirs is a party for everyone they've ever met.
A: Have you chosen a definite date yet?
B: September the tenth. My mum says it's a special occasion, so we need a big celebration.
A: What does Sam think?
B: He agrees with me. We want to spend our money on our life together. The marriage matters more than one day.
A: You should explain that to your families.
B: We have. Then his mum started talking about the bride arriving in a special car.
A: Do you want that?
B: No. And I don't want a diamond ring either. I lose things too easily.
A: You seem quite sure about what makes you happy.
B: I am. I just want everyone to understand that our happiness doesn't depend on an expensive party.
A: They probably want to give you a day you'll remember forever.
B: I know. We've agreed to have lunch with our families, then invite friends for cake in the evening.
A: That sounds like a good plan. How many people is that?
B: I'm afraid to count. Our small wedding is growing every time we talk about it.

Vocabulary: bride, celebration, definite, diamond, engaged, forever, happiness, marriage, occasion, ours, theirs
