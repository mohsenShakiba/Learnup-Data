My Brother on Television
A: Are you free on Thursday evening? My brother's going to be on a TV quiz.
B: Really? How did he get on it?
A: He sent in an application to participate. They wanted people who know a lot about films.
B: That's perfect for him. He remembers every actor and every line of a script.
A: He does. The first category is old films, so he's happy about that.
B: Is the programme live?
A: No. The production takes a whole afternoon, but viewers only see half an hour.
B: What happens during the rest of the time?
A: They stop to check the lights and move things around the set. The editor chooses what to keep later.
B: Did your brother find it difficult?
A: At first. He had to stand behind some tape on the floor, and he kept forgetting to ring his bell before answering.
B: Has he met the host? She seems friendly on television.
A: Yes. She talked to everyone before they started. He was less nervous after that.
B: Good. What did he wear?
A: He arrived in his bright orange shirt.
B: Oh, right! Did it look good on camera?
A: The costume team asked him to change. They gave him a dark blue one.
B: Did he win?
A: He's not allowed to tell us yet. I keep asking.
B: Then I'll come over on Thursday. We can find out together.

Vocabulary: bell, category, costume, editor, host, participate, production, script, tape, viewer
