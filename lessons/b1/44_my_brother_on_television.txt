My Brother on Television
A: Are you free on Thursday evening? My brother's going to be on a TV quiz.
B: Really? How did he get the chance to participate?
A: He sent in an application a few months ago because they wanted people who know a lot about films.
B: That's perfect for him. He remembers every actor and almost every line of a script.
A: Exactly, and when they recorded his episode last week, the first category was old films, which is his favourite.
B: Oh, so the programme isn't live on Thursday?
A: No, they recorded it last Tuesday. The whole production took an afternoon, even though viewers only see half an hour.
B: Why does it take so long?
A: They have to stop to check the lights and move things around the set, and then the editor chooses the best parts later.
B: Did your brother find it difficult?
A: He was nervous at first. There were yellow lines of tape on the floor to show everyone where to stand, and he kept forgetting to ring his bell before answering.
B: What was the host like? She always seems very friendly on television.
A: He said she was lovely. She talked to everyone before they started, so he felt much less nervous after that.
B: Do you know what he wore?
A: Well, he arrived in his bright orange shirt, but it was too bright for the cameras.
B: Oh no! Did they ask him to change it?
A: Yes, the costume team gave him a dark blue shirt to wear instead.
B: Most importantly, did he win?
A: He's not allowed to tell us until Thursday, even though I keep asking him.
B: Then I'll come over on Thursday evening so we can find out together.

Vocabulary: bell, category, costume, editor, host, participate, production, script, set, tape, viewer
