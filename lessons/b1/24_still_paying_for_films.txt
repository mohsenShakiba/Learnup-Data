Still Paying for Films
A: Do you still watch that film channel?
B: Sometimes. Why?
A: I'm trying to spend less on entertainment. It's ten pounds a month.
B: Oh, right. I watched the last episode of my show on Sunday.
A: Then can we stop the payments?
B: There's a new documentary I want to see.
A: Is one programme worth ten pounds a month?
B: You're right. I don't watch enough of their content.
A: Some other channels are absolutely free.
B: True, but I don't like the ads.
A: Is their basic plan better value?
B: Let me check. It's five pounds, but that has ads too.
A: Let's stop paying, then.
B: Do we have to call them?
A: No. We can simply do it in our account.
B: Okay. Will we lose access today?
A: No. We can watch until the end of the month.
B: Good. I'll watch the documentary this week.
A: What’s the documentary about?
B: It’s about nature. It looks fascinating.

Vocabulary: absolutely, basic, channel, content, documentary, entertainment, episode, fascinating, simply, value
