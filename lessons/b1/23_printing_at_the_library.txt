Printing at the Library
A: Hello. Can I print something here? My printer at home isn't working.
B: Yes. Do you have a library card?
A: I do. Do I need it for printing?
B: Yes. We require a card for access. Scan it here.
A: Okay, I've opened my file. What next?
B: Click on Print. How many pages do you need?
A: Just two, in colour.
B: That's a pound. Your current balance is here.
A: It's zero. Can I pay by card?
B: Yes. Hold your card here. It won't print unless you pay first.
A: I've paid two pounds.
B: Good. Now you can print your file.
A: There's my file. Do I press Print again?
B: Click on your file to highlight it. Then press Print.
A: It's working. Will the extra pound remain on my library card?
B: Yes. You can use it next time.

Vocabulary: access, balance, click, current, highlight, press, remain, require, scan, unless
