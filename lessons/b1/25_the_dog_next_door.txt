The Dog Next Door
A: Hi. Is that your dog in my back yard?
B: Oh, yes! I was looking for him. How did he get there?
A: I think he came through the fence. There's a gap behind that tree.
B: He tried to escape yesterday too. Can you open the gate?
A: Of course. He's hiding behind a pot. I can see his tail.
B: Max! Come here!
A: Does he bite? I'm afraid of dogs a bit.
B: No, he's very gentle. There, he's coming over now.
A: His fur is covered in mud. He was rolling around between my flowers.
B: I'm so sorry. Has he damaged anything?
A: He pulled up one small plant. You can see the empty spot over there.
B: I'll replace it. I'll take him home first, then come back and fix the fence.
A: One of the boards has come off. Look, there's a nail on the ground.
B: I'll pick it up. Can you hold Max for a moment?
A: Of course. Let's check for other nails too.
B: Good idea. We should make sure it's safe to walk.

Vocabulary: bite, covered, escape, fence, fur, mud, nail, roll, spot, tail, yard
