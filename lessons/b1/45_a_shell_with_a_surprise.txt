A Shell with a Surprise
A: Did you bring anything back from your holiday?
B: Just some photos. I nearly brought home a live animal, though.
A: On purpose?
B: No. I noticed a beautiful shell on the beach. It was shining in the water.
A: So you picked it up?
B: Yes. There was a sharp edge, so I carried it in my cap.
A: What were you going to do with it?
B: Hang it by my window on a piece of string. I had no idea there was anything inside.
A: How did you find out?
B: I put it on the hotel table. There were grains of sand everywhere, and then the shell moved.
A: That sounds scary!
B: I called my brother. Meanwhile, the shell kept moving across the table.
A: Did you pick it up again?
B: Not till I saw a pair of little legs under it!
A: What did you do with it?
B: I carried it straight back to the beach in my cap to release it.
A: Did you find another shell?
B: No. That was enough excitement for one holiday.
A: At least you have a story to tell.
B: And some sand in my cap. I'm still finding it.

Vocabulary: cap, excitement, grain, meanwhile, release, sharp, shell, shine, string, till
