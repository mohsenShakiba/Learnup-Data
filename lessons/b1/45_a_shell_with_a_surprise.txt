A Shell with a Surprise
A: Did you bring anything back from your holiday?
B: Just some photos, though I nearly brought home a live animal by mistake.
A: How did that happen?
B: I noticed a beautiful shell on the beach while it was shining in the water.
A: So you picked it up to take home?
B: Yes. I didn't have a bag, and one edge was quite sharp, so I carried it back to the hotel in my cap.
A: What were you planning to do with it?
B: Hang it by my window on a piece of string. I had no idea there was anything inside it.
A: How did you find out?
B: When I took it out of my cap and put it on the hotel table, a few grains of sand fell out, and then the shell moved.
A: That sounds scary!
B: It surprised me! I went to call my brother from the next room, and meanwhile, the shell walked right across the table.
A: What was inside it?
B: I wasn't sure till I looked under it and saw a pair of little legs.
A: Poor thing! What did you do with it?
B: I carefully put it back into my cap and carried it straight down to the beach to release it.
A: Did you look for another shell while you were there?
B: Definitely not. That was enough excitement for one holiday.
A: Well, at least you came home with a good story.
B: True, and plenty of sand in my cap. I'm still finding it.

Vocabulary: cap, excitement, grain, meanwhile, release, sharp, shell, shine, string, till
