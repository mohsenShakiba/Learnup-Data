When a Hobby Becomes Work
A: Are you still working for that engineering company?
B: No, they closed our office last month, so I'm unemployed at the moment.
A: Oh, I'm sorry to hear that. Are you looking for another job like that?
B: Yes, I've sent out a few applications, but I'm also thinking about earning a living from photography.
A: That's a great idea. You've always loved taking pictures.
B: I have, though doing it in my leisure time is very different from doing it for money.
A: True. What's your next step?
B: I'm taking an advanced photography course this month, and then I need to promote my work.
A: Do you have a website yet?
B: Not yet, but my sister advised me to build one, and she's going to help me with it this weekend.
A: Having a website can give you a global audience.
B: Maybe one day, though right now I just need a few local customers to start with.
A: Actually, my cousin's a travel agent, and she needs some new photos of places near here for her website.
B: Really? I've taken hundreds of photos around here over the last few years.
A: Send me a few, and I'll ask if she wants to see them. I can't promise anything, though.
B: Thanks, that's really helpful. Looking through my photos tonight will be a nice mental break from writing job applications.
A: You certainly sound much more cheerful when you talk about your pictures.
B: I feel it too. I'll choose a few good ones tonight and send them to you.

Vocabulary: advanced, advise, agent, engineering, global, leisure, living, mental, promote, unemployed
