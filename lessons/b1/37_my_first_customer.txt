My First Customer
A: Why is the kitchen table covered in cards?
B: I've started selling the cards I make. I've just had my first order.
A: I thought you only made them for friends.
B: I did. Then I saw a commercial for a website that helps people sell their own goods.
A: Was it expensive to join?
B: No. They have free resources to help you open a little online shop.
A: That's useful. Are you making a profit yet?
B: Not yet. I'm still learning about the financial side. I spent too much on paper.
A: Where did you buy it?
B: From a shop that imports paper from Japan. It's beautiful, but expensive.
A: Are you going to buy more?
B: Not now. I don't want to invest more money until I've sold these.
A: Sensible. Do you only sell them online?
B: For now. I want to expand later. The Saturday market does a lot of trade before Christmas.
A: So who's your first customer?
B: My cousin in France. She ordered six cards.
A: Your first order is an export! You're doing better than I expected.
B: Thanks. But right now, I just need somewhere to eat my dinner.

Vocabulary: commercial, expand, export, financial, goods, import, invest, profit, resource, trade
