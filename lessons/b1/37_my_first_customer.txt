My First Customer
A: Why is the kitchen table covered in cards?
B: I've started selling the cards I make, and I've just had my first order.
A: Really? I thought you only made them for friends.
B: I did, until I saw a commercial for a website that helps people sell their own goods.
A: Was it expensive to join?
B: Not at all. They have free resources online that show you how to open a little shop.
A: That's useful. Will you make a profit on this first order?
B: Not yet. I'm still learning about the financial side, and I spent too much on the paper.
A: Why? Where did you buy it?
B: From a shop that imports paper from Japan. It's beautiful, but it costs a lot.
A: Are you going to buy more of it?
B: Not for now. I don't want to invest any more money until I've sold all of these.
A: That's sensible. Do you plan to sell them only online?
B: For now, yes, though I want to expand later. The Saturday market does a lot of trade before Christmas.
A: Good idea. So who's your first customer?
B: My cousin in France. She ordered six cards this afternoon.
A: Your cousin? Does family really count as a customer?
B: Of course she counts! She paid full price, and because she lives in France, I'm calling it my first export.

Vocabulary: commercial, expand, export, financial, goods, import, invest, profit, resource, trade
