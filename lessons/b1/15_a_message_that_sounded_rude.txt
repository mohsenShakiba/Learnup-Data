A Message That Sounded Rude
A: Can you look at this message on my phone? Sam hasn't answered, and I'm starting to worry.
B: What did you send him?
A: He invited me to dinner on Friday, and I wrote, "Great, please don't cook this time!"
B: Oh, that sounds a bit cruel. What happened last time?
A: Last time he burned our dinner and we ordered pizza. We both laughed about it. I didn't mean to upset him.
B: I see. He might take it seriously in a message.
A: I wonder if I've annoyed him. I feel really embarrassed now.
B: When did you send it?
A: Half an hour ago. Should I call him to apologize?
B: Personally, I'd send a message. He may still be at work.
A: True. I don't want to bother him there. How about "Sorry, that was a silly thing to say. I was thinking about the pizza. I'd love to come on Friday"?
B: Yes, send that. The bit about the pizza helps.
A: Done. I'll give him time to reply.

Vocabulary: annoy, apologize, bother, cruel, embarrassed, personally, seriously, silly, upset, wonder
