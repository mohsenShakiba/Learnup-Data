A Message That Sounded Rude
A: I think I've annoyed Sam. He hasn't replied to my message.
B: What did you say?
A: He invited me to his house for a meal, and I wrote, "Please don't cook this time!"
B: That sounds a bit unpleasant.
A: I was trying to be funny. I didn't intend to upset him.
B: He might think you're serious.
A: Now I feel embarrassed. Should I call him?
B: Personally, I think you should send a message first. He may be at work.
A: I don't want to bother him at work, but I ought to apologize.
B: Keep it short. Just explain what you meant.
A: "Sorry, that was a silly thing to say. I was trying to be funny. I'd love to come." Is that enough?
B: Yes. You can apologize again when you see him.
A: All right. I'll send it now.
B: Good. And give him some time to reply.

Vocabulary: annoy, apologize, bother, embarrassed, intend, ought, personally, silly, unpleasant, upset
