Selling My Old Bike
A: I've decided to sell my bike. Can you help me with the ad?
B: Sure. Why are you selling it?
A: I take the train to work now, and this bike's too big for the train. It doesn't fold.
B: Right. Start with the brand and a few clear photos.
A: I've taken some already. The frame has no marks on it, and the chain's new.
B: Good. How much do you want for it?
A: I paid two hundred pounds for it originally. What's it worth now?
B: It's in great condition, and that brand is very reliable. Try a hundred pounds.
A: Fair enough. Should I offer to deliver it if someone lives near here?
B: No, let people come and try it first.
A: Good point, but I prefer to avoid giving my home address to strangers.
B: We can meet them somewhere busy instead, like outside the station.
A: Can you come with me if someone wants to see it?
B: Yes, I'm free on Saturday afternoon.
A: Saturday works well. I'll put that in the ad.

Vocabulary: ad, brand, chain, deliver, fold, frame, originally, reliable, stranger, worth
