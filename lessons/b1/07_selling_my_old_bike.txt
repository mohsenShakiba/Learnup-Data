Selling My Old Bike
A: I'm going to sell my bike online. Can you help me with the price?
B: Sure. Why are you selling it?
A: I take the train to work now. This bike's too big to take with me, and it doesn't fold.
B: Have you taken any photos for the ad yet?
A: Yes, look. The frame has no marks on it, and the chain's new.
B: It looks good. How much did you pay for it?
A: Two hundred pounds originally. What's it worth now?
B: It's a reliable brand. Try a hundred pounds.
A: Fair enough. Should I offer to deliver it if someone lives near here?
B: People usually like to try a bike first.
A: True, but I don't want to give my home address to strangers.
B: How about meeting them outside the station? It's always busy there.
A: Can you come with me if someone wants to see it?
B: Yes, I'm free on Saturday afternoon.
A: Saturday works well. I'll put that in the ad.

Vocabulary: ad, brand, chain, deliver, fold, frame, originally, reliable, stranger, worth
