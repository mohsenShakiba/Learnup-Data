Selling My Old Bike
A: I've decided to sell my bike. Can you help me write an ad?
B: Sure. Why are you selling it?
A: I need to take a bike on the train. This one's too big, and it doesn't fold.
B: Okay. Start with the brand and a few photos.
A: I've taken some already. The frame looks good, and the chain's new.
B: Good. How much do you want for it?
A: I paid two hundred pounds originally. What's it worth now?
B: It's still reliable. Try a hundred.
A: Fine. Should I offer to deliver it?
B: Let people come and see it first.
A: I'd like to avoid meeting strangers at home.
B: We can meet them outside instead.
A: Can you come with me?
B: Yes. I'm free on Saturday afternoon.
A: Saturday works. I'll put that in the ad.

Vocabulary: ad, brand, chain, deliver, fold, frame, originally, reliable, stranger, worth
