A Day Without Plans
A: What are your plans for Sunday?
B: Nothing at all. Just thinking about it puts me in a good mood.
A: Really? I feel guilty if I don't do something useful on my day off.
B: I tend to make plans during the week, so I love a day without a list.
A: I always write a list for Sunday. Last week, I wanted to clean the kitchen, study Spanish, and finish a book.
B: That's a bit ambitious for one day!
A: It's obvious why I never finish, isn't it?
B: What did you actually do?
A: Watched TV. I enjoyed it at first, but eventually I got bored.
B: Usually when I get bored, I ask my friends to join me. 
A: That's a good idea. I usually get lonely on Sundays.
B: You should ask your friends, or go for a walk.
A: I doubt I can stop thinking about all the things I should do.
B: That's the challenge for me too. But I do try to be more relaxed.
A: Fair enough. Can I join you on Sunday?
B: Of course. Join me whenever you like.

Vocabulary: ambitious, challenge, doubt, eventually, guilty, lonely, mood, obvious, tend, whenever
