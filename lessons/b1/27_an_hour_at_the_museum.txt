An Hour at the Museum
A: Hi. I've never visited this museum before, and I only have an hour. Where should I start?
B: What are you particularly interested in?
A: Photography, and maybe sculpture.
B: Entry to both is included in your ticket. I recommend starting with the photos.
A: Great. What's the photo exhibition about?
B: The theme is how our town has changed.
A: Are they all old photos?
B: No. We have historical photos next to current ones, so you can see the contrast.
A: Are there any of the old market? My grandfather used to work there.
B: Yes. There's a large photo of it on that wall.
A: Can I take a picture to show him?
B: Of course. Go ahead.
A: And what about that statue by the stairs? It looks familiar.
B: That's the old statue from the town square.
A: I remember it! My friends and I used to meet there. Why is it here now?
B: It was moved here to protect it from the weather.
A: I'll take a photo for my friends too. Then I'll explore the rest of the exhibition.

Vocabulary: contrast, entry, exhibition, explore, historical, particularly, photography, sculpture, statue, theme
