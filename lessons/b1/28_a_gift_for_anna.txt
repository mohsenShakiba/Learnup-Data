A Gift for Anna
A: Anna retires on Friday. 
B: I'm really going to miss her. She is such a lovely person.
A: I know. When I first started working here, I almost gave up, but she encouraged me to keep going.
B: We should throw her a party.
A: I agree. We should also buy her a gift.
B: She says she doesn't want an expensive gift. How about a photo album as a surprise?
A: I like that. But we only have two days to get everything ready for Friday.
B: We can gather the photos today and print them tomorrow. Do you have any?
A: Here's one from the day she opened the shop. She looks so proud.
B: It was a big achievement. That should go on the first page.
A: We can ask everyone to write a message too. Mine can start, "It's been a pleasure working with you."
B: Put that in. She'll love it.
A: And I'll say our friendship doesn't end on Friday. We still have our Saturday coffee!
B: Exactly. I'll buy the album. We can decorate it tomorrow after work.
A: Great. I'll ask for the photos in our work group now.
B: Wait! Anna's in that group too.
A: Oh, good thing I haven't sent it. I'll message everyone separately.
B: Ask them to send the photos to you, too. Anna might offer to help if she sees them!

Vocabulary: achievement, album, celebration, decorate, encourage, friendship, gather, pleasure, proud, retire
