Joining the Book Group
A: Are you going to your book group tonight?
B: Yes. Do you want to come along?
A: Maybe. What's it like? I enjoy reading, but I haven't studied literature.
B: Oh, it isn't academic at all! It's just a friendly discussion over tea.
A: What's the book for this month?
B: A mystery novel by Clara Hill. The plot's quite complex, so we'll have plenty to talk about.
A: I'm not familiar with her books, but I'm really keen on mystery stories. Can I come without reading it first?
B: Of course. If you don't mind hearing the ending, you can just listen on your first visit.
A: That helps. I'm a bit shy in new groups.
B: Don't worry, it's a very relaxed atmosphere. Nobody expects a long speech from you.
A: Do you only read novels? I'm interested in modern poetry too.
B: Mostly novels and short stories, but we read a poetry book in the spring. Everyone takes turns choosing.
A: Sounds great. I'll come tonight, then. Where do you meet?
B: At the library at six, in the small room to the left of the main entrance.

Vocabulary: academic, atmosphere, complex, entrance, familiar, keen, literature, mystery, plot, poetry, shy
