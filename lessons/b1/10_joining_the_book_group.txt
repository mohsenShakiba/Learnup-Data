Joining the Book Group
A: Are you going to the book group tonight?
B: Yes. Do you want to come?
A: Maybe. I'm keen on reading, but I don't know much about literature.
B: You don't need any academic knowledge. It's just a friendly discussion.
A: What's the book for this month?
B: A mystery. The plot's quite complex, so we'll have plenty to talk about.
A: I'm not familiar with it. Can I come without reading it?
B: Of course. You can just listen on your first visit.
A: That helps. I'm a bit shy in groups.
B: It's a relaxed atmosphere. Nobody expects a speech.
A: Do you only read novels? I'm interested in poetry too.
B: We read poems sometimes. You can suggest one for next month.
A: All right. I'll come tonight. Where do you meet?
B: At the library at six. We meet in the room left of the entrance.

Vocabulary: academic, atmosphere, complex, entrance, familiar, keen, literature, mystery, plot, poem, poetry, shy
