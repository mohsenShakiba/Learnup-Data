Games with Grandma
A: How was your evening with your family?
B: Good, until we started a board game. Everyone gets so competitive!
A: Who won?
B: Grandma. She's still our family champion.
A: Do you let her win?
B: No! We bought a new game, and she set it up before we arrived.
A: So she knew the rules before everyone else?
B: Yes. I used a coin because one of the little pieces was missing. Then she told me to move backwards.
A: Is that allowed?
B: I didn't want to risk an argument, so I checked the instructions. She was right.
A: Good. At least she doesn't cheat.
B: She always says we must stick to the rules. Everyone gets an equal number of turns.
A: Then what's her technique? How does she keep winning?
B: She watches everyone carefully while we talk about other things.
A: Maybe you should stop talking for a change.
B: I tried that. Then she asked me about my new job.
A: And you forgot about the game?
B: Completely. She's very good at choosing her moment.

Vocabulary: backwards, champion, cheat, coin, competitive, equal, risk, set, stick, technique
