Games with Grandma
A: How was your evening with your family?
B: Relaxing at first, until we started a board game. Everyone in my family gets so competitive!
A: Who won this time?
B: Grandma, of course. She's still our family champion.
A: Do the rest of you let her win?
B: Not at all! We bought a new game, and she set it up and read the rules before we arrived.
A: So she already knew how to play before everyone else?
B: Exactly. Everyone starts with an equal number of coins, and in the middle of the game, she told me to give her two of mine and move backwards.
A: Is that actually allowed in the game?
B: Well, I didn't want to risk an argument, so I quietly checked the instructions, and she was right.
A: Good. At least she doesn't cheat.
B: Never. She always says we must stick to the rules.
A: Then what's her technique? How does she keep winning every time?
B: She watches the board carefully while the rest of us are busy talking about other things.
A: Maybe you should stop talking and watch the board for a change.
B: I tried that last night, right until she asked me how my new job was going.
A: And you forgot all about the game again?
B: Completely. She's very good at choosing her moment.

Vocabulary: backwards, champion, cheat, coin, competitive, equal, risk, set, stick, technique
