More Than a Good Grade
A: Did you use much French on your holiday?
B: I tried. I got good grades at secondary school. Reading was my strength, but real conversations were different.
A: Was understanding people the main difficulty?
B: Yes. Native speakers don't stop after every sentence like my old teacher.
A: Did you ask them to speak more slowly?
B: I did, and that helped. I also used an app to translate a few things.
A: I can relate to that. I know lots of words, but I sometimes forget them when I need them.
B: Exactly. I can read a definition in a dictionary and understand it. Using the word is another thing.
A: Did communication get easier by the end of the trip?
B: Yes. I stopped trying to say everything perfectly.
A: That sounds like progress.
B: It was. At school, I used to revise for hours before a test. On holiday, I just wanted to order lunch.
A: And did you get the lunch you wanted?
B: Not on the first day. I asked for six sandwiches instead of two.
A: Six? What did you do with them?
B: Shared them with some people in the park. We ended up talking all afternoon.
A: So, are you glad you went?
B: Yes. I made a few mistakes, but I made some new friends too.

Vocabulary: communication, definition, difficulty, grade, native, relate, revise, secondary, strength, translate
