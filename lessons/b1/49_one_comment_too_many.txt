One Comment Too Many
A: You've been staring at your phone for ten minutes. What's wrong?
B: I'm really mad about a reply to my comment on the local town page.
A: What did you post?
B: I just suggested adding an evening bus from the station, and some guy rejected the idea straight away and called it stupid.
A: Why do people talk like that online? Surely he could just say he disagrees.
B: Exactly. I typically ignore rude replies, but he's acting as if I'm his enemy.
A: Wait, are you typing an answer to him right now?
B: Yes, a long one. I want to show him why his attitude is the problem.
A: Don't do it. Turning a question about buses into an online battle is a waste of your evening.
B: So I should just let him be rude?
A: If you send a careless reply while you're angry, the only consequence will be ten more arguments tonight.
B: You're probably right, but it's hard to close the page without saying anything.
A: Here's a little trick I use: write the reply, wait five minutes, and read it again before you press send.
B: Well, I've already written it... Actually, reading it back now, I sound just as angry as he does.
A: Remove it and come and help me choose a film instead.

Vocabulary: attitude, battle, careless, consequence, enemy, mad, reject, surely, trick, typically
