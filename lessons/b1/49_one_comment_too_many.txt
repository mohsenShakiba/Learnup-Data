One Comment Too Many
A: Why are you looking so annoyed?
B: I'm mad about a reply to my comment on the local news website.
A: What did you write?
B: I said our town needs more buses. A stranger said that was a stupid idea.
A: He can reject your idea without being rude.
B: Exactly. We've never met, but he's treating me like an enemy.
A: You don't have to turn it into a battle.
B: Surely people should be able to disagree without being rude!
A: Of course. Are you going to reply?
B: I typically ignore comments like that. But this time I'm writing something equally rude.
A: Don't post a careless reply while you're angry.
B: Why should I be polite when he isn't?
A: I tried your way once. The only consequence was more angry messages all evening.
B: Did the other person change their mind?
A: No. It's rare for those arguments to end well, in my experience.
B: Then how do you stop thinking about them?
A: I use a little trick. I write my reply but wait before sending it. Later, I usually decide not to send it.
B: I've finished mine. Let me read it again.
A: Do you still want to send it?
B: No. It's longer than the article. I'll close the page and go for a walk.

Vocabulary: battle, careless, consequence, enemy, mad, rare, reject, surely, trick, typically
