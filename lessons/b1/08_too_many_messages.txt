Too Many Messages
A: I can't concentrate. People keep sending me messages.
B: Is it the marketing group again?
A: Yes. I need to finish this presentation for a client.
B: Can't you stop checking the messages for half an hour?
A: But what if there’s a serious issue?
B: I'll answer their questions until you finish. You can ignore the messages for now.
A: Thanks. I'll tell them. There, I've sent the message.
B: Good. Put your tablet on silent too.
A: All right. I feel under a lot of pressure today.
B: Write a short summary first. You can add the details later.
A: Good idea. Can you remind me to send the presentation at four?
B: Yes. I'll come back at quarter to four. We can check it together.

Vocabulary: client, concentrate, ignore, issue, marketing, presentation, pressure, remind, silent, summary
