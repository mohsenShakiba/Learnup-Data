Too Many Messages
A: Can you watch the office chat for a bit? My phone hasn't stopped all morning.
B: Sure. What's going on?
A: The marketing team keeps sending me questions, and I can't concentrate. I need to finish a presentation for a client by four.
B: Why don't you put your phone on silent?
A: I don't want to ignore them if there's a serious issue with the project.
B: I'll answer them. If there's a problem, I'll come and tell you.
A: Thanks. I'll put my phone on silent, then.
B: How much of the presentation do you still have to do?
A: Just the last part. I'm under pressure to keep it short, but there's so much to say.
B: That client likes a clear summary. What matters most to them?
A: The price and the start date. I'll just put those on the last page. Can you remind me to stop at quarter to four so we have time to check it?
B: Sure. I'll be at your desk then.

Vocabulary: client, concentrate, ignore, issue, marketing, presentation, pressure, remind, silent, summary
