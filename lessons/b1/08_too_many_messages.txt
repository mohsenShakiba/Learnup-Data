Too Many Messages
A: I can't concentrate in here. People keep sending messages to the group chat.
B: Is it the marketing team again?
A: Yes, and I really need to finish this presentation for a client before four.
B: Can't you stop checking the chat for half an hour?
A: What if there's a serious issue with their project?
B: I'm in that chat too, so I'll answer their questions until you finish. You can ignore the messages for now.
A: Thanks. I'll tell them I'm busy for the next hour. There, I've sent a quick message.
B: Good. Put your phone on silent too.
A: Done. I'm under a lot of pressure today, and I still haven't finished the last part of the presentation.
B: Keep it simple. That client prefers a clear summary of the main points, not pages of detail.
A: True, that helps. My phone's on silent now, so can you remind me to stop writing at quarter to four?
B: Of course. I'll come back at quarter to four, and we can check everything together before you send it.

Vocabulary: client, concentrate, ignore, issue, marketing, presentation, pressure, remind, silent, summary
