The Late Meal Order
A: Has our lunch arrived yet? Our meeting starts in fifteen minutes.
B: No. They've confirmed the order on the app, but there's been no update since then.
A: Have we paid already?
B: Yes, they've already taken the payment from my account.
A: Have you tried to contact the restaurant?
B: I've called twice, but no one's answered.
A: Send them a message on the app, then.
B: Good idea. There, I've sent one.
A: I can get us something quick from the cafe downstairs instead.
B: Wait, they've just replied. They don't have enough staff in the kitchen today, so they haven't even started our food yet.
A: That's really disappointing. It can't possibly get here before our meeting now.
B: No. I'll ask for a refund on the app. The receipt says eighteen pounds in total.
A: Good. I'll go down to the cafe now. What do you want?
B: A sandwich, please. I'm really hungry!

Vocabulary: account, confirm, contact, disappointing, payment, possibly, receipt, staff, total, update
