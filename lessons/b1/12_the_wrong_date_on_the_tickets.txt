The Wrong Date on the Tickets
A: Hi. I'm a bit confused. These tickets are for Friday, but I asked for Saturday.
B: I'm sorry. Was the booking made here?
A: Yes, yesterday. Here's my receipt.
B: You're right. The wrong date was printed on the tickets.
A: Can I exchange them for Saturday's performance?
B: Of course. I can switch them to the seven o'clock show.
A: Yes, please. Can we have two seats together?
B: Yes. I have two in the second row.
A: Do I have to pay anything extra?
B: No. The price is the same, and your discount still applies.
A: Good. Is there usually a queue to get in?
B: Yes. Try to arrive approximately half an hour before the show. 
A: Okay, I really don't like long lines.
B: The queue is usually shorter then. After your tickets are checked, you can go directly to your seats.
A: Thanks. I appreciate your help.
B: Here are the new tickets.
A: Saturday at seven. Thank you.

Vocabulary: appreciate, approximately, confused, directly, discount, exchange, performance, queue, row, switch
