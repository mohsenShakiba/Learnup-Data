The Wrong Date on the Tickets
A: Hi. I'm a bit confused about these theatre tickets. They're for tonight, Friday, but I asked for Saturday.
B: Let me check for you. Was the booking made here at the desk?
A: Yes, yesterday afternoon. Here's my receipt.
B: Oh, I'm sorry. Your booking was entered for Friday by mistake.
A: Can I exchange them for tomorrow's performance?
B: Of course. I can switch them to Saturday's seven o'clock show right now.
A: Yes, please. Do you still have two seats together?
B: You're lucky. Two seats in the second row were just released this morning.
A: That's great! Do I have to pay anything extra? The tickets were bought with a student discount.
B: No, the Saturday price is the same, and your student discount still applies.
A: Good. Is there usually a long queue to get in?
B: Only right before seven. The doors are opened forty-five minutes early, so come around half past six.
A: Perfect, half past six is easy for us.
B: After your tickets are checked at the door, you can go directly to your seats.
A: Thanks. I really appreciate your help.
B: Here are your new tickets for Saturday at seven. Enjoy the show!

Vocabulary: appreciate, confuse, directly, discount, exchange, performance, queue, release, row, switch
