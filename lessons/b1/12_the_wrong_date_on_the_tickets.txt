The Wrong Date on the Tickets
A: Hi. Could you check the date on these tickets for me? I asked for Saturday.
B: Of course. Did you buy them here at the desk?
A: Yes, yesterday. Here's my receipt. I'm a bit confused because both tickets say Friday.
B: Let me check. Oh, I'm sorry. Your booking's for tonight's performance.
A: Can I exchange them for tomorrow's show?
B: Yes, I can switch them to Saturday at seven.
A: Do you still have two seats together?
B: Yes, two seats in the second row were released this morning.
A: Great, I'll take those. I had a student discount on the first tickets. Do I pay anything extra?
B: No, the price is the same. Here are your new tickets.
A: Thanks. Can we go directly to our seats at half past six? My friend can't stand in a queue for long.
B: Yes. The doors open at quarter past six. It's usually quiet at half past.
A: That's good to know. I really appreciate your help.
B: You're welcome. Enjoy the show!

Vocabulary: appreciate, confuse, directly, discount, exchange, performance, queue, release, row, switch
