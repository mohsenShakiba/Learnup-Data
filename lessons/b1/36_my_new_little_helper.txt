My New Robot
A: Why is there a little robot under your chair?
B: That's my new cleaner. I turn it on whenever I go out, and it cleans the flat.
A: Does it actually clean well?
B: Yes, it's quite powerful for something so small.
A: How does it know where to go without hitting the furniture?
B: The instructions say it uses a camera and some kind of intelligence to learn the shape of each room.
A: Sounds technical. Is it difficult to use?
B: A bit. The app has lots of features, but I only use the main function to clean the floors.
A: Still, that's a big improvement on your old cleaner.
B: Definitely. The old one was heavy. In comparison, this is easy. I can just press a button on the remote before we leave.
A: Then why are you picking up your shoes and bags first?
B: Because it only cleans effectively if the floor is clear.
A: So you have to tidy up before the cleaner can clean?
B: Exactly. My aim was to do less housework, but now I tidy the floor every day.
A: Your flat really looks better, though.
B: It does. I'm just not sure how much of that is the robot's work.

Vocabulary: aim, comparison, effectively, function, improvement, intelligence, powerful, remote, robot, technical
