My New Robot
A: Why is there a little robot under your chair?
B: That's my new cleaner. It goes around the flat while I'm at work.
A: Does it actually clean well?
B: Yes. It's quite powerful for something so small.
A: And it knows where to go?
B: The box says it uses computer intelligence to learn where everything is.
A: Sounds technical. Was it difficult to get started?
B: A bit. I only use its main function: cleaning the floor. I haven't tried the other features.
A: So it's an improvement on your old cleaner?
B: The old one was heavy. In comparison, this is easy. I just press a button on this remote.
A: Then why are you picking everything up?
B: It only cleans effectively if I move my shoes and bags first.
A: So you have to prepare the room for it?
B: Yes. My aim was to do less housework, but now I tidy the floor every morning.
A: Your flat does look better, though.
B: It does. I'm just not sure how much of that is the robot's work.

Vocabulary: aim, comparison, effectively, function, improvement, intelligence, powerful, remote, robot, technical
