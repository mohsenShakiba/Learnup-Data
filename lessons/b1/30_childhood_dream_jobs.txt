Childhood Dream Jobs
A: What did you want to be when you were a child?
B: My childhood dream was to sing in a famous band.
A: Really? What made you choose that?
B: My uncle was a big influence on me. He sang in a local band, and I really admired him.
A: Did he teach you how to sing?
B: He tried, but unlike him, I had very little talent.
A: Oh, so did you quit right away?
B: No, I was determined to improve, so I kept trying for about a year.
A: What did your family say when they heard you?
B: Everybody pretended that I was doing well, but in reality, I knew I couldn't become a singer.
A: At least they were trying to be kind.
B: True! Anyway, what about you? What did you want to do?
A: I always wanted to be a pilot.
B: That sounds exciting. What changed your mind?
A: My first flight on a plane convinced me that it wasn't the right job for me.
B: Why? What happened?
A: I was too scared to look out of the window, so I spent the whole flight with my eyes closed.

Vocabulary: admire, childhood, convince, determine, influence, pretend, quit, reality, talent, unlike
