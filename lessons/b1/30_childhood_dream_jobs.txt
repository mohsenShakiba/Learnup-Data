Childhood Dream Jobs

A: What did you want to be when you were a child?
B: My childhood dream was to sing in a famous band.
A: A band? What made you choose that?
B: My uncle was a big influence. He sang in a band, and I really admired him.
A: Did he teach you to sing?
B: He tried, but unlike him, I had very little talent.
A: Oh, so you quit?
B: Not right away. I was determined to improve, so I kept trying for about a year.
A: It was probably hard for you.
B: Everybody pretended that I was good, but in reality I knew that I couldn't become a singer.
A: Sorry to hear that.
B: Anyway, what about you?
A: I wanted to be a pilot.
B: That sounds exciting. What changed your mind?
A: My first flight convinced me it wasn't the right job.
B: Why, What happened?
A: I was too scared. I spent the whole flight with my eyes closed.

Vocabulary: admire, childhood, convince, determine, influence, pretend, quit, reality, talent, unlike
