An Old News Story
A: My brother says he can't help us move.
B: Why? What's wrong?
A: He's sent this headline to the family group: "Bridge closed after flood."
B: That's odd. I crossed it this morning.
A: The article claims it's too dangerous to use.
B: We've hardly had any rain. What's the date on it?
A: Oh, it's ten years old!
B: A whole decade? Let me check the town's official travel page.
A: Is there anything about the bridge?
B: Yes. It's currently open.
A: I'll send that link to the group before this spreads.
B: Good. That'll save everyone some unnecessary worry. Ask your brother what time he's coming.
A: He's replied. Now he says his car won't start.
B: I'll pick him up, then.
A: Thanks. I think he was hoping to spend the afternoon playing games.
B: He can do that once we've finished moving.

Vocabulary: claim, currently, decade, flood, hardly, headline, odd, official, spread, unnecessary
