An Old News Story
A: My brother says he can't help us move our boxes today.
B: Why not? What's happened?
A: He's just shared a news article in the family chat. Look at the headline: "River Bridge closed after flood."
B: That's odd. I drove across that bridge an hour ago, and we've hardly had any rain this month.
A: Well, the article claims the road is too dangerous to use.
B: Check the date at the top of the page.
A: Wait, this story is from a decade ago!
B: Did he even read past the title? Your mum's in that chat too, and she'll worry if she sees that.
A: Good point. I should tell everyone before the story spreads to the rest of the family.
B: Here, I've checked the town's official travel page. Send them this link. It shows the bridge is currently open, so it'll save everyone unnecessary worry.
A: Done! I've posted the link and asked my brother what time he's coming over.
B: Let's see what reason he gives next.
A: Oh, he's already replied! Now he says his car won't start.
B: Tell him I'll pick him up in ten minutes, then.
A: Thanks. I think he was hoping to spend the afternoon playing computer games.
B: He can do that once we've finished moving!

Vocabulary: claim, currently, decade, flood, hardly, headline, odd, official, spread, unnecessary
