An Unexpected TV Evening
A: Did you watch that science programme with your daughter last night?
B: Yes. I expected to leave her watching it after five minutes, but I stayed for the whole thing.
A: What made it so interesting?
B: It was educational, of course, but the teacher on the show explained how ordinary things in the kitchen work.
A: What kind of things?
B: They started inside a laboratory, where she mixed some white powder into a glass of warm water.
A: Was that a chemical experiment?
B: Yes, it produced thick blue bubbles that came out of the glass and covered the table.
A: Your daughter must have loved that!
B: She did, though she was hoping the glass would explode.
A: I hope not in a children's show! What else did they test?
B: They heated a dark substance in a pan to show why chocolate burns so easily.
A: I always wonder why mine burns whenever I try to heat it for a cake.
B: My theory was that my pan was too thin, but she said you should heat chocolate over warm water instead.
A: That's actually useful to know. Is the show on again next week?
B: Possibly on Wednesday, if the sports programme doesn't take its place.

Vocabulary: bubble, chemical, educational, explode, laboratory, possibly, powder, substance, theory, wonder
