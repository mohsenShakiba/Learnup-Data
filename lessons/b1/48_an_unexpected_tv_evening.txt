An Unexpected TV Evening
A: Did you watch that science programme with your daughter last night?
B: Yes. I expected to leave her watching it after five minutes, but I ended up staying for the whole thing.
A: Was it really that good?
B: It was great. It was educational, of course, but it felt more like a game show than a school lesson.
A: What were they doing on it?
B: They were in a big laboratory, showing how different substances change when you mix them together.
A: Do you mean chemical reactions?
B: Exactly. In the first one, they added some white powder to a tall glass of clear liquid.
A: And what happened next?
B: Thick blue bubbles came out of the top of the glass, and within seconds they covered the whole table.
A: That sounds fun! Did anything explode?
B: My daughter was hoping something would explode, but it just made a huge mess across the floor.
A: I wonder how long it took them to clean up after that.
B: Funny you say that, because my daughter asked if real scientists have to clean their own tables.
A: That's a very good question. Did the programme answer it?
B: No, they moved straight on to the next experiment, though possibly we'll find out next week.
A: Are the two of you going to watch the next episode together too?
B: Definitely. She wants to see more experiments, and I want to see what happened to that table.

Vocabulary: bubble, chemical, educational, explode, laboratory, liquid, possibly, powder, substance, wonder
