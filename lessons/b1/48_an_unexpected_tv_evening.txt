An Unexpected TV Evening
A: Did you watch that science programme with your daughter last night?
B: Yes. I expected to leave her watching it, but I stayed for the whole thing.
A: Was it that good?
B: Really good. It was educational, but it felt more like a game than a lesson.
A: What were they doing?
B: They were in a laboratory, showing how different substances change when you mix them.
A: You mean chemical reactions?
B: Exactly. First they added a white powder to a clear liquid.
A: And what happened?
B: Bubbles came out of the top of the glass. Then more and more of them covered the table.
A: Did anything explode?
B: No! The people on the show knew what they were doing. We just watched.
A: I wonder how they cleaned up after that.
B: My daughter asked about that too. Do scientists have to clean their own tables?
A: A useful question. Did the programme answer it?
B: No. Possibly in the next episode!
A: Are you going to watch that one together too?
B: Yes. She wants to see more experiments. I want to see what happens to that table.

Vocabulary: bubble, chemical, educational, explode, laboratory, liquid, possibly, powder, substance, wonder
