The Tablet Update
A: This app isn't working properly on my tablet. I've just finished my assignment, and I'm trying to check it, but the app closes by itself every few minutes.
B: Look at the top of your screen. Your tablet needs a software update, and you're still using an old version.
A: Should I do the update now? The assignment's due in fifteen minutes, and if it's late, I'll get a bad grade.
B: Not now! Updates can take twenty minutes. Save your file first. If that app closes before you save, you lose your latest changes.
A: Done, I've saved it. I'll attach the file to an email for my teacher and send it now.
B: Good idea. Then you know your teacher has it on time.
A: There, I've sent it! Can I start the update now?
B: Check your battery first. Oh, it's really low, and the school wifi isn't great in this room.
A: Does that matter?
B: Yes. If you lose your wifi connection during an update, the download stops. Can you wait until you get home?
A: Yes, I'll charge the tablet when I get home and do the update tonight.
B: Good plan. If the app still doesn't work after the update, we can check it together tomorrow.

Vocabulary: assignment, attach, battery, connection, due, file, grade, properly, software, version
