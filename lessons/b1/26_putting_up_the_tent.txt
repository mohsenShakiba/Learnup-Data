My First Camping Trip
A: I've never been camping before. 
B: Really? I think you’ll enjoy it.
A: Can we put our tent here by the lake?
B: On the sand? Let's try the grass by that path. It's higher up.
A: But the view's better here!
B: It's going to rain heavily tonight. It's more sensible to stay on higher ground.
A: All right. I'll get the bags.
B: What's in that giant bag? We're only staying one night.
A: My coffee machine. You told me to bring the essential things.
B: I meant food and warm clothes! There's no electricity here.
A: Oh. I didn't think of that.
B: Are you sure you can survive the morning without the luxury of coffee?
A: To be honest, I'm not sure.
B: There's a cafe in the village. We passed it on the way here.
A: Perfect. Let's have breakfast there.
B: But I brought a pan and some eggs. I was going to fry them for breakfast.
A: Let's have them for lunch instead. I'll check when the cafe opens.
B: After we put the tent up! Come and hold this side.

Vocabulary: essential, fry, giant, heavily, luxury, path, sand, sensible, survive, tent
