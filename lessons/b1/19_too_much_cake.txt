Too Much Cake
A: That looks like an enormous bowl of mixture for one cake!
B: Does it? I weighed the flour carefully and checked every single ingredient on the list.
A: Let me see the recipe. Ah, look at the top. It serves twelve people, and there are only four of us for dinner tonight!
B: Oh, no! I didn't check how many people it was for. What am I going to bake it in? It won't all fit in this cake tin.
A: The recipe says to divide it between two tins. Wait, I have another round tin the same size in the cupboard.
B: Oh, good! Should we bake both and put jam in the middle? A tall cake with two layers will look really impressive.
A: It'll look lovely, although four of us will never eat a cake that big! Why don't we serve one tonight and freeze the other one for next weekend?
B: Can you freeze a cake whole after you bake it?
A: Yes, once it's completely cool. I usually cut mine into slices before I freeze it, because then I can take out just a slice or two when I want some.
B: That's a smart idea. One fresh cake for tonight and another one ready for next week!
A: Here's the second tin. I'll put a little butter inside it for you now.
B: Thanks! As soon as it's ready, I'll pour half the mixture into each tin.

Vocabulary: bake, flour, freeze, impressive, ingredient, layer, mixture, pour, slice, tin, weigh
