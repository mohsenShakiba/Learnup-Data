Too Much Cake
A: That looks like a lot of mixture for one cake.
B: I know. I've weighed the flour carefully and checked every ingredient.
A: How many people is the recipe for?
B: Twelve. I made a bit more just to make sure we have enough.
A: That explains it. What are you going to bake it in?
B: In this tin, but it won't fit.
A: I have another tin the same size. We can make two cakes.
B: And put one on top of the other? Two layers will look impressive.
A: Then we'll still have too much cake. Why don't you freeze one for another day?
B: Can I freeze it whole?
A: Yes, but I usually cut mine into slices first. Then I can take out just what I need.
B: Good idea. One cake for tonight and one for another day.
A: I'll get the other tin.
B: Thanks. I'll pour half the mixture into each one.

Vocabulary: bake, flour, freeze, impressive, ingredient, layer, mixture, pour, slice, tin, weigh
