Another Way to Get There
A: Hello, sorry to ask. The departure board still says "Late" for the ten o'clock train to Milton. Do you know why it hasn't left yet?
B: I'm sorry. We made an announcement a moment ago, although it's very noisy in the station. Another train has broken down on the line ahead, so we're unable to run trains that way this morning.
A: Oh, dear. I really need to get to Milton. My daughter graduates from university today, and her ceremony starts at one o'clock.
B: Don't worry, you'll still get there in time. We've arranged extra buses outside the main entrance.
A: I walked past a bus with "Northbridge" on the front when I came in. Is that the one?
B: Yes. Northbridge is its final destination, but it stops at Milton station on the way.
A: What time does it leave?
B: At half past ten, so in about twenty minutes. The roads are clear, so you should reach Milton within an hour.
A: That's good to hear! Can I use my train ticket on the bus?
B: Yes, just show it to the driver when you get on.
A: Thank you. I'll go and wait outside now.
B: It's quite cold out there, so you can wait indoors in the warm cafe area for ten minutes. There's plenty of space on the bus, so you don't need to hurry.
A: Good idea. I'll call my daughter now and let her know I'm coming by bus.

Vocabulary: ahead, announcement, ceremony, departure, destination, graduate, hurry, indoors, unable, within
