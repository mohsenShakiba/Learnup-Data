Another Way to Get There
A: Why hasn't the ten o'clock train left?
B: We made an announcement about the 10 o'clock train.
A: What happened?
B: A train has broken down on the line ahead. We're unable to run trains that way for now.
A: I need to get to Milton. My daughter graduates today, and the ceremony starts at one.
B: We've arranged a bus outside the main entrance.
A: Is that the one with "Northbridge" on the front?
B: Yes. That's its final destination, but it stops at Milton station first.
A: When does it leave?
B: The next departure is at ten fifteen. You should reach Milton within two hours.
A: Can I use my train ticket?
B: Yes. Just show it to the driver.
A: Thank you. I will wait outside.
B: You can wait indoors for now, no need to hurry.
A: Good. I'll call my daughter and let her know I'm taking the bus.

Vocabulary: ahead, announcement, ceremony, departure, destination, graduate, hurry, indoors, unable, within
