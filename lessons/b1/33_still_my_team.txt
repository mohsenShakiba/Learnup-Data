Still My Team
A: You're wearing your team shirt again. Didn't they lose yesterday?
B: They did. Being a supporter isn't always fun.
A: How did you start supporting them?
B: My dad used to take me to their stadium when I was little, and I've followed them ever since.
A: I hope they were better back then, because yesterday's score was terrible!
B: Don't judge them just by the score. They actually played much better than last week.
A: Even so, that was their third loss in a row.
B: I know, but I really respect their new captain. After the match, he said they need to improve.
A: So what's your prediction for Saturday's game?
B: I think they'll win two to one.
A: On what basis? They haven't won all month!
B: They still have plenty of team spirit, and they kept trying right until the end yesterday.
A: Working hard doesn't necessarily mean they'll win on Saturday.
B: True, I'm just hoping, not promising!
A: Is that your lucky shirt, then?
B: It used to be, but after three losses, I just wear it because it's comfortable.

Vocabulary: basis, captain, judge, loss, necessarily, prediction, respect, spirit, stadium, supporter
