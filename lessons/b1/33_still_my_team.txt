Still My Team
A: You're wearing your team shirt again. Didn't they lose yesterday?
B: They did. Being a supporter isn't always fun.
A: How did you start supporting them?
B: My dad used to take me to their stadium when I was little. I've followed them ever since.
A: They were good then, I hope. Yesterday's score was terrible!
B: Don't judge them just by the score. They played better than last week.
A: But that was their third loss in a row.
B: I know. But I respect their new captain. He admits they need to improve.
A: So what's your prediction for Saturday?
B: They'll win two to one.
A: On what basis?
B: There's still plenty of team spirit. They kept trying right until the end.
A: That doesn't necessarily mean they'll win.
B: I know. I'm hoping, not promising!
A: Is that your lucky shirt, then?
B: It used to be. Now I just wear it because it's comfortable.

Vocabulary: basis, captain, judge, loss, necessarily, prediction, respect, spirit, stadium, supporter
