Planting Seeds
A: Can you help me plant these flower seeds in the garden? I've never grown anything from seeds before.
B: Sure, but it's still quite cold at night. Does the bag say they can go outdoors yet?
A: Oh, it says to start them indoors. I have some empty pots in the kitchen.
B: Let's use those, then. Where's the soil?
A: In this bag. Do I fill each pot right up to the edge?
B: Leave a little space at the top for water.
A: Okay. How deep do I put the seeds?
B: Let me see the instructions. This diagram shows them just below the surface.
A: Like this?
B: Yes, that's right. Now put a little more soil on top.
A: Done. I'll get some water.
B: Be gentle, or you'll move the seeds around. Use this bottle. It has small holes in the top.
A: Thanks. So when can I move the plants into the garden?
B: The bag says to wait until they have a few leaves and the weather is mild.
A: Where can I keep them until then?
B: By the kitchen window. There's plenty of light there.
A: I'll move those books to make some space.
B: All right. I'll carry the pots over.

Vocabulary: diagram, edge, gentle, leaf, mild, outdoors, pot, seed, soil, surface
