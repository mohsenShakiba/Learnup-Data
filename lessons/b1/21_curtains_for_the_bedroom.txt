Curtains for the Bedroom
A: I'm looking for bedroom curtains. I work at night, so I need to sleep during the day.
B: These thick ones block the light well. We have a range of colors.
A: I like the blue. What's it made of?
B: Cotton. Here's a sample you can feel.
A: It's quite rough, Do you have anything smoother?
B: We do, but these will block the light better.
A: Will they make the room totally dark?
B: Let me hold the sample up to the window. There. You can see how much light it blocks.
A: Oh, those look good. I'm not sure about the size, though.
B: Have you measured your window?
A: Yes. I wrote the numbers on this piece of paper. The window goes almost up to the ceiling.
B: Then you need the longer pair. These short ones won't reach the floor.
A: Can I take the sample home before I decide? I'm not sure about the color.
B: Of course. Here's a lighter blue too. Try them both next to the wall.
A: Thanks. I'll bring them back tomorrow.

Vocabulary: block, ceiling, cotton, curtain, range, rough, sample, smooth, though, totally
