After the Job Interview
A: How did the interview go?
B: Quite well. They need someone for their new branch.
A: Is that the one near the station?
B: Yes. The manager said they wanted a candidate with some sales experience.
A: You have that. What did she say about the work?
B: She told me I would be responsible for five other employees.
A: That's a lot of responsibility. Do you feel qualified for it?
B: I think so, but I was honest with her. I said I needed some training.
A: How did she react?
B: She said an experienced colleague would help me for the first month.
A: That sounds good. What about the hours?
B: There's an early shift and a late one. She said I could choose.
A: Did she offer you the job?
B: Not yet. She asked for a reference from my previous manager.
A: Have you contacted him?
B: Yes. He's happy to help. I'll send her his details tonight.
A: And which shift are you going to choose?
B: I wanted the early one to keep my evenings free. Then she told me it started at five.
A: Five in the morning? You complain when I call you at eight!
B: I know. I think I'll ask for the late shift.

Vocabulary: branch, candidate, experienced, honest, previous, qualified, reference, responsibility, responsible, shift
