After the Job Interview
A: How did your job interview go this afternoon?
B: Quite well, I think. It's for that new shop branch inside the station.
A: Oh, nice! Did many people apply for it?
B: Quite a few. The manager said they wanted a candidate with plenty of sales experience.
A: Well, you have that. Is it a normal sales job?
B: Actually, it's a team leader job. As well as the usual shop duties, she told me I would be responsible for five other staff.
A: Have you ever managed a team before?
B: Not really, so I was honest with her. I said I didn't feel completely qualified for that part yet and needed some training.
A: How did she react?
B: Really well. She said an experienced colleague would help me during my first month.
A: That's good to hear. It's a lot more responsibility, so is the pay better too?
B: Yes, much better, and she said I could choose between the early shift and the late one.
A: Did she offer you the job today?
B: Not yet. She asked for a reference from my previous manager first.
A: Have you contacted him?
B: Yes, he's happy to help. I'll send her his details tonight.
A: And which shift are you going to choose?
B: I wanted the early one to keep my evenings free. Then she told me it started at five in the morning, for the first trains!
A: Five in the morning? You complain when I call you at eight!
B: I know! I think I'll ask for the late shift.

Vocabulary: branch, candidate, duty, honest, previous, qualify, reference, responsibility, responsible, shift
