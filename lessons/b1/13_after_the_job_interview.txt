After the Job Interview
A: How did your job interview go this afternoon?
B: Quite well, I think. I liked the manager.
A: Is this for the new branch at the station?
B: Yes. It's a team leader job. The manager said I'd be responsible for five people.
A: Five? That's a lot of responsibility.
B: It is, and I'd still have all the usual shop duties too.
A: At least you know the sales side of it.
B: Yes. I was honest with her about the rest. I said I didn't feel qualified to run a team on my own yet.
A: How did she react?
B: Really well. She said an experienced colleague would work with me for the first month.
A: That's good. Did she offer you the job?
B: Not yet. She has one more candidate to see. She asked for a reference, so I called my previous manager. He's happy to help.
A: Good. What are the hours like?
B: I can choose my shift if I get the job. I wanted the early one until she told me it started at five in the morning!
A: Five? You complain when I call you at eight!
B: I know! I think I'll ask for the late shift.

Vocabulary: branch, candidate, duty, honest, previous, qualify, reference, responsibility, responsible, shift
