Indoor Practice
A: Are you ready for the match this afternoon?
B: Almost, though my shoulder muscle still feels a bit tight from Thursday's practice.
A: Oh, be careful. You don't want to injure it before the game even starts.
B: Don't worry, the doctor said it's fine as long as I warm up properly.
A: Good. Are we still playing on the outdoor court?
B: No, the organizer moved our match to the indoor one because the wind is quite strong today.
A: I'm glad to hear that. It's no fun trying to hit the ball over the net in strong wind.
B: Have you played against our competitors before?
A: Once, last summer. They were very competitive, and one of them was the local youth champion two years ago.
B: Really? Then we'll have to play our best today.
A: We will, and they play fast, so I was out of breath after ten minutes last time.
B: Good thing we're playing as a pair, then. Come on, let's go and warm up before they call our names.

Vocabulary: breath, champion, competitive, competitor, court, indoor, injure, muscle, net, organizer, youth
