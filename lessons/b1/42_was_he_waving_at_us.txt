Was He Waving at Us?
A: Is that man outside the cafe waving at us?
B: I think so. Do you know him?
A: I have no clue who he is, though his face looks a little familiar.
B: Maybe he knows one of your relatives. There's a strong similarity between you and your older brother.
A: That's true, though my brother moved abroad last year.
B: Well, he has a pleasant smile. Why don't you wave back and see if he comes over?
A: No, keep looking at your menu! I don't want to attract his attention if I don't actually know him.
B: Come on, you can just say hello and ask if you've met before.
A: You won't persuade me to walk over there. What if I've never met him?
B: All right, we'll stay at our table.
A: Knowing me, I'll probably remember where I know him from once he's out of sight.
B: Wait, I have another theory. That woman at the table behind us is waving back at him.
A: Oh! So he wasn't waving at us at all?
B: I don't think so. Look, she's getting up to meet him at the door.
A: Well, whatever his name is, I'm very glad I didn't call him over.
B: Me too. Now we can stop investigating strangers and finish our coffee.

Vocabulary: attract, clue, investigate, persuade, pleasant, relative, sight, similarity, theory, whatever
