Was He Waving at Us?
A: Is that man outside the cafe waving at us?
B: I think so. Do you know him?
A: I have no clue who he is, but his face looks familiar.
B: Is he a relative? He looks a bit like you.
A: There is a similarity. But no, all my relatives live abroad.
B: He has a pleasant smile. Let's go and say hello.
A: I don't want to attract his attention if I don't know him.
B: You don't have to know his name to say hello.
A: You won't persuade me to walk over there. What if I've never met him?
B: All right. We'll stay here.
A: I'll probably remember his name once he's out of sight.
B: I have another theory. That woman behind us is waving back.
A: Oh! So he wasn't waving at us?
B: I don't think so. Look, she's getting up to meet him.
A: Well, whatever his name is, I'm glad I didn't call him over.
B: Good. Now we can stop investigating strangers and finish our coffee.

Vocabulary: attract, clue, investigate, persuade, pleasant, relative, sight, similarity, theory, whatever
