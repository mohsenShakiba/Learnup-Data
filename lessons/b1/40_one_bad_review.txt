One Bad Review
A: Have you booked a table for Friday?
B: Not yet. I liked your recommendation, but I've just read a terrible review.
A: Of the fish restaurant? Most people speak highly of it.
B: This person gave it one star. They complained about the lack of choice.
A: What did they want to eat?
B: A burger. They say they specifically asked for one, but the waiter said no.
A: A burger at a fish restaurant? That's an unusual request.
B: They say, "The food is terrible. We left without eating."
A: Then how did they reach a conclusion about the food?
B: Good point. They didn't try it.
A: One review doesn't prove a place is bad. Have you looked at the menu?
B: Yes. There are various fish dishes, and a couple of other things too.
A: Anything you like?
B: Plenty. Despite that review, I think we should go.
A: Good. It's unlikely that we'll have the same problem. We both like fish.
B: You've successfully changed my mind. I'll book it now.
A: Great. What time?
B: Seven. And I'll ask for a table, not a burger.

Vocabulary: conclusion, despite, highly, lack, prove, recommendation, specifically, successfully, unlikely, various
