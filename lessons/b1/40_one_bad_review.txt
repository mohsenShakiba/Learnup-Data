One Bad Review
A: Have you booked our table for Friday yet?
B: Not yet. I was about to book the fish restaurant from your recommendation, but the top review on the page gave it one star.
A: Really? My friends at work speak so highly of that place. What did the person complain about?
B: Hold on, let me read the whole thing. First, they complained about the lack of choice on the menu.
A: Well, it's a small fish restaurant. Don't they just cook the fresh fish that comes in each morning?
B: Yes, the menu has various fish dishes and a couple of other things.
A: Then what did this person want to eat?
B: Wait, they say they specifically asked the waiter for a burger and chips!
A: At a fish restaurant? I'm not surprised the waiter said no.
B: Listen to the last line: "The waiter refused to make us burgers, so we walked out. In conclusion, the food here is terrible."
A: They didn't even eat there! That only proves they went to the wrong restaurant, not that the food is bad.
B: Fair point. I stopped reading as soon as I saw the one star at the top.
A: What do the rest of the reviews look like?
B: Really good, actually. Despite that one review, everyone else loved it, and the photos look amazing.
A: And since we're going there for the fish, it's very unlikely that we'll ask for burgers.
B: True! Give me a second to finish the booking... Done! I've successfully booked a table for two at seven o'clock.

Vocabulary: conclusion, despite, highly, lack, prove, recommendation, specifically, successfully, unlikely, various
