Fixing My Hair
A: Hi. Could you tell me whether you have any appointments today?
B: There's one at two. Do you just need a cut?
A: Yes. I tried to cut my own hair last night. I only meant to cut a tiny bit, but look at it now!
B: Let me see. One side is shorter than the other. We can make both sides the same length.
A: I'm glad you can fix it. My sister's wedding is tomorrow, and I can't go looking like this.
B: Do you want something very different?
A: Nothing fancy. I just don't want to look horrible in the photos.
B: I should warn you: I'll need to take quite a lot off this side.
A: Could you show me how short it will be?
B: Here's a photo of a similar cut. What do you think?
A: I like it, but I'm not sure I'm brave enough to have hair that short.
B: We can leave it longer on top, like in this other photo.
A: Oh, I prefer that one. Do you know how long it will take?
B: About half an hour.
A: Great. I'll take the appointment at two.
B: What's your name?
A: Alex Green. I'm so grateful. I nearly tried to fix it myself this morning.
B: Then I'm glad you came here first!

Vocabulary: appointment, brave, fancy, glad, grateful, horrible, length, tiny, warn, whether
