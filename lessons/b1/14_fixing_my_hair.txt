Fixing My Hair
A: Hi. Could you tell me if you have an appointment free to cut my hair today?
B: I'm free at two o'clock. Do you just need a cut?
A: Yes, please. I tried to cut the front myself last night. I only meant to take a tiny bit off, and look at it now!
B: Let me have a quick look before my next customer arrives. Hmm, one side is quite a bit shorter than the other.
A: My sister's wedding is tomorrow, and I can't go to it like this! I'm so glad you have space today.
B: Don't worry, we can make both sides the same length, but I should warn you: I'll need to take quite a lot off the longer side.
A: Could you show me how short it will be? I don't want anything fancy. I just don't want to look horrible in the wedding photos.
B: Here's a photo of a similar cut. What do you think?
A: I like that cut, but I'm not sure whether I'm brave enough to have hair that short all over.
B: Then we can leave more length on top, like in this second photo.
A: Oh, I prefer that one. Do you know how long the cut will take?
B: About half an hour.
A: Great, I'll take the appointment at two. My name's Alex Green, and I'm so grateful! I nearly tried to fix the other side myself this morning.
B: Then I'm very glad you came in here first! See you at two.

Vocabulary: appointment, brave, fancy, glad, grateful, horrible, length, tiny, warn, whether
