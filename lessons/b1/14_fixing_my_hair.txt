Fixing My Hair
A: Hi. Do you have time to cut my hair today?
B: I'm free at two. What would you like me to do?
A: Look at the front here. I cut it myself last night. I only meant to take a tiny bit off.
B: Ah, I see. This side's quite a bit shorter than the other.
A: My sister's wedding is tomorrow. I'm glad you have an appointment free!
B: I can make both sides the same length. I should warn you, your hair will only come down to here at the front.
A: Could you show me what it would look like? I don't need anything fancy. I just don't want to look horrible in the wedding photos.
B: Here's a photo. See how short it is above her eyes?
A: I like the front. I'm not sure whether I'm brave enough to go that short at the back too.
B: We can leave the back as it is.
A: Oh, good. I'll take the appointment at two, then.
B: Great. Can I have your name?
A: Alex Green. I'm so grateful you can fit me in. I nearly tried to fix the other side myself this morning.
B: I'm glad you came in first! See you at two.

Vocabulary: appointment, brave, fancy, glad, grateful, horrible, length, tiny, warn, whether
