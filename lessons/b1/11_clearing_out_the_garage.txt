Clearing Out the Garage
A: Can you help me clear out the garage? There's so much stuff in here that I can't get the car inside.
B: Sure. What's in these boxes by the door?
A: Those are the art books that my grandfather gave me. Some of them are quite valuable, so I shouldn't leave them out here in the cold.
B: Let's carry them into the house first, then. What about this old computer keyboard?
A: That's the one that I used with my old computer. It still works, so it seems a waste to throw it away.
B: There's a community shop in our neighbourhood that collects old computer equipment for families who need it.
A: Good idea, I'll take it there tomorrow. And what should we do with this wooden table?
B: It looks fairly solid under all this dust.
A: I know, and it's a nice table, but we rarely use it. It takes up too much space out here.
B: Maybe the woman who moved into the flat next door needs a kitchen table.
A: Oh, let's offer it to her! Can you send her a photo?
B: Let's clean the dust off it with a cloth first. Then I'll measure the top so she can check the size.
A: Great. Let's move it against the wall for now, and there'll finally be room for the car.
B: All right, take that end and I'll lift this side.

Vocabulary: dust, fairly, garage, keyboard, measure, neighbourhood, rarely, solid, stuff, valuable, waste
