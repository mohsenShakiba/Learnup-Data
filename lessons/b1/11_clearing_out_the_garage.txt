Clearing Out the Garage
A: Can you help me clear out the garage? There's too much stuff in here.
B: Let's check the boxes to see what's inside first.
A: These boxes have books inside. Some of those books are quite valuable.
B: All right. What about this old keyboard?
A: That's the one that doesn't work. It seems a waste to throw it away.
B: There's a shop in our neighbourhood that collects old equipment. They recycle it.
A: Good idea. I'll take it there. And what about this table?
B: It looks fairly solid. There's just a lot of dust on it.
A: I know, but we rarely use it. It takes up too much space.
B: Maybe the new neighbour needs a table for her flat.
A: Then let's offer it to her. Can you send her a photo?
B: Sure. I'll measure it first, so she can check the size.
A: We can put it by the wall for now. That will leave room for the car.
B: All right. I'll help you move it.

Vocabulary: dust, fairly, garage, keyboard, measure, neighbourhood, rarely, solid, stuff, valuable, waste
