Clearing Out the Garage
A: Can you give me a hand with these boxes? There's no room in here for the car.
B: Sure. Where do you want the ones by the door?
A: In the house, please. They're full of art books that my grandfather gave me. Some are quite valuable. I don't want to leave them in the garage.
B: What about this old keyboard?
A: It still works. It seems a waste to throw it away.
B: There's a shop in our neighbourhood that takes old computer equipment.
A: Good idea. I'll take it there tomorrow. What about this wooden table?
B: It looks fairly solid under all this dust. Do you want to keep it?
A: We rarely use it. Mostly we just leave stuff on it.
B: Maybe the woman who moved into the flat next door needs a kitchen table.
A: Let's offer it to her. Can you send her a photo?
B: Sure. Pass me that cloth first. I'll measure the top too, so she can check the size.
A: Thanks. If we move it against the wall, there'll be room for the car tonight.
B: All right, take that end and I'll lift this side.

Vocabulary: dust, fairly, garage, keyboard, measure, neighbourhood, rarely, solid, stuff, valuable, waste
