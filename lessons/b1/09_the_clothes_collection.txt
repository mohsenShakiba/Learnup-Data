The Clothes Collection
A: Hi, I've brought some winter clothes. Am I in the right hall?
B: Yes, come in! Do you need a hand with those bags?
A: Thanks. I'd like to donate these. I can stay and help for an hour, too.
B: Oh, good. We need another volunteer. Let's sort out your bags first.
A: Here's a warm coat and a pair of leather gloves.
B: Lovely. Can you hang the coat up over there? There's a box for gloves just under it.
A: Sure. I've brought some shirts too. Is the collection mainly for winter coats?
B: We need plenty of shirts as well. Fold them up and put them on this table, please.
A: And what about this little jacket? My cousin grew out of it.
B: That goes in the children's box. Is there room for it on top?
A: Yes, it fits perfectly. Shall I close the box?
B: Yes, please. There's a label on the table. Can you write "Children" on it?
A: Sure. Do you want to keep my empty bags?
B: Yes, thanks. People can use them to take clothes home this afternoon.

Vocabulary: collection, donate, glove, hang, label, leather, mainly, perfectly, plenty, volunteer
