The Clothes Collection
A: Is this the winter clothes collection?
B: Yes. Are you here to donate or volunteer?
A: Both. I've brought two bags, and I can stay for an hour.
B: Great. Let's sort out your bags first.
A: Here's a coat and some leather gloves.
B: Those will be useful. Can you hang the coat up over there?
A: Sure. I have some shirts too. Are you mainly looking for coats?
B: Yes, but we need plenty of shirts too. Fold them and put them on this table, please.
A: And this little jacket?
B: That goes with the children's clothes. Can you make a label for that box?
A: Sure. I'll write "Children" on it.
B: Thanks. Then we can put the empty bags away.
A: Where do you keep them?
B: On the shelf by the door. We can use them again for the next collection.

Vocabulary: collection, donate, glove, hang, label, leather, mainly, plenty, tight, volunteer
