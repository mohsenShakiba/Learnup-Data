The Clothes Collection
A: Hi, is this the hall for the winter clothes collection?
B: Yes, come in! Are you here to donate clothes or volunteer?
A: Both. I've brought two bags from home, and I can stay and help for an hour.
B: Wonderful. Let's sort out your bags first.
A: Here's a warm coat and a pair of leather gloves.
B: Those will be really useful. Can you hang the coat up over there?
A: Sure. I have some shirts too. Are you mainly looking for winter coats?
B: Warm coats first, yes, but we need plenty of shirts as well. Fold them up and put them on this table, please.
A: And what about this little winter jacket? My cousin grew out of it, and it's still clean and warm.
B: Put that in the children's box here. Oh, it fits in on top perfectly! Can you write "Children" on a label for the front of the box?
A: Done. Where should I put my empty bags away?
B: On the shelf by the door. We use extra bags when people come to choose clothes this afternoon.
A: Good idea. Shall we start sorting those big bags by the wall next?
B: Yes, please! People have dropped off a lot this morning.

Vocabulary: collection, donate, glove, hang, label, leather, mainly, perfectly, plenty, volunteer
