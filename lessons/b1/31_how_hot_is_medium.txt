How Hot Is Medium?
A: Why did you order the spicy soup? I thought you disliked hot food.
B: I do, but the menu says this one is only medium, and the red one is much stronger.
A: Well, I saw your reaction when you tasted it. You went very quiet!
B: I didn't want to say anything, but my tongue is still burning.
A: You don't have to suffer just because I like hot food. Going out for dinner is a treat, not a test.
B: I know, but it actually tastes really good for a second, and then it gets painful.
A: Here, take some of my rice and mix a little soup with it.
B: Thanks, let me try that. Oh, that's much better.
A: Good. We can share the rice, and I'll have some of your soup too.
B: Are you sure? It's still quite hot.
A: You don't eat hot food, so obviously it feels strong to you. Let me try a little first.
B: All right, here you are.
A: Oh! Wait, I think I need some rice too.
B: So are we both ordering the mild soup next time?
A: Definitely. And a large bowl of rice, just in case.

Vocabulary: dislike, medium, mix, obviously, painful, reaction, spicy, suffer, tongue, treat
