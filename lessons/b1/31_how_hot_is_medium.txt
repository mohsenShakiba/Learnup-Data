How Hot Is Medium?
A: Why did you order the spicy soup? I thought you disliked hot food.
B: I do, but this is only medium. The menu says the really hot one is much stronger.
A: Have you tried it yet?
B: Just a little. My tongue's still burning.
A: I saw your reaction. You went very quiet!
B: I didn't want you to know how hot it was.
A: You don't have to suffer just because I like hot food. This meal is a treat, not a test.
B: I know. But it tastes good for about a second. Then it gets painful.
A: Do you want some of my rice? You can mix a little soup with it.
B: Thanks. Let me try that. Oh, that's better.
A: Good. We can share the rice, and I'll have some of your soup.
B: Are you sure? It's still quite hot.
A: Yes, but obviously we don't have to finish it. I'll try a little first.
B: All right. Here you are.
A: Oh! I think I need some rice too.
B: So we're both ordering the mild soup next time?
A: Yes. And a large bowl of rice, just in case.

Vocabulary: dislike, medium, mix, obviously, painful, reaction, spicy, suffer, tongue, treat
