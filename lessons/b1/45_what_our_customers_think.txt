What Our Customers Think
A: Have you finished reading the results of our customer questions?
B: Yes, I've just finished the analysis, and I'm putting the main points on one page for tomorrow's meeting.
A: Great. Did a high percentage of customers reply this year?
B: Nearly half of them, which is enough to indicate what people like and what we need to change.
A: Did you notice any clear trends in the answers?
B: Yes. Most consumers still like our products, but younger customers say our packages use too much paper and card.
A: I'm not surprised. Several people have mentioned that to our IT team on the website too.
B: In addition, the statistics show that most orders now come from phones rather than computers.
A: What do you conclude from that?
B: We need to improve our phone app, and therefore I've asked the IT team to join tomorrow's meeting.
A: Good idea. Can you summarize these points in a short message before five o'clock?
B: Sure, I'll send it over as soon as I've checked the final numbers.

Vocabulary: IT, addition, analysis, conclude, consumer, indicate, package, percentage, statistic, summarize, therefore, trend
