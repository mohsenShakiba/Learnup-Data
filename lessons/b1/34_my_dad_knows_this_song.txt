My Dad Knows This Song
A: You know that new song I keep playing? My dad started singing along to it yesterday.
B: Your dad? I thought he hated that band.
A: He does, but it turns out the song is actually an old folk song. He showed me an old recording of it. Here, listen.
B: Oh, it's much slower without the drums.
A: To tell you the truth, I like this one better.
B: Yeah, there's a lot more emotion in the way he sings. Who is the singer?
A: I don't remember his name, but Dad played me a few more of his songs, and he's incredibly talented.
B: Does your dad sing like that too?
A: He tries! When he was younger, he used to entertain everyone at family parties.
B: Was he any good?
A: I have no idea. He says everyone at those parties was usually a bit drunk, so nobody cared.
B: Including him?
A: Probably! Mum says his throat always hurt the next day from singing so loudly.
B: Well, if he knows the song, you should invite him to see the band with you.
A: I actually asked him yesterday, and he bought tickets for both of us this morning.
B: That's very generous of him! So does he like the band now?
A: Not really. He still says they're too loud, but he wants to hear them play his song.

Vocabulary: drum, drunk, emotion, entertain, folk, generous, incredibly, talented, throat, truth
