My Dad Knows This Song
A: You know that new song I keep playing? My dad started singing along yesterday.
B: Your dad? I thought he hated that band.
A: He does! Turns out it's an old folk song. He found me an old recording. Here, listen.
B: Oh, it's much slower without the drums.
A: To tell you the truth, I like it better.
B: Yeah, there's more emotion in the way he sings. Who is he?
A: I'm not sure. Dad played me a few more of his songs. He's incredibly talented.
B: Does your dad sing like that?
A: He tries! He used to entertain everyone at family parties.
B: Was he any good?
A: No idea. He says everyone was usually a bit drunk.
B: Including him?
A: Probably. Mum says his throat always hurt the next day from singing so loudly.
B: Well, you should take him to see the band. He can sing along there.
A: I asked him yesterday. He bought us both tickets this morning.
B: That's generous! So he likes them now?
A: He still says they're too loud. But he wants to go, so I'm taking him.

Vocabulary: drum, drunk, emotion, entertain, folk, generous, incredibly, talented, throat, truth
