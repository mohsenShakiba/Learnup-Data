No Phones at Dinner
A: My mum has announced a ban on phones at the dinner table.
B: Really? Do you agree with her?
A: Yes, I strongly agree with her. We spend most of the week apart. It's nice when everyone's involved in the conversation.
B: That's a new attitude for you. You used to read messages while we ate.
A: I know. I noticed how annoying it was when my brother did it.
B: What does he think of the ban?
A: He has this belief that it's rude if you don't answer a message immediately.
B: But he can answer after dinner, can't he?
A: Exactly. I thought he was going to be the problem. It didn't occur to me that Mum might forget her own rule.
B: What happened?
A: The sudden sound of her phone made everyone stop talking.
B: Did she answer it?
A: Yes. It was my aunt. Mum said she was only going to talk for a minute.
B: Was she?
A: No. My dad gave her a signal to finish the call, but she kept talking.
B: What was so interesting?
A: She was telling my aunt about our new phone ban.
B: So your aunt heard more of the family conversation than anyone at the table!
A: Yes. My brother enjoyed that much more than his messages.
B: I think your mum needs to leave her phone in another room next time.

Vocabulary: announce, apart, attitude, ban, belief, involved, occur, signal, strongly, sudden
