A Walk Through the Old Port
A: I'm glad you suggested walking down to the old port this afternoon.
B: It's a pleasant area now, isn't it? Ten years ago, nobody came here unless they worked on the boats.
A: Really? It's become a popular attraction for visitors since they opened these cafes by the water.
B: Yes, and that historic stone building by the water used to store coal for the ships.
A: Look at the little wooden boat in front of it with the blue flag!
B: My uncle used to be a sailor on a boat like that when he was young.
A: Did he? What do they use that thick rope along the wall for?
B: The boats tie up there when they return for the evening.
A: Wait, what's that shiny round thing between those two stones?
B: Hold on, let me bend down and look... It's an old coin, and there's a white shell next to it.
A: Keep the shell! You can put it on your desk to remind you of the trip.
B: Good idea, and let's get some coffee before we walk back to the station.

Vocabulary: attraction, bend, coal, coin, flag, historic, pleasant, port, rope, sailor, shell
