When a Hobby Becomes Work
A: Are you still working for that engineering company?
B: No. I left last month. I'm unemployed at the moment.
A: Are you looking for another job like that?
B: Maybe. But I'm also thinking about earning a living from photography.
A: You've always loved taking pictures.
B: I have. But doing it in my leisure time is different from doing it for money.
A: What's your next step?
B: I'm taking an advanced photography course. Then I need to promote my work.
A: Do you have a website?
B: Not yet. My sister advised me to build one. She's going to help me with it.
A: That can give you a global audience.
B: True, but I just need a few local customers to start with.
A: My cousin's a travel agent. She needs some new photos of places near here for her website.
B: Really? I've taken hundreds of those.
A: I can ask if she wants to see them. I can't promise anything, though.
B: Thanks. It's nice to have a mental break from writing job applications.
A: You do sound more cheerful when you talk about your pictures.
B: I feel it too. I'll choose a few tonight and send them to you.

Vocabulary: advanced, advise, agent, engineering, global, leisure, living, mental, promote, unemployed
