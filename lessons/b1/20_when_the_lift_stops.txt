When the Lift Stops
A: Hello? Is anyone there? The lift has stopped.
B: Yes. We heard the alarm. I'm the security guard. Are you hurt?
A: No, but I'm alone, and the doors won't open.
B: What floor does the number show?
A: Three. I'm frightened. It's such a small space.
B: My colleague is going to fix it. You can keep talking to me while we wait.
A: There's a small gap between the doors. Can I push them open?
B: Please don't force them. Someone needs to check the lift first for your safety.
A: Okay. I'm feeling really uncomfortable. can you hurry please.
B: We are fixing it right now. Try to breathe slowly.
A: I'm trying, how long will it take?
B: A couple of minutes probably. try to stay calm.
A: My friend is waiting for me downstairs.
B: It won't take long, I promise.

Vocabulary: alarm, breathe, calm, force, frighten, guard, safety, security, uncomfortable
