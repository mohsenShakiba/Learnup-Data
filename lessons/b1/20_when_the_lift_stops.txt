When the Lift Stops
A: Hello? Can anyone hear me? The lift just made a sudden stop between floors!
B: Hello, yes, I can hear you clearly. Your alarm came through to the front desk. I'm the building security guard. Are you hurt?
A: No, I'm fine, although I'm in here alone and the doors won't open.
B: Which floor number is showing on the little screen above the door?
A: It says three. I'm quite frightened. It's such a small space.
B: You're completely safe, I promise. Our repair worker is already in the control room checking the power, and you can keep talking to me while you wait.
A: There's a narrow gap between the doors. Can I push them open?
B: Please don't force them. Wait for the lift to reach the floor properly. That's really important for your safety.
A: Okay, I won't touch them. It's getting warm in here, and I'm feeling really uncomfortable. Will it take much longer?
B: Only another minute or two. Try to breathe slowly.
A: I'm trying. My phone has no signal in here, and my friend is waiting for me downstairs by the main entrance.
B: Try to stay calm. I'm right by the entrance desk, so tell me your friend's name and I'll let them know what's happening.
A: Thank you, it's Maya. Oh, wait! The lights just came back on, and the lift is moving down!
B: Great, the power's back on. The doors will open on the ground floor in a few seconds, and Maya is right here waiting for you.

Vocabulary: alarm, breathe, calm, force, frighten, guard, safety, security, signal, sudden, uncomfortable
