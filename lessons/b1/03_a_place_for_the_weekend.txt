A Place for the Weekend
A: Have you looked at any accommodation for our weekend away?
B: Yes. Look at this cottage in the countryside. Here are some photos.
A: It looks peaceful. How much does it cost to rent?
B: Eighty pounds a night. It has two bedrooms and a private garden.
A: Is the location convenient? We won't have a car.
B: There's a bus, but it only runs twice a day.
A: That won't work. Have you stayed anywhere more central in the city?
B: Yes. I stayed at a small hotel by the station last year.
A: Was it noisy?
B: A little, but my room was at the back.
A: That sounds more suitable. How much is it?
B: It's sixty pounds a night. I've just checked the prices.
A: We can afford that. Let's make a reservation.
B: For Friday and Saturday?
A: Yes, two nights. Can you ask for a room at the back?
B: Of course. I'll book it now.

Vocabulary: accommodation, afford, central, convenient, cottage, countryside, location, peaceful, private, rent, reservation, suitable
