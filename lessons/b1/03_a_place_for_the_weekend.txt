A Place for the Weekend
A: Have you looked at places to stay for our weekend away?
B: Yes, a few. Look at the photos of this cottage in the countryside, just outside town.
A: Oh, it looks so peaceful. How much does it cost to rent?
B: Eighty pounds a night. It has a nice kitchen and its own private garden.
A: With the train tickets as well, that's a bit more than we can afford. And how do we get there without a car?
B: There's a bus from town, but it only runs twice a day.
A: Twice a day isn't very convenient. Have you ever stayed somewhere more central, in town?
B: Yes, I stayed at a small hotel near the station last year. It's in a great location, and you can walk everywhere from there.
A: Was it noisy by the station?
B: A little at the front, but my room was at the back and it was quiet.
A: How much is a room there now?
B: Sixty pounds a night. I've just checked their website too.
A: That's much better. Let's stay there instead.
B: For Friday and Saturday night?
A: Yes, two nights. Can you put a request on the reservation for a room at the back?
B: Of course. I'll book it now.

Vocabulary: afford, central, convenient, cottage, countryside, location, peaceful, private, rent, reservation
