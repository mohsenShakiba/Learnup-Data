Just One More Chapter
A: You're still reading? You said you were going to bed an hour ago.
B: I know, but I keep telling myself, "Just one more chapter."
A: What's the book about?
B: It's about a young woman who moves into an old house. The setting is a quiet little village by the sea.
A: That sounds peaceful.
B: It seemed like a romantic story at first, until she met the ghost in the garden.
A: A ghost? I thought you hated horror stories.
B: I usually do, but this one isn't scary, and I need to know why he's there.
A: Was there a murder in the house?
B: Maybe. The narrative keeps moving between the present and the past, so I'm still trying to work it out.
A: Is the ghost trying to hurt her?
B: Not at all. He was a poet, so he just keeps reading his work to her.
A: Maybe he's just lonely and wants someone to listen.
B: That's what I think! Listen to this quote from the page I'm on: "You are the only person who can hear me."
A: Poor man! So does she stay and listen to him?
B: Not yet. She thinks he's imaginary, so she keeps closing her eyes to make him disappear.
A: Is that working?
B: No, he's still there. And closing the book isn't working for me either, because I keep opening it again.
