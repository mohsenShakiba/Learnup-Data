Just One More Chapter
A: You're still reading? You said you were going to bed an hour ago.
B: I know. I keep telling myself, "Just one more chapter."
A: What's the book about?
B: A young woman moves into an old house. The setting is a small village by the sea.
A: That sounds nice.
B: It seemed romantic at first. Then she met the ghost.
A: A ghost? I thought you disliked horror stories.
B: I do. But I need to know why he's there.
A: Is there a murder to solve?
B: Maybe. The narrative keeps moving between the present and the past, so I'm still working it out.
A: Is the ghost trying to hurt her?
B: No. He's a poet, and he keeps reading to her.
A: Maybe he just wants someone to listen.
B: That's what I think! Listen to this quote: "You are the only person who can hear me."
A: Poor man. So does she listen?
B: She thinks he's imaginary. She keeps closing her eyes to make him disappear.
A: Is it working?
B: No. And closing the book isn't working for me either. I keep opening it again.

Vocabulary: chapter, ghost, horror, imaginary, murder, narrative, poet, quote, romantic, setting
