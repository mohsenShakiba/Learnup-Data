Collecting a Hire Car
A: Hi. I booked a small hire car online for the weekend.
B: Hello. Everything's ready for you. Here's the hire agreement. It's forty pounds for two days, including tax.
A: Great. Do you have a daily limit on miles?
B: Two hundred miles a day.
A: That's more than enough. What's your fuel policy?
B: The car's half full now, so please buy enough fuel to bring it back at the same level.
A: Will I have to pay extra if there's less fuel when I return it?
B: Yes, we'll charge you for the missing fuel, plus ten pounds.
A: All right. Can we check the car outside before I sign the agreement?
B: Of course. Come with me. We'll check it for damage together before you drive away.
A: There's a small mark on the passenger door here.
B: Yes, that mark's on the agreement already.
A: Good. That front tyre looks a bit low, too.
B: You're right, it needs a bit more air. And I'm sorry, there's some dirt on the back seat from this morning.
A: Do we have time for both now?
B: Yes, I'll put air in the tyre and clean the seat before you leave.
A: Thanks. What time do I have to return the car on Sunday?
B: Any time before six. We'll sign the agreement inside as soon as I finish the tyre and the seat.

Vocabulary: agreement, charge, dirt, fuel, hire, limit, plus, policy, tax, tyre
