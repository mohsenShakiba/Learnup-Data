Everything in One Photo
A: Excuse me, can you take a photo of me?
B: Of course. Stand by the river.
A: Please get the whole bridge in the picture too.
B: Okay. I need to step back a little.
A: Can you also get the tall tree on the right?
B: I can, but part of the bridge will not be in the picture.
A: What about the mountain behind me?
B: The mountain is too high. I cannot get everything in one photo.
A: Can you step back more?
B: Yes, but then you will look very small.
A: How small?
B: People will see the bridge, the tree, and the mountain—but not you!
