Everything in One Photo
A: Excuse me, can you take a photo of me?
B: Of course. Do you want the river in the picture?
A: Yes please. Can you get the bridge in the picture too.
B: Okay. It will be a nice picture.
A: Can you also get the tall tree on the right?
B: I can try.
A: What about the mountain behind me?
B: I can't fit everything in the picture.
A: Can you go back a little?
B: Yes, but then you will look very small.
A: You are right. Just take the photo without me.
B: Oh my God. 

Vocabulary: behind, by, high, little, mountain, photo, picture, river, stand, tall
