A Spider in the Bathroom
A: Sam, can you come upstairs?
B: What is wrong?
A: There is a big spider in my bathroom.
B: Where is it?
A: Behind the basket.
B: Oh no. I am afraid of spiders.
A: Me too.
B: What should we do?
A: Let us close the bathroom door.
B: Then I can call my brother.
A: Isn't he at work?
B: Yes but this is more important.

Vocabulary: bathroom, big, brother, call, close, door, he, in, more, of, should, then, there, this, too, upstairs, where
