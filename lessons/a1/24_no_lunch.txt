No Lunch
A: Ben, where is your lunch?
B: My lunch is at home.
A: Can you go home and bring it?
B: No. My home is far away.
A: You can order from the cafe across the street.
B: Good idea. I can order pizza.
A: Will the cafe bring the pizza?
B: Yes. They will bring your order.
A: OK. Then I want a pizza too.
B: Really? What will you do with your lunch?
A: Don't worry. I am hungry.
B: OK. We should each pay twelve dollars.
A: Actually, I want two pizzas.

Vocabulary: across, away, each, far, pay, street
