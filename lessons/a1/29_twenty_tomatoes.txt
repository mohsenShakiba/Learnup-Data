Too Many Tomatoes
A: Why are there so many tomatoes on the table?
B: I ordered them from the supermarket.
A: Did you really need all of these?
B: No! I only wanted a small bag.
A: Look at your order. It says one large box.
B: Oh no. What can we do with all of them?
A: We can make ketchup.
B: Good idea! Is it easy?
A: First, we need to wash and cut all the tomatoes.
B: And then?
A: Then we need to cook them slowly.
B: How long will that take?
A: With this many tomatoes, we will be cooking all night.
B: All night? Then we need a lot of coffee too!

Vocabulary: cook, cut, ketchup, large, order, supermarket, tomato, wash
