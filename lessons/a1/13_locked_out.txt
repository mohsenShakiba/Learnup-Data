Locked Out
A: Sam, are you home?
B: Yes. What happened?
A: I left my keys inside.
B: Can you open a window?
A: No. They are all closed.
B: I am sorry. I do not have a key for your house.
A: That is OK. I do not know what to do.
B: It is cold outside. You can stay at my house tonight.
A: Thank you. We can ask for help tomorrow.
B: Yes. You can get a new key tomorrow.

Vocabulary: all, ask, cold, house, new, open, out, stay, tonight, tomorrow, window
