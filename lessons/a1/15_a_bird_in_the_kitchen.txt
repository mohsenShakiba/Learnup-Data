A Bird in the Kitchen
A: Nora, can you help me?
B: Sure. What is wrong?
A: A small bird is in my kitchen.
B: Is the window open?
A: Yes. It came through the window.
B: Ok let me think.
A: It is on a chair. I can catch it.
B: No, Stop, Do not touch it.
A: What should I do?
B: Turn off the lights make sure the room is dark.
A: Ok, should I wait?
B: Keep waiting until the bird finds the way out.
A: Okay. It is flying out now.
B: Great. Close the window after it leaves.

Vocabulary: chair, dark, fly, keep, light, room, stop, through, turn, until, way
