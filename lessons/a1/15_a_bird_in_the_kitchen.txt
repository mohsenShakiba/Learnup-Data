A Bird in the Kitchen
A: Nora, can you help me?
B: Sure. What is wrong?
A: A small bird is in my kitchen.
B: Is the window open?
A: Yes, but it is behind the chair.
B: Do not touch it.
A: What should I do?
B: Turn off the lights and open the window.
A: Okay. It is flying out now.
B: Great. Close the window after it leaves.

Vocabulary: bird, chair, fly, leave, light, touch, turn
