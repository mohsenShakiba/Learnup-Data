Two Customers Named Alex
A: Why does your coffee cup say "Captain"?
B: Because my name is Alex.
A: I do not understand.
B: There are always a lot of people named Alex here.
A: My name is Alex too!
B: See? When they call Captain, I know the coffee is for me.
A: That is clever. Maybe I need a coffee name too.
B: What name will you use?
A: Doctor.
B: Are you a doctor?
A: No. Are you a captain?

Vocabulary: cup, people, say
