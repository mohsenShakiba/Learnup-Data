Rain on the Balcony
A: Leila, are those your clothes outside?
B: Yes. Why?
A: It is starting to rain.
B: Oh no! I forgot them.
A: I will help you.
B: Thanks. Please put the clothes on the chair in the kitchen.
A: Okay. But the clothes are all wet.
B: I need to check the weather before washing clothes.
B: I hope the weather is better tomorrow.
A: Me too. Then the clothes can dry outside.

Vocabulary: before, better, chair, clothes, dry, kitchen, outside, rain, them, wash, weather, wet
