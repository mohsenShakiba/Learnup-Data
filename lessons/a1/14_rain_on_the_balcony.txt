Rain on the Balcony
A: Leila, are those your clothes outside?
B: Yes. Why?
A: It is raining.
B: Oh no! I forgot the clothes.
A: I will help you.
B: Thanks. Can you put the clothes on the chair in the kitchen?
A: Okay. But the clothes are wet.
B: I should check the weather next time.
B: No problem. It happens sometimes.

Vocabulary: chair, clothes, kitchen, rain, sometimes, those, weather, wet
