Rain on the Balcony
A: Leila, are those your clothes outside?
B: Yes. Why?
A: It is starting to rain.
B: Oh no! I forgot them.
A: I will help you.
B: Thanks. Take the towels, please.
A: Okay. Where is the basket?
B: It is in the kitchen, next to the door.
A: Got it. Everything is dry.
B: Great. Thank you, Sam.

Vocabulary: clothes, dry, everything, rain, them
