Returning a bag

A: Mya, is this your bag?
B: Yes, I lost it.
A: You left it on my desk yesterday.
B: Thank you. You brought my umbrella too.
A: Now do you want to buy me a coffee?
B: Why? Because you returned my bag?
A: Yes, I really need a coffee.
B: Ok, but only a small coffee.

Vocabulary: because, desk, them, yesterday
