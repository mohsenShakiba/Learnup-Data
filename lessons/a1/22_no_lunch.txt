No Lunch
A: Ben, where is your lunch?
B: Oh no. I left it on the kitchen table.
A: Can you go home and get it?
B: No. My home is too far away.
A: You can call the cafe across the street.
B: Good idea. A sandwich is six dollars.
A: Ask them to bring one here.
B: Wait. They only come here for two sandwiches.
A: Then ask for two sandwiches.
B: But I only need one.
A: Well, I have no lunch.
B: Really? Then we can get two.
A: Yes. You pay six dollars, and I pay six.
B: Great. I will call the cafe now.

Vocabulary: across, away, bring, cafe, far, pay, street, well
