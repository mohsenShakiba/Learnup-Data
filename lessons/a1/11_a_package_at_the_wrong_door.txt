A Package at the Wrong Door
A: Hi Sara. I have something for you.
B: Hi Ben. What is it?
A: Your package is at my door.
B: Oh, is it a small brown box?
A: Yes. It says "Sara Ali."
B: Great! It is a gift for my sister.
A: Do you need help with the gift?
B: Yes. I still need wrapping paper.
A: I have green paper with stars.
B: Perfect. You saved the birthday.

Vocabulary: box, brown, gift, green, help, package, paper, perfect, save, star, still, wrap
