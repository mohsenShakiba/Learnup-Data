A Package at the Wrong Door
A: Hi Sara. I think this package is for you. It was at my door.
B: Oh, thank you. I did not see it.
A: The driver left it here this morning.
B: I live next to you, but I was not at home.
A: That is OK. Here you are.
B: Thanks! It is a birthday gift for my sister.
A: Nice. I hope she likes it.
A: Do you need help with the party?
B: No, thank you. My mom and dad will come and help me.
A: That is nice. When is the party?
B: This evening, You can come if you want.
A: Thank you. I will come after work.
B: Great. See you tonight.


Vocabulary: birthday, come, dad, door, driver, evening, here, live, morning, next to, party, sister, tonight
