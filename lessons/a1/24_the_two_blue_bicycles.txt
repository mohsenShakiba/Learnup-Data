The Two Blue Bicycles
A: Sam, my bicycle key does not work.
B: Is that your bicycle?
A: Yes. I left it near this tree.
B: Oh. I moved your bicycle near the shop door.
A: You moved it? Why?
B: A big truck needed the space.
A: Then whose bicycle is this?
B: I do not know. It looks like yours.
A: It even has a small light.
B: Your light is white, not red.
A: You are right. This one is red.
B: Your bicycle is over there.
A: Next time, please tell me when you move it.

Vocabulary: bicycle, red, space, tree, truck
