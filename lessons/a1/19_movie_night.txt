Movie Night
A: Sam, why is the screen black?
B: I do not know. Maybe the cable is not connected.
A: Can you fix the cable?
B: Sure. Let me take a look.
A: Wait. It works now!
B: Great. Shall we start the movie?
A: Wait. Where is the popcorn?
B: Sorry! I was hungry.
A: You ate all the popcorns!
B: Don't worry I can make more.
A: Hurry up then. 

Vocabulary: black, cable, connect, eat, fix, hungry, hurry, movie, night, popcorn
