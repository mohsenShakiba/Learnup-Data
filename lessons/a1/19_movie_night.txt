Movie Night
B: I have a few movies here. Can you choose between them? 
A: This Nolan movie is the best.
B: I know. Now why is the TV not working?
A: Maybe you should take a look.
B: Okay. 
A: It is Still not working.
B: I'm working on it.
A: Wait. It works now!
B: Great.
A: We can start the movie now.
B: Wait. Where is the popcorn?
A: Sorry! I was hungry.
B: You ate all the popcorn!
A: I will make more.

Vocabulary: all, look, maybe, movie, best, still, between, few
