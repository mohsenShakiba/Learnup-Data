Coffee time with alex
A: Why does your coffee cup say "Pilot"?
B: Because my name is Alex.
A: I do not understand.
B: There are always a lot of people named Alex here.
A: My brother's name is Alex too!
B: See? When they call "Pilot", I know the coffee is for me.
A: That is clever. Maybe I need a coffee name too.
B: Yes, you need a strong name.
A: What about "Doctor"?
B: Are you a doctor?
A: No. Are you a pilot?
B: Of course!
A: Well, they don't know that.

Vocabulary: brother, cup, name, people, strong
