The Art Class
A: When does the art class begin?
B: It begins next month, in autumn.
A: Is it for children or adults?
B: It is for adults of all ages.
A: I am not very good at art.
B: That is OK. We can learn together.
A: Do I need to bring anything?
B: We have paint here. Just bring a pencil.
A: What will we draw?
B: We can draw anything: animals, flowers, people, anything you like.

Vocabulary: adult, age, animal, art, autumn, begin, children, draw, flower, learn, month, paint
