A Cat in the Bag
A: Mia, why is your bag moving?
B: Moving? There are only books in it.
A: I can hear something too.
B: Open it slowly.
A: Oh! My cat is in your bag.
B: How did she get there?
A: Your bag was open on the floor.
B: And we could not find her ten minutes ago!
A: She likes warm places.
B: And my bag is a nice bed.
A: She does not want to come out.
B: Come on, Coco. I need my books for class.

Vocabulary: ago, bed, could, floor, hear, place, something
