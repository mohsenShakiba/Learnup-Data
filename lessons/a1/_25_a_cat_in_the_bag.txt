A Cat in the Bag
A: Mia, why is your bag moving?
B: Moving? There are only books inside it.
A: I can hear an animal in there too.
B: Open it slowly.
A: Oh! My cat is in your bag.
B: How did she get there?
A: Your bag was open on the floor in my bedroom.
B: And nobody could find her ten minutes ago!
A: She likes small, dark places.
B: My bag is not a bed, Coco. Please come out.
A: She isn't moving. Maybe she wants to sleep there.
B: Then take her and the books out together.
A: Come on, Coco. You have a real bed below the window.
B: Next time, I'll close my bag.

Vocabulary: ago, animal, bed, bedroom, dark, floor, nobody, out, place
