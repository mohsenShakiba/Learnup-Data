Where Are My Glasses?
A: Mia, can you help me find my glasses?
B: Of course. Where did you have them last?
A: In the front room. I was reading the newspaper.
B: Is this black case yours?
A: Yes, but there is nothing inside it.
B: What about that paper below the table?
A: No. I can read every word on it, so my glasses must be near.
B: Don't move. I can see them now.
A: Where are they?
B: On your head, just above your eyes!
A: Oh! I put them there when I moved my hair.
B: At least you didn't lose them.

Vocabulary: above, below, black, eye, front, hair, head, move, newspaper, nothing, paper, read, room, word
