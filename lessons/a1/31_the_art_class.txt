The Art Class
A: When does the art class begin?
B: It begins next month, in autumn.
A: Is there an age limit?
B: Yes. It's only for adults.
A: What do we learn in this class?
B: We learn to paint and draw with watercolors.
A: Should I bring anything?
B: Yes. A notebook and a pencil.
A: Great. I'm excited to start the class.
