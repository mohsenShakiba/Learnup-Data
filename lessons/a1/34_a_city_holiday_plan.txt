A Spring City Trip
A: Do you want a short holiday in spring?
B: Yes. Where can we go?
A: There is a nice city in the north of the country.
B: Is it far?
A: Not very far. We can take the train.
B: Good. I do not want to drive on a long road.
A: We can meet mear my office and walk to the train station.
B: Great. What can we do in the city?
A: We can see the old meseum and go to the theatre.
B: Nice. I like history.
A: The people are also very friendly.
B: Are there a lot of tourists in the city?
A: Yes both in summer and spring.

Vocabulary: city, country, drive, friendly, history, holiday, north, office, road, spring, summer, theatre, train
