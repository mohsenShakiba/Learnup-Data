A Boring Party
A: Do you know anyone here?
B: Yes. Just my boyfriend over there.
A: Where is he?
B: He is standing by the door.
A: Who is that boy he is talking to?
B: I think that's his friend.
A: You look sad. Are you bored?
B: Yes. this party is so boring.
A: That's true.
B: Let’s go home and relax.
A: Good idea. We can watch a movie.
B: I have a funny movie at home.
A: Great. We can order some food too.
B: That sounds fun.

Vocabulary: bored, boring, boy, boyfriend, fun, funny, he, sound, talk, watch, sad, anyone, true
