A LEGO Show
A: What is your favourite hobby?
B: I usually play games. What about you?
A: I like playing with LEGO.
B: What do you build with LEGO?
A: I’m building a small LEGO town.
B: There is a LEGO show at the museum next week.
A: That's cool. Can we go?
B: Yes. Everyone can go, but we need tickets.
A: Should we ask our parents to come?
B: No. Most people there are students.
A: Then let's go together.
B: I agree. 

Vocabulary: agree, build, cool, everyone, game, hobby, most, our, parent, play, show, student, ticket
