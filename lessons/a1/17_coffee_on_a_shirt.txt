Coffee on a Shirt
A: Oh no! There is coffee on my shirt.
B: On your new white shirt?
A: Yes, and the meeting starts soon.
B: I have an extra shirt in my bag.
A: Really?
B: Yes, but it is bright yellow.
A: Yellow? I do not like yellow.
B: It is the only clean shirt.
A: How much time do we have?
B: Ten minutes.
A: Okay. Please give it to me.
B: Here. I hope it fits.
A: It is short, but okay.
B: You can wear my sweater over it.
A: What color is your sweater?
B: It is red.
A: I can't wear a red sweater in a meeting.
B: Don't worry just go to the meeting.
A: I hate myself now, I want to go home and take a shower.
B: You will go home soon, just relax.

Vocabulary: hate, meeting, red, shirt, short, shower, soon, sweater, wear, white, yellow
