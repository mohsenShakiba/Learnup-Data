Coffee on a Shirt
A: Oh no! There is coffee on my white shirt.
B: You need to change your shirt.
A: Yes. The meeting starts soon.
B: I have another shirt in my bag.
A: Really?
B: Yes, but it's yellow.
A: Yellow? I do not like yellow.
B: It's the only clean shirt.
A: How much time do we have?
B: Ten minutes.
A: Okay. Please give it to me.
B: Here. I hope it's not too small.
A: It's short, but it's okay.
B: You can wear my sweater over it.
A: What color is your sweater?
B: It's red.
A: I can't wear a red sweater to the meeting.
B: It's okay. Just go to the meeting.
A: Fine. I hate this shirt. After the meeting, I will go home and take a shower.
B: Good idea. Now go. The meeting starts soon.

Vocabulary: give, hate, meeting, over, red, shirt, short, shower, soon, sweater, wear, white, yellow
