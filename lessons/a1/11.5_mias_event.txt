Mia's Message

A: Will you come to Mia's house tonight?
B: No, I want to practice for tomorrow's lesson.
A: Please. Everybody will be there.
B: Sorry, but I want to be ready for next year's exam.
A: Okay, what if we go there for just one hour?
B: Just one hour?
A: Yes, and then we can go to the library and study together.
B: Okay, you win. But you have to buy me dinner.
A: Ok, we can stop at your favourite restaurant.

Vocabulary: between, everybody, lesson, library, practice, study, win, year
