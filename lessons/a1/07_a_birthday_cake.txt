A Birthday Cake
A: Hi. Do you have a small cake?
B: Hello. What kind of cake do you want?
A: Chocolate, please. My sister has a birthday party today.
B: I am sorry. I do not have any cakes ready now.
A: Oh no. The party starts at six.
B: I can make a small cake for you.
A: Really? How long does it take?
B: One hour.
A: That is great. Can you write "Happy Birthday, Sara" on it?
B: Yes, of course.
A: What time can I come back?
B: Please come back at five.

Vocabulary: any, back, birthday, course, happy, hello, hour, how, kind, long, make, party, small, take, time, what, write
