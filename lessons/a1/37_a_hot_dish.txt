At the Restaurant Again
A: This restaurant is nice.
B: Yes. You should order the chicken. It is really good.
A: Do you know the menu?
B: Of course.
A: The waiter knows you too.
B: We come here all the time.
A: Who do you come here with?
B: With my business partners.
A: OK.
B: What? Why are you angry?
A: You never spend time with me.
B: Sorry, but I have to work. You know I am busy.
A: We can talk about this later.
B: Sure. Let's get a bottle of water.
A: Are you getting the chicken?
B: Yes, but we need to be quick.
A: Why? Are you busy tonight?
B: No. I have two tickets for a movie tonight.
A: Really?
B: Yes. I want to spend time with you.

Vocabulary: again, angry, bottle, later, menu, never, partner, waiter, who
