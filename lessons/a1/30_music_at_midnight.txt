Music at Midnight
A: Can you hear that music?
B: Yes. Is it coming from the apartment upstairs?
A: It is very loud, and it is midnight.
B: Let us ask them to turn it down.
A: Wait. I know this song.
B: Do you like it?
A: Yes, and I know the band. My brother plays with them.
B: Is your brother upstairs?
A: No. His band is on the radio tonight.
B: Then is the music coming from your radio?
A: Oh! I left it on in the bedroom.
B: So the noise was coming from your home!
A: Sorry. I will turn it off now.

Vocabulary: apartment, band, bedroom, loud, midnight, music, noise, play, radio, song
