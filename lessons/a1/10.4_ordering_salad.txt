At the Restaurant

A: Hey, this restaurant is open!
B: Great. We can eat here. I am very hungry.
A: Where should we sit?
B: I think that table is free.
A: Good. What do you want?
B: I want a small salad and water.
A: I want a big pizza.
B: Wait, I want a pizza too.
A: I knew it.

Vocabulary: eat, food, free, hungry, restaurant, salad, sit, table, water
