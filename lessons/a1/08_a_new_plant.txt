A New Plant
A: Leila, is that a new plant?
B: Yes. I buy my plants at the local market.
A: This plant is beautiful.
B: Thank you. It is easy to care for.
A: Does it need a lot of water?
B: No. I water it once a week.
A: How much is it?
B: The small plants are 5 dollars.
A: I have money for a small plant.
B: You can put one in your garden.

Vocabulary: beautiful, buy, care, dollar, easy, garden, local, lot, market, money, much, once, plant, put, this, water, week
