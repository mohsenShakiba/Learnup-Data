A New Plant
A: Leila, is that a new plant?
B: Yes. I bought it at the local market.
A: It is beautiful.
B: Thank you. It is easy to care for.
A: Does it need a lot of water?
B: No. I water it once a week.
A: How much was it?
B: Twenty dollars.
A: Oh, that is too expensive for me.
B: They have smaller plants for five dollars.
A: I will buy one when I have the money.
B: Yes, and you can put it in your garden.

Vocabulary: beautiful, buy, dollar, easy, garden, local, lot, market, money, much, once, plant, thank, week
