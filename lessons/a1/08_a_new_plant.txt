A New Plant
A: Leila, is that a new plant?
B: Yes. I got it at the market.
A: It is very nice.
B: Thank you. It is easy to care for.
A: Does it need a lot of water?
B: No. I water it once a week.
A: I want one for my balcony. How much was it?
B: Twenty dollars.
A: Oh, that is too expensive for me.
B: They have smaller plants for five dollars.

Vocabulary: balcony, care, dollar, expensive, lot, market, plant, water, week
