Unexpected Guests
A: Maya, why are you cleaning so fast?
B: My family is coming to visit us tonight. 
A: Today? Why didn't you tell me?
B: I'm sorry I forgot. 
A: Are your grandmother and grandfather coming too?
B: Yes, my father and my mother too.
A: When will they arrive?
B: Around 7.
A: Do you have anything for them?
B: Only some tea.
A: Then we need to go shopping.
B: The shop closes at seven.
A: We have time. What do we need?
B: Some fruit, cake, and juice.
A: Okay. You get the cake and juice.
B: Can you get the fruit?
A: Yes. I will meet you at home in fifteen minutes.

Vocabulary: anything, arrive, around, family, father, grandmother, grandfather, mother, shop, some, visit
