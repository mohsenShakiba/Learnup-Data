Unexpected Guests
A: Maya, why are you cleaning so fast?
B: My aunt and uncle are coming.
A: Today?
B: Yes. Please don't be angry.
A: I'm not, but you must tell me if we have guests.
B: Sorry I forgot.
A: Do you have anything for them?
B: Only some tea.
A: Then we need to go shopping.
B: The shop closes at seven.
A: We have time. What do we need?
B: Some fruit, cake, and juice.
A: Okay. You get the cake and juice.
B: Can you get the fruit?
A: Yes. I will meet you at home in fifteen minutes.
B: Perfect. Thank you, Tom.

Vocabulary: angry, anything, aunt, clean, must, shop, some, uncle
