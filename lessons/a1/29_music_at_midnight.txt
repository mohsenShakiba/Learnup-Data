Music at Midnight
A: Can you hear that music?
B: Yes. Is it coming from the apartment upstairs?
A: It is very loud, and it is midnight.
B: Let's ask them to turn it down.
A: Wait. I know this song.
B: So what? I want to sleep.
A: But this is my favourite song.
B: I don't care. Just ask them to turn it down.
A: Okay. I will speak with them.
B: You are back! What took you so long?
A: Sorry. I explained to them that it was midnight.
B: And?
A: There was a party, and the music was great.
B: Oh my God!
A: They also invited me for the next Wednesday.

Vocabulary: apartment, down, hear, midnight, music, song, turn, upstairs, Wednesday
