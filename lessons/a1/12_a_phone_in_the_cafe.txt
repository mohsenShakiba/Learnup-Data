A Phone Under the Table
A: Omar, are you looking for something?
B: Yes, I cannot find my phone.
A: Is it in your bag?
B: No. I looked there.
A: Wait. It is under your table.
B: Oh, thank goodness!
A: You dropped it when you stood up.
B: I need to call my mom.
A: Is the screen okay?
B: Yes. It works. Thanks, Mia.
