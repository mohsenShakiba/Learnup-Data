A Phone Under the Table

B: What? I can't find my phone.
A: It should be in your bag.
B: No, I had it in my hand.
A: I can call your number.
B: I will listen.
A: It is under your table.
B: Yes, but how did this happen?
A: You dropped it when you stood up.
B: I hope it is not broken.
A: Is the screen okay?
B: Yes. It works. Thanks, Mia.

Vocabulary: cannot, hand, happen, listen, number, under
