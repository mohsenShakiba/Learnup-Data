Playing outside
A: Why are you so dirty, what happened to your face?
B: I was riding my bike and I fell to the ground.
A: Show me your face, are you ok?
B: I think I broke one of my tooth.
A: Let me see, it is a piece of your tooth.
B: I'm sorry. It was snowing and I couldn't see.
A: Go clean yourself quickly and we will see a doctor tomorrow.
B: You told me not to go but I didn't listen.
A: It's ok, be careful playing outside during the winter.

Vocabulary: dirty, face, tooth, piece, yourself, play, during, ride, bike, winter, snow, fall
