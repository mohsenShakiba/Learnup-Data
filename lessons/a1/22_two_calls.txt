Two Calls

A: Omar, can I use your phone?
B: Sorry, but I need it too.
A: My phone is at two percent. I need to call my wife.
B: I need to call my mom.
A: Can you call her later?
B: No. My mom needs the hospital's address.
A: Can't she use the map on her phone?
B: No, her phone is old.
A: Fine. Your call is important. You can speak with your mom first.
B: Thank you. I only need one more minute.
A: Okay. Give me the phone after your call.
B: No problem.

Vocabulary: address, hospital, important, later, map, more, old, speak, wife
