Where Are My Glasses?
A: Mia, can you help me find my glasses?
B: Okay. Where did you use them last?
A: I read a book on the chair.
B: Are they under the book?
A: No, they are not there.
B: What about the table?
A: I looked there too.
B: Wait. Do not move.
A: Why? Can you see them?
B: Yes. They are on your head.
A: Oh! I put them there when I used my phone.
B: Good. You have them now.

Vocabulary: glasses, head, move, read, see, use
