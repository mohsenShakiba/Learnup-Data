A Table for Tomorrow
A: Hello. I have a table for two at seven.
B: What name is it under?
A: Maya Green.
B: I see your name, but the date here is tomorrow.
A: Tomorrow? Then I chose the wrong day.
B: I am sorry. We are full tonight.
A: Oh dear. My friend is coming in five minutes.
B: Let me look. There may be a table outside.
A: Thank you. She is new in the city, and this is her first night here.
B: Good news! One person is leaving now.
A: That's great. Is the table outside?
B: Yes, near the green door. You can see the park from there.
A: Perfect. This is our lucky day.

Vocabulary: city, date, day, dear, green, news, person
