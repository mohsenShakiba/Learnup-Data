A School Game
A: What is your favourite hobby?
B: I like to build things with my lego.
A: Are you free after class today?
B: Yes. Why?
A: We have a small game at school.
B: What kind of game.
A: You create something with paper.
B: Is it for students?
A: No, everyone can come. But you have to buy tickets first.
B: That sounds interesting.
A: It is. And since you like to build, you should enjoy it too.
B: Good. What should I build.
A: Well, me and my team are creating a mini version of our school. 
B: Hmm, Maybe we I can design our town museum.
A: That would be really nice.
B: Can I ask my parents to come too? 
A: Yes, Most of the students will come with their parent.


Vocabulary: build, enjoy, everyone, game, hobby, most, museum, our, paper, parent, student, their, thing, ticket, would
