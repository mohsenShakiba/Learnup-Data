Too Many Tomatoes
A: Why are there so many tomatoes on the table?
B: I ordered them from the supermarket.
A: Did you really need all of these?
B: No! I only wanted a small bag.
A: Look at your order. It says one large box.
B: I know. I made a mistake. 
A: This is a mess. Can we send them back?
B: No we can't.But we can make ketchup.
A: Good idea! Is it hard?
B: First, we need to wash and cut all the tomatoes.
A: And then?
B: Then we need to cook them slowly.
A: How long will that take?
B: With this many tomatoes, it will take all night.
A: All night? Then we need a lot of coffee too!

Vocabulary: cook, cut, hard, large, many, mess, send, supermarket
