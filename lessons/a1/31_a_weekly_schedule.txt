The Weekly Schedule
A: Is the gym open on Monday?
B: Yes. It is open from Monday to Friday.
A: What about Saturday and Sunday?
B: It is open on weekends too, but only from 6PM to 10PM.
A: Is it true that the pool is half the price on workdays?
B: Yes, it is. There is also a discount if you come in the morning.
A: So, if I come on Tuesday and Thursday morning, it will be half the price plus the discount.
B: Yes, that is right. Plus, the gym is quieter then.
A: Is the gym busy in the afternoon?
B: Yes, it is often busy.
A: Then I will come in the morning to exercise.
B: That is a good plan.

Vocabulary: afternoon, exercise, Friday, gym, if, Monday, often, plan, pool, price, Saturday, Sunday, Thursday, TRUE, Tuesday, weekend
