The Weekly Schedule
A: When is the gym open?
B: We are open from Monday to Friday. 
A: Is it open on weekends?
B: Yes, it’s open on Saturday and Sunday from 8 AM to 12 PM.
A: When is the gym quiet?
B: It's usually quiet in the morning and it's half the price.
A: Is it busy in the afternoon?
B: Yes, it's often busy.
A: Okay. I will choose Tuesday and Thursday.
B: Great. That's a good exercise plan.

Vocabulary: afternoon, busy, exercise, Friday, gym, Monday, often, plan, price, Saturday, Sunday, Thursday, Tuesday, weekend
