A Boring Party
A: Is your boyfriend at the party?
B: Yes. He is talking to the boy by the door.
A: I thought they were brothers.
B: No. They were both born in the same town, but they are friends.
A: This party is a little boring.
B: I know. I am bored too.
A: Let us go home and relax.
B: Good idea. We can watch a movie.
A: I have a funny movie at home.
B: Great. We can order some food too.
A: That sounds more fun.

Vocabulary: bored, boring, born, boy, boyfriend, fun, funny, he, sound, talk, town, watch
