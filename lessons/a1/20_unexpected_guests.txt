Unexpected Guests
A: Maya, why are you cleaning so fast?
B: My family is coming to visit us tonight. 
A: Today? Why didn't you tell me?
B: Sorry, I forgot.
A: Are your grandmother and grandfather coming too?
B: Yes. My mother and father are coming too.
A: When will they arrive?
B: Around 7.
A: Do you have anything for them?
B: Only some tea.
A: We need to buy something for them.
B: The shop closes at seven.
A: We have time. What do we need?
B: Some fruit, cake, and juice.
A: You get the cake and juice.
B: What about you?
A: I will buy some fruit.

Vocabulary: anything, around, arrive, clean, family, fast, father, grandfather, grandmother, mother, shop, some, tell, them, visit
