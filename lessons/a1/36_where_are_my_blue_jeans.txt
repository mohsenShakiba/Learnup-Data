Where Are My Blue Jeans
A: Mom, Where are my jeans?
B: In the closet, next to your trousers.
A: And my grey jacket?
B: The jacket is behind your coat.
A: What about my hat?
B: It's by the door.
A: No my other hat, the orange one.
B: I think I washed it yesterday.
A: I also can't find my boots.
B: They are under the table.
A: No these are not mine.
B: Well then I don't know.
A: I need to find them, they are my favourite boots.
B: Well maybe you should have put them in the closet.
A: Mom, please.
B: I will help you find them but this is the last time.
A: I love you mom.

Vocabulary: boot, coat, grey, hat, jacket, jeans, love, orange, trousers
