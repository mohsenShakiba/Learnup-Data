Meeting Someone New
A: Hi, are you Tom?
B: Yes. You are Maya, right?
A: Yes. Anna told me about you.
B: She says you work near here.
A: I do. I work at the bookshop.
B: Nice. I come here after work.
A: Do you want a coffee?
B: Sure. I have ten minutes.
A: Great. Let us sit outside.
B: Okay. Nice to meet you, Maya.

Vocabulary: bookshop, coffee, come, meet, minute, right, say, sit, tell, want, work
