A Busy Evening

A: Mia, we close in ten minutes. 
B: Okay. What should I do?
A: The customer at table two says there is too much salt in his meal.
B: Ok, should I change the meal?
A: Yes. Let the customer choose a new meal.
B: Okay. What next?
A: Some customers are asking for drinks.
B: Ok, I will fill their glasses.
A: Also, take this bill to the customer at table two.
B: Okay. What about these dirty dishes?
A: Bring them into the kitchen.
B: Should we wash them?
A: Yes. Wash them, then finish the work. Be quick.
B: Okay. Can we go home after that?

Vocabulary: bill, bring, change, choose, customer, dirty, dish, fill, finish, glass, his, into, meal, quick, salt, say, some, their, them, these, wash
