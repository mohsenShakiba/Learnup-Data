Talking About People
A: Look at that woman.
B: Which woman? The fat woman?
A: You can't just call people fat.
B: Ok, you mean the very large woman.
A: Yes, do see her husband, the person standing next to her.
B: Ok, the tall man with grey dress.
A: He is a scientist, he is very rich.
B: I see and that must be his car.
A: Yes, and she is their daughter.
B: You mean the young girl wearing pink?
A: Yes, she is an actor and she is very famous.
B: Good thing she doesn't look like her mother.
A: I know, but stop laughing they are looking at us now.

Vocabulary: actor, car, daughter, dress, famous, fat, girl, husband, man, mean, must, person, pink, rich, scientist, thing, which, woman, young, laugh
