We Missed the Bus
A: Sam, the bus is gone!
B: What? I thought it came at four fifteen.
A: It came at four.
B: You said we had time.
A: I looked at the wrong time.
B: So it is not my fault.
A: No, but you wanted coffee.
B: Yes, and you agreed.
A: Okay. We both made a mistake.
B: The next bus is in thirty minutes.
A: We both have to be at the airport at five.
B: Let us take a taxi.
A: Okay. We can share the cost.
B: Good. I will call one. We almost missed our flight last time.

Vocabulary: agree, airport, and, both, bus, flight, have to, last, look, our, taxi, think
