We Missed the Bus

A: Sam, we missed the bus.
B: Why? I thought it came at four.
A: No. It came at three.
B: But you said the bus comes at four.
A: I made a mistake. I’m sorry. 
B: When does the next bus come?
A: In about thirty minutes.
B: We should get a coffee then.
A: Sure. We should take a taxi after that.
B: That is a good idea.

Vocabulary: about, after, bus, but, minute, miss, mistake, taxi, then, we, why
