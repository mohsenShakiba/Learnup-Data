At the Doctor
A: Hello. I am here for my flu shot.
B: Is this your first one?
A: Yes. I am a little nervous.
B: Do not worry. It only takes a moment.
A: Does it hurt?
B: It feels like a small pinch.
A: Okay. What do I do now?
B: Please sit down here.
A: I am ready.
B: Great. Let us start.

Vocabulary: doctor, down, feel, little, only
