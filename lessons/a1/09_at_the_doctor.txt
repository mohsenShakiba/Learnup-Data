At the Doctor
A: Hello, doctor. I feel sick.
B: Hello. How can I help you?
A: I have a bad cold. I feel very tired.
B: I am sorry. You should stay at home today.
A: Will I get better soon?
B: It will take a few days.
A: What should I do?
B: Rest, sleep well, eat healthy food, drink a lot, and stay warm at home.

Vocabulary: bad, better, cold, doctor, drink, feel, food, healthy, help, home, lot, rest, sick, sleep, stay, tired, warm, well
