The New Baby
A: My sister sent a message.
B: What does it say?
A: She has a new baby.
B: That is great! Is the baby a boy or a girl?
A: The baby is a boy. His name is Leo.
B: How is your sister?
A: She is well. A nurse is there now.
B: Is this her first child?
A: Yes. She has a son now.
B: Are these your sister's flowers?
A: Yes. I am an aunt now. I am very happy.
B: That is good news.

Vocabulary: aunt, baby, child, flower, message, news, nurse, son
