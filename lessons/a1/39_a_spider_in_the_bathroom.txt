A Spider in the Bathroom
A: Sam, can you come upstairs?
B: What is wrong?
A: There is a big spider in the bathroom.
B: Where is it?
A: On the wall above the toilet.
B: Ok there it is.
A: I saw it when I got out of the bath.
B: It's so small, are you afraid?
A: Yes. You must take it out.
B: Don't worry. It won't hurt you.
A: Please, Be careful!
B: Can you give me a piece of paper?
A: Here you are. I will open the window.
B: It's just an animal with 8 legs!
A: Can we please not talk about its legs?
B: It's outside now, you don't need to be afraid.
Vocabulary: afraid, bath, bathroom, toilet, wall, must, paper, animal
