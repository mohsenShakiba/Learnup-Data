A Book at the Library
A: Nora, are you ready to go?
B: Wait. I need to return this book.
A: Is it due today?
B: Yes. The library closes in five minutes.
A: Then let us go to the return desk now.
B: Is it downstairs?
A: Yes, next to the door.
B: Can we get there in time?
A: Yes. Come on.
B: Okay. Let us go.

Vocabulary: book, desk, downstairs, get, go, library, next, next to, today, wait
