A Table for Tomorrow
A: Hello. I have a table for two at seven.
B: What name is it under?
A: Maya Green.
B: I see your name, but the table is for tomorrow.
A: Tomorrow? I chose the wrong day.
B: I am sorry. We are full tonight.
A: My friend is coming in five minutes.
B: Let me look at the tables outside.
A: Okay. Thank you.
B: Good news. One table is free now.
A: That is great. Is it outside?
B: Yes, and you can see the park from there.
A: Great. The wrong day was good for us.

Vocabulary: choose, free, friend, from, full, news, park
