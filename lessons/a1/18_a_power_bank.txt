A Haircut Before an Interview
A Power Bank
A: Omar, do you have a power bank?
B: Yes, but I need it too.
A: My phone only has two percent.
B: Mine has three percent.
A: I need to call my boss.
B: I need to call my mom.
A: Calling your mom can wait.
B: No. She is waiting for me.
A: My boss is waiting for me too.
B: Fine. We can share it.
A: Can I charge first, please?
B: Okay, but only for five minutes.
A: Deal.

Vocabulary: bank, boss, charge, deal, percent, power
