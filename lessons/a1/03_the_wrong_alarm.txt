The Wrong Time
A: Sam, are you at home?
B: Yes. I am late.
A: Your class starts now.
B: I know. What time is it?
A: It is nine.
B: I need to go.
A: Call your teacher first.
B: Good idea. Thank you.

Vocabulary: at, call, first, go, home, idea, know, late, nine, now, start, teacher, time, what, your
