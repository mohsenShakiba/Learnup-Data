The Wrong Alarm
A: Sam, why are you at home?
B: I missed my class.
A: What happened?
B: I set my alarm for seven p.m.
A: Oh no. What time did you wake up?
B: At ten. Class starts at nine.
A: Did you tell your teacher?
B: I am afraid she will be very upset.
A: Send her a message now.
B: Okay. but she is goind to kill me.

Vocabulary: afraid, alarm, happen, kill, message, miss, send, set, start, time, upset, wake, wrong
