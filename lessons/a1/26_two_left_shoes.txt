Two Left Shoes
B: Is everything okay?
A: I have a problem with these shoes.
B: Are they the wrong size?
A: No. They are both for my left foot.
B: Oh, I am sorry! I will bring another pair.
A: Thank you.
B: Here you are. This pair has a left shoe and a right shoe.
A: Good. But it's the wrong size.
B: You are right. I will bring another pair.
A: Great.
B: Here you are. Try these.
A: But these aren't the same color.
B: I am so sorry. I will get another box.
A: No, thank you. I think I should try another store.

Vocabulary: another, both, box, everything, foot, other, pair, same, shoe, these, try
