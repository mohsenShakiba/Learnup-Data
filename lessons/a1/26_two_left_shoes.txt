Two Left Shoes
A: Excuse me, I have a problem with these shoes.
B: Are they the wrong size?
A: No. They are both for my left foot.
B: Oh, I am sorry! I will bring you another pair.
A: Thank you.
B: Here you are. These are the same size.
A: Good. This pair has one left shoe and one right shoe.
B: Do they fit?
A: Yes, they fit perfectly.
B: Is everything okay now?
A: Not quite. One shoe is black, and the other is brown.
B: Oh no! I will get another box.
A: No, thank you. I think I will try another store.
