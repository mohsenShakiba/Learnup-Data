At the doctor.
A: Hello, Doctor. I don’t feel well.
B: Hello. What is the problem?
A: I have a cold. I feel tired.
B: You should rest at home today.
A: Should I take medicine?
B: Yes. You should also drink water and eat healthy food.
A: Thank you, Doctor.
B: You are welcome.

Vocabulary: also, cold, doctor, drink, eat, feel, food, healthy, medicine, problem, rest, should, tired, well
