The Two Blue Bicycles
A: Sam, my bicycle key does not work.
B: Is that your bicycle?
A: Yes. I left it near this tree.
B: No, yours is across the road. I moved it near the shop.
A: You moved it? Why?
B: A big truck needed this space.
A: Then whose bicycle is this? It looks the same.
B: Look at the light. Yours has a white light, and this one has a red one.
A: You're right. I didn't see that.
B: Are you going for a ride now?
A: Yes. I'm going east to the river, and then west to the park.
B: Be careful on the road, and don't go too fast.
A: I won't. The streets are busy, so I'll be slow and careful.

Vocabulary: bicycle, east, light, road, ride, slow, space, west
