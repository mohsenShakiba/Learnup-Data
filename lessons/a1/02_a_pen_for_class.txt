A Pen for Class
A: Maya, can I borrow a pen?
B: Sorry, I do not have one.
A: Really? I left mine at home.
B: I left mine on the kitchen table.
A: The teacher is coming. 
B: We need to do something fast.
A: What can we do?
B: Maybe we can ask the student behind us.
A: Good idea. Do you know her?
B: No, but I can ask.
A: Please do it quickly.

Vocabulary: ask, borrow, class, home, idea, kitchen, know, left, need, pen, quickly, student, table, teacher
