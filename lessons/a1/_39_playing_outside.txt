Playing Outside
A: Why are you so dirty? What happened to your face?
B: I was riding my bike, and I fell in the snow.
A: Can you move your arms and legs?
B: Yes. My body is fine, but my mouth hurts.
A: Let me see. Is that a piece of your tooth?
B: I think so. I'm sorry. I was riding too fast.
A: You don't need to be sorry. Falls can happen during winter.
B: You told me to ride slowly, but I didn't listen.
A: First, clean yourself. Then we need to see a doctor today.
B: Okay. Next time, I'll be more careful outside.

Vocabulary: arm, bike, body, during, face, fall, leg, mouth, piece, snow, tooth, winter, yourself
