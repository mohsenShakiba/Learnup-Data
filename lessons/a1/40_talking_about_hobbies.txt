Talking About Hobbies

A: What do you do in your free time?
B: I usually read books.
A: Do you ever watch movies?
B: Sometimes. I usually watch movies with my husband.
A: What is your favourite movie?
B: I really enjoyed the new Batman movie.
A: I enjoyed Batman too. I watched the movie twice.
B: What about cooking?
A: I really enjoy cooking too.
B: Really? Is cooking hard?
A: Not really. It's easy when you learn it.
B: Maybe I should cook too.
A: Yes, you can come to my place one day, and we can cook together.
B: Maybe I can meet your children too.

Vocabulary: children, cooking, day, enjoy, ever, place, read, twice
