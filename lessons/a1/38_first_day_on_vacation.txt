On Vacation
A: Do you have our plane tickets?
B: Yes. They are in my bag. The plane will land soon.
A: I am happy. Our vacation can finally start.
B: Me too. I can't believe we are traveling to Italy.
A: Can we go to the beach before going to our hotel?
B: Sure, we can eat something too.
A: Tomorrow we can swim in the sea.
B: Yes, under the warm summer sun.
A: I'm so happy I can cry.
B: I just feel bad for our kids at home.
A: Well I don't. I wish I could leave them home with my parents all the time.
B: You are right, it feels so much better without childrent.

Vocabulary: beach, hotel, land, plane, sea, sun, swim, ticket, travel, vacation, child, children, kid
