On Vacation
A: Will the plane land soon?
B: Yes. We will be in Italy in ten minutes.
A: I am happy. Our vacation can finally start.
B: Me too. I can't believe we are traveling to Italy.
A: Can we go to our hotel before the beach?
B: Sure. We can eat something there too.
A: Tomorrow we can swim in the sea.
B: Yes, under the warm summer sun.
A: I'm so happy I could cry.
B: I feel a little bad for our kids at home.
A: Don’t worry. They are happy with my parents.
B: You are right. I feel better now.
A: They are having fun without us.

Vocabulary: bad, beach, before, better, hotel, kid, land, plane, sea, sun, swim, travel, vacation, without
