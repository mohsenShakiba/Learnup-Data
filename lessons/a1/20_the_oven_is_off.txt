The Oven Is Off
A: Leila, is the pizza ready?
B: Not yet. It needs five more minutes.
A: Wait. The oven is not warm.
B: What? I thought you turned it on.
A: I thought you did.
B: Oh no. My cousin is here in ten minutes.
A: The pizza will not be ready.
B: Do we have bread?
A: Yes. We can make sandwiches.
B: Good. I will get the bread.
A: I will make some drinks.
B: That will be fine.

Vocabulary: bread, cousin, drink, oven, pizza, sandwich, warm
