Talking About People
A: Look at that woman.
B: Which woman? Do you mean the woman in the blue dress?
A: Yes. Do you see the man next to her? He is her husband.
B: OK, the tall man in grey?
A: Yes. He is a scientist, and he is very rich.
B: I see. Is that his car?
A: Yes, it is. And she is their daughter.
B: You mean the young girl in pink?
A: Yes. She is an actor, and she is very famous.
B: She looks like her mother.
A: Yes, she does. They are looking at us now.

Vocabulary: actor, blue, car, daughter, dress, famous, girl, husband, man, mean, pink, rich, scientist, which, woman, young
