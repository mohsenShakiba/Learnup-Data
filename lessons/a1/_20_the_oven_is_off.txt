The Oven Is Off
B: Tom sent a message. They will be here in ten minutes.
A: Did you answer him?
B: Not yet. Is the pizza ready?
A: No, but it needs to keep cooking until seven.
B: Cooking? The oven isn't hot.
A: What? Oh no. It's off!
B: Then the pizza won't be ready when they arrive.
A: That's okay. The sandwiches are ready, and the fridge is full. We can make a few more.
B: Good. They always enjoy your sandwiches.
A: You answer Tom while I make them.
B: Okay. We can eat the pizza later.

Vocabulary: answer, cooking, enjoy, full, hot, message, until
