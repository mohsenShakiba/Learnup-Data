The Oven Is Off
B: Tom sent a message, he says they will be here in 10 minutes.
A: Ok, is the pizza ready?
B: Not yet. It needs five more minutes.
A: Are you sure? The oven is cold.
B: What, I forgot to turn it on.
A: We can order some pizza.
B: No wait I have a better idea.
A: What are you thinking?
B: Do we have bread?
A: Yes.
B: Good. We can make sandwiches.
A: But I wanted pizza!

Vocabulary: send, message
