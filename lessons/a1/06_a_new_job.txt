A New Job
A: When does your sister start her new job?
B: Tomorrow at seven in the morning.
A: Seven? That is early.
B: Yes. She is excited, but she has to wake up before six.
A: Does she usually wake up that early?
B: No. She likes to sleep late.
A: Then she will be tired tomorrow.
B: I know. Her first day will be busy.
A: She needs to sleep early tonight.
B: Yes. I will tell her.

Vocabulary: before, busy, excited, have to, job, late, morning, person, sister, sleep, tired, tomorrow, usually
