A New Job
A: Maya, when does your sister start her new job?
B: Tomorrow at seven in the morning.
A: Seven? That is early.
B: Yes. She is not a morning person.
A: What time does she usually get up?
B: At nine.
A: Then she needs an alarm.
B: I know. I will call her at six.
A: Maybe she needs two alarms.
B: Good idea. I will tell her.
