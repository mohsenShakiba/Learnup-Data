A New Job
B: Maya starts her new job tomorrow.
A: When does it start?
B: It start at seven in the morning.
A: Does she usually get up early?
B: No. She always gets up late.
A: Does she sleep late?
B: Yes. She sleeps late every night.
A: Where is her new job?
B: It is near the park.
A: Great. she can walk there.

Vocabulary: always, early, every, get up, job, late, morning, near, new, park, sleep, usually, walk
