A Meal at the Hotel
A: This hotel is nice.
B: Yes. I am happy we can stay here tonight.
A: Are you hungry?
B: A little, but I am more thirsty.
A: There is a restaurant downstairs.
B: Good. I do not want to go out again.
A: Me too. I do not want to travel more today.
B: We can have a small meal here.
A: Waiter, can we have some drinks and food, please?
B: This table is good. I like it.
A: How was your day?
B: Long, but nice. I liked the old street near the river.
A: Me too. I want to walk there again tomorrow.
B: Yes, but not too early.
A: Okay, later in the morning.
B: Great. Can you pay the bill tonight?
A: Yes. I have my card.
B: Thank you. I can pay tomorrow.
A: That is OK. This is a good first night.
B: It is. I think this vacation will be fun.

Vocabulary: bill, card, hotel, later, meal, restaurant, thirsty, travel, vacation, waiter
