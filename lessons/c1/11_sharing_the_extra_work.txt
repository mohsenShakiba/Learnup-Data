Sharing the Extra Work
A: Could we go through my task list before you leave the office? I've been dealing with Sam's orders this week, and I'm falling behind with my own reports.
B: Of course. You looked a bit overwhelmed yesterday. How much of your day are his orders taking?
A: Most of the morning. I thought I could manage, but I'm getting twice as many customer queries as I expected.
B: Is there anything we could delegate to Leo? He's been asking for more responsibility.
A: Ordinary orders, yes. He still needs supervision when a customer asks for a different price.
B: Could he pass those calls to me? I'm at my desk most of the day.
A: That would take a real burden off me. Is there any scope to move Friday's report to Monday as well?
B: I'll check with my supervisor. She needs the sales figures on Friday, but we might be able to postpone the rest.
A: That would give me time to check the figures. I don't want to rush them and miss something.
B: Agreed. Have you shown Leo where Sam keeps the order records?
A: Yes, we went through them yesterday. He did the last three orders himself. I'd be happy to let him carry on, if you're there for the difficult calls.
B: I will be. He's competent, and he knows when to ask for help.
A: Thanks for being so supportive. I was worried this would sound like I didn't want to help Sam.
B: We gave you too much to do. I'll speak to Leo now and let you know about the report this afternoon.

Vocabulary: burden, competent, delegate, overwhelm, postpone, query, scope, supervision, supervisor, supportive
