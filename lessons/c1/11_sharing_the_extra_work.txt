Sharing the Extra Work
A: Could we go through my task list before you leave the office? I've been dealing with Sam's orders this week, and I'm falling behind with my own reports.
B: Of course. You looked a bit overwhelmed yesterday. How much of your day are his orders taking?
A: Most of the morning. I thought I could manage during his absence, but there seem to be more customer queries than I expected.
B: We might want to delegate some of those calls to Leo. He's been asking for more responsibility.
A: I'm not entirely convinced he's ready to handle complaints on his own. He still needs supervision when a customer asks for a different price.
B: He's competent with ordinary orders, though. Could he take those and pass any complaints to me?
A: Yes, that would help. Is there any scope to move Friday's report to Monday as well?
B: I'll check with my supervisor. She needs the sales figures on Friday, but she may be happy to wait for the rest.
A: Even that would take a real burden off me. I don't want to rush the figures and miss something.
B: Agreed. Have you shown Leo where Sam keeps the order records?
A: Yes, we went through them yesterday. I'm happy to answer his questions, but checking every call would take almost as long as doing them myself.
B: Then perhaps we could give him a little more autonomy with those orders. I'll stay nearby for anything he can't resolve.
A: That could work. I appreciate you being supportive; I was worried this would look like I wasn't willing to help.
B: Your willingness isn't in doubt. We just gave you too much at once. I'll speak to Leo now and confirm the report deadline this afternoon.

Vocabulary: absence, autonomy, burden, competent, delegate, overwhelm, query, scope, supervision, supervisor, supportive, willingness
