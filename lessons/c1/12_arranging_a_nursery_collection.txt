Arranging a Nursery Collection
A: Before I take Ella home, could I ask about tomorrow? I'm working late. Can my sister pick her up?
B: Certainly. Is your sister already on Ella's list of approved contacts?
A: No. The nursery app says you need parental consent. Is verbal consent enough since I'm here in person?
B: We do need it in writing so whoever's here tomorrow can check. It's a straightforward form. Could you put her full name and phone number here?
A: Right. Where it says "documentation", do I put my details or hers?
B: Hers. What will she bring for identification? A passport would be fine.
A: She'll bring that, then. She got married recently, so I'll check which name is on it.
B: Good idea. The name on the form needs to match.
A: And what if her train's delayed? Could my brother come instead?
B: Yes. You can authorize him through the app if plans change.
A: Will I know when he's been approved? I don't want him coming all the way here if he can't take her home.
B: You'll get a confirmation message in the app. If it hasn't come through by the time he needs to leave, ring the nursery and ask for the administrator. She can check it for you.
A: Thanks. I'll call my sister now about the name on her passport.
B: Take your time. I'll get Ella for you in the meantime.

Vocabulary: administrator, authorize, confirmation, documentation, identification, meantime, nursery, parental, straightforward, verbal
