The Receipt for the Staff Meeting
A: Can you help me with this expense form? I bought the tea and cake for tomorrow's staff meeting, but my lunch is on the same receipt.
B: That's fine. The guidelines say you can use the receipt as long as you only claim what you bought for work.
A: I've already put in the full amount. Can I adjust it here?
B: Yes, right there. Put in the total for the tea and cake.
A: Do you need the paper receipt, or can I just take a photo?
B: A photo's sufficient. Add it as an attachment, with a note to clarify why the totals are different.
A: Right. It also says "Obtain prior approval." Maya asked me in person. Does that count?
B: Ask her to confirm it by email. That's the usual procedure when there's nothing in writing.
A: Now it wants me to justify the expense. Do I need to write much?
B: Just put "Tea and cake for the staff meeting." That's enough.
A: Thanks. I'll ask Maya before she goes.

Vocabulary: adjust, attachment, clarify, expense, guideline, justify, obtain, prior, procedure, sufficient
