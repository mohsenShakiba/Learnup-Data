The Receipt for the Staff Meeting
A: Have you filled in one of these online expense forms before? I bought the tea and cake for tomorrow's staff meeting, and the cafe put my cheese sandwich on the same receipt.
B: Don't tell me you're trying to make the company pay for your lunch.
A: No! I uploaded the photo as an attachment and typed in the full total before I noticed. Can I still adjust the amount?
B: Yes, just click on the total and take your sandwich off. Then add a quick note at the bottom to clarify why the figure you're claiming doesn't match the receipt.
A: Oh, good. Will finance still ask for the paper receipt, or is a clear photo sufficient?
B: A photo is fine. Under the new guidelines, staff only have to keep paper receipts for travel and hotels.
A: I'm nearly done, then. Hang on—this box says I needed to obtain prior approval from a manager, and Maya only asked me in the kitchen this morning.
B: Normal procedure is to have something in writing, so ask Maya to send you a quick email before you send the form off.
A: Right, I'll message her now. One last thing—where it asks me to justify the cost, how much detail do they want?
B: One line is plenty. Just write "Tea and cake for Friday's staff meeting"—accountants hate blank boxes, that's all.
A: Fair enough. I'll catch Maya before she goes home.

Vocabulary: adjust, attachment, clarify, expense, guideline, justify, obtain, prior, procedure, sufficient
