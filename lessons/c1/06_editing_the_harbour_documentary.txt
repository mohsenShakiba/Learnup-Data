Editing the Harbour Documentary
A: Can I hear the bit of the harbour documentary you've been working on?
B: Pull up a chair. Listen to the start of the captain's interview. Can you hear that low noise when he stops talking?
A: Just about. Was the wind hitting the window again?
B: It was the fridge behind the counter. Before I put a filter on the track, you could barely hear him.
A: I can hear every word now. I'd read the transcript, but it's the way he says his dad's name that gets me.
B: That's what I'd like to open with. Do we need any commentary, though? You can't really see which boat he's talking about.
A: Play me the first few seconds again. What's on screen when he starts talking?
B: You get a glimpse of the boat as he points to it, but that's all the footage we have of it.
A: There's a photo in the town archive of him and his dad on that boat. You can see the whole fishing fleet behind them.
B: That would work. Who owns the copyright? I'd love to use it in the trailer too.
A: The museum does. They emailed permission for the film and the trailer this morning. The captain's signed the consent form too.
B: Good. Let's try it with the photo and see how it looks.
