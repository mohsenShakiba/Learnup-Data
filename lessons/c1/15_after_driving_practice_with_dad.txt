After Driving Practice with Dad
A: Dad, now that I've parked, do me a favour. Open your door all the way.
B: Why? I can see that wall from here.
A: Go on.
B: Oh. There's room for another car there.
A: Exactly. I was halfway through reversing when you yelled my name. That man walking his dog? He picked it up.
B: Did he? From this seat it looked like we were about to hit the wall.
A: And what am I supposed to do with "Anna!" anyway? Stop? Steer the other way?
B: Stop, I suppose. Sorry, love. It was pure instinct.
A: What about at the junction? I'm waiting for a gap and you go, "Are we planning to live here?"
B: I was trying to make you laugh. Oh, listen to me. That's my dad.
A: What is?
B: "Are we planning to live here?" Word for word. He'd say it and I'd tighten my grip on the wheel and wish he'd get out and walk.
A: I came pretty close to asking you to. I couldn't see past that bus, and the idiot behind me had his hand on the horn.
B: Hang on. You couldn't see past it? Then you were right to wait. I wish I'd said that at the time.
A: So do I. Look, I've booked a lesson with the driving school for Saturday. I didn't quite know how to tell you.
B: Oh. Are we still going for breakfast after?
A: Of course we are. I just want you to drive us there for a change.

Vocabulary: grip, halfway, horn, idiot, instinct, junction, reverse, steer, tighten, yell
