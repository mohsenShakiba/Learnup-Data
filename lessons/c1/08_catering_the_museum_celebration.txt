Catering the Museum Celebration
A: Have we heard back from the museum? I need to know how much food to pack for tomorrow.
B: Just checked my messages. We're catering for a hundred and ten. Sixty want chicken and the rest want fish.
A: Good, that's what we bought. How did the apple cake for the dairy-free guests turn out?
B: Try a slice. I used oil as a substitute for butter this time.
A: The texture's lovely. There's a hint of orange, too. What's that from?
B: The dried fruit. I soaked it in orange juice overnight.
A: It's superb. I'd keep the fruit, but cut the apple into smaller chunks. My slice fell apart when I picked it up.
B: Mine did too. Could you squeeze a couple of oranges for tomorrow's cakes while I weigh out the fruit?
A: Sure. Are all these apples going in the cake?
B: No, the farm had a surplus and offered us an extra box. I thought we could put some out for people who don't want cake.
A: There's room for a bowl on the table by the windows. Here, look at the seating layout. Shall we put the cake there too?
B: Yes. And let's pack the little plates in case tomorrow's cake falls apart too.

Vocabulary: cater, chunk, hint, layout, soak, squeeze, substitute, superb, surplus, texture
