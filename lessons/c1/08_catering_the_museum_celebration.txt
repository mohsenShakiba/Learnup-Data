Catering the Museum Celebration
A: Have we heard back from the museum manager with the final guest numbers for tomorrow's celebration?
B: Just checked my messages—yes, we're catering for a hundred and ten people in total. Sixty guests ordered the herb chicken, and the rest the baked fish.
A: Great, that matches what we bought at the market. What about the chocolate cake for the dairy-free guests?
B: Already in the oven. I used vegetable oil and rice milk as a substitute for the butter, and the cake still has a lovely light texture.
A: Did you soak the dried fruit in orange juice overnight like last time?
B: I did, and the warm lemon blend smells superb. Once the cakes cool, I'll whip the egg whites for the top right before we pack the boxes.
A: Good plan. Have you looked at the seating layout they sent for the main hall?
B: Yes—some tables seat eight people, others ten. The round tables fit neatly along the windows, whereas the long ones leave very little room by the kitchen door.
A: Let's ask them to change those two long ones for round ones if they can.
B: They mentioned changing the plan this morning, and I told them that if they wanted to do so, we needed the final map by four today.
A: Fair enough. Bringing a wider array of fruit plates for the welcome table too?
B: Might do, since the local farm sent an extra box of apples courtesy of the farmer. Any surplus portions can go home with our kitchen staff afterwards.
A: Nothing goes to waste with our team! Let's label the cold boxes before the delivery driver gets here.

Vocabulary: array, blend, cater, courtesy, layout, soak, substitute, superb, surplus, texture, whip
