After the Overnight Power Surge
A: Did last night's storm cause trouble here at the office? I saw the lights go out across the street.
B: We had a power surge just before two. It cut the power to the back room.
A: Is that why you came in so early?
B: Yes. I got an alert on my phone that the cooling equipment had stopped. It was already pretty warm in there when I arrived.
A: Is the hardware all right?
B: So far, yes. We plugged the cooling equipment in across the hall, and the temperature's starting to stabilize.
A: What about the orders that came in overnight? Will I need to ring any customers?
B: No. The backup battery kept our computers running. I checked the log this morning to verify the payments. Every transaction went through.
A: Good. Can I use the back room to print the orders, then?
B: Use my printer. The contractor found a damaged cable by the door. He's coming back at ten to replace it.
A: Does everyone know to stay out? The rest of the staff will be here in a minute.
B: I've put a sign on the door. Should anyone ask, they can print up here until the contractor gives us the all clear.

Vocabulary: alert, backup, contractor, hardware, log, plug, stabilize, surge, transaction, verify
