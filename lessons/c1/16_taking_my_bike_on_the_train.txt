Taking My Bike on the Train
A: Morning. Is this the eight forty to York? Where do I get on with my bike?
B: It is. Have you booked a space for it?
A: Oh, do I need to? I took it on the train last Sunday and nobody asked.
B: There are fewer restrictions on Sundays. At peak times, it's compulsory to reserve a space.
A: Could I leave it there by the door? I'm only going two stops.
B: We need to keep that clear for disabled passengers. The two dedicated bike spaces are further down, but they're both booked.
A: Ah, okay. Will my ticket still be valid on the next train?
B: Yes. The next one departs at nine ten.
A: And is there an extra fare for the bike?
B: No. I'd book now, though. That one can get busy.
A: Can you see if there's any space left?
B: I can only see the bookings for this train. You can check availability via the website or at the ticket office.
A: Thanks. Where's the ticket office?
B: Just past the stairs, on your left.

Vocabulary: availability, compulsory, dedicated, depart, fare, peak, reserve, restriction, valid, via
