Renewing the Rental Agreement
A: Did you get a letter from the landlord too?
B: Yes, I just opened mine on my way through the lobby. Is yours the rental agreement for next year?
A: It is. I was hoping to renew when my contract expires next month, but that new monthly maintenance charge took me by surprise.
B: Me too. Eighty pounds a month? Calling that a "minor update" is a bit misleading.
A: Frankly, I'd like to know what we're paying for. The heating in here barely worked all winter, and the lift still stops between floors.
B: I think he's counting on us paying it. Who wants to move at the moment?
A: Well, I'm not signing it like this. Look at page two—he's included a garden fee too, when we don't even get a key to the garden.
B: Oh, good catch. Maybe he uses the same form for everyone and just overlooked that bit.
A: Should we write back together and ask for a full breakdown of the figures before we sign anything?
B: Definitely. Once the other tenants get home from work, we can ask if they want to add their names too.
A: Good idea. I'll type up a short email tonight and send it to you to check first.
B: Thanks. Knock on my door if you want a hand with it.

Vocabulary: breakdown, expire, frankly, landlord, lobby, maintenance, misleading, overlook, renew, rental, tenant
