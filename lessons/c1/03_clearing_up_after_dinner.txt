Clearing Up After Dinner
A: Hey, there you are! Have you really washed almost all the dinner plates on your own?
B: I just needed five quiet minutes, honestly. I wandered in here with our plates to get some water, saw the pile on the counter, and started washing.
A: Was my family too much? Every time I glanced across the table, my dad was in the middle of another ten-minute story.
B: Not at all—they're lovely! I just lost the thread of his camping story once your brother joined in, so I kept smiling and nodding at the right moments.
A: Half of us lose track of that story too, and we were actually on the trip. Still, Mum noticed you clearing the plates—she leaned over to me in the hall just now and said how thoughtful you were.
B: Oh, great. She's going to expect me to wash up every time I visit now, isn't she?
A: Probably! You didn't have to hide in here, though.
B: I wasn't hiding—it was just a relief to have something useful to do. You always feel a bit like an outsider when everyone else has known each other for years.
A: You did really well. I even saw my dad grin when you asked for a second slice of his chocolate cake.
B: That cake was the best part of the meal. Please tell me there's a bit left.
A: Few slices ever survive with my brother around—hang on, let me check the cake tin by the fridge... Yes! He missed a piece.
B: Now that is good news. Pass me two forks before anyone else comes in, and I reckon we can finish it right here.

Vocabulary: counter, glance, grin, lean, nod, outsider, reckon, relief, thoughtful, thread, wander
