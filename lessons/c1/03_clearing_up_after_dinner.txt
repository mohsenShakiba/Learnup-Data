Clearing Up After Dinner
A: Everyone left in time to catch the last bus. What was that big sigh for?
B: Look at the kitchen counter. It's complete chaos in here, and I really don't want to come down to eight people's dirty plates in the morning.
A: It won't take long if we do it together. You start washing the plates, and I'll put away the food that's left and get rid of these empty bottles.
B: Hang on—each of our friends brought something to drink, so two of those bottles haven't even been opened yet.
A: Good catch. I'll put those two in the cupboard, then. Is there any soup left in the big pot, or did everyone finish it?
B: Everybody had a good appetite tonight. Every bowl came back empty, almost all the bread went, and there's very little soup left in here—just about one portion at the bottom.
A: Save that for your lunch tomorrow. What did you do differently with it tonight, by the way? Even Sam asked for the recipe, and he usually skips soup.
B: I cooked some fresh herbs from the garden with the onions first, and then I blended half the carrots to make it thicker.
A: So that's why it tasted so rich. Did you add butter at the end too?
B: Just a bit—I stirred that in right before we sat down. Honestly, it was a relief when people asked for more, because I wasn't sure vegetable soup would feel special enough for Saturday night.
A: Nobody had any complaints, trust me. There are still a few slices of chocolate cake on the dish, though, so I'll wrap those up for tomorrow.
B: Leave a little space on the middle shelf for the milk bottle too. Why did I make a full pot of coffee after the cake? Very few people drink strong coffee at ten at night.
A: Pour it away, and clear those bits of food out of the sink first so they don't block the drain.
B: I've just cleared them out. Could you bring the tall glasses through from the other room? I'll wash those while the water's still hot.
A: Sure, I'll go and get them. Oh, when I glanced into the hall just now, I saw a dark grey scarf on the chair by the door.
B: That belongs to Clara. She took it off when she arrived because the flat was so warm.
A: I'll message her in a minute so she doesn't worry about where she left it. Can we leave the big pan in warm water overnight?
B: Definitely—that pan can wait till morning. Bring those glasses through and we'll be upstairs in five minutes.

Vocabulary: appetite, blend, chaos, counter, drain, glance, herb, portion, relief, rid, sigh, skip, stir
