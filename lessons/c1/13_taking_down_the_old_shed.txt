Taking Down the Old Shed
A: Now we've got all the boxes out, come and look at the gap under this side wall.
B: Oh. I hadn't noticed how far that end of the shed had sunk. I thought we only needed a new roof.
A: So did I. But the contractor who came yesterday said there was structural damage to the frame as well.
B: Then buying a new roof won't solve much. Did he give you a price for removing the whole thing?
A: Three hundred pounds. His quote says "removal of shed and disposal of timber". We just need to leave a clear path from the gate.
B: Could we take it apart ourselves and save the money?
A: I'd rather not. Some of the screws have broken off, and the wall moved when I pushed the door. I don't want it coming down on us.
B: Fair enough. What about putting a smaller shed in the same place?
A: I'm not sure we need one. Most of what we found in there was empty boxes. We only need storage for the garden tools now.
B: And the folding chairs. I don't want those sitting on the lawn whenever it rains.
A: There's room for them in the garage if we move the bikes to the wall. We could assemble a tall cupboard beside them for the tools.
B: That would leave this area free. I'd like to grow something here once the shed has gone.
A: Me too. We'll need to check the ground for screws and other debris before we start digging.
B: Does the contractor's price include clearing all that away?
A: It says he'll leave the area clear. I'll ask him to confirm exactly what that includes before we accept.
B: Good. Let's measure the space in the garage first, so we know everything will fit.

Vocabulary: assemble, debris, disposal, lawn, removal, screw, shed, storage, structural, timber
