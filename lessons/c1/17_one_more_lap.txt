One More Lap
A: I know the lesson's over, but could you watch me do one more lap? I keep running out of breath halfway down the pool.
B: Sure. Is it just the front crawl, or does it happen with the other strokes too?
A: Just the crawl. I'm fine on my back. I start rushing as soon as my face goes in.
B: Use this lane by the wall and head for the shallow end. I'll walk beside you.
A: Made it. I was nearly out of breath again.
B: I saw you lift your whole head to breathe. Did you get water in your mouth?
A: A bit, yeah. I kept lifting my head higher after that.
B: No wonder your neck looked stiff. Here, watch my shoulder. Let your body rotate a little as you turn to breathe.
A: Can I try that here first? I can stand up at this end.
B: Go ahead. Breathe out steadily while your face is in the water.
A: Oh, that feels better. I was holding my breath until I turned.
B: Ease off a bit, too. You started pretty fast.
A: I was trying to keep up with the man in the next lane. He made it look easy.
B: Don't worry about him. Pick a pace you can maintain all the way back.
A: Will you walk with me again?
B: Of course. Ready when you are.

Vocabulary: crawl, ease, lap, maintain, pace, rotate, shallow, steadily, stiff, stroke
