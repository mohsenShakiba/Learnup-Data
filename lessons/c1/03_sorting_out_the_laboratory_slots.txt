Sorting Out the Laboratory Slots
A: Have you checked the sign-up sheet for next week's first-year laboratory sessions yet?
B: I've just opened it on my screen. Almost all the morning groups are full, whereas few students picked Friday afternoon.
A: How many names are down for Friday so far?
B: There's only a handful—four at two o'clock, with no names in the four o'clock slot whatsoever.
A: It's hardly worth running Friday afternoon for four people. Could we merge them into Thursday's two o'clock group?
B: Let's see—there's still a little space in Thursday's room. It has eleven students at the moment.
A: Good, that brings it to fifteen. Each bench only takes two people safely, and we have little spare equipment once a group goes over sixteen.
B: Hang on—six undergraduates who enrolled yesterday still haven't chosen a group. A few of them emailed this morning because the only open morning slot clashes with their seminar.
A: Since next week's safety session is mandatory, let's allocate places in Wednesday afternoon's group to all six so nobody misses it.
B: Let me check Wednesday afternoon... That group has ten students right now, so adding six takes it to sixteen.
A: Perfect. Shall I update the schedule now and notify those students before lunch?
B: Yes, please—then I can print the attendance sheets for the faculty office this afternoon.

Vocabulary: allocate, attendance, bench, clash, enrol, faculty, handful, mandatory, merge, notify, slot, undergraduate, whatsoever
