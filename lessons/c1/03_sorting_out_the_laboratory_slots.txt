Sorting Out the Laboratory Slots
A: Have you checked the sign-up sheet for next week's first-year laboratory sessions yet?
B: I'm looking at it now. The mornings are nearly full. Friday afternoon's a different story.
A: How many names are down for Friday so far?
B: Just a handful. Four at two o'clock and nobody at four.
A: Could we merge them with the two o'clock group on Thursday?
B: Thursday at two? Eleven students so far.
A: That makes fifteen. With two to a bench, we can manage sixteen, but there's very little spare equipment after that.
B: Hang on—six undergraduates who enrolled yesterday still haven't chosen a group. A few of them emailed this morning because the only open morning slot clashes with another class.
A: Could we fit all six into the mandatory safety session on Wednesday afternoon?
B: Let me check... Yes, there are ten on the list. Six more would just fit.
A: Perfect. Shall I update the schedule now and notify those students before lunch?
B: Yes, please—then I can print the attendance sheets for the faculty office this afternoon.

Vocabulary: attendance, bench, clash, enrol, faculty, handful, mandatory, merge, notify, slot, undergraduate
