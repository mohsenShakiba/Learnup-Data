Choosing Seats for Mum
A: Before you book the theatre tickets for Mum's birthday, can we look at the dates again? She told me she could barely hear the actors last time.
B: I thought she just didn't like the play. She barely said a word at the interval.
A: She was frustrated. On the way home I made some remark about the ending and she had no idea what I was talking about.
B: Oh. Would it help if we sat closer this time? There are seats in the second row on Friday.
A: Maybe. But look at this leaflet from the theatre. It says they sometimes put the words up on a screen next to the stage.
B: Let me check the dates on the website. Here, under "Accessible performances". The one with the screen is on Sunday at two.
A: Does it specify where they'll put the screen? It'd be good if she could see it without having to look away from the stage all the time.
B: Hang on. There's a number for access enquiries. It says to call them for guidance on where to sit.
A: Let's ask Mum first. She might find a screen distracting. She never wants to read along when we watch films at my place.
B: She likes having the words on at mine. She says she switches them off at yours because you complain.
A: Fair enough. I'd still rather ask her. Has she said she wants to see this play?
B: Yes. She sent me a photo of the cast and said she'd love to go. That woman Mum thought was hilarious in the Christmas play is in this one too.
A: Great. I'll see if she'd like to go on Sunday and ask her about lunch too. Two's a bit early if we eat first.
B: Good idea. I'll leave this page open so we still have the number when we hear back.

Vocabulary: cast, distract, enquiry, frustrated, guidance, hilarious, interval, leaflet, remark, specify
