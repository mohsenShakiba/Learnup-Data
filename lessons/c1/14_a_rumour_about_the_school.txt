A Rumour About the School
A: Could you pass me the school letter from the kitchen table? There's a message in the parents' group saying the school's closing for good.
B: Here. I saw that rumour too. It started with an anonymous post last night, as far as I can tell.
A: The post says the children are moving to another school in January. That's what this letter says as well.
B: Read the next line. They're going for six weeks while the heating's replaced.
A: Oh, I missed that. Has the school put out a denial? My sister's going to be worried if she sees the post. Her son starts there next year.
B: Nothing since this letter. I'm sceptical about the post, though. The writer's pretty vague about how they know all this.
A: The writer claims to work there. Maybe they're afraid of losing their job.
B: Maybe. But to say the head teacher's hiding a permanent closure is quite an allegation. Did they actually see something in writing?
A: There's a photo of a letter below the post. Presumably that's meant to be the evidence.
B: Look at the top. There's no date or school name. It could be from the neighbouring school for all we know.
A: I nearly sent that to my sister. I'd like to give her a more definite answer, though.
B: Why don't we send the post to the school office and ask? They'll want to know it's circulating.
A: I'll ask. In the meantime I'll post a correction in the group about the move in January. I'll send the full letter so people can read the bit about the six weeks.
B: Send your sister the school's letter too. I'll let you know if I hear anything else.

Vocabulary: allegation, anonymous, circulate, closure, correction, denial, neighbouring, presumably, rumour, sceptical, vague
