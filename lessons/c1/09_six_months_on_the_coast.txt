Six Months on the Coast
A: What a glorious morning for a walk along the beach! Look how far out the tide goes here.
B: You picked the best weekend to visit. Honestly, if I'd stayed at my old solicitor's firm in the city, I'd probably be answering client emails right now.
A: You really don't miss it, do you? Everyone was surprised when you handed in your resignation in the spring.
B: Even I was nervous at the time. If I weren't so cautious about money, I'd have left two years earlier.
A: Were the hours really that bad towards the end?
B: They were endless—staff turnover in our team was twice as high as usual, and the constant strain was ruining my sleep.
A: You look ten years younger already. Were you ever worried about isolation out here once the summer visitors went home?
B: A little at first, though this coastal town is surprisingly vibrant all year round. Half the neighbours came to help when I started my own small legal practice from the cottage.
A: How's that new venture going so far?
B: Better than I hoped—local shops and boat businesses keep me busy, and I actually have evenings free to thrive outside work. If my sister hadn't sent me the listing for that cottage, I still wouldn't live five minutes from the harbour today.
A: Speaking of the harbour, is that your little blue boat tied up near the wall? If I knew anything about painting boats, I'd have come down last month to help you finish it!
B: Next spring will do! Come on, the cafe by the path is open—let's linger over a second coffee in the sun. That view alone makes the whole move worthwhile.

Vocabulary: cautious, coastal, endless, glorious, isolation, linger, listing, resignation, solicitor, strain, thrive, tide, turnover, venture, vibrant, worthwhile
