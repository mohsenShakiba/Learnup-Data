Six Months on the Coast
A: I'm glad you brought me down to the beach. Can you walk this far out every morning?
B: Only when the tide's out. Glorious, isn't it? If I'd stayed at the city firm, I'd probably be answering client emails right now.
A: Even on a Sunday? I knew things were bad when you handed in your resignation, but I didn't realize you were working weekends.
B: Staff turnover was so high that every time another solicitor left, I got half their clients. The emails were endless. The strain was starting to affect my sleep.
A: I remember you looking at places out here a couple of years ago. What made you wait?
B: You know how cautious I am about money. I wanted to save enough to last six months without a single client.
A: Have you had to use much of it?
B: Hardly any of it. My first client runs a shop by the harbour. She sent three of her neighbours to see me, and it's gone on like that.
A: Sounds like your practice is starting to thrive. Do you miss anything about the city?
B: Being able to walk round to your place for coffee. Still, you're here now. Fancy one? That little cafe by the path is open.
A: I'd love one. Can we sit outside and linger over it? I want a bit longer by the sea.
B: There's no hurry. That table by the wall's free.

Vocabulary: cautious, endless, glorious, linger, resignation, solicitor, strain, thrive, tide, turnover
