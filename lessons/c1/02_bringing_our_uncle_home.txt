Bringing Our Uncle Home
A: Is Uncle Paul's room ready? I'd better get over to the hospital to pick him up.
B: Come and see. Fresh sheets, and I've moved the little table so he can reach his tea from bed.
A: That's thoughtful. He rang me from the ward at seven this morning to ask where I was.
B: Seven? I thought they weren't going to discharge him till noon.
A: They're not. After two weeks in hospital, I'd be pretty eager to get home too.
B: Fair enough. Do you want me to pick up his prescription?
A: Thanks, I'll get it on the way out.
B: Ask the nurse when he had his last dose, will you? I'll write it down.
A: Will do. Can you come out when we get back? I'm worried he'll stumble on the front steps.
B: Of course. Do you think he'll let me give him a hand?
A: He was a bit reluctant yesterday. I held out my arm and he handed me his coat.
B: That sounds like Paul. I'll keep a chair handy by the door so he can sit down.
A: Thanks. I'll ring when we're outside.

Vocabulary: discharge, dose, eager, handy, noon, prescription, reluctant, stumble, thoughtful, ward
