Bringing Our Uncle Home
A: It's nearly noon—does this downstairs room look ready for Uncle Paul before I drive over to the hospital?
B: Almost. I've put fresh sheets on the bed and moved the small table closer so he can reach his water glass easily.
A: That's really thoughtful, thanks. After two weeks in hospital, he's so eager to sleep in a quiet room again instead of a noisy ward.
B: Have they confirmed that they'll discharge him before lunch?
A: Yes. The surgeon who checked him this morning signed his forms, though we still need to collect his prescription on the way out.
B: Make sure you ask when he had his last dose of pain medicine, so we don't give him the evening tablets too early.
A: Good point—I'll ask the nurse before we leave. One of the nurses showed me a few daily leg exercises yesterday to help him regain his strength.
B: How's he managing on steps now?
A: He's still quite slow on them. He needs a walking frame for the next month, and someone has to stay right beside him on the front steps in case he stumbles.
B: He won't enjoy that. Patience has never been easy for him, and he hates asking anyone for help.
A: I know—he seemed reluctant to take my arm even when he stood up from his chair yesterday.
B: Once he gets here, I'll reassure him that it's only temporary. Let's keep a strong chair handy near the hall door too, so he can sit down while he takes his coat off.
A: Good thinking. I'd better head off now so I can park near the main entrance.

Vocabulary: discharge, dose, eager, handy, noon, prescription, reassure, regain, reluctant, stumble, thoughtful, ward
