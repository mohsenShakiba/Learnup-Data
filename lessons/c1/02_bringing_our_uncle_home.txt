Bringing Our Uncle Home
A: Is Uncle Paul's room ready? I'd better get over to the hospital to pick him up.
B: Yes, come and see. I've put fresh sheets on and moved the little table so he can reach his tea from bed.
A: That's thoughtful. He rang me at seven this morning to ask if I was on my way yet.
B: Seven? I thought they weren't going to discharge him till noon.
A: They're not—he's just eager to get home. I can't blame him after two weeks on that ward.
B: Have they sorted out his prescription?
A: Yes, they said I can pick it up on our way out.
B: Ask the nurse when his next dose is due, will you?
A: I will. Can you come out when we get back? I'm worried he'll stumble on the front steps.
B: Of course. Do you think he'll let me help him?
A: He was a bit reluctant yesterday. I held out my arm and he handed me his newspaper.
B: That sounds like Paul. I'll keep a chair handy by the door so he can sit down.
A: Thanks. I'll ring when we're outside.

Vocabulary: discharge, dose, eager, handy, noon, prescription, reluctant, stumble, thoughtful, ward
