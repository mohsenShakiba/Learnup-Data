Making Room on the Front Path
A: Dan, can you give me a hand? Mum's coming home from hospital on Friday. I'm checking whether her walking frame will fit down the front path.
B: Here, I'll take that side. Oh, the front leg's caught on the bush.
A: I told the hospital the front entrance was accessible. I checked the door and everything. I didn't even think about the bush.
B: Could she come in through the side gate for now?
A: It's too steep round there. She'd have to get up the slope before she even reached the gate. I'll just cut this back a bit.
B: Hang on. Look under that branch. Can you see the nest?
A: Oh, there are eggs in it. Right, I'll leave it alone.
B: What if we widen the path on this side instead?
A: Wouldn't that take us over the boundary?
B: By a few inches, maybe. I don't mind giving up a strip of grass. How much extra width do you think she needs to get round that bend?
A: Maybe a row of bricks alongside the path, level with the rest? There are some left over from the wall. Do you have a tape measure handy?
B: I'll get one. Bring a brick out too, and we'll see if one row's enough.

Vocabulary: accessible, alongside, boundary, handy, nest, slope, steep, strip, widen, width
