The Drop in Subscriptions
A: Pull up a chair. Can you help me with this week's figures? Look how many people haven't paid for another year.
B: Let's see. Forty annual subscriptions to the newsletter have ended since Tuesday? Is that right?
A: Yes. That's the day we changed the payment page. Can you open the payment reminder on your phone and see where the link takes you?
B: It opens in my browser. I can see the price, but how do I pay?
A: There should be a button at the bottom. Can you move down the page?
B: There it is. It's half hidden. And why is the price higher than last year's?
A: See that little box for the extra monthly issue? It's selected by default. That's the price for the premium plan.
B: So I have to opt out to keep my old price? I didn't even see the box. Have any subscribers said that's why they left?
A: One wrote to ask about the price. Two others said they never got a reminder at all.
B: Could it have gone to spam?
A: Possibly. Let me check the email log. Six messages bounced on Tuesday, so those six definitely didn't get through.
B: And the link only works for three days. Let's ask the technical team to give everyone another week and fix the button on phones.
A: I'll ask them to leave that box empty by default too. Once the page is ready, I'll get in touch with the forty people whose plans ran out.

Vocabulary: bounce, browser, default, newsletter, opt, premium, reminder, spam, subscriber, subscription
