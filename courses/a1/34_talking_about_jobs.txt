Talking About Jobs

How's your new job going?
It's good. The work is hard, but I like it.
That's great. Where do you work now?
In a big office in the city. I start early every day.
How many hours do you work?
About eight, sometimes more when we're busy.
Do they pay you well?
Yes, the money is good. What do you do again?
I'm a teacher. My wife is a doctor, so we're both busy people.
Long hours! At least the driver who brings me in has it easy.
Ha! No job is really easy.
True. But it's good to like what you do.
