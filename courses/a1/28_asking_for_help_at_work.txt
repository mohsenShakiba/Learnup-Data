Asking for Help at Work
New vocabs: below, full, missing, type, wrong
Used vocabs: help, meeting, send, letter, busy, desk, address, could, easy, get, go, let, minute, miss, much, name, really, right, see, soon, thank, today, try, work
Could you help me for a minute?
Yes, of course. What's wrong?
I can't send this email, and I have a meeting soon. Can you come to my desk?
Let me see... Maybe your mailbox is full?
No, it's not full. It just won't go.
Did you type the address right? Look, below the name — a letter is missing.
Oh, you're right! Let me try again... Yes, it worked!
Good. Now you can get to your meeting.
Thank you so much. You really helped me today!
You're welcome. It's easy to miss things when you're busy.
