Watching the News
New vocabs: news, fire, because, anyone, police, programme, ever, also
Used vocabs: 
Did you see the news last night?
No, I missed it. What happened?
There was a big fire, because of the dry weather.
Oh no. Was anyone hurt?
No. The police helped everyone out in time.
Which programme showed it?
The evening one. Do you ever watch it?
Yes, I watch it a lot.
