Buying a Bus Ticket
bus, ticket, town, pound, money, driver, stop, market, leave, next, near, get, tell, minute, course, please, hello, thank
Hello. A ticket to town, please.
Of course. That is two pounds.
Here is the money. When does the next bus leave?
In about ten minutes.
Thank you. Where do I get off?
The driver will tell you the stop. It is near the market.
