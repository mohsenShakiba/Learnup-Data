Hello! Did you just move into this house?
Yes, we moved in last week.
I live next door, so we are neighbours now. It is a friendly street.
That is good to hear. Is there a park near here?
Yes, there is a garden across the road. Our dog likes it.
Great. Our dog needs a long walk every day.
What work do you do?
I am a writer. I work at home most days.
