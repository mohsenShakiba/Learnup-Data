Talking About the Weather
New vocabs: between, outside, south, sun, weather
Used vocabs: day, enjoy, get, go, good, like, nice, river, think, today, walk, yes
How's the weather today?
It's lovely — warm and sunny, between twenty and twenty-five degrees.
That's nice! Do you think it will rain?
No, I don't think so. The rain is staying in the south — we get the sun all day.
Then it's a good day for a walk. Do you want to go outside?
Yes, I'd like that. Where should we go?
Let's walk to the river.
