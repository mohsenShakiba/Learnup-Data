A Phone Call
call, answer, hear, talk, hello, send, text, meet, time, late, trip, home, wait, morning, speak
Hello? Sorry, I almost didn't answer.
Hi! Can you hear me? You sound far away.
Better now. But you speak too fast — slow down!
Sorry. I'm calling about our trip on Thursday.
Oh yes! What time do we meet?
Early — the bus leaves at seven in the morning.
Seven! I hate waiting at the station so early.
Don't be late, then. It's a long trip.
How many hours?
About twelve. We'll be home by night.
A whole day away! I can't wait.
Me neither. I'll send you a text with everything.
Perfect. I have to go, but let's talk again soon.
