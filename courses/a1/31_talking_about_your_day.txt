Talking About Your Day
New vocabs: around, begin, front, shower, wake
Used vocabs: bed, bus, busy, day, door, early, evening, fine, get, good, home, long, look, morning, time, tired, tonight, walk, way, work
You look tired. Long day?
Very. I was busy from early this morning — a quick shower, and out the front door.
What time do you wake up?
Around six. Then I walk to work — no bus for me. It wakes me up.
That's a good way to begin the day.
Yes. How about you?
I'm fine, but I don't get home until the evening.
That's a long day. Go to bed early tonight.
I will. Early to bed for me!
