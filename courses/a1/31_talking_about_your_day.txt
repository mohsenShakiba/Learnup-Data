Talking About Your Day
day, wake, morning, afternoon, evening, tired, early, late, work, eat, walk, busy, tell, often, home
How was your day? You look a little tired.
I'm fine, just a busy day. I wake up very early every morning.
Tell me, what do you do first?
I eat something small, then I walk to work.
You walk every day? That's good for you.
Yes, I often walk in the morning. It wakes me up.
By the afternoon, are you still busy?
Very. I don't get home until late in the evening.
That's a long day. Do you get tired?
Sometimes, but I like my work, so it's fine.
You should rest more.
I will. Tonight I want to eat well and go to bed early.
