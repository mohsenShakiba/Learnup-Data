Describing a Friend
New vocabs: born, dark, everyone, girlfriend, mean
Used vocabs: friendly, funny, great, hair, have to, kind, like, long, love, meet, music, new, party, really, soon, sound, tall
Have you met Tom's new girlfriend?
No, I haven't. What's she like?
Really lovely. Tall, with long dark hair.
Is she friendly?
Very. She's not mean at all — she's kind to everyone and very funny.
She sounds great.
She is. Good for Tom!
Where's she from?
She was born here.
How did they meet?
At a party — they love the same music.
I'll have to meet her soon.
