Asking for Directions
New vocabs: left, right, street, map, walk, stop, road, here, far, near, end, go, little, minute, much, thank, turn, way
Used vocabs: yes
Excuse me, is the station near here?
No, it's a little far.
Can I walk there?
Yes, you can. It's a ten-minute walk.
Okay. Which way do I go?
Go down this street and turn left.
Left at the end of the road?
Yes. Then the station is on your right.
Thank you so much!
