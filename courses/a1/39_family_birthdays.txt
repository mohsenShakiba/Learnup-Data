Family Birthdays

All the family is coming this Saturday.
Really? Why is everyone visiting?
It's grandmother and grandfather's big day. Fifty years together!
That's wonderful. How many people is that?
My aunt, my uncle, all my cousins... about twenty.
Where will everyone sit? Your house is small.
We'll eat in the garden. My brother and sister are cooking.
What food are you making?
Lots! And grandfather's birthday is the same week, so it's a party for two things.
Two birthdays in one day!
Yes. The whole family in one house — it happens once a year.
Then it's going to be a special day.
