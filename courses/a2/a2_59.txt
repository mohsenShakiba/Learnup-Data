059. Seasons and Climate
Words: climate, season, temperature, wind, storm, cloud, wet, dry, heat, sky, predict, change

Topic: Protecting the Garden

Amy: Luke, the garden is very dry today.
Luke: Yes. The heat has been strong all week.
Amy: Can the weather report predict any rain?
Luke: It says the temperature will change tonight.
Amy: I can see one dark cloud in the sky.
Luke: A storm may arrive after midnight.
Amy: Will the wind be strong?
Luke: Yes, so we should move the small plants inside.
Amy: This season usually has calm weather.
Luke: Our climate is changing, so days are less easy to predict.
Amy: The ground needs rain, but not a wild storm.
Luke: We can cover the young plants to keep them safe.
Amy: Then they will get wet without breaking.
Luke: Good. I will bring the empty pots inside too.
Amy: After that, we can close the garden gate.
Luke: Perfect. The garden will be ready for the weather.
