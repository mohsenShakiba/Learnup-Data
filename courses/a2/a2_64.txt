064. Rules and Permission
Words: rule, law, permission, allow, refuse, accept, request, instruction, prevent, control, public, behave

Topic: Rules at the Public Pool

Ella: Max, is this your first visit to the public pool?
Max: Yes. Do I need permission to use the deep pool?
Ella: The main rule says you must be a strong swimmer.
Max: I can swim well. Will the guard accept my request?
Ella: He may ask you to swim one short length first.
Max: That is fair. What other rules should I know?
Ella: Follow every safety instruction near the water.
Max: Are people allowed to run beside the pool?
Ella: No. The guards refuse to allow it.
Max: That rule can prevent an accident.
Ella: Exactly. The guards also control how many people enter.
Max: Is that required by law?
Ella: Yes, because the pool must not become too crowded.
Max: I will behave and listen to the guards.
Ella: Good. Then we can enjoy our swim safely.
Max: Great. I will ask about the deep pool now.
