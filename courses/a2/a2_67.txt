067. University Life
Words: university, college, education, subject, exam, homework, project, research, schedule, career, training, knowledge

Topic: Planning the First University Week

Maya: Ben, how was your first day at university?
Ben: Good, but my new schedule looks very busy.
Maya: Which subject are you most excited about?
Ben: History, because we have a museum research project.
Maya: That should add to your knowledge of the city.
Ben: Yes, but we also have homework every week.
Maya: When is your first exam?
Ben: It is not until the end of next month.
Maya: Then you have time to prepare.
Ben: The college also offers writing training on Fridays.
Maya: That could help with your project.
Ben: I think so. Good education needs more than classes.
Maya: Have you thought about your future career?
Ben: I may work in a museum after university.
Maya: This course sounds right for you.
Ben: It does. I just need to follow my schedule.
