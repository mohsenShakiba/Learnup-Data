075. Science and Research
Words: science, research, experiment, data, result, method, evidence, fact, discover, discovery, identify, explanation

Topic: The Fastest-Growing Plant

Nina: Leo, are you ready for our plant experiment?
Leo: Yes. Our research question is simple.
Nina: We want to discover which plant grows fastest.
Leo: What method will we use?
Nina: We will give each plant the same water and light.
Leo: Then we can measure them every three days.
Nina: I made a chart for all the data.
Leo: Good. The numbers will show the result clearly.
Nina: We also need a photo as evidence each week.
Leo: That will help us identify any change in the leaves.
Nina: Our science teacher said this is a fair test.
Leo: It is a fact that plants need light.
Nina: But our final discovery may still surprise us.
Leo: We should not write an explanation before the test ends.
Nina: Right. First, we collect careful information.
Leo: Let us water the plants and start today.
