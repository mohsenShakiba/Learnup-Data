072. Advertising and Media
Words: advertise, poster, article, image, print, product, public, audience, profile, information, review, feature

Topic: Advertising a School Water Bottle

Anna: Paul, we need to advertise our school water bottle.
Paul: Let us start with a bright poster.
Anna: I took a clear image of the product this morning.
Paul: Good. We should show its best feature too.
Anna: It keeps water cold for six hours.
Paul: That information will interest our audience.
Anna: Should we print the price under the photo?
Paul: Yes, and add the school shop address.
Anna: I can also write a short article for the website.
Paul: Include a review from someone who used the bottle.
Anna: Mia wrote that it was easy to carry.
Paul: Perfect. We can share that on the public page.
Anna: I will add the poster to our school profile.
Paul: When should we print the paper copies?
Anna: Today, so we can put them up tomorrow.
Paul: Great. People will know about the bottle soon.
