047. Housework and Repairs
Words: washing, tidy, fix, repair, broken, tool, equipment, wooden, wood, roof, lock, cupboard

Topic: Fixing the Kitchen Cupboard

Nora: Sam, the wooden cupboard door is broken again.
Sam: I can fix it after we finish the washing.
Nora: The clean dishes are ready, so I will put them away.
Sam: Be careful. The cupboard door may fall.
Nora: What tool do you need for the repair?
Sam: A screwdriver and two small screws.
Nora: Are they with your other equipment?
Sam: Yes, in the tidy box under the table.
Nora: The door is made of heavy wood.
Sam: That is why the old screws became loose.
Nora: Can you fix the lock while you are here?
Sam: Yes, but I will repair the door first.
Nora: After this, we should check the roof.
Sam: Why? Is water coming into the kitchen?
Nora: No, but a bird keeps dropping leaves from it.
Sam: One repair at a time, please!
