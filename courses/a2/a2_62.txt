062. Crime and the Law
Words: law, lawyer, thief, steal, gun, attack, kill, evidence, case, public, government, protect

Topic: Discussing a Crime Novel

Mia: Jack, did you finish the crime novel?
Jack: Yes. The lawyer solved the case in the final chapter.
Mia: I thought the shopkeeper was the thief.
Jack: So did I, but he did not steal the diamonds.
Mia: The broken window was important evidence.
Jack: Yes. It showed how the real thief entered.
Mia: Why did he carry a gun?
Jack: He wanted to frighten people, not kill anyone.
Mia: The attack at the station was a scary scene.
Jack: It was, but the police arrived quickly.
Mia: They must protect the public and follow the law.
Jack: The book also showed how the government supports the police.
Mia: Did you guess the thief before the end?
Jack: No. I trusted the wrong character.
Mia: That made the story exciting.
Jack: I agree. Let us choose another mystery next week.
