040. Accidents and Emergencies
Words: accident, broken, hurt, blood, pain, medical, danger, attack, burn, immediately, serious, careful

Topic: Packing a First-Aid Kit

Ella: Max, we need a first-aid kit for our hiking trip.
Max: Do you think there is much danger on the path?
Ella: No, but a small accident can happen anywhere.
Max: I will pack bandages in case someone sees blood.
Ella: Good. Add this cream for a small burn too.
Max: What should we do if someone has serious pain?
Ella: We should stop walking and ask for medical help.
Max: And what if an arm or leg looks broken?
Ella: Do not move it. Call for help immediately.
Max: I will also take a whistle and a phone.
Ella: The whistle could help during an animal attack.
Max: Are there dangerous animals near our camp?
Ella: No, but the whistle can also help if we get lost.
Max: I understand. It is better to be careful.
Ella: Exactly. We want everyone to stay safe and not get hurt.
Max: The kit is ready. Now we can pack our food.
