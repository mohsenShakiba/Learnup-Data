032. Jobs and Careers
Words: career, worker, employee, manager, boss, assistant, professional, salary, employ, working, training, interview

Topic: Preparing for a Job Interview

Nina: Leo, can you help me prepare for my interview tomorrow?
Leo: Of course. What kind of job is it?
Nina: It is an assistant job at a travel company.
Leo: That could be a good start to your career.
Nina: Yes. I like working with people and planning trips.
Leo: What training does the company provide?
Nina: Every new employee gets two weeks of training.
Leo: Good. Do you know who will do the interview?
Nina: The office manager and her boss will speak with me.
Leo: They may ask why they should employ you.
Nina: I will say I am helpful, organized, and professional.
Leo: They might also ask about your past work.
Nina: I was a cafe worker for one year.
Leo: Have they told you the salary?
Nina: Yes, and it is fair for a first office job.
Leo: Then smile, speak clearly, and be yourself.
