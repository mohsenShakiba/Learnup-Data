070. Business and Sales
Words: sale, advertise, product, cost, cash, credit, store, deal, offer, quantity, produce, order

Topic: Planning a Bakery Sale

Sofia: Alex, we need to plan Saturday's bakery sale.
Alex: Which product should we advertise in the window?
Sofia: Our new lemon cake is the best choice.
Alex: How much does each cake cost to produce?
Sofia: It costs four pounds, so we can sell it for seven.
Alex: We could offer two cakes for twelve pounds.
Sofia: That is a good deal for families.
Alex: What quantity should we prepare?
Sofia: Let us make thirty cakes for the store.
Alex: Should customers order early?
Sofia: They can, but we will keep ten cakes for visitors.
Alex: Can people pay with cash or credit?
Sofia: Yes, we accept both.
Alex: I will advertise the sale on our town page.
Sofia: Great. Add a clear photo and the price.
Alex: Done. Now we only need to bake thirty cakes!
