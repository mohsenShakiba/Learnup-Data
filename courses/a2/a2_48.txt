048. Buildings and Construction
Words: building, wood, wooden, stone, ground, roof, tower, bridge, hole, tool, equipment, repair

Topic: Building a Garden Playhouse

Lena: Mark, the children's playhouse is almost finished.
Mark: Yes. The small wooden building looks strong.
Lena: Did you use wood for every wall?
Mark: Yes, but the floor stands on stone blocks.
Lena: That keeps it above the wet ground.
Mark: Exactly. Now I need to finish the roof.
Lena: Which tool do you need?
Mark: The small hammer from my equipment box.
Lena: Here it is. What is that hole beside the door?
Mark: It is for a round window.
Lena: The children want a little tower too.
Mark: We can add one later if we have enough wood.
Lena: They also asked for a bridge to the tree.
Mark: That sounds fun, but safety comes first.
Lena: Is there anything we need to repair now?
Mark: No. We only need to paint the playhouse.
