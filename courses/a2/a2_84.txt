084. Instructions and Processes
Words: instruction, following, firstly, secondly, finally, step, method, correctly, carefully, prepare, complete, result

Topic: Making a Paper Plane

Ella: Max, can you show me how to make a paper plane?
Max: Yes. Prepare one clean sheet of paper.
Ella: Done. What is the first instruction?
Max: Firstly, fold the paper in half carefully.
Ella: Am I following the line correctly?
Max: Yes. Press the fold with your finger.
Ella: What is the second step?
Max: Secondly, fold both top corners into the middle.
Ella: They look the same on each side.
Max: Good. Now fold the new edges in again.
Ella: This method makes a narrow point.
Max: Exactly. Finally, fold down the two wings.
Ella: Is the plane complete now?
Max: Yes. The result looks very good.
Ella: Let us see if it can fly.
Max: Throw it gently towards the open door.
