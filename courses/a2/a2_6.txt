006. School and Study
Words: education, homework, exam, subject, teach, college, university, dictionary, spelling, question, instruction, knowledge

Topic: The Dictionary Hat

Mina: Daniel, why do you have a dictionary on your head?
Daniel: I have an English exam at college tomorrow.
Mina: That is not how education works, but it is a nice hat.
Daniel: English is my hardest subject. Can you teach me?
Mina: Yes. My first instruction is to put the dictionary down.
Daniel: Done. Should I show you my homework too?
Mina: Please do. Your spelling is good, with two small mistakes.
Daniel: I knew the hat was not working. What is my first question?
Mina: Spell the word "university."
Daniel: U-N-I-V-E-R-S-I-T-Y.
Mina: Correct! Maybe some knowledge entered your head.
Daniel: I want to study science at university one day.
Mina: Then study each day, not only before an exam.
Daniel: You sound like a real teacher.
Mina: Thank you. Now spell "education."
Daniel: Can I wear the dictionary again first?
