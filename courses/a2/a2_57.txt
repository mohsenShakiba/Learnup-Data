057. Recycling and Waste
Words: recycle, rubbish, pollution, environment, reduce, remove, collect, oil, gas, product, protect, throw

Topic: Sorting Waste in the Garage

Ruby: Ben, can you help me sort the rubbish in the garage?
Ben: Sure. Which things can we recycle?
Ruby: We can collect the paper, glass, and metal cans.
Ben: Should I throw this empty oil bottle away?
Ruby: No. We must take it to the special waste centre.
Ben: What about this old gas can?
Ruby: It goes there too, because it may still be dangerous.
Ben: I found a broken cleaning product under the shelf.
Ruby: Put on gloves before you remove it.
Ben: Good idea. We need to protect our hands.
Ruby: Careful recycling also protects the environment.
Ben: It can reduce pollution in our town.
Ruby: Exactly. Put normal waste in the black bag.
Ben: The garage already looks much better.
Ruby: We have only one box left to check.
Ben: Then we can take everything to the centre.
