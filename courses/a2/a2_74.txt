074. Online Profiles and Networks
Words: network, profile, user, connect, link, data, information, social, communicate, share, public, program

Topic: Making a Safer Online Profile

Lily: Noah, can you check my new social profile?
Noah: Sure. Is it on the school network?
Lily: Yes. Every student can be a user.
Noah: Your photo is nice, but your phone number is public.
Lily: I did not mean to share that information.
Noah: Open the privacy program and hide it.
Lily: Done. Does the network keep my data safe?
Noah: It helps, but you must choose what you post.
Lily: Can I connect with students from other classes?
Noah: Yes, if you know who they are.
Lily: I added a link to our art club page.
Noah: That is fine because the page is public.
Lily: We can communicate about the next meeting there.
Noah: Good. Never share your password with anyone.
Lily: I will not. My profile is safer now.
Noah: Great. Now you can enjoy the network carefully.
