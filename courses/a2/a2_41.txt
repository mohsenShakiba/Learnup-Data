041. Feelings and Emotions
Words: feeling, happily, sadly, scared, nervous, worried, surprised, pleased, fear, stress, smile, cry

Topic: Waiting for Play Results

Mia: Jack, the teacher will choose the school play actors today.
Jack: I know. I feel nervous while we wait.
Mia: I am worried too, but try to smile.
Jack: My biggest fear is forgetting my lines on stage.
Mia: You practised a lot, so do not feel scared.
Jack: What feeling did you have during your audition?
Mia: I was surprised because I enjoyed it.
Jack: I heard the teacher laugh happily at your funny part.
Mia: I hope that was a good sign.
Jack: Here she comes with the results.
Mia: I got the part! I am so pleased.
Jack: Well done. I got a part too.
Mia: Great! All our stress was for nothing.
Jack: I almost wanted to cry while we waited.
Mia: Now you can cry happily instead of sadly.
Jack: I will just smile and start learning my lines.
