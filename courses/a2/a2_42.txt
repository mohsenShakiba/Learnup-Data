042. Personality
Words: personality, character, polite, rude, lazy, clever, stupid, quiet, noisy, helpful, independent, creative

Topic: Creating a Cartoon Hero

Lucy: Adam, let us create a new character for our cartoon.
Adam: Good idea. What kind of personality should she have?
Lucy: She should be clever and solve problems quickly.
Adam: She can also be creative and build useful machines.
Lucy: Should she be quiet or noisy?
Adam: Quiet at school, but noisy when she sings.
Lucy: That makes her more interesting.
Adam: She should be polite to people who need help.
Lucy: Yes, but the rude king will not listen to her.
Adam: Then she can be independent and find another way.
Lucy: We should not make the king look stupid.
Adam: Right. He can be lazy, but he can still learn.
Lucy: A helpful robot could travel with our hero.
Adam: Perfect. The robot can tell simple jokes.
Lucy: Now both characters have a clear personality.
Adam: Let us start drawing the first cartoon scene.
