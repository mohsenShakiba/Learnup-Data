036. Basic Health Problems
Words: health, medical, dentist, patient, ill, illness, disease, flu, virus, headache, pain, weak

Topic: Calling the Health Clinic

Anna: Paul, you look ill. How do you feel?
Paul: I have a headache, and my legs feel weak.
Anna: Do you have pain anywhere else?
Paul: My throat hurts, and I feel very tired.
Anna: It may be the flu. A virus is going around.
Paul: Should I call the medical clinic?
Anna: Yes. Your health is important.
Paul: I hope it is not a serious disease.
Anna: Most flu cases are a short illness.
Paul: The clinic says every new patient should call first.
Anna: I can make the call while you rest.
Paul: Thanks. I also have a dentist visit this afternoon.
Anna: You should move it to another day.
Paul: Yes, I do not want to make other people ill.
Anna: The clinic can tell us what to do next.
Paul: Good. I will sit down and drink some water.
