029. Talking and Writing
Words: communicate, conversation, chat, reply, response, question, statement, comment, mention, express, discussion, dialogue

Topic: Writing a Clear Message

Lily: Noah, can you check my message to the book club?
Noah: Sure. What do you want to communicate?
Lily: I want to move our discussion from Friday to Saturday.
Noah: Your first statement is clear, but add the new time.
Lily: Good point. I will mention that we can meet at three.
Noah: You should also explain why the date changed.
Lily: I can say the library is closed on Friday.
Noah: Great. End with a question about Saturday.
Lily: How about, "Can everyone join us then?"
Noah: That works. It makes a response easy.
Lily: Should I ask people to reply before tomorrow?
Noah: Yes, and invite them to add a comment about the book.
Lily: Then our online chat can continue before we meet.
Noah: Exactly. Clear writing helps every conversation.
Lily: This message now reads like a friendly dialogue.
Noah: Send it. It will express everything clearly.
