083. Comparing Ideas
Words: compare, difference, similar, opposite, different, worse, worst, advantage, benefit, positive, negative, prefer

Topic: Bus or Bicycle?

Maya: Leo, should we take the bus or cycle to the park?
Leo: Let us compare the two ideas.
Maya: The travel times are similar on a quiet day.
Leo: The main difference is the weather.
Maya: Yes. Cycling in rain is much worse.
Leo: But the bus can be crowded at the worst time.
Maya: One advantage of cycling is free exercise.
Leo: That is a positive health benefit.
Maya: A negative point is that we must carry our bags.
Leo: The bus is the opposite. It is easy with bags.
Maya: Today is sunny, so the conditions are different.
Leo: Which option do you prefer?
Maya: I prefer cycling when the road is dry.
Leo: So do I. We can use the bike path.
Maya: Great. I will get my helmet.
Leo: And I will put water in my bag.
