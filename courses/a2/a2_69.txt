069. Communication at Work
Words: colleague, employee, manager, boss, assistant, professional, discussion, comment, reply, report, schedule, task

Topic: Preparing for a Work Meeting

Nina: Leo, our manager moved the meeting to two o'clock.
Leo: Thanks. I will update my schedule.
Nina: The main task is to discuss the customer report.
Leo: I read it and added one comment about delivery times.
Nina: Good. We can include that in the discussion.
Leo: Has the new employee read the report?
Nina: Yes. Our assistant sent it to every colleague.
Leo: Did anyone reply with a question?
Nina: The sales team asked about next month's plan.
Leo: Our boss can answer that during the meeting.
Nina: We should keep our presentation professional and short.
Leo: I will use only three simple charts.
Nina: Perfect. That leaves time for questions.
Leo: Do you need help setting up the room?
Nina: Please connect the computer to the screen.
Leo: I will do it now. We will be ready early.
