043. Physical Appearance
Words: appearance, attractive, pretty, lovely, male, lady, thin, thick, height, weight, size, clothing

Topic: Choosing Clothes for a Photo

Emma: Noah, can you help me choose clothing for my photo?
Noah: Sure. What kind of appearance do you want?
Emma: Simple and attractive, but not too formal.
Noah: This pretty blue shirt would look good.
Emma: I like it, but the material is very thin.
Noah: Then try this lovely green one. It is thicker.
Emma: Is the size right for me?
Noah: Yes. It fits your height and looks comfortable.
Emma: The photographer said weight does not matter in the picture.
Noah: That is true. Feeling comfortable matters more.
Emma: Should I wear the thick black jacket too?
Noah: No, the room will be warm.
Emma: The photo is for the male and female staff page.
Noah: Then every man and lady will use the same background.
Emma: Great. I will choose the green shirt.
Noah: Good choice. You are ready for the photo.
