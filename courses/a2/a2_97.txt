097. Personal Growth
Words: personal, independent, ability, skill, knowledge, experience, progress, improve, achieve, success, decision, future

Topic: Learning to Travel Alone

Amy: Luke, I booked my first trip alone today.
Luke: That is a big personal decision.
Amy: I want to become more independent.
Luke: Travelling alone will give you useful experience.
Amy: I hope it will improve my planning skill.
Luke: You already have the ability to organize things well.
Amy: My knowledge of train travel is still small.
Luke: You can learn before you leave.
Amy: I made some progress by booking the hotel myself.
Luke: That is a real success.
Amy: My next goal is to achieve something on my own.
Luke: A simple schedule will help.
Amy: This trip may also help my future work.
Luke: Yes, confidence is useful in every job.
Amy: I am nervous, but I am ready.
Luke: You will return with many good stories.
