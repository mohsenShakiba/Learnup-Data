096. Communication Styles
Words: communicate, informal, polite, rude, loud, loudly, quiet, quietly, expression, speech, dialogue, behaviour

Topic: Practising a Welcome Speech

Ella: Max, can you listen to my welcome speech?
Max: Of course. Is it for the new students?
Ella: Yes. I want to communicate in a friendly way.
Max: Start with a polite greeting.
Ella: Should my speech be formal or informal?
Max: Keep it informal because the students are young.
Ella: I also wrote a short dialogue with the school captain.
Max: Good. That will make the welcome more natural.
Ella: Am I speaking too quietly?
Max: A little. Use a loud, clear voice.
Ella: I do not want to speak loudly and scare everyone.
Max: You will not. Just be clear in the quiet hall.
Ella: Should I change my serious expression?
Max: Yes, smile so you do not look rude.
Ella: My behaviour must match my friendly words.
Max: Exactly. Now try the speech once more.
