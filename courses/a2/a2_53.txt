053. Public Transport
Words: public, transport, passenger, station, underground, crowded, seat, route, schedule, journey, distance, vehicle

Topic: Taking the Underground to Work

Mia: Jack, is this the right underground station for the office?
Jack: Yes. The green route stops near our building.
Mia: I usually use a different kind of public transport.
Jack: The underground is the fastest vehicle at this hour.
Mia: How long is the journey?
Jack: Only fifteen minutes from this station.
Mia: That is a short distance.
Jack: Yes, but the train may be crowded.
Mia: I do not mind standing if there is no seat.
Jack: Every passenger seems to be going to work now.
Mia: Does the schedule show our next train?
Jack: Yes, it arrives in two minutes.
Mia: Great. I can use this route every morning.
Jack: Buy a weekly ticket if you do.
Mia: That will make each journey cheaper.
Jack: Here comes our train. Let us get on.
