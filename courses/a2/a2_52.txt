052. Road Travel
Words: driving, vehicle, lorry, wheel, gas, speed, route, direction, distance, accident, safe, transport

Topic: Planning a Safe Road Trip

Sara: Tom, are you ready for the long drive tomorrow?
Tom: Almost. I am checking our route on the map.
Sara: What distance will we travel?
Tom: About two hundred kilometres in one direction.
Sara: We should fill the car with gas before leaving.
Tom: Yes, and I checked every wheel this morning.
Sara: Is our small vehicle comfortable for four people?
Tom: It is, but we must pack light.
Sara: Which road is the safest?
Tom: The new road is safe, but many lorry drivers use it.
Sara: Then keep a good distance from each lorry.
Tom: I will, and I will watch my speed.
Sara: Careful driving can prevent an accident.
Tom: We can also stop and rest after one hour.
Sara: Good. The car is our best transport for this trip.
Tom: Everything is planned. We can leave at seven.
