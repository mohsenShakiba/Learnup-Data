082. Opinions and Discussion
Words: opinion, discussion, discuss, argument, argue, disagree, comment, statement, response, reason, explain, believe

Topic: Discussing School Uniforms

Lina: Ben, our class will discuss school uniforms today.
Ben: Good. What is your opinion about them?
Lina: I believe uniforms make mornings easier.
Ben: I disagree because students cannot choose their clothes.
Lina: That is a fair reason.
Ben: Can you explain why choice is less important to you?
Lina: I can wear what I like after school.
Ben: My response is that school lasts most of the day.
Lina: We have different ideas, but we do not need to argue.
Ben: Right. A discussion is not an angry argument.
Lina: I will make a statement about uniform cost too.
Ben: I can add a comment about comfort.
Lina: Then our points will show both sides.
Ben: We may still disagree at the end.
Lina: That is fine if we listen politely.
Ben: Agreed. We are ready for the class discussion.
