073. News and Reporting
Words: recent, recently, article, report, event, fact, information, source, public, comment, statement, interview

Topic: Reporting a Park Opening

Maya: Ben, we need to report the new park opening.
Ben: Yes. It was the biggest public event this week.
Maya: I recently interviewed the park manager.
Ben: Did she give a clear statement about the cost?
Maya: Yes. The town paid for half of the work.
Ben: We need a second source for that fact.
Maya: The recent town report has the same information.
Ben: Good. Then we can include it in our article.
Maya: I also did a short interview with a family.
Ben: What was their main comment?
Maya: They liked the playground and the quiet garden.
Ben: That gives our story a human voice.
Maya: Do we have enough photos from the event?
Ben: Yes, I took six clear pictures.
Maya: Great. I will finish the article this afternoon.
Ben: I will check every fact before we publish it.
