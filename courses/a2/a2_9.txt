009. Friends and Social Visits
Words: guest, invite, invitation, greet, chat, conversation, share, join, helpful, polite, smile, offer

Topic: Welcoming a New Neighbour

Lena: Mark, our new neighbour will be our guest this afternoon.
Mark: Great. Did you invite her for tea?
Lena: Yes, and she accepted my invitation this morning.
Mark: What should we do when she arrives?
Lena: We should greet her at the door with a smile.
Mark: I can offer her tea or coffee.
Lena: Good. It is polite to ask what she likes.
Mark: Shall we share the biscuits on the table too?
Lena: Of course. Then we can chat about the neighbourhood.
Mark: I hope the conversation does not become too quiet.
Lena: Ask about her garden. She was working there yesterday.
Mark: That is helpful. I like talking about plants.
Lena: She may also join our weekend walking group.
Mark: Nice. She will meet more people that way.
Lena: I think she is at the door now.
Mark: I will bring the tea while you welcome her.
