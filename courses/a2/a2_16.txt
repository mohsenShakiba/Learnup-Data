016. My Neighbourhood
Words: neighbour, community, building, hall, station, store, area, public, crowded, noise, visitor, village

Topic: Showing a Neighbour Around

Hana: Omar, welcome to our neighbourhood.
Omar: Thanks. I am glad to be your new neighbour.
Hana: I can show you the area before dinner.
Omar: That would help. Where is the nearest store?
Hana: It is inside the red building across the street.
Omar: Is the train station far from here?
Hana: No, it is a five-minute walk past the public library.
Omar: The street is a little crowded now.
Hana: Yes, but the noise becomes lower after six.
Omar: What is the large hall beside the park?
Hana: It is our community hall. We meet there on Fridays.
Omar: Can a new visitor join the meetings?
Hana: Of course. Everyone from the neighbourhood is welcome.
Omar: This area feels friendly, like a small village.
Hana: It does, although we are near the city centre.
Omar: I think I will enjoy living here.
