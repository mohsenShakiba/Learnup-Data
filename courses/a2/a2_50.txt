050. The Sea and the Coast
Words: coast, ocean, wave, ship, wind, sky, storm, wet, dry, rock, lake, danger

Topic: Leaving the Beach Before a Storm

Eva: Liam, the sky is getting dark over the coast.
Liam: Yes, and the wind is much stronger now.
Eva: The weather report said a storm might arrive.
Liam: Look at that large wave near the rocks.
Eva: We should move away from the ocean.
Liam: Agreed. Wet rock can be very dangerous.
Eva: Is that ship returning to the harbour?
Liam: Yes. It should not stay at sea in this weather.
Eva: Our bags are still dry under the beach chair.
Liam: I will get them while you fold the towel.
Eva: Be careful. Do not go close to the water.
Liam: I know there is danger near strong waves.
Eva: We can visit the lake tomorrow instead.
Liam: Good idea. The lake is away from the storm.
Eva: I have everything. Let us go to the cafe.
Liam: Perfect. We can watch the storm from inside.
