015. Life in the Wild
Words: wild, forest, field, grass, feed, catch, attack, danger, alive, protect, kill, natural

Topic: Watching a Fox Family

Lucy: Adam, look at the wild fox near the forest.
Adam: Stay quiet. It is walking into the field.
Lucy: Is it looking for food in the grass?
Adam: Yes. It may catch a mouse to feed its babies.
Lucy: Could the fox attack us?
Adam: Not if we keep our distance and stay calm.
Lucy: So we are not in danger here?
Adam: No, but we should never touch a wild animal.
Lucy: Its babies need it to stay alive.
Adam: That is why we must protect its natural home.
Lucy: Do people ever kill foxes near this forest?
Adam: It is not allowed in this protected area.
Lucy: I am glad. The fox is beautiful from here.
Adam: It has seen us, but it is not afraid.
Lucy: Now it is going back into the forest.
Adam: Let us leave quietly and give the family space.
