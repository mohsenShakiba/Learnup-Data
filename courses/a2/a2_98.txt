098. Predicting the Future
Words: future, predict, possibility, possible, probably, likely, chance, expect, believe, imagine, change, result

Topic: The Town in Twenty Years

Sara: Tom, can you imagine our town in twenty years?
Tom: I believe transport will change a lot.
Sara: Do you think electric buses are likely?
Tom: Yes, and they will probably be quieter too.
Sara: Is there a chance that cars will drive themselves?
Tom: It is possible, but people may still want control.
Sara: I expect more homes to use solar power.
Tom: That could result in cleaner air.
Sara: Can anyone really predict the future?
Tom: No, but we can study what is happening now.
Sara: There is also a possibility of more public parks.
Tom: I hope so. The population will probably grow.
Sara: Then the town will need more green spaces.
Tom: Our choices today can change tomorrow.
Sara: I like that idea about the future.
Tom: Me too. Let us hope we make good choices.
