081. Making Decisions
Words: decision, decide, choice, option, consider, reason, advantage, benefit, effect, result, possible, likely

Topic: Choosing a New Apartment

Nora: Sam, I need to decide between two apartments.
Sam: What is the main reason for moving?
Nora: I want to live closer to work.
Sam: Then consider the apartment near the station.
Nora: Its main advantage is the short journey.
Sam: What is the other option?
Nora: A larger apartment beside the park.
Sam: More space is a clear benefit.
Nora: Yes, but the rent will have a big effect on my budget.
Sam: Is it possible to visit both again?
Nora: Yes, I can see them on Saturday.
Sam: That will likely make the choice easier.
Nora: I should not make a quick decision.
Sam: Right. Compare the cost and travel time.
Nora: Then the result of my comparison will be clear.
Sam: Good. You will know which home feels right.
