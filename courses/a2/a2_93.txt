093. Environmental Policy
Words: environment, pollution, climate, recycle, reduce, protect, government, law, public, society, benefit, future

Topic: A New Plastic Bag Rule

Maya: Ben, the government announced a new plastic bag law.
Ben: What will the law change?
Maya: Stores will charge for each new plastic bag.
Ben: That may reduce the number people use.
Maya: Yes. It should also lower street pollution.
Ben: Can customers recycle old bags?
Maya: They can return them to special store boxes.
Ben: How does this protect the environment?
Maya: Fewer bags will reach rivers and the ocean.
Ben: That is a clear benefit for animals.
Maya: It may also help our climate a little.
Ben: Will the public support the change?
Maya: Many people already use cloth bags.
Ben: Small rules can change society over time.
Maya: I hope our town is cleaner in the future.
Ben: I will keep a cloth bag by the front door.
