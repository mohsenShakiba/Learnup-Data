001. Colours, Shapes, and Sizes
Words: colour, shape, size, round, square, circle, wide, narrow, thick, thin, clear, pattern

Topic: Choosing a New Rug

Lily: Max, can you help me choose a rug for my room?
Max: Sure. What size do you need?
Lily: The room is narrow, so I do not want a very wide rug.
Max: This blue one is long and thin. Do you like the colour?
Lily: Yes, but its pattern is too busy for my room.
Max: What about this thick yellow rug?
Lily: I like it, but the square shape looks too large.
Max: Then we could try the round green rug.
Lily: The circle in the middle is nice and clear.
Max: It also has a simple pattern around the side.
Lily: Do you think the green colour will look good by my bed?
Max: Yes, and the round shape will make the room feel softer.
Lily: Is it wide enough for the small table too?
Max: Yes. I checked the size on the label.
Lily: Great. This rug is the best one.
Max: Good choice. Now we only have to carry it home!
