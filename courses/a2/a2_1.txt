001. Colours, Shapes, and Sizes
Words: colour, shape, size, round, square, circle, wide, narrow, thick, thin, clear, pattern

Topic 001: Colours, Shapes, and Sizes

Scenario: Mia and Daniel are at a pottery-painting cafe. They are decorating a plate for their friend Emma's birthday.

Mia: This round plate is lovely, but is it the right size for Emma's present?

Daniel: I think so. The square plate is fun too, but this one gives us more space to paint.

Mia: Good point. What colour should we use?

Daniel: Emma loves blue. We could paint a wide blue line around the edge.

Mia: And then a narrow yellow line beside it. Blue and yellow look cheerful together.

Daniel: Great idea. What shape should we paint in the middle?

Mia: How about a flower? We can make one big circle and add six small circles around it.

Daniel: That sounds easy. Pass me the thick brush for the big circle, please.

Mia: Here you are. I'll use this thin brush for the small ones.

Daniel: Oh no! My big blue circle looks like a fried egg.

Mia: A blue fried egg? Emma will love it. She always laughs at our cooking.

Daniel: Then let's add a simple pattern around it. Maybe little yellow dots?

Mia: Yes, but don't put them too close together. We want the pattern to be clear.

Daniel: You're right. Three dots here, three there... What do you think?

Mia: It looks great! It is not a perfect flower, but it is a very funny birthday plate.

Daniel: Perfect gifts are boring anyway. This one has a story.

Mia: And a blue fried egg!

Daniel: Exactly. Now let's hope Emma never tries to eat breakfast from it.
