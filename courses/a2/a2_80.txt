080. Solving Problems
Words: problem, solution, solve, method, explanation, reason, decision, choice, option, consider, result, improve

Topic: Saving the School Garden

Mia: Jack, the plants in our school garden look dry.
Jack: Yes. We need to find the reason for this problem.
Mia: The soil is wet, so they have enough water.
Jack: Maybe they do not get enough sunlight.
Mia: That is a possible explanation.
Jack: We should consider moving them away from the wall.
Mia: That is one option. What other choice do we have?
Jack: We could cut back the tree above them.
Mia: Moving the pots is an easier method.
Jack: Agreed. Let us make that our decision.
Mia: We can solve the light problem this afternoon.
Jack: Then we should watch the result for one week.
Mia: If the plants improve, our solution worked.
Jack: If they do not, we can try the tree idea.
Mia: Good. We have a clear plan now.
Jack: Let us move the first pot together.
