003. Parts of the Body
Words: finger, neck, knee, shoulder, skin, blood, heart, stomach, middle, bottom, side, human

Topic: Nina's Funny Roller-Skating Fall

Nina: Leo, I tried to stop, but my bottom reached the floor before my feet did!
Leo: That happens to many beginners. Are you hurt anywhere?
Nina: My knee hurts a little, but I think I am okay.
Leo: Let me look. The skin is red, but there is no blood. Can you move your knee?
Nina: Yes, I can. Look, my finger can still point at it too.
Leo: Very funny! Does your shoulder or neck hurt?
Nina: No, they feel fine. My heart is beating very fast, though.
Leo: That is normal after a fall. Take a slow breath and relax.
Nina: My stomach feels strange too, but I think that is because I am laughing.
Leo: Good. Let us sit at the side of the rink for a minute.
Nina: Do all beginners fall in the middle of the rink?
Leo: Every human who learns to skate falls sometimes. You just chose a very public place!
Nina: I wanted everyone to see my special way of stopping.
Leo: They certainly saw it. Next time, bend your knees and move toward the side.
Nina: And if that does not work?
Leo: Then please try not to land on my foot.
Nina: No promises! Are you ready for lesson number two?
Leo: I am, but my foot is already nervous.
