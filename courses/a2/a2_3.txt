003. Parts of the Body
Words: finger, neck, knee, shoulder, skin, blood, heart, stomach, middle, bottom, side, human

Topic: A Funny Skating Fall

Nina: Leo, I tried to stop, but my bottom reached the floor first!
Leo: That happens to many beginners. Are you hurt?
Nina: My knee hurts a little, but I think I am okay.
Leo: Let me look. The skin is red, but there is no blood.
Nina: I can move my knee and point at it with my finger.
Leo: Good. Does your shoulder or neck hurt?
Nina: No, but my heart is beating very fast.
Leo: That is normal after a fall. Take a slow breath.
Nina: My stomach feels strange because I am laughing.
Leo: Let us sit at the side of the rink for a minute.
Nina: Did everyone see me fall in the middle of the rink?
Leo: Yes, but every human falls when learning to skate.
Nina: I showed them my special way of stopping.
Leo: Next time, bend your knees and move towards the side.
Nina: What if that does not work?
Leo: Then please try not to land on my foot!
