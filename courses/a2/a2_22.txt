022. Sports and Competitions
Words: competition, compete, match, score, goal, race, training, running, active, fit, beat, award

Topic: A Busy Sports Day

Zoe: Ryan, are you ready for the school sports competition?
Ryan: Yes. Our football match starts in ten minutes.
Zoe: I hope your team can beat the blue team.
Ryan: We have worked hard in training this month.
Zoe: You look fit and ready to play.
Ryan: Thanks. I want to score at least one goal.
Zoe: I will watch the match before my race.
Ryan: Are you still running in the eight-hundred-metre event?
Zoe: Yes. Four students will compete in it.
Ryan: You have been very active, so you can do well.
Zoe: I do not need to win. I just want my best time.
Ryan: That is a good goal for the race.
Zoe: What award does the winning football team get?
Ryan: A small cup and a big chocolate cake.
Zoe: Now I understand why your team trained so hard!
Ryan: Exactly. Sport is important, but cake also helps.
