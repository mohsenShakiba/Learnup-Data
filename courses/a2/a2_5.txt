005. The Kitchen and Cooking
Words: bowl, plate, fork, knife, recipe, boil, lemon, biscuit, sweet, vegetable, fridge, fresh

Topic: Ben's Secret Soup Ingredient

Maya: Ben, are you ready to make vegetable soup with me?
Ben: Yes. I found Grandma's recipe, but I need help.
Maya: First, take the fresh vegetables out of the fridge.
Ben: Should I put them on this plate?
Maya: Put them in the large bowl. The plate is for bread.
Ben: All right. Now I need a knife for the carrots.
Maya: Be careful. I will boil some water in the pot.
Ben: The carrots are ready. Do we add every vegetable now?
Maya: Yes, and then we cook them for twenty minutes.
Ben: Can I stir the soup with this fork?
Maya: You can, but a spoon will work much better!
Ben: The recipe also says to add some lemon.
Maya: Use only half a lemon, so the soup is not too sour.
Ben: Would one sweet biscuit make it taste better?
Maya: Put that biscuit back. It is for dessert!
Ben: Fine. Your soup is safe from my secret ingredient.
