013. Weather
Words: cloud, wind, storm, temperature, climate, wet, dry, heat, sky, bright, season, predict

Topic: The Picnic Forecast

Ella: Noah, can we still have our picnic this afternoon?
Noah: I think so, but that dark cloud worries me.
Ella: The sky was bright and clear this morning.
Noah: Weather can change quickly in this season.
Ella: Can your phone predict a storm?
Noah: It says there may be strong winds at four o’clock.
Ella: What is the temperature now?
Noah: It is twenty-eight degrees, I can already feel the heat.
Ella: The grass is dry now, but it could become very wet.
Noah: Summer rain is common in our climate.
Ella: Then let us have the picnic under the park shelter.
Noah: Good idea. We can stay dry if it rains.
Ella: And if there is no storm, we can sit by the lake.
Noah: I will bring a light coat in case it gets cold and windy.
Ella: Perfect. Our picnic plan is ready.
Noah: Almost. We still need to pack the sandwiches!
