024. Concerts and the Stage
Words: concert, audience, stage, act, scene, speech, expression, loud, noise, record, poster, public

Topic: The School Concert Rehearsal

Mia: Owen, the school concert starts in one hour.
Owen: I know. The audience is already waiting outside.
Mia: Did you put the poster near the public entrance?
Owen: Yes, and the concert time is clear on it.
Mia: Great. Our band is the first act on stage.
Owen: Then the drama group performs its funny scene.
Mia: I saw them practise. Every expression was very clear.
Owen: Their teacher will give a short speech after the play.
Mia: Will your parents record the show?
Owen: Yes, but they will sit near the back.
Mia: That is wise because our music is quite loud.
Owen: We should check that the microphone makes no strange noise.
Mia: I tested it five minutes ago. It sounds fine.
Owen: Good. Are you nervous about going on stage?
Mia: A little, but I am also excited.
Owen: Me too. Let us give the audience a great show.
