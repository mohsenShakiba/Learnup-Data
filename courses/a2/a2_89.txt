089. Planning a Project
Words: project, task, goal, schedule, arrange, arrangement, organize, prepare, method, equipment, result, complete

Topic: Planning a Science Display

Lily: Noah, we need to plan our science project today.
Noah: Yes. Our goal is to make a display about clean water.
Lily: What is the first task?
Noah: We should prepare a simple water filter.
Lily: Which method will we use?
Noah: We will pass dirty water through sand and small stones.
Lily: I can organize the equipment on this table.
Noah: Good. I will arrange the poster and photos.
Lily: That arrangement will make each step easy to see.
Noah: When should we test the filter?
Lily: Our schedule gives us Friday afternoon.
Noah: Then we can write about the result on Saturday.
Lily: Will the whole project be complete by Monday?
Noah: Yes, if the test works.
Lily: Great. We know what each person must do.
Noah: Let us start by finding the equipment.
