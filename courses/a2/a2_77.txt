077. Space and Planets
Words: moon, planet, sky, earth, discover, research, science, distance, further, appear, exist, bright

Topic: Watching the Night Sky

Ella: Max, the sky is very clear tonight.
Max: Yes. The moon looks bright above the trees.
Ella: Is that small light a planet?
Max: I think it is Mars, but let us check the app.
Ella: The app says Mars is much further away than the moon.
Max: The distance from earth is hard to imagine.
Ella: How do scientists know that other planets exist?
Max: They use science, careful research, and strong machines.
Ella: Do new stars ever appear in the sky?
Max: Sometimes a star becomes bright enough for us to notice.
Ella: I would love to discover a new planet.
Max: You may need a larger telescope first.
Ella: This little one is good for the moon.
Max: Look, we can see a dark area on it.
Ella: Space makes our garden feel very small.
Max: It does, but this is a perfect place to watch it.
