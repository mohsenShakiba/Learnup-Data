010. Shopping and Money
Words: shopping, cost, cheap, expensive, cash, credit, sale, store, product, item, quantity, order

Topic: Mugs, Money, and Muscles

Nora: Sam, can you help me with some shopping for the cafe?
Sam: Sure. What item do we need today?
Nora: We need twelve new mugs, so your arms can relax. We are not buying a fridge.
Sam: Good! I was not ready to carry a fridge across the store.
Nora: Look, they have a large quantity of blue mugs on this shelf.
Sam: The sign says this product is part of the weekend sale.
Nora: Great. How much does each mug cost?
Sam: Four pounds. That is quite cheap.
Nora: The white mugs are more expensive.
Sam: They have gold lines, but the coffee will taste the same.
Nora: True. Let us order twelve blue ones.
Sam: First, we should check that none of them are broken.
Nora: They are all fine, and one has a picture of a sleepy cat.
Sam: That one is perfect for me before my morning coffee.
Nora: Should we pay with cash or credit?
Sam: You have enough cash, and the shopkeeper can pack them in two boxes.
Nora: Perfect. We can carry one box each.
Sam: I knew this shopping trip had a hidden cost: exercise!
