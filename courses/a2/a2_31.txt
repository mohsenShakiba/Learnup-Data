031. Learning in Class
Words: knowledge, skill, subject, homework, exam, question, practise, improve, teach, education, college, instruction

Topic: A Lunch-Time Study Group

Emma: Jack, will you join our study group at lunch?
Jack: Yes. I need to prepare for the maths exam.
Emma: Maths is my best subject, so I can help.
Jack: Great. Can you teach me how to work with fractions?
Emma: Yes, but first show me the homework question.
Jack: Here it is. I do not understand the instruction.
Emma: It asks you to put the numbers in order.
Jack: Oh, I was trying to add them.
Emma: We can practise with an easier example first.
Jack: That will help me improve this skill.
Emma: Then your knowledge will grow step by step.
Jack: You would make a good teacher.
Emma: Maybe I will study education at college.
Jack: I think you should. Your answers are very clear.
Emma: Thanks. Now let us finish this question together.
Jack: After that, I will try the next one alone.
