085. Goals and Success
Words: goal, success, achieve, progress, ability, training, practise, improve, competition, award, fail, manage

Topic: Training for a Swimming Race

Amy: Luke, what is your goal for the swimming competition?
Luke: I want to finish the race in two minutes.
Amy: Do you think you can achieve that time?
Luke: Yes, if I continue my training.
Amy: How often do you practise?
Luke: I swim four mornings each week.
Amy: Your speed should improve with that work.
Luke: I have already made good progress.
Amy: You have the ability to do very well.
Luke: Thanks, but I may still fail to reach two minutes.
Amy: Success is not only about winning.
Luke: True. Finishing my training plan is also important.
Amy: What award does the winner receive?
Luke: A small cup, but I just want my best time.
Amy: I am sure you will manage the whole race.
Luke: I will try, and I will enjoy it too.
