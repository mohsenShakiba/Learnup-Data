033. Office Tasks
Words: schedule, task, project, report, data, chart, form, list, copy, print, organize, manager

Topic: Preparing for a Team Meeting

Sara: Tom, can you help me prepare for the team meeting?
Tom: Sure. Which task should we do first?
Sara: We need to organize the sales data for our project.
Tom: I can put the numbers into a clear chart.
Sara: Great. I will finish the written report.
Tom: Is the meeting on today's schedule?
Sara: Yes, the manager moved it to three o'clock.
Tom: Then we have two hours left.
Sara: Please make a list of the main results too.
Tom: Should I add a copy to every meeting form?
Sara: Yes, and print ten copies of the chart.
Tom: I will do that after I check the numbers.
Sara: Good. We do not want incorrect data in the meeting.
Tom: Everything is checked and ready to print.
Sara: Excellent. The manager will be pleased.
Tom: And we may even finish before three!
