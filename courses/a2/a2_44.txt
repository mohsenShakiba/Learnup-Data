044. Personal Relationships
Words: relationship, couple, married, divorced, marry, argument, argue, disagree, support, promise, care, feeling

Topic: Helping After an Argument

Anna: Paul, you look upset. Did something happen?
Paul: Mia and I had an argument about our holiday.
Anna: What did you disagree about?
Paul: I want the mountains, but she wants the coast.
Anna: Every couple can want different things.
Paul: I know, but we began to argue instead of listening.
Anna: A good relationship needs calm conversation.
Paul: We have been married for three years, and I care about her.
Anna: Then tell her that feeling first.
Paul: I will. I also promise to listen to her idea.
Anna: Good. You can support each other and find a fair choice.
Paul: Maybe we can spend two days in each place.
Anna: That sounds possible.
Paul: My parents divorced because they never talked calmly.
Anna: Your relationship does not have to follow theirs.
Paul: You are right. I would marry Mia again tomorrow.
