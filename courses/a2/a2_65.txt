065. Government and Society
Words: government, society, public, community, population, state, law, benefit, support, provide, decision, control

Topic: A New Community Bus

Amy: Luke, did you hear about the new community bus?
Luke: Yes. The local government made the decision yesterday.
Amy: It will provide transport to the hospital.
Luke: That will benefit many older people.
Amy: Our town's population has grown quickly.
Luke: Public services need to grow with it.
Amy: The state will support half of the bus cost.
Luke: Who will control the daily schedule?
Amy: The town transport office will do that.
Luke: Is there a law about free travel for children?
Amy: Yes, children under five can travel free.
Luke: A good bus service helps the whole society.
Amy: It also connects our community to the train station.
Luke: When does the first bus start?
Amy: Next Monday at seven in the morning.
Luke: Great. I will use it for my hospital visit.
