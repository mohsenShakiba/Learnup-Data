002. Clothes and What We Wear
Words: clothing, pants, sock, belt, suit, tie, pocket, button, size, item, attractive, comfortable

Topic: Dressing for a Wedding

Emma: Jack, are you ready for your cousin's wedding?
Jack: Almost, but I cannot decide what clothing to wear.
Emma: Your dark suit looks attractive and fits the event.
Jack: I agree, but these pants feel a little tight.
Emma: Are they the right size?
Jack: Yes, but I need a different belt with them.
Emma: Try this brown belt. It matches your shoes.
Jack: Good idea. Now I need to choose a tie.
Emma: The blue tie looks nice with your suit.
Jack: Thanks. Have you seen the missing button from my jacket?
Emma: It is in your pocket. You put it there yesterday.
Jack: I forgot! I also have only one clean sock.
Emma: The other sock is under that comfortable chair.
Jack: You have found every missing item in my room.
Emma: Yes, and now you look ready for the wedding.
Jack: I am ready, but my room needs your help too!
