023. Body Movement
Words: movement, step, jump, climb, lift, push, pull, throw, drop, rise, reach, carry

Topic: Moving the Old Sofa

Lina: Ben, can you help me carry this small sofa upstairs?
Ben: Yes, but every movement must be slow and careful.
Lina: I will lift this end, and you take the other end.
Ben: Good. Now pull it towards the first step.
Lina: Should I push while you climb?
Ben: Yes, but do not let your end drop.
Lina: The sofa cannot jump up the stairs by itself!
Ben: Sadly, it cannot. Let us rise together on three.
Lina: One, two, three. We reached the second step.
Ben: Only ten more steps to climb.
Lina: I cannot reach the side because this cushion is moving.
Ben: Throw the cushion onto the top floor.
Lina: Done. That made the sofa lighter.
Ben: Now push again while I move backwards.
Lina: We made it! The sofa is finally upstairs.
Ben: Great. I hope you never want it downstairs again.
