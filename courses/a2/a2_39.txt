039. Personal Safety
Words: safe, danger, dangerous, careful, carefully, prevent, protect, avoid, accident, hurt, kill, serious

Topic: A Safe Bike Ride

Nora: Sam, are you ready for our bike ride?
Sam: Almost. I am putting on my helmet now.
Nora: Good. It will protect your head in an accident.
Sam: I also checked my brakes carefully.
Nora: That can prevent a serious problem on the hill.
Sam: Is the main road dangerous today?
Nora: It is busy, so we should avoid it.
Sam: We can use the safe path through the park.
Nora: Yes, but be careful near people who are walking.
Sam: I will ring my bell before I pass them.
Nora: Do not ride too fast around the lake.
Sam: I know. Falling there could hurt.
Nora: The warning sign says deep water can kill.
Sam: I will stay far from that danger.
Nora: Great. We have checked everything now.
Sam: Then let us enjoy a safe ride.
