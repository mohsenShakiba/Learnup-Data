045. Parties and Celebrations
Words: celebrate, event, invitation, invite, guest, gift, surprise, concert, crowd, loud, arrange, wedding

Topic: A Garden Wedding Party

Sofia: Alex, shall we arrange the evening party for my sister's wedding?
Alex: Yes. How many people will you invite?
Sofia: There are forty names on the guest list.
Alex: That is a crowd, but the garden is large enough.
Sofia: I will send each person an invitation today.
Alex: What kind of music will help everyone celebrate?
Sofia: My cousin's band can give a short concert.
Alex: Great, but the music should not be too loud.
Sofia: I will ask them to use the small speakers.
Alex: Do you have a gift for the happy couple?
Sofia: Yes, but it is a surprise.
Alex: I will not ask what it is.
Sofia: This will be the biggest family event this year.
Alex: Do you need help with the tables?
Sofia: Please put them near the flowers.
Alex: Perfect. The wedding party is coming together.
