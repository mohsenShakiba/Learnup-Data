037. Staying Healthy
Words: health, fit, rest, asleep, stress, nervous, worried, relax, comfortable, care, pain, active

Topic: A Better Evening Routine

Eva: Liam, you look tired and worried today.
Liam: Work gives me a lot of stress before Friday.
Eva: Are you getting enough rest at night?
Liam: No. I am often not asleep until midnight.
Eva: That is not good for your health.
Liam: I know, but I feel nervous when I stop working.
Eva: You need to care for your body as well as your job.
Liam: What can I do to relax?
Eva: Take a short walk and stay active after dinner.
Liam: Walking might also help me stay fit.
Eva: Yes, and use a comfortable chair when you work.
Liam: My back has some pain because my chair is hard.
Eva: Stop using your computer one hour before bed too.
Liam: I will try that tonight.
Eva: Good. A quiet evening can help you sleep.
Liam: Thanks. I already feel calmer about it.
