038. At the Doctor's
Words: medical, patient, condition, disease, illness, drug, blood, heart, stomach, skin, headache, dentist

Topic: A Check After Dental Work

Mia: Daniel, what brings you to the medical centre today?
Daniel: I have felt ill since I visited the dentist yesterday.
Mia: Did the dentist give you a drug for pain?
Daniel: Yes, and I took it without eating.
Mia: That may explain your stomach problem.
Daniel: I also have a small red area on my skin.
Mia: I will check it. Do you have a headache?
Daniel: No, my head feels fine.
Mia: Good. I will also check your heart and blood pressure.
Daniel: Is my condition serious?
Mia: No. I do not see signs of a disease.
Daniel: That is good news.
Mia: You should take this drug only after food.
Daniel: I understand. Should I call the dentist too?
Mia: Yes, tell him about your short illness.
Daniel: Thank you. I am a less worried patient now.
