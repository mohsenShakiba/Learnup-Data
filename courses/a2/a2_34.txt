034. Time and Plans
Words: schedule, period, century, recent, recently, already, yet, moment, future, beginning, final, arrange

Topic: Planning a Museum Visit

Maya: Ben, can we arrange our history club visit now?
Ben: Yes. I recently received the museum schedule.
Maya: Is the new exhibition open yet?
Ben: Yes, it opened at the beginning of this week.
Maya: Which period of history does it show?
Ben: It shows daily life in the nineteenth century.
Maya: I read a recent article about that exhibition.
Ben: The museum has already added more old clothes to it.
Maya: Could we visit next Saturday?
Ben: Let me check the schedule for a moment.
Maya: We should avoid the final hour because it gets busy.
Ben: Then we can arrive at ten in the morning.
Maya: Great. I will tell the other club members.
Ben: Should we plan another visit in the future?
Maya: Yes, but let us enjoy this one first.
Ben: Agreed. I will book our tickets today.
