088. Creativity and Imagination
Words: creative, create, imagine, drawing, painting, fiction, novel, author, invent, invention, pattern, image

Topic: Designing a Story Cover

Nina: Leo, can you help me create a cover for my novel?
Leo: Of course. What kind of fiction story is it?
Nina: It is about an author who can enter her own books.
Leo: That is a creative idea. What image do you imagine?
Nina: A drawing of an open door inside a book.
Leo: We could add a blue light around the door.
Nina: Yes, and a star pattern in the background.
Leo: Should the cover look like a painting?
Nina: I think soft painted colours will work well.
Leo: Does the character invent anything in the story?
Nina: Yes. Her main invention is a magic pen.
Leo: Then put the pen beside the open book.
Nina: Great. That will show an important part of the story.
Leo: Do you want your name at the top?
Nina: Yes, because I am the author.
Leo: Perfect. Now we can start the final drawing.
