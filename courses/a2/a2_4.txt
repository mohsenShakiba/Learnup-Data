004. Things in the Home
Words: furniture, fridge, cupboard, lamp, mirror, roof, gate, lock, stair, hall, seat, towel

Topic: The Mixed-Up Moving Boxes

Maya: Ben, thank you for helping me unpack these boxes.
Ben: No problem. Which box should we open first?
Maya: Open the one in the hall marked "kitchen."
Ben: It has a lamp and a towel, but no kitchen things.
Maya: The moving company put the wrong names on the boxes.
Ben: Then unpacking your furniture is now a treasure hunt.
Maya: Put the lamp on the table beside the mirror.
Ben: Done. Should I take the towel upstairs?
Maya: Yes, but the top stair makes a loud noise.
Ben: I heard it! For a moment, I thought it was the roof.
Maya: The roof is fine. This old house just makes sounds.
Ben: Where is the box for the fridge?
Maya: It is next to the green seat by the cupboard.
Ben: This box says "fridge," but it is full of books.
Maya: At least the front gate is closed and the lock is on.
Ben: Good. Nothing can escape while we find the cups!
