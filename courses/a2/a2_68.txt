068. Applying for a Job
Words: apply, career, interview, experience, skill, ability, training, employee, salary, professional, form, reply

Topic: Completing a Job Application

Lily: Noah, can you help me apply for this hotel job?
Noah: Sure. Have you started the online form?
Lily: Yes, but it asks about my work experience.
Noah: Write about the year you worked in the cafe.
Lily: That job improved my customer service skill.
Noah: It also showed your ability to work in a team.
Lily: The hotel offers training to every new employee.
Noah: That could help you build a hotel career.
Lily: The salary is good for a first job too.
Noah: Your answers sound clear and professional.
Lily: Should I send the form now?
Noah: Check your phone number one more time.
Lily: It is correct. I will press the send button.
Noah: When will they reply?
Lily: They may invite me to an interview next week.
Noah: Great. Then we can practise your answers.
