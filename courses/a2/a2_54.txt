054. Geography and Maps
Words: continent, region, state, capital, population, area, coast, valley, desert, ocean, distance, earth

Topic: A Geography Map Game

Lina: Ben, will you help me with this map game?
Ben: Sure. Which continent should we study first?
Lina: Let us start with this region near the ocean.
Ben: Can you name the largest state in that area?
Lina: I think it is the yellow one on the map.
Ben: Correct. Now find its capital.
Lina: Is it the city beside the wide river?
Ben: Yes. It also has the largest population.
Lina: The coast looks very far from the capital.
Ben: The distance is about five hundred kilometres.
Lina: What is the brown area in the south?
Ben: That is a desert, and this green line is a valley.
Lina: Geography shows how different places on earth can be.
Ben: It does. Maps make those differences easier to see.
Lina: I think I understand this region now.
Ben: Great. You win the first round of our game.
