011. Food and a Healthy Diet
Words: diet, health, delicious, fresh, sweet, lemon, vegetable, biscuit, boil, recipe, plate, bowl

Topic: A Healthier Lunch

Amy: Luke, I want to make a healthier lunch today.
Luke: Good idea. A balanced diet is important for our health.
Amy: I found a simple vegetable soup recipe.
Luke: Great. We have fresh carrots and potatoes.
Amy: Please cut them while I boil some water.
Luke: Should I put the cut vegetables in this bowl?
Amy: Yes. Then we can add them to the hot water.
Luke: The recipe says to add a little lemon too.
Amy: That will make the soup taste fresh.
Luke: What should we put on this plate?
Amy: We can have some bread with the soup.
Luke: And can I have one sweet biscuit after lunch?
Amy: Yes, one biscuit is fine.
Luke: The soup smells delicious already.
Amy: Healthy food can taste very good.
Luke: I agree, especially when a biscuit is waiting!
