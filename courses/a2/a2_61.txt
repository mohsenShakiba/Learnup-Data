061. Problems in Big Cities
Words: crowded, noise, noisy, pollution, rubbish, transport, factory, smoke, danger, community, government, reduce

Topic: Improving a City Street

Lena: Mark, our street is very crowded this morning.
Mark: Yes, and the traffic noise is getting worse.
Lena: The old buses are noisy and produce dark smoke.
Mark: That smoke adds to the city's air pollution.
Lena: Public transport should be cleaner.
Mark: The government plans to buy electric buses next year.
Lena: That could reduce traffic and dirty air.
Mark: The factory outside the city is also a problem.
Lena: I heard it will get new, cleaner equipment.
Mark: Good. What can our community do now?
Lena: We can start with the rubbish near the station.
Mark: Let us organize a clean-up on Saturday.
Lena: We should wear bright jackets because cars are a danger.
Mark: I will ask our neighbours to join.
Lena: Small changes can make city life better.
Mark: Yes, and we do not need to wait until next year.
