086. Learning from Mistakes
Words: mistake, error, fail, correct, improve, practise, experience, remember, forget, understand, progress, success

Topic: Baking a Better Cake

Sara: Tom, why did our first cake fail yesterday?
Tom: I made a mistake with the oven temperature.
Sara: Was that the only error?
Tom: No, I also forgot to add the milk.
Sara: We should write the correct amounts on this card.
Tom: Good idea. Then I will not forget again.
Sara: Do you understand every step now?
Tom: Yes. The first experience taught me a lot.
Sara: We need to practise if we want to improve.
Tom: I will remember to check the oven first.
Sara: The new cake is rising well.
Tom: That is real progress!
Sara: It also smells much better.
Tom: Success may be only twenty minutes away.
Sara: Let us wait before we celebrate.
Tom: Agreed. This time, I will watch the oven.
