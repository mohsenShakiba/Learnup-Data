018. Directions and Position
Words: direction, towards, along, cross, straight, forward, inside, bottom, top, middle, side, somewhere

Topic: Finding the Museum Cafe

Nora: Sam, the museum cafe is somewhere on this map.
Sam: I think it is inside the building on the east side.
Nora: Which direction should we go from here?
Sam: Walk straight towards the large stairs.
Nora: Do we go to the top?
Sam: No, stay at the bottom and turn left.
Nora: Then do we walk along this long hall?
Sam: Yes, and cross the hall near the middle.
Nora: I see a sign pointing forward.
Sam: That sign says the cafe is beside the gift shop.
Nora: Is the gift shop on the right side?
Sam: Yes. We should see it after the next door.
Nora: There it is, and the cafe is open.
Sam: Good. Following a museum map made me hungry.
Nora: We only walked for five minutes!
Sam: Reading directions uses a lot of energy.
