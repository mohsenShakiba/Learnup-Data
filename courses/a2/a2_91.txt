091. Causes and Effects
Words: cause, effect, affect, result, reason, matter, condition, situation, problem, solution, increase, reduce

Topic: Solving a High Power Bill

Emma: Jack, our power bill had a large increase this month.
Jack: We need to find the cause of the problem.
Emma: Could the cold weather be the reason?
Jack: It may affect how often we use the heater.
Emma: The heater is old and in poor condition.
Jack: That can have a big effect on energy use.
Emma: What solution can reduce the bill?
Jack: We can turn the heat down at night.
Emma: Will one degree really matter?
Jack: Yes. Small changes can have a useful result.
Emma: We should also close the living room door.
Jack: Good. That will keep heat in one area.
Emma: If the situation does not improve, what then?
Jack: We can ask someone to check the heater.
Emma: Let us try the simple steps first.
Jack: Agreed. We will compare the bill next month.
