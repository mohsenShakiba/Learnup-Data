049. The Countryside
Words: village, field, grass, forest, hill, valley, ground, grow, natural, wild, season, lake

Topic: A Walk Around the Farm

Ruby: Ben, your village is quieter than the city.
Ben: Yes. Would you like to walk around the farm?
Ruby: I would. What do you grow in this field?
Ben: We grow corn here during the warm season.
Ruby: The grass beside it is very green.
Ben: The ground stays wet because the lake is near.
Ruby: Can we see the lake from that hill?
Ben: Yes, and the whole valley is clear from the top.
Ruby: What is beyond the valley?
Ben: There is a large forest with natural walking paths.
Ruby: Do many wild animals live there?
Ben: Mostly rabbits, deer, and small birds.
Ruby: This area has everything: fields, water, and trees.
Ben: That is why my family likes living here.
Ruby: Let us climb the hill before lunch.
Ben: Good idea. I will show you the best view.
