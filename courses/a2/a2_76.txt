076. Inventions and Technology
Words: invent, invention, discover, discovery, equipment, tool, program, tablet, network, power, modern, useful

Topic: A Solar School Bag

Sara: Tom, what will you make for the invention contest?
Tom: I want to invent a solar school bag.
Sara: How will your invention work?
Tom: A small solar panel will collect power from sunlight.
Sara: What will the power be used for?
Tom: It can charge a tablet or another useful tool.
Sara: That is a modern idea for students.
Tom: I need some equipment to build the first model.
Sara: Can the school science room help?
Tom: Yes, and our teacher found a simple design program.
Sara: Will the bag connect to a network?
Tom: No. It only makes and stores power.
Sara: What do you hope to discover during the test?
Tom: I want to know how fast it charges a tablet.
Sara: That discovery will improve your final model.
Tom: Exactly. I will start building it tomorrow.
