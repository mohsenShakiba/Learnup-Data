087. Memory and Thinking
Words: memory, remember, forget, thinking, thought, mind, imagine, realize, understand, knowledge, believe, guess

Topic: Finding the Missing Keys

Mia: Jack, I cannot remember where I put my keys.
Jack: What is your last clear memory of them?
Mia: I believe I had them when I opened the door.
Jack: Think about every step after that.
Mia: I am thinking, but my mind is blank.
Jack: Did you put them on the kitchen table?
Mia: No. I would not forget them there.
Jack: My next guess is your coat pocket.
Mia: I checked it already.
Jack: Imagine you are entering the house again.
Mia: I opened the door and carried in the shopping.
Jack: What did you do with the bags?
Mia: I put them on the chair. Now I realize something.
Jack: You thought the keys were in your hand.
Mia: Yes, and now I understand. They are inside one shopping bag!
Jack: Good. Our little test improved your knowledge of yourself.
