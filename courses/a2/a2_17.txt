017. Places in Town
Words: station, university, college, building, hall, tower, bridge, store, factory, underground, gate, public

Topic: A Quick Town Tour

Sofia: Alex, shall I show you the town before lunch?
Alex: Yes, please. I need to find the station first.
Sofia: The underground station is behind that glass building.
Alex: Is that building part of the university?
Sofia: No, it is the public library.
Alex: Then where is the university?
Sofia: Cross the bridge and walk past the old clock tower.
Alex: I can see a large gate over there.
Sofia: That gate leads to the university hall.
Alex: Is the college in the same area?
Sofia: Yes, it is the smaller building beside it.
Alex: Good. I also need a store that sells notebooks.
Sofia: There is one between the college and the station.
Alex: What is the tall brick building outside town?
Sofia: It was a factory, but now it is an art centre.
Alex: Your short tour has answered all my questions!
