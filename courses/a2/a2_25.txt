025. Art and Pictures
Words: drawing, painting, image, colour, shape, pattern, creative, cartoon, poster, print, create, appearance

Topic: Making a Festival Poster

Anna: Jake, can you help me create a poster for the school festival?
Jake: Sure. What image should we put in the middle?
Anna: I made a drawing of students dancing.
Jake: It is very creative. We could add a bright colour behind it.
Anna: How about a round yellow shape like the sun?
Jake: Good idea. A simple pattern can go around the edge.
Anna: Should we include this small cartoon of our teacher?
Jake: Yes, but give him a happy appearance.
Anna: He is smiling in my cartoon.
Jake: Perfect. Is this painting part of the festival too?
Anna: Yes, so I will add its name under the main image.
Jake: The poster looks full now, but it is still clear.
Anna: Shall we print twenty copies?
Jake: First, let us check the date and time.
Anna: They are correct. We can print it now.
Jake: Great. Our poster should bring many people to the festival.
