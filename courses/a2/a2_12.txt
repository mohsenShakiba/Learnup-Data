012. Eating at a Restaurant
Words: serve, order, bowl, plate, fork, knife, recipe, bar, tip, guest, delicious, choice

Topic: Dinner at the New Restaurant

Mia: Jack, thank you for bringing me to this new restaurant.
Jack: You are my guest tonight, so choose anything you like.
Mia: The soup looks delicious. I think I will order that.
Jack: Good choice. It comes in a large bowl.
Mia: What are you having?
Jack: I will have the fish on the blue plate.
Mia: The menu says it is a family recipe.
Jack: Then it should be good. Shall we order drinks too?
Mia: Yes, they serve fresh juice at the bar.
Jack: I will ask for two orange juices.
Mia: The waiter brought a fork, but I also need a knife.
Jack: I will ask him when he brings our food.
Mia: Everything arrived quickly, and the soup smells lovely.
Jack: My fish is excellent too.
Mia: We should leave a tip for the kind waiter.
Jack: Definitely. He made our first visit very pleasant.
