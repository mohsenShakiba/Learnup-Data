007. Everyday Routines
Words: daily, everyday, habit, usually, normally, washing, brush, prepare, rest, asleep, schedule, tidy

Topic: The Missing Alarm

Sara: Tom, why are you still asleep at eight o'clock?
Tom: My alarm did not ring, so my daily schedule is late.
Sara: You normally get up at seven. Is the alarm broken?
Tom: No, I forgot to turn it on last night.
Sara: That is becoming an everyday habit.
Tom: I know. I usually prepare everything before bed.
Sara: Start now. I am washing my face, so the bathroom is free soon.
Tom: Great. I need to brush my teeth and wash my hair.
Sara: Your clean clothes are on the tidy chair.
Tom: Thanks. Did you also prepare breakfast?
Sara: Yes, there is toast and fruit in the kitchen.
Tom: You are saving my morning.
Sara: Only today. You need a better evening routine.
Tom: I will set the alarm before I rest tonight.
Sara: Put it across the room, so you must stand up.
Tom: Good idea. Tomorrow, I will be awake before you!
