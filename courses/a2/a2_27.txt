027. Films and Cartoons
Words: cartoon, scene, character, act, audience, stage, fiction, image, title, review, exciting, popular

Topic: Reviewing a Cartoon Film

Ella: Max, did you enjoy the cartoon film?
Max: Yes. It was funny and exciting.
Ella: My favourite character was the little green dragon.
Max: Mine too. The audience laughed whenever he tried to fly.
Ella: Which scene did you like best?
Max: The final scene on the theatre stage was great.
Ella: I liked how the dragon tried to act like a king.
Max: The story was fiction, but the feelings seemed real.
Ella: The title, "A Dragon at School," was perfect.
Max: It is already a popular film with children.
Ella: The bright image on the poster helped too.
Max: We should write a short review for the school page.
Ella: I will say the story is simple and fun.
Max: I will mention the music and the funny characters.
Ella: Do you think other students will enjoy it?
Max: Yes. I would happily watch it again.
