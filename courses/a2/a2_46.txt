046. Having Guests at Home
Words: guest, invite, invitation, greet, prepare, tidy, comfortable, seat, towel, sheet, offer, serve

Topic: Preparing the Guest Room

Maya: Ben, Aunt Lucy accepted our invitation for the weekend.
Ben: Great. She will be our first overnight guest.
Maya: We need to prepare the small bedroom for her.
Ben: I will tidy the desk and clean the floor.
Maya: Please put a fresh sheet on the bed too.
Ben: Done. The bed looks comfortable now.
Maya: Put a clean towel beside the lamp.
Ben: Should we add a soft seat near the window?
Maya: Yes, she likes to read in the morning.
Ben: What food will we serve when she arrives?
Maya: I will make soup and fresh bread.
Ben: We can offer her tea before dinner.
Maya: Good idea. I will greet her at the door.
Ben: Why did you invite her this weekend?
Maya: She wants to see our new home.
Ben: Then everything is ready for her visit.
