079. Measuring Things
Words: size, height, weight, distance, temperature, speed, amount, quantity, level, average, exact, figure

Topic: Measuring for a New Shelf

Amy: Luke, will this new shelf fit beside the window?
Luke: Let us measure the exact distance first.
Amy: The space is one hundred centimetres wide.
Luke: Good. What is the height of the shelf?
Amy: The figure on the box says eighty centimetres.
Luke: Then the size is right for the room.
Amy: Can the floor take its weight?
Luke: Yes. The shelf is light when it is empty.
Amy: What quantity of books can it hold?
Luke: About sixty books of average size.
Amy: That is a large amount for a small shelf.
Luke: Do not fill it above the safe level.
Amy: The room temperature will not affect it, will it?
Luke: No. Only water could damage the wood.
Amy: We measured everything at great speed.
Luke: Yes. Now building it will take much longer!
