055. Countries and Cultures
Words: culture, foreign, abroad, continent, state, capital, traditional, tradition, language, population, society, visitor

Topic: Meeting an Exchange Visitor

Nora: Sam, our new exchange visitor arrives at school today.
Sam: Great. Which foreign country is she from?
Nora: She is from a small state in South America.
Sam: So she travelled from another continent.
Nora: Yes. This is her first time abroad.
Sam: Does she speak our language?
Nora: A little, and she wants to practise with us.
Sam: What is the capital of her country?
Nora: I do not know yet, but we can ask her.
Sam: We should also ask about its population and society.
Nora: Yes, but one question at a time.
Sam: Good point. I would like to learn about her culture.
Nora: She is bringing traditional food for our class.
Sam: Food is a great way to share a tradition.
Nora: Here she comes with the teacher now.
Sam: Let us welcome her and show her our school.
