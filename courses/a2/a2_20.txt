020. Ways of Travelling
Words: transport, vehicle, lorry, ship, driving, passenger, wheel, gas, underground, station, speed, cycle

Topic: Choosing Transport for the Trip

Ivy: Mark, how should we travel to the coast tomorrow?
Mark: We could take the underground to the central station.
Ivy: Then we could catch a train from there.
Mark: Yes. Public transport will be easier than driving.
Ivy: Your car is a small vehicle for such a long trip.
Mark: It also needs gas, and that costs a lot now.
Ivy: A lorry moved very slowly in front of us last time.
Mark: I remember. One wheel had a problem.
Ivy: Could we travel by ship from the river?
Mark: The ship is for tours, not for this route.
Ivy: I would like to cycle, but the coast is too far.
Mark: My cycling speed would make the trip take two days.
Ivy: Then the train is our best choice.
Mark: We can relax as a passenger instead of watching the road.
Ivy: Great. I will buy the tickets online now.
Mark: And I will check the train time from the station.
