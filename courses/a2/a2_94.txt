094. Population and Communities
Words: population, community, society, region, state, capital, continent, individual, member, neighbour, crowded, government

Topic: Planning a New Community Centre

Lena: Mark, our community needs a larger meeting centre.
Mark: Yes. The town population has grown quickly.
Lena: The old hall becomes crowded every weekend.
Mark: Has the local government offered any help?
Lena: Yes, the state will pay half the building cost.
Mark: That is good for every member of the town.
Lena: My neighbour says we also need a children's room.
Mark: One individual idea can help the whole plan.
Lena: This region has many young families now.
Mark: People are moving here from the capital.
Lena: Some have even come from another continent.
Mark: A welcoming centre can help them join our society.
Lena: We should ask everyone what the building needs.
Mark: Let us hold a public meeting next Tuesday.
Lena: I will book the school hall.
Mark: Great. The community can plan it together.
