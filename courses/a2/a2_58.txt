058. Energy and Heat
Words: power, gas, oil, heat, burn, temperature, factory, equipment, produce, effect, environment, pollution

Topic: Visiting a Modern Power Factory

Maya: Leo, this power factory is quieter than I expected.
Leo: Yes. Most of its equipment is new.
Maya: What does the factory use to produce energy?
Leo: It mainly uses sunlight, but it keeps gas for emergencies.
Maya: Does it burn oil too?
Leo: No. Burning oil would create more pollution.
Maya: How do these large machines use heat?
Leo: The heat makes water move through the equipment.
Maya: That water must reach a high temperature.
Leo: It does, so workers cannot touch the pipes.
Maya: Does this factory have a good effect on the town?
Leo: Yes. It gives clean power to many homes.
Maya: That is better for the environment.
Leo: It also costs less to operate now.
Maya: I learned a lot during this visit.
Leo: Me too. Clean energy is easier to understand here.
