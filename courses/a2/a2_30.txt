030. Learning a Language
Words: language, phrase, spelling, meaning, dictionary, repeat, explain, understand, remember, conversation, dialogue, practise

Topic: Practising at a Pretend Cafe

Maya: Ben, can we practise our English cafe dialogue?
Ben: Yes. I want to remember each useful phrase.
Maya: I will be the server, and you can be the customer.
Ben: Good. Please speak slowly so I can understand.
Maya: Welcome. What would you like to order?
Ben: Could you repeat that question, please?
Maya: Of course. What would you like to order?
Ben: I would like tea and cake, please.
Maya: Excellent. You used the language very naturally.
Ben: What is the meaning of "excellent"?
Maya: It means very good. I can explain it after our conversation.
Ben: Thanks. I will check the spelling in my dictionary too.
Maya: Now ask me how much the cake costs.
Ben: How much does the cake cost?
Maya: Perfect. Our short conversation is finished.
Ben: This kind of practice helps me learn quickly.
