056. The Natural Environment
Words: environment, nature, earth, climate, pollution, recycle, rubbish, oil, gas, forest, ocean, protect

Topic: Cleaning the Beach

Ella: Max, thank you for joining the beach clean-up.
Max: I want to help protect the ocean and its animals.
Ella: This plastic rubbish is bad for the environment.
Max: We can recycle these bottles and metal cans.
Ella: Put them in the blue bags, please.
Max: What should we do with this old oil bottle?
Ella: Keep it separate. Oil can cause water pollution.
Max: Can gas also harm the air?
Ella: Yes. Using less oil and gas can help the climate.
Max: Our choices affect nature in many ways.
Ella: They affect the whole earth, not only this beach.
Max: I also joined a clean-up in the forest last month.
Ella: Was there much rubbish there?
Max: Yes, but thirty people helped remove it.
Ella: Small actions can protect many places.
Max: Agreed. Let us fill one more bag today.
