090. Managing Change
Words: change, replace, remove, add, increase, reduce, improve, become, continue, progress, result, effect

Topic: Improving the Cafe Menu

Sofia: Alex, customers want us to change the cafe menu.
Alex: What do they want us to improve?
Sofia: They want more healthy lunch choices.
Alex: We could add a fresh vegetable soup.
Sofia: Good. We can replace one fried meal with it.
Alex: Should we remove all the sweet cakes?
Sofia: No, but we can reduce the number we make.
Alex: That may increase fruit sales.
Sofia: It could also have a good effect on food waste.
Alex: When will the new menu become available?
Sofia: Next Monday, if we continue making progress.
Alex: We should ask customers about the result.
Sofia: Yes, we can give them a short form.
Alex: Their answers will help with the next change.
Sofia: Great. I will write the new menu today.
Alex: And I will test the soup recipe.
