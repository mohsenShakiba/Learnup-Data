060. Natural Disasters
Words: disaster, storm, wave, wind, earth, destroy, danger, dead, death, coast, protect, suddenly

Topic: Preparing for a Coastal Storm

Sara: Tom, the town has sent a storm warning.
Tom: Is there danger near our part of the coast?
Sara: Yes. Strong wind and a high wave may reach the road.
Tom: Then we should leave before the weather changes suddenly.
Sara: I have packed water, food, and our papers.
Tom: Good. I will close the windows to protect the house.
Sara: A storm can destroy things, but people come first.
Tom: Where will we stay during the disaster?
Sara: The school on the hill is a safe centre.
Tom: The last large storm caused one death here.
Sara: I remember. No one wants another person dead.
Tom: The earth can be powerful near the ocean.
Sara: Yes, so we must follow the warning.
Tom: The car is ready, and our neighbours are leaving too.
Sara: Let us check that the old couple next door knows.
Tom: Good idea. Then we can all go together.
