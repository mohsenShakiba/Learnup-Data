078. Numbers and Data
Words: data, chart, figure, amount, quantity, average, per cent, count, level, result, compare, exact

Topic: Studying a Travel Survey

Ruby: Ben, have you finished counting our travel survey?
Ben: Yes. I put all the data into this chart.
Ruby: What is the exact number of students?
Ben: The total figure is one hundred.
Ruby: That makes each per cent easy to understand.
Ben: Yes. Forty students travel by bus.
Ruby: Can we compare that with the bicycle result?
Ben: Of course. Only twenty students cycle.
Ruby: What amount of time does the average trip take?
Ben: The average is eighteen minutes.
Ruby: Did we count the quantity of car trips too?
Ben: Yes. Thirty students come by car.
Ruby: The bar for walking is at a low level on the chart.
Ben: It is. Only ten students walk.
Ruby: This chart makes every result clear.
Ben: Great. We can show it to the class tomorrow.
