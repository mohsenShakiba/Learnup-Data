026. Books and Stories
Words: author, novel, fiction, article, title, page, character, scene, beginning, final, description, dialogue

Topic: Choosing a Book Club Story

Sara: Tom, which book should we read for our book club?
Tom: I found a short fiction novel by a local author.
Sara: What is the title?
Tom: It is called "The Last Green Door."
Sara: What happens at the beginning?
Tom: The main character finds a key on page one.
Sara: Does the book give a clear description of the key?
Tom: Yes. It is old, silver, and very small.
Sara: Is there much dialogue in the story?
Tom: Yes, especially in the cafe scene.
Sara: That sounds easier to read than a long history article.
Tom: It is. The story is only eighty pages.
Sara: Do not tell me the final part.
Tom: I will not. The ending is the best part.
Sara: Then let us choose this novel for Friday.
Tom: Great. I will tell the other club members.
