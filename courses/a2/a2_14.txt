014. Nature Near Us
Words: nature, grass, forest, field, hill, valley, rock, stone, ground, lake, wood, wild

Topic: A Walk to the Lake

Ruby: Ben, this path through the forest is beautiful.
Ben: Yes, I enjoy being close to nature.
Ruby: The grass in that field is very green.
Ben: It grows well because the ground is wet here.
Ruby: Shall we climb the small hill ahead?
Ben: Yes. We can see the valley from the top.
Ruby: Be careful of that large rock on the path.
Ben: Thanks. There are many small stones here too.
Ruby: Is that old bridge made of wood?
Ben: Yes, and it crosses a small river.
Ruby: The river must flow into the lake below.
Ben: It does. We can rest beside the water.
Ruby: Do any wild animals live in this forest?
Ben: Only small animals, such as rabbits and birds.
Ruby: Good. I brought lunch, but not enough for a bear.
Ben: Then we are lucky there are no bears here!
