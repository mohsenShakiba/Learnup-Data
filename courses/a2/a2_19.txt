019. Travel and Tourism
Words: journey, tour, visitor, guest, passenger, station, route, distance, transport, abroad, foreign, guide

Topic: The First Train Tour

Maya: Leo, is this your first journey abroad?
Leo: Yes. I have never visited a foreign country before.
Maya: Then I am happy to be your guide today.
Leo: Thank you. This station is much larger than mine at home.
Maya: Our train is the fastest transport to the old town.
Leo: How long is the route?
Maya: The distance is only thirty kilometres.
Leo: Good. I can enjoy the view as a passenger.
Maya: After the train ride, we will join a walking tour.
Leo: Will there be another visitor with us?
Maya: Yes, a small group will meet us at the town gate.
Leo: I hope I can understand the guide.
Maya: She speaks slowly for every foreign guest.
Leo: Perfect. I want to learn about the old castle.
Maya: You will, and we will have lunch beside it.
Leo: This is already a great first trip abroad.
