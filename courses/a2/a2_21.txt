021. Holidays by the Coast
Words: coast, ocean, lake, tour, journey, visitor, guest, pack, abroad, foreign, adventure, relax

Topic: Packing for the Coast

Eva: Liam, are you ready to pack for our holiday?
Liam: Almost. This is my first trip abroad.
Eva: Mine too. Visiting a foreign coast will be exciting.
Liam: The hotel is beside the ocean, so I packed my swimsuit.
Eva: Good. We can relax on the beach after our journey.
Liam: Did you book the boat tour?
Eva: Yes. Every guest meets at the harbour at nine.
Liam: Will the tour visit the small island?
Eva: It will, and each visitor can walk around it.
Liam: That sounds like a real adventure.
Eva: We can also visit a lake near the hotel.
Liam: Then I should pack my walking shoes.
Eva: Yes, and do not forget your sun hat.
Liam: It is already in my bag.
Eva: Great. We only need our tickets now.
Liam: I have them. The coast is waiting for us!
