051. Flying Abroad
Words: flying, passenger, abroad, foreign, journey, transport, distance, pack, gate, seat, route, tour

Topic: A First Flight Abroad

Amy: Luke, have you finished packing for our journey?
Luke: Yes. I cannot believe we are flying abroad tomorrow.
Amy: It will be my first visit to a foreign country.
Luke: Mine too. Did you pack your passport?
Amy: Yes, it is in the front of my bag.
Luke: Good. Our airport transport arrives at six.
Amy: Which gate does our flight leave from?
Luke: Gate twelve, but we should check the screen.
Amy: Do you know our seat numbers?
Luke: Yes. You have the window seat, and I am beside you.
Amy: Great. I want to see the whole route from the air.
Luke: It is a long distance, so bring a book too.
Amy: Every passenger also gets a small meal.
Luke: After we land, we join a city tour.
Amy: This trip has a very busy beginning.
Luke: We can sleep on the plane!
