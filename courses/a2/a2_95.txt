095. Culture and Tradition
Words: culture, tradition, traditional, ancient, century, society, language, character, public, event, foreign, modern

Topic: Preparing a Culture Festival

Nora: Sam, our culture festival is next Saturday.
Sam: Yes. The public event will fill the town square.
Nora: My group will perform a traditional dance.
Sam: Is it an ancient tradition?
Nora: It began more than a century ago.
Sam: Does the dance tell a story?
Nora: Yes. Each dancer plays a different character.
Sam: I am helping at the language table.
Nora: Will foreign visitors learn simple local words?
Sam: Yes, and they can teach us words from their countries.
Nora: That shows how culture can connect people.
Sam: Our modern society includes many traditions.
Nora: We should put signs beside each activity.
Sam: Good idea. Everyone will know what is happening.
Nora: I am excited to share our dance.
Sam: And I am ready to learn some new words.
