066. The Education System
Words: education, university, college, teach, subject, exam, homework, training, knowledge, skill, improve, government

Topic: Choosing the Next Step

Sara: Tom, have you decided what to study next year?
Tom: I want to improve my computer skill.
Sara: You could take a training course at the local college.
Tom: Does the college teach web design?
Sara: Yes, it is a popular subject there.
Tom: Will I have a lot of homework?
Sara: Some, and there is one final exam.
Tom: That sounds possible while I work.
Sara: The course can also prepare you for university.
Tom: I may study computer science there later.
Sara: More education can increase your knowledge and choices.
Tom: Does the government help with course costs?
Sara: Yes, this training course has some support.
Tom: Great. I will visit the college tomorrow.
Sara: Take the course information with you.
Tom: I will. This may be my next big step.
