035. Special Days and Events
Words: event, wedding, celebrate, invitation, invite, guest, gift, surprise, concert, arrangement, prepare, crowd

Topic: A Surprise Anniversary Party

Lily: Noah, let us prepare a surprise for Mum and Dad.
Noah: Good idea. Their wedding anniversary is next Friday.
Lily: We can celebrate with a small garden party.
Noah: How many people should we invite?
Lily: I made a guest list with twelve names.
Noah: That is enough. A large crowd would spoil the surprise.
Lily: I will send each person an invitation tonight.
Noah: What food arrangement should we make?
Lily: We can prepare sandwiches and one big cake.
Noah: I also found their first wedding photo.
Lily: Lovely. We can put it beside their gift.
Noah: Should we play music from their favourite concert?
Lily: Yes. They always tell stories about that event.
Noah: How will we get them into the garden?
Lily: I will say that the flowers need help.
Noah: Perfect. They will not guess our plan.
