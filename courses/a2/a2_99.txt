099. Global Challenges
Words: climate, pollution, population, environment, disaster, disease, oil, gas, government, society, protect, prevent

Topic: Ideas for a School Display

Mia: Jack, our school display is about global challenges.
Jack: Let us start with the environment and climate.
Mia: We can show how air pollution affects cities.
Jack: Cars that use oil and gas are part of the problem.
Mia: Public transport can help prevent some pollution.
Jack: What should we include about population growth?
Mia: We can explain that more people need more food and water.
Jack: A fair society should share these things well.
Mia: We also need a section about disease.
Jack: Clean water can protect people from some illnesses.
Mia: Should we mention a natural disaster too?
Jack: Yes, and explain how towns can prepare.
Mia: The government has a role in every section.
Jack: It can make rules and support useful projects.
Mia: Our final display will offer simple actions.
Jack: Good. Big challenges need many people to help.
