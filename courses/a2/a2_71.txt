071. Money and Budgeting
Words: cash, credit, cost, expensive, cheap, salary, earn, spend, amount, quantity, per cent, benefit

Topic: Saving for a Laptop

Emma: Jack, I want to save for a new laptop.
Jack: How much does the one you like cost?
Emma: It is expensive. The price is six hundred pounds.
Jack: What amount can you save from your salary each month?
Emma: I earn eight hundred pounds and can save one hundred.
Jack: Then you need six months if you spend carefully.
Emma: The store offers ten per cent off next month.
Jack: That discount is a real benefit.
Emma: I also found a cheaper laptop online.
Jack: Check its quality. Cheap is not always better.
Emma: True. I need a good laptop for work.
Jack: Will you pay with cash or credit?
Emma: Cash, because I do not want to borrow money.
Jack: Buy a smaller quantity of takeaway food each week.
Emma: Good idea. I can cook at home more often.
Jack: Then your laptop money will grow faster.
